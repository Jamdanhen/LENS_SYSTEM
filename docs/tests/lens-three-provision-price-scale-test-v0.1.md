# Three-Provision Acquisition Price and Scale Test

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: Executed pricing proposals for discussion. No item price or Magnitude adopted.

## Objective

Following user agreement to continue with the straight x5 allowance, compare
ordinary equipment, a one-person working facility, and a larger holding.

```text
Additional funds = B x 5 x purchased Assets Rank
Trial acquisition price = B x within-frame price units x Scale multiplier
B = 200 in the displayed fantasy illustration
```

The free starting package is separate throughout. Scale multiplies the
acquisition price, never the buyer's allowance. This is a deterministic
construction comparison, not an acquisition roll or upkeep test.

## Pricing Proposals and Provenance

| Provision | Within-frame price in B units | Trial acquisition magnitude | Final cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Additional ordinary personal package | 1 | M1, x1 | 200 |
| Established one-person workshop | 25 | M1, x1 | 5,000 |
| Larger defensible holding | 40 | M2, x1.6 | 12,800 |

The ordinary package price follows directly from the defined Profile reference.

The workshop price is deliberately selected to preserve the earlier five-Rank
construction reference as a control under the new allowance. It is NOT a
verified currency conversion of the former five allocation points. Choosing
this endpoint tests the consequences; it does not prove the workshop is worth
twenty-five ordinary packages.

The holding's 40 B unscaled price carries forward the previous sensitivity
fixture. M2 is a trial placement only. Neither is inferred from a published
castle price. The resulting 12,800 cost is not evidence, by itself, that a
particular fictional holding deserves M2. Do not select Magnitude solely to
force it beyond a starting budget.

## Contents Held Constant

**Ordinary package:** A complete alternative ordinary loadout comparable to
the Profile's free package. It equips a person or provides useful reserves;
it does not create a follower, training, or an equipment-modifier upgrade.

**Workshop:** Controlled tools and installed equipment for one stated trade,
storage, usable rented workspace, and materials for an initial ordinary job.
It supports one professional's work. It is not an independently staffed
business, unlimited materials, perpetual prepaid rent, or purchased income.
These contents preserve the earlier whole-provision example.

**Holding:** Owned habitable defensible premises with accommodation, storage,
water, stable space, ordinary defenses, and immediate grounds. No garrison,
productive estate income, workshop, or governing jurisdiction is automatically
included. This tests a higher-acquisition-magnitude treatment of such a
provision without overwriting the earlier provisional ten-point manor entry.

## Scale Sensitivity

Hold the same 40 B unscaled price constant:

| Magnitude | Price | Individual Assets 10 comparison |
|---|---:|---|
| M1 | 8,000 | Fits with 2,000 remaining |
| M2 | 12,800 | Short by 2,800 |
| M3 | 20,480 | Short by 10,480 |

This establishes the price effect of the current Scale candidate, not which
row any named property belongs in. A Profile must justify the acquisition
frame as well as the within-frame expense. Expensive personal equipment is
not automatically higher Scale, and owning a property does not amplify every
Expression by its owner.

## Check Against Using Peer Prices

Do not apply the trial M2 factor to a peer's completed manor price and call
the result a verified translation. The values above are proposed LENS inputs,
not copied final prices with a second magnitude charge. Peer catalogues remain
comparisons for relative purchasing power, not independent proof of this
particular workshop or holding placement.

## Findings

The proposed references produce meaningful allocation alternatives and allow
larger purchases to exceed individual starting funds while remaining possible
through actual pooled resources. No nonlinear buyer budget or new financial
permission system is needed.

The comparison does NOT establish equal utility or objective economic value.
Its substantive unresolved judgment is the relative valuation: twenty-five
ordinary packages for the specified workshop and sixty-four for the trial
holding after Scale. These amounts should be shown to the user as proposals,
not reported as independently validated prices.

Recommendation: the x5 allowance remains usable; review these concrete
provision valuations before adopting a price ladder. If the workshop is too
expensive or inexpensive for its contents, revise that valuation rather than
changing the allowance or assigning Scale to produce a desired minimum Rank.

Upkeep, HOLT benefits, and limitation discounts remain deferred. The next
real decision is the proposed relative prices and the Profile's acquisition
frame, not another approval to perform the same arithmetic.

## Subsequent User Candidate: Maintained Household

Date: 2026-09-06. Retained for later valuation; not adopted or numerically tested.

The user suggested that a modest manor alone might fall below the highest
starting provision, while a modest staffed holding with enough established
self-sufficiency to support one staff household might justify a higher value,
without specifying a target Rank. The user's mention of nine was illustrative
only; the user explicitly rejected retaining it as a candidate placement.
Determine the contents and justified acquisition cost first, then derive
affordability from the Assets allowance. Do not work backward from Rank nine.

The proposed purchase includes an ongoing functioning arrangement: habitable
premises, a small resident staff household, and the ordinary support that lets
them maintain the place. It is not merely an empty building whose owner must
repeatedly roll to prevent routine collapse or abandonment.

When such ordinary support is part of the stated provision, routine operation
can remain established without a sustaining Difficulty check every month.
A meaningful narrative disruption can create actual uncertainty or pressure;
the passage of time alone need not manufacture it. This candidate does not
make the holding immune to loss, promise unlimited surplus income, or supply
unlimited services beyond the household's established role.

Future comparisons should distinguish premises alone, premises with ordinary
staffing, and an ordinarily self-supporting household. These are differences
in what is acquired, not three mandatory tracks or new subsystems. No staff
roster, production ratio, upkeep frequency, or limitation discount is fixed.
The preceding 12,800 fixture does not constrain the eventual price of this
different whole provision. The user has not adopted the asset system as a
finished whole or approved the separate stocked-workshop proposal by making
this observation.

The subsequent bottom-up test is recorded in
`lens-maintained-household-bottom-up-test-v0.1.md`.
