# Dwarf Package: Dense Build Context Test

Status: User selected Dense Build as the leading negative candidate and
authorized this comparison. Applicability and package-interaction test only;
no modifier magnitude, discount, final package, or species-wide rule adopted.

## Matched Construction

Compare the working package with and without Dense Build, holding everything
else equal: darkvision, natural-poison Resistance Resonance, stone-related
Observation Resonance, ordinary ratings, Scale, equipment, and training.
Keep extraordinary stone sense as a separate expanded variant.

Working candidate:

```text
Dense Build
The Character's bodily density creates relevant Dissonance when it hinders
jumping, buoyancy, or crossing weight-sensitive surfaces.
```

Jumping was subsequently added by the user and confirmed for continued testing;
it can qualify even when takeoff and landing supports are sound.

This is a setting-defined fictional trait, not a claim about real anatomy.
The fixture assumes a meaningful additional bodily-weight or buoyancy burden
relative to the control. It grants no automatic Strength, Toughness, or
Stability bonus. It is not a blanket reduction to Mobility or movement rate.

## Three Contexts

| Context and attempted action | Control package | Package with Dense Build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Underground: inspect stonework in an unlit chamber | Darkvision grants its defined sensory Access; stone Observation Resonance may apply | Same benefits; density does not interfere with inspection |
| Underground: cross an ordinary solid stone floor | No trait-specific difficulty | Same; the surface supports the Character |
| Underground: cross an uncertain decayed wooden platform | Resolve the actual platform hazard | Added bodily weight can supply relevant Dissonance when it aggravates that hazard |
| Surface: walk a road or dodge on firm ground | Ordinary resolution when needed | No automatic Dense Build penalty |
| Surface: jump a gap from one sound support to another | Ordinary applicable Expression | Dense Build applies to the jump even without fragile surfaces |
| Surface: cross fragile ice or a weak footbridge | Resolve the support hazard | Dense Build contributes when the additional weight makes the attempted crossing harder |
| Water: swim while maintaining buoyancy in deep water | Ordinary applicable Expression | Relevant Dense Build Dissonance; trained attempts remain possible |
| Water: wade over firm footing with no need to float | Ordinary applicable Expression | No automatic buoyancy penalty; other actual hazards still apply |
| Water: use a sound boat with sufficient capacity | Boat and piloting circumstances govern | Density alone does not penalize every passenger or piloting Expression |

These are nine declared situations, not encounter probabilities or a measured
frequency of drawback activation. In the support cases, the fixture requires
weight to make a meaningful difference; not every wooden surface is fragile.

## Package Interaction

Darkvision and stone-related Observation may reveal evidence of an unsafe
crossing. That information can support choosing a different route or preparing
a solution. Dense Build still matters if the Character attempts the precarious
crossing; better observation does not change bodily weight.

In the expanded package, contact stone sense may reveal a hidden weakness in
stone within its defined scope. It does not reinforce the structure or solve
buoyancy. This supplies an alternate planning opportunity, not removal of the
negative trait or a mandatory additional roll for every crossing.

Poison-resistance Resonance remains independent. Dense Build neither reduces
it nor generates poison immunity. A poisoned-water scene may involve both
toxicity and swimming, but no compound or multi-roll procedure is invented here.

## Countermeasures and Boundaries

Adequate flotation, a sufficiently strong bridge, a suitable boat, or another
effective solution can remove the relevant problem. Do not preserve a penalty
after the fiction supplies a working answer. Inadequate equipment is a separate
case; the word "boat" alone does not guarantee capacity for any load.

The scene's ice, platform, or water conditions remain objective. Dense Build
describes how this Character interacts with them. Do not charge the same
additional body-weight burden twice under different modifier labels. Carried
equipment may make a distinct contribution when it genuinely adds further load.

If a structure plainly cannot support a crossing as proposed, resolve that
fiction through existing possibility and Access principles rather than assuming
every impossible route becomes possible by assigning a Dissonance value.
Likewise, routine supported travel does not require a roll just to activate
the drawback.

Dense Build does not automatically cause sinking, drowning, Physical Load,
failed climbing, poor reflexes, or closed swimming Access. Actual Expression
outcomes and applicable consequences govern what happens. Other fictional
uses of body mass may later justify different Lens roles, but no additional
positive function is adopted or priced through this negative-feature test.

## Assessment

The drawback is operationally distinct from merely losing one of the package's
benefits. It can make an ordinary permitted activity harder even while the
Character retains darkvision and both Resonance traits.

Its scope does not dominate routine physical action: the firm-ground, ordinary
floor, wading, and adequate-boat controls leave it inactive. Its numerical
severity has not been established, so this test cannot yet show how much it
reduces success or whether it balances all granted benefits.

Campaign significance varies. Solid-ground adventures with little unsupported
crossing may make this a modest or infrequent drawback. Aquatic travel or
frequent fragile-support hazards can make it more consequential. Do not turn
that observation into a universal band or apply a HOLT scope discount to the
entire species package automatically.

Recommendation: retain Dense Build as the negative candidate for complete
package comparisons. Next, test candidate numerical strengths for the two
Resonance features and this Dissonance feature together, using existing
Expression construction. No new provision currency is needed for that test.
