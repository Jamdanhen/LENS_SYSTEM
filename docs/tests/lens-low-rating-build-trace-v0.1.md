# Low-Rating Pacing: Character-Build Trace

Date: 2026-09-10

Status: User-approved diagnostic. No mechanical adjustment or character
reallocation adopted. Rating 20 is not established as a universal competence
benchmark by this trace.

## Finding

Rating 20 is a plausible complete Expression, but it does not identify one
kind of character. It can represent moderate broad training without narrow
expertise, an underdeveloped route on an otherwise accomplished character,
or substantial situational resistance. It is not the current guide's neutral
signature attack. No excessive penalty was hidden in the preceding simulation:
that simulation accepted 20 as an input and applied Function Bands afterward.

The principal calibration error was treating one supplied rating as attack,
defense, and recovery for each actor. Existing characters have very different
ratings for those activities. The simulation remains a valid controlled
experiment, but its equal-rating encounters are not demonstrated character
matchups. Its low-rating pacing warning remains real, not automatically a
reason to change the system or dismiss low-rated characters.

## Provenance and Budget

The older equipment record used Common Specialized 16.875, producing 20.25
with a x1.20 service handgun. The older working reference explicitly derives
its maximum 80 as Attribute x Derived x Skill x Specialization, or
10 x 2 x 2 x 2. Those labels predate the expanded training architecture.
With all five currently applicable development multipliers at Rank 10, the
arithmetic ceiling before equipment and other modifiers is 10 x 2^5 = 320.
This is not a normal starting build, an automatic entitlement to every
multiplier, or a new Rank-horizon decision. The baseline's remaining claim
that 80 is the current ordinary ceiling is stale; it is flagged here without
silently rewriting that authority file.

The original 25/50/100 portfolios also predate weighted Training prices.
This trace instead uses the A150, B150, and C150 Ordinary variants at Band 175
from the five-group experiment already referenced by the main starting-band

The guide's signature attack multiplied by the historical D1 x0.98 reproduces
the source probe exactly: 176.67490176. The other-handgun probe likewise
reproduces 98.1527232. Thus the larger figures are a source trace, not a new
bonus or invented development purchase.

Dodge here means a feasible evasion of a developing threat, not permission
to react to a bullet already in flight. Recover requires an appropriate
recoverable physical Condition and method; this rating does not authorize
instant wound removal. A clinician's Medicine and Treat ranks do not
automatically multiply every self-recovery action.

The low Dodge route is not a ruling that Fields cannot support defense.
If knowledge of firearms genuinely carries an evasion of a firearm threat,
the guide's Firearms x1.9 would yield 29.792, final 29. This is a conditional
applicability comparison, not an adopted universal pairing. Narrower expertise
must be justified separately. Purchased shooting equipment does not provide
an automatic defensive multiplier. The same applicability discipline governs
recovery: do not invent missing expertise, but do not omit it when it really
does apply.

## Where Rating 20 Appears

1. **Moderate broad training:** Attribute 5 x Derived Rank 5 x Task Rank 5 x
   Field Rank 5 x equipment 1.20 = 20.25, without Specialty or Precision.
   This illustrative route uses the current architecture. It is not a new
   character allocation or a declaration that all ordinary competence is 20.
   It demonstrates that low output is not necessarily incompetence or a bad
   fictional approach. Routine uncontested work still need not be rolled.
2. **A weaker route on an existing specialist:** the guide's Dodge is 15.68
   without an applicable Field, despite a signature attack of 180.28. The
   civilian exemplars have firearm ratings of 6.24 and 14.28. Their professional
   expertise does not constitute combat training.
3. **Distinct resistance:** the guide's broad firearm 58.9152 x D3 0.3528 =
   20.78528256, final 20. That is a difficult application of substantial
   broad training, not the neutral signature attack. D3 is a sensitivity
   input here; no specific range-to-Difficulty mapping is being adopted.
4. **Combined pressures:** other handgun 100.15584 x D3 0.3528 / two actions
   = 17.667490176. A 50% Function Band instead of the action divisor gives
   the same arithmetic for a different fictional reason. Neither pressure
   is imposed automatically or counted twice for the same contribution.

There is no evidence here that Difficulty, Function Bands, or the ordinary
equipment band should change. Whether a specific penalty is excessive must
be judged against its actual fictional cause, not merely the resulting number.

## Implication for the Pacing Result

The preceding 20-versus-20 encounters assigned 20 to every attack, defense,
and recovery attempt and played until loss of function. Their median of
14 rounds and heavy dependence on 00 complications describe that fixture.
They do not establish the expected length of ordinary combat or a fight
between these three example characters. Moreover, converting every modeled
00 complication into personal Load was a simulation assumption, not a
universal rule. The ordinary-roll-only control's much longer encounters show
that simply ignoring this assumption does not erase low-output stagnation.

The next useful test is a bounded character-based encounter comparison with
attack, defense, and recovery calculated separately, and defensive expertise
admitted only when the declared situation supports it. Preserve allocations
and rules. Do not transfer the guide's shooting rating into defense or recovery,
and do not transplant close-contact counter-effects into a ranged exchange.
This tests actual encounter pacing before any balance adjustment.
