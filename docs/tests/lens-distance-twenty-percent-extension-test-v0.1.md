## Reuse Rather Than Repeat

The user's observation is correct: at the same doubling references, distance
extension and covered-area extension have identical numerical behavior. The
companion script reruns and reuses the 264 checked area comparisons, verifies
the distance mapping, and adds ten distance-specific accounting fixtures.
It does not present the reused numbers as a newly discovered balance result.

Test distance = original declared distance x (1 + 0.20 x allocated Effects).
The original distance stays fixed; growth does not compound. Use additional
Effects and retain at least one successful Effect for the manifestation.
No extra delivery roll or second baseline Effect is introduced for an attack
whose travel is part of the same Expression.

For comparison, use upper references 25/50/100/200/400 increments at D1-D5,
continuing to double within the tested D10 horizon. These range thresholds
and their covering-band lookup are controls, not adopted by this test. What an
increment represents belongs to the Profile and applicable Lens. No meters,
seconds, or other universal unit is selected.

The fixtures use the adopted additive foundation and optional Axis development:
ordinary ranks / Axis of 3/3, 5/0, 5/5, 8/8, 10/0, and 10/10. Shared challenges
are absent, area D3, or area D3 plus independent duration D1. These are assessed
test demands, not new area thresholds. Distinct demands add, then the existing
Difficulty curve applies once, with final-only flooring. Other controls and
the natural-01 continuation distribution are inherited from the area test.

Five Effects double the original distance, not every subsequent distance band.

| Route, range challenge alone | Extend 25 to 50 | Declare 50 initially |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary ranks 5, Axis 5: reach probability | 24% | 44% |
| Ordinary ranks 10, Axis 10: reach probability | 99% | 99% |
| Ordinary ranks 10, Axis 10, roll 40: Effects remaining | 54 | 34 |

These are reach probabilities and remaining output, not opposed hit chances.
For the highest fixture, declaring 25 produces Final Rating 627 and 59 Effects
at roll 40. Five buy reach to 50, leaving 54. Declaring 50 produces Final Rating
376 and 34 Effects. The extension route's twenty extra remaining Effects are
a real high-output advantage, not removed merely by charging for extension.

With area D3 and independent duration D1 also required, the same highest route
reaches 50 through extension 31% of the time versus 48% by direct declaration.
At range alone, extending 25 to 400 reaches the target about 0.0075% of the
time versus 81% directly. Thus the larger declaration is not redundant across
the tested capabilities and requirements, although it is not always preferable.
Neither 320 nor 640 is a global output ceiling.

## What Changes For Distance

The intended endpoint must be represented honestly. In the comparison the
target is at 50; the candidate route deliberately manifests a base reach of 25
and relies on earned extension to reach it. That is the variable-reach candidate
being tested, not a claim that the existing fixed-objective procedure already
lets a known 50-increment delivery be assessed as a 25-increment delivery.
Ordinary direct delivery continues to assess its actual range before rolling.
Adopting extension would make the variable-reach alternative explicit; it would
not subtract Effects from an already assessed Difficulty afterward.

The manifesting character's actual means permit variable reach in these cases:

- Six generated Effects: five extend 25 to 50, leaving one impact Effect.
- Five generated Effects: at most four can extend reach while retaining the
  successful manifestation. Reach is 45, so the target at 50 is not affected.
  A shorter wave or beam may still have a fictional result; it does not receive
  an automatic substitute victim or permission to damage the distant target.
- A target blocks the one arriving impact Effect: the attack reached the target,
  but no impact remains. A successful block does not retroactively shorten its
  travel merely because both use Effect accounting.
- An earlier intervention cancels two of the six Effects supporting the carrier:
  only four remain for that carrier. It cannot both buy five extension Effects
  and retain its manifestation, so a dependent payload cannot reach 50 by it.
  This fixture assumes an intervention that actually removes carrier output,
  not every possible defense or opposition automatically shortening distance.
- The carrier's necessary means are destroyed: a large numerical remainder
  cannot deliver through an absent carrier. Viable residuals remain where their
  actual circumstances permit; output is not copied locally and remotely.
- The means have a hard reach of 40, or required Access is absent: twenty
  generated Effects cannot authorize reaching 50. This tests a genuine defined
  limit, not an invented cap imposed on every manifestation.
- Eight Effects, five allocated to reach, one matching impact defense, and one
  distinct mitigation leave one impact Effect. Equipment is not re-added after
  resolution; mitigation here is a separately defined applicable protection.

For teamwork, a six-Effect wave can reach 50 and carry a partner's four sting
Effects. Those four are not automatically spare carrier Effects if they cannot
contribute to extending the wave. Conversely, two actual carrier-strengthening
contributions of three Effects each can supply six compatible Effects. This
preserves full contribution and narrative flexibility, not an initial-purpose
lock. Each actor still resolves its actual challenge and pays its actual action.
Other supported outcomes remain available from unspent Effects.

These fixtures check specified outcomes and conservation; they do not adopt
a universal defense timing sequence or prove every fictional interaction.
Target selection, obstruction, connection disruption, applicable opposition,
and protection retain their existing scope. Extending reach grants no extra
attack, target, area, or character movement allowance.

## Recommendation

The 20%-of-original candidate is numerically viable with the same tradeoff as
area: strong output can purchase extension more efficiently than a larger
declaration, while modest output and larger combined demands favor declaring
the full distance initially. Distance adds a meaningful risk: a result that
falls short cannot affect the intended remote target.

Recommend adopting it as a working variable-reach option if accepting that
tradeoff. No additional numerical sweep or universal ceiling is justified by
this check. Do not claim the candidate leaves fixed-objective declaration
incentives unchanged. Do not adopt it automatically from this test.
