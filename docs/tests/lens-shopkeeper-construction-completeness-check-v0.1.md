## First Real Decision: Starting Assets Translation

The first unresolved construction dependency is what the shopkeeper actually
starts with, not whether they can succeed at routine business.

Already established:

1. The Campaign Profile interprets stable Assets in its own economy.
2. Starting equipment is assigned during construction, not won through rolls.
3. Material position, current Access, learned competence, and item function
   have different jobs.
4. Assets 7.2 is neither 7.2 currency units nor a 7% chance of owning a shop.
5. Good Retail commerce or Bookkeeping does not automatically multiply the
   starting estate. Those purchased Fields improve applicable Expressions.

Proposed test package, not an adopted entitlement:

```text
Modest independent shopkeeper in an ordinary urban economy

Access to rented shop premises
An ordinary working stock appropriate to that shop
Basic shop tools, records, and fixtures
Ordinary household possessions and local transport Access

Not automatically included:
Ownership of the commercial building
A substantial vehicle fleet
Rare or restricted stock
A large disposable reserve
Unspecified staff or institutional authority
```

The package must state whether stock and equipment are owned, financed, or
otherwise controlled. Ordinary rent/upkeep describes the established position;
it does not automatically impose beginning Framework Load or earn extra points.
Any actual arrears, contested ownership, or onerous obligation needs its own
explicit fiction and treatment. No debt or limitation is assigned in this check.

Test the package against a premises-owning shop and an employed shop manager.
Those cases distinguish personally controlled Assets from professional
competence and employment-based Access. Do not assign numerical breakpoints
or require them all to fit 7.2 before that comparison establishes the intended
differences. Owning a building need not automatically mean more liquid wealth.
