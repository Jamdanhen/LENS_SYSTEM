# Defensive Surplus: Scope, Permission and Repeated Reactions

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: Candidate test only. Neither independent opposed resolution nor surplus
defensive consequences are adopted into the baseline by this record.

## Tested Proposition

A successful independent defensive Expression may use output remaining after
opposition for an Effect that the same Expression actually supports. It does
not automatically return the incoming attack, grant a separate attack, or
create permission solely because a positive number remains.

This is more than a change of narration if it creates Conditions, changes Access
or materially harms an opponent. Current rules keep open defenses from becoming
free offensive actions. Adoption would therefore require an explicit boundary
between consequences of the defensive act and a separate counterattack.

## Calculation and Controls

```text
Matched output = min(offensive Effects, defensive Effects)
Offensive remainder = offensive Effects - matched output
Defensive surplus = defensive Effects - matched output
```

No extra baseline Effect follows subtraction. The script verifies eight output
fixtures and 37,636 paired ordinary rolls, conditional on faces 02-98. It checks
that both sides cannot simultaneously have unmatched output and that the
matched and remaining quantities account for each independently generated total.
These checks do not prove that a particular narrated consequence is permissible.

Special outcomes use the preceding candidate's logic: 01 produces successful
output, 00 produces none and may produce a separate complication. A fixed
thirteen-Effect defense illustrates large output; this is not an enumeration
of special-result probability. Equipment, mitigation and actual Condition
application are omitted from the numerical distribution.

## Scope Cases

### 1. Deflecting a Blade

Three offensive Effects against five defensive Effects leaves two surplus.
If the defense actually turns the blade and pulls the attacker out of position,
two Effects of compromised footing or weapon position are plausible consequences
of that same interaction. This does not establish a universal Condition name,
automatic disarm threshold, two free wounds, or a new follow-up sword strike.

The form must be grounded in how the defense occurred, not selected solely
because two points remain. A separate deliberate attack still needs its normal
Expression and action accounting. Compound contributions remain subject to their
existing rules where applicable.

Result: coherent candidate application.

### 2. Dodging a Distant Shot

Three offensive Effects against five defensive Effects again leaves two surplus.
A dodge may establish a better position if the movement and surroundings support
it. No movement allowance, extra distance per Effect, or free movement beyond
existing permission is established here.

That dodge alone does not wound the distant shooter or redirect the projectile.
Where the action accomplished only escape from the shot and supports no further
consequence, the remaining arithmetic is not an entitlement to invent one.

Result: supports a scope-dependent benefit, rejects automatic retaliation.

### 3. An Attack That Failed

Zero offensive Effects against five defensive Effects leaves five surplus
arithmetically. A missed shot that never reaches a reflecting barrier supplies
no projectile Effect there to reflect. A failed attempt to seize someone may,
depending on the actual interaction, still expose the would-be grappler's
position; failure of the grab does not by itself determine whether contact
occurred.

Assess the resolved fiction. Do not manufacture contact, a new target or a new
attack from the unused output. Nor impose a blanket requirement that attacks
must succeed before a defense can have any meaningful result.

Result: arithmetic passes; applicability is consequential in this case.

### 4. Publicly Exposing a Deception

Three deceptive Effects against five Effects from a defensive Expression that
actually exposes the contradiction can leave two Effects against the deceiver's
credibility, within the established audience and stakes.

Silently noticing a lie is not automatically public exposure. If the defensive
Expression involved only private observation, it has not also delivered an
unperformed public rebuttal. No ordinary success forces belief or behavior;
the adopted social agency boundaries still apply.

Result: crosses Attribute families without a special social counter subsystem,
provided the defensive Expression actually carries the outward consequence.

### 5. Resisting Persuasion

Refusing to be persuaded does not inherently make the speaker afraid, ashamed
or discredited. A composed response that actually challenges the appeal may
produce a suitable counter-effect, but it must be part of the resolved
interaction. Do not add a second independent task merely by renaming surplus.

Result: counter-effects are possible, not mandatory for every successful defense.

### 6. Extraordinary Reflection

A permission-bearing Lens can make reflection a valid result. Merely having
defensive surplus cannot supply that permission.

One incoming Effect against ten defensive Effects leaves nine surplus. It does
not provide nine incoming Effects to reflect. Redirection must trace the actual
source Effect, and the same source unit cannot also be applied to the original
target or separately counted as fully mitigated. Greater newly generated output
would require a genuine creation or enhancement contribution, not the word
'reflection'.

This demonstrates the boundary but does not establish a universal exchange rate
between defensive surplus and a HOLT's redirection capability. In particular,
do not first destroy all incoming Effects as mitigation, then recreate them as
reflected damage. Identify the actual route when applying the granted Lens.

Result: permission and conservation pass; numerical redirection provisioning
remains outside this candidate, rather than being silently invented.

### 7. Large Special Defense and Failed Defense

Four offensive Effects versus thirteen from a successful 01 defense leaves
nine surplus, not automatic reflection or unrestricted permission. A failed 00
defense produces no successful defensive surplus; its complication does not
become the defender's counterattack. If both sides fail, there are no successful
Effects to apply, although existing complication handling may still matter.

Result: follows independent outcome determination without special counter rules.

## Repeated Open Defenses

With three separate eligible attacks each producing two Effects and each valid
defense producing five, the arithmetic offers three surplus per exchange: nine
in total. These are three opportunities from actual exchanges, not stored actions.
Each opportunity must remain valid after earlier results change circumstances.

There is a real mechanical throughput change even if no additional attack roll
is made. Conditions applied through a defensive consequence can change subsequent
Expressions and the pace of combat. Do not call the change harmless merely
because it uses existing Effect rules or does not spend another action.

| Attack / defense Ratings | Chance of defensive surplus | Mean surplus when present | Mean surplus per exchange |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 80 / 80 | 44.13% | 3.482 | 1.536 |
| 20 / 80 | 78.47% | 4.337 | 3.403 |
| 80 / 20 | 4.59% | 1.375 | 0.063 |
| 160 / 160 | 44.98% | 3.584 | 1.612 |

These are conditional ordinary-roll arithmetic opportunities, not automatic
Condition awards. At unchanged 80/80 Ratings, three such exchanges offer expected
surplus 4.609. At unchanged 20/80, the total is 10.210. Do not present that sum as
ten Load on one target: there may be multiple attackers and different outcomes.
Actual sequential play must reassess Ratings, permission and Conditions.

The test does not impose a reaction cap, another action divisor, banking of
surplus, or a per-Effect Resonance conversion. Those would be new rules.

## Recommendation

Retain scope-dependent surplus Effects as a candidate. The defensive Expression
must itself support the consequence, with existing permission and conservation.
Do not adopt universal reflected damage or permit a surplus to purchase an
otherwise unperformed attack.

This establishes a usable boundary, not proof of combat balance. Before adopting
general opponent-affecting defensive consequences, the user must accept that open
defensive reactions can exert meaningful pressure beyond stopping attacks.
If selected for continued development, a short sequential exchange with actual
Conditions is the next concrete test, rather than another catalogue of defenses.
