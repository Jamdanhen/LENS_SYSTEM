## Purpose

Preserve the user's earlier distinction: a less inaccurate throw may win a
closest-to-target contest without becoming a successful hit. Determine whether
independent successful Effects alone retain that information.

## Finding

Effects alone erase relative differences among all failed attempts and among
successful attempts within one ten-point band. This is acceptable for Effect
cancellation but cannot alone preserve closer failed throws as distinct results.

## Proposed Interpretation

For declared relative placement, compare the existing signed Margins. Use the
independently generated Effects to determine successful output; do not regenerate
Effects from Net Margin. Where the Expressions actually counter each other,
compare their Effects under the candidate's cancellation and counter rules.

A closer miss can win a contest but remains a miss. Two successful independent
throws retain their own hits even when one scores better. Three offensive versus
three defensive Effects cancel even if their raw successful Margins differ.
The sub-band difference does not create a minimum attack Effect or counter.

This is not a combat-only distinction. A judged debate can compare performance;
an attempted deception opposed by its exposure can involve opposing consequences.
Declare the actual stakes before rolling, not after seeing the result.

The user subsequently chose finer relative placement through Margin, preserving

amplification. This decision is bounded to ordinary rolls. Special-result
placement requires a further check before a complete procedure is adopted.
