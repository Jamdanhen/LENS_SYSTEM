# LENS 100 Training Point Character Profile Test v0.1

Date: 2026-09-05

Status: Diagnostic construction comparison at user direction. Not adopted
Campaign Profiles, demographic benchmarks, or proof of encounter performance.
All Field and narrower subject placements are illustrative test selections.

## Objective

Determine what kind of developed character the current 100 Training Points
can describe now that Task, Field, Specialty, and optional Precision are
available. Compare breadth and depth before assigning a universal experience
label to this budget. Do not assume ordinary means untrained or that a veteran
must have more points than every civilian professional.

## Controls

- Same illustrative modern ordinary-human campaign for all three concepts.
- Same 25 Attribute Points: Might, Mobility, Psyche, Influence, Leverage at 5.
- Same 50 Derived Attribute Points: all 25 Derived Attributes at Rank 2.
  Every Derived Rank is below its parent. Uniform allocation isolates Training;
  it is not a recommended allocation or a measured average-human benchmark.
- Attribute contributes 5 directly; Derived Rank 2 contributes x1.2.
  Every displayed route therefore has the same core of 6.
- Exactly 100 shared Training Points for each complete Training portfolio.
- One point per Rank and x(1 + Rank/10), using the existing working curve.
  Precision uses that curve and cost as a test control; no separate price
  or additional budget is introduced.
- Each Rank is within 0-10. Specialty can exceed Field Rank under the working
  model. Precision's parent-Rank cap is not imposed in this test.
- User agreed that Precision is optional and may be purchased directly under
  its Field without a Specialty. Its scope remains narrower and Field-bound.
  When Specialty is omitted there is no Specialty multiplier. Owning a unique
  sentimental or extraordinary object is not another Training layer.
- Only applicable layers contribute. Multiple sibling Specialties are not
  automatically combined; the displayed routes use one applicable entry per
  layer and do not test Compound Expressions.
- Unpurchased Tasks have no Training multiplier, not Closed Access.
- No equipment, HOLT, changed Scale, active Resonance, Dissonance, action
  division, or Difficulty adjustment is included in displayed Base Expressions.
  These are not to-hit probabilities or judgments about success at Standard
  Difficulty. No generic combat Difficulty has been invented.
- Identity, languages actually known, licenses, equipment, and other Access
  remain narrative/construction inputs, not free consequences of these ranks.
- These are full Training allocations, not complete playable sheets: starting
  possessions, exact condition capacities, and every prepared action are not
  resolved by this test.

## A. Everyday Adult With A Developed Life

Concept: a local shopkeeper who drives, cooks, gardens, keeps accounts, and
plays music. No dedicated combat or advanced specialist training is assumed.

Tasks, all Rank 4 (15 x 4 = 60):

    Exert, Traverse, Pilot, Navigate, Create, Repair, Observation, Research,
    Inference, Planning, Teaching, Convey, Persuade, Conceal, Perform.

Fields, all Rank 5 (8 x 5 = 40):

    Retail commerce, Bookkeeping, Cooking, Gardening, Music,
    Passenger vehicles, Local geography, English.

Specialties: none. Precision: none. Total: 100.

Retail commerce covers shop trade and transactions; Bookkeeping covers
financial records. Overlap does not yield duplicate contributions. The
language entry describes developed use of this known language, not ownership
of all languages. These candidate boundaries are test assumptions.

Representative routes:

    Coordination / Create / Cooking: 6 x 1.4 x 1.5 = 12.60
    Cognition / Planning / Bookkeeping: 6 x 1.4 x 1.5 = 12.60
    Presence / Perform / Music: 6 x 1.4 x 1.5 = 12.60
    Coordination / Discharge, no applicable purchased Training: 6.00

Finding: 100 supports a substantial breadth of ordinary practice without any
Specialty or Precision. This is not a novice with no developed expertise,
but it does not require professional combat or elite technical credentials.

    Other handgun: 6 x 1.4 x 1.6 x 1.5 = 20.160

and one narrower Precision, but not every Task or equally deep expertise

## C. Deep Medical Specialist

Concept: a clinician with substantial medical depth and a modest selection
of ordinary life competencies. This does not assert that the resulting Base
Ratings meet all expectations for expert medical success in play.

Tasks, all Rank 3 (10 x 3 = 30):

    Treat, Inference, Research, Teaching, Observation, Planning,
    Convey, Pilot, Create, Persuade.

Fields, all Rank 6 (5 x 6 = 30):

    Medicine, Biology, Passenger vehicles, Cooking, English.

Specialties, all Rank 10 (3 x 10 = 30):

    Surgery, Emergency medicine, Diagnostic medicine, all under Medicine.

Precision (1 x 10 = 10):

    Neurosurgery under Medicine, within the purchased Surgery Specialty.

Total: 100. Rank 10 is the current individual-entry horizon, not a claim that
the Character is the best clinician overall. Overlapping medical Specialties
are alternatives unless distinct contributions justify an actual Compound.

Representative Coordination / Treat routes:

    Applicable neurosurgical procedure:
    6 x 1.3 x 1.6 x 2.0 x 2.0 = 49.92

    Surgery outside Precision:
    6 x 1.3 x 1.6 x 2.0 = 24.96

    Treatment outside all purchased Specialties:
    6 x 1.3 x 1.6 = 12.48

Finding: 100 can buy several maximum-rank narrower entries and some everyday
coverage. It does not force every Character to have moderate ranks everywhere.
This portfolio also demonstrates that narrow Rank ceilings do not establish
an overall veteran/elite category or guarantee expert success rates.

## Same-Budget Alternatives Without Precision

Remove only Precision and reallocate its points within each same portfolio:

| Portfolio | Reallocation | Relevant Specialty route | Original with Precision | Original outside Precision |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Guide | Six points: Discharge 4 to 7; Firearms 6 to 9. | 6 x 1.7 x 1.9 x 1.5 = 29.07 | 32.256 | 20.16 |
| Clinician | Ten points: Treat 3 to 10; Medicine 6 to 9. | 6 x 2.0 x 1.9 x 2.0 = 45.60 | 49.92 | 24.96 |

Each alternative still totals exactly 100. The Precision version has the
higher narrow route; the alternative improves more applications. This is not
an exhaustive optimization over every possible portfolio.

Skipping Specialty also works: in the guide build, omit Handguns 5 and use
those five points for a new justified Field at Rank 5. Keep Revolvers Precision
6 directly under Firearms. Its Discharge route becomes 6 x 1.4 x 1.6 x 1.6 =
21.504; other firearms are 13.44. Total remains 100, and the omitted Specialty
contributes no multiplier. The additional Field buys a different area of
expertise rather than broader handgun development.

## Conclusion And Next Decision

The test shows that 100 is not a unique experience label. It can support a
broadly practiced everyday adult, an experienced adventurer, or a deeply
specialized civilian. These are constructed examples, not population norms.
Nothing about having no combat training implies no accumulated life training.

There is no demonstrated need to raise the budget simply because Precision is
available. It fits, and reallocating away from it yields a useful broader
alternative. Nor does this establish that 100 is the correct universal
starting budget: Field breadth and campaign demands influence its value.

Recommended working description: a developed ordinary Character, with either
broad life competence or concentrated professional depth. Do not label the
budget untrained citizen, veteran, or elite as a mechanical fact. Campaign
Profiles can specify starting experience and adjust budgets when a desired
concept or performance target demonstrates a need.

The next meaningful calibration is to select the default Campaign Profile's
intended starting experience and test representative Expressions in its actual
scenes using the established Difficulty/Resonance/opposition rules. Do not
infer low competence from these deliberately unmodified Base Ratings.
