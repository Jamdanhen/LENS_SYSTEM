# LENS Representative Examples: Core Book Handoff

Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-09-13
Status: User-authorized preservation for later main-LENS integration.
Delivery: Saved locally; not sent to another task or merged into core doctrine.

## Purpose and Book Structure

Preserve the demonstrative examples developed during the Heralds Domain review
for the main LENS package. The presentation package belongs to the reusable
core book, not solely to the Heralds setting roster.

The user's working product frame is:

- LENS is the setting-agnostic core rulebook.
- Heralds of the Source is the included core/default setting, presented toward
  the back of the book and demonstrating the engine through a playable world.
- Heralds is a setting worth developing in its own right, not a disposable
  tutorial or an obligation to illustrate every possible capability.
- Later guidebooks may deepen Heralds, introduce other settings, or develop
  particular genres and applications. The core should be usable on its own.
- Design for a readable, playable, potentially marketable product without
  treating commercial success or a publication schedule as established.

## Status and Authority

The user approved preserving representative examples, not an exhaustive power
catalogue. Specific sample selections and manifestations below remain editorial
proposals unless an explicit working decision is identified. Their use in a
generic example does not adopt them into Heralds canon or every LENS campaign.

Use current LENS terminology and rules when integrating. At this handoff date,
HOLTs supply defined Access without a numerical parent Rank; Axis development
is separate. Do not import historical parent-rank allocation examples or invent
new prices, bonuses, or required purchases from these illustrations.

Domain is Heralds' setting-facing vocabulary. Generic examples illustrate HOLT
subjects and setting choices without declaring Domain a compulsory universal
category. HOLD/HOLT placement of new examples must follow actual definitions,
not a genre list. No new core rules are adopted here.

Enhancements are in the lab for testing, as reported by the user. This package
does not dispatch another test, report a lab result, or settle enhancement-dependent
concepts. Those questions need not block preservation of these examples.

## Presentation Principle

Use representative subjects to show how the same engine supports different
fictional identities. Do not require a complete list of environments, substances,
energies, or powers. Descriptive headings are reading aids, not purchased layers,
exclusive classifications, equal-price categories, or fixed scope ceilings.

Keep two questions separate:

1. What subject or relationship organizes this extraordinary capability?
2. Which setting makes that capability available, and in what form?

Use one environmental discussion. Do not put Sea and Sky in a "World and
Environment" category and then create another peer "Environments" category
for Jungle and Coast simply because those examples were explored later.

## Environmental Illustrations

The user specifically favors Jungle as an illustrative example and tentatively
suggests Coast. Arctic, Desert, and Underground were recognized as obvious
examples. Proposed treatment: fuller Jungle and Coast descriptions with shorter
contrasts from the other three, not five required packages.

| Sample | Identity to preserve | Illustrative use and teaching purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jungle | Abundant life, hidden danger, isolation, alertness, adaptation. | A character senses danger through surrounding life, brings forth concealing growth, or adapts to a hostile passage. The environment supplies connected fictional relationships, not only plants plus animals. |
| Coast | Weathering, exposure, storm intensity, refuge, arrival, and bounty. | A character provides passage through crashing surf or shelter amid a battered shore. Overlap with Sea does not require merging the identities or buying every contributing subject. |
| Arctic | Cold, exposure, hunger, sustenance. | Endure a frozen crossing or sustain companions through scarcity. The identity is more than producing ice. |
| Desert | Heat, dryness, ordeal, fortitude, rationing, subsistence. | Find or preserve a means of sustaining a journey. A physical and mythic identity need not be reduced to temperature manipulation. |
| Underground | Enclosure, depth, stone, darkness, hidden passage, sanctuary. | Establish passage or shelter beneath the surface. The environment is more than a material inventory. |

Sea, Sky, Earth, and Wilderness provide existing comparison material. Other
retained environmental possibilities include Mountain, Swamp/Wetland, Volcanic,
Forest, River/Floodplain, and Plains/Grassland. Their presence in the source
exploration is not a requirement for full core-book entries.

The examples above are possible Expressions, not newly approved complete scope
definitions. Actual means, circumstances, Access, and opposition remain relevant.

## Material Illustrations

Metal and Wood are the proposed representative pair. The user supported using
samples rather than cataloguing every material; this does not finalize either
as a Heralds roster entry.

- Metal: presence, shaping, joining, embodiment, tempering, endurance, and
  workmanship. A crossing could be formed as metallic ribs or a supporting arch.
- Wood: grain, branching, flexibility, continuity from growth, shelter, and
  carried objects. The same crossing could be formed as interlocking timbers
  or a branching support.

Both examples can achieve passage through the shared engine while remaining
different fictional identities. Neither is confined to rigidity or flexibility.
Raw and worked material can remain relevant. Wood need not mean dead material
only; Metal's presence in a machine does not automatically grant its operating
capabilities. The material's possible symbolism is more than decoration but
does not automatically confer every metaphor.

Gold illustrates a possible particular substance with its own fictional identity;
Plastic can illustrate a different campaign. These are alternatives to draw on,
not further mandatory entries or a purchased Material parent above them.

## Energy-Related and Phenomenon Illustrations

The proposed compact selection was Fire, Light, Electricity, and Momentum.
Ice, Magnetism, and Darkness remain developed comparison material, not removed
because the shorter selection omitted them. This is not a scientific hierarchy.

| Sample | Illustrative direction | Teaching purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Manifest flame, burning, warmth, or flame-based movement. | A recognizable subject supports several uses rather than one damage package. |
| Light | Illumination, directed radiance, or luminous embodiment. | A subject can be useful beyond attack, without becoming every related physical phenomenon. |
| Electricity | Supply or disrupt a device, discharge, or manifest electrical movement. | Extraordinary movement need not mean exact biological regulation or ordinary scientific causation. |
| Momentum | Accelerate, arrest, redirect, or remain anchored. | Momentum is not the Movement Derived Attribute; a related Domain identity need not share the same name or scope. |
| Darkness | Concealment, tangible shadow, and shadow step. | The Heralds working example includes actual passage through shadow, not merely a false appearance of movement. |

Sound was explored but need not receive a featured entry simply because a record
exists. Radiation is better retained here as a possible campaign-specific example
than treated as a required Heralds addition. Broad Energy remains unresolved;
these samples do not depend on adopting it.

## Other Identities Must Remain Visible

The three sample groups above are not the complete range of LENS or Heralds.

- Living subjects: Beast, Plant, and Health illustrate related but different
  subjects. Generated vines, animal-nature embodiment, or bodily restoration
  can demonstrate different Expressions without automatically granting all Life.
- Personal and relational subjects: Strength, Awareness, Authority, Knowledge,
  Identity, and Civilization remain available as examples. They are not all
  simple synonyms for Derived Attributes.
- Broad means: Technology and Sorcery illustrate setting-defined means and
  Access. Do not universalize a particular Heralds limitation across all settings.

Selected examples can show how a Derived Attribute subject becomes a HOLT
identity without turning every Derived Attribute into a HOLT. Strength and
Awareness illustrate that relationship. Knowledge remains broader than Learning,
Cognition, or Insight. Momentum remains distinct from Movement.

Heralds adopted Awareness as the replacement name for Perception, preserving
the established non-fabricating scope. The ordinary Awareness definition already
distinguishes reception and attention from understanding; no new semantic boundary
is required. Its Domain can support outward sharing or suppression as well as
personal sensing, using applicable Derived Attributes rather than only its namesake.

Authority illustrates directing agency, not issuing commands to arbitrary reality.
Civilization can embody shared life in a nomadic community: temporary manifested
mounts carry the old or infirm, or guardians keep children with the travelers.
These user-proposed images need not be permanent summons or a fixed power list.

## Campaign Choice Illustrations

These user-supplied contrasts explain how settings can choose differently:

| Campaign direction | Possible examples |
| --- | --- |
| Space or futuristic | Radiation, Outer Space, Plastic. |
| Medieval fantasy | Sorcery, Necromancy, Alchemy, a dungeon environment. |

Outer Space means the environment, not universal spacetime control. Alchemy and
dungeons are illustrative mentions, not requests for full scope development.
The genres do not exclusively own these subjects. Sorcery can appear in this
contrast without being removed from Heralds. A campaign example is not by itself
a formal HOLD definition or an adopted Heralds Domain.

## Deferred and Excluded Work

Do not restart the candidate sweep. Gravity was left unpursued because no concrete
Heralds need had been identified. Dream remains parked outside the working roster.
Broad Energy, Life, Death, Transformation, Absorption, property adoption, Conversion,
and reserve Heat retain their existing statuses.

Protection, Destruction, Corruption, Deception, and related activity-like concepts
must retain the pending enhancement distinction. Preservation was grouped within
Protection. Creation as an activity must not be conflated with Heralds' cosmological
Creation. No enhancement result or numerical benefit is implied by this package.

## Later Integration

When the user is ready, send this self-contained package to the main LENS task
for editorial integration. Adapt examples to then-current core rules, preserving
the status distinctions. Do not move source records, rewrite Heralds lore, dispatch
the handoff now, or schedule follow-up merely because this document exists.
