### Preparation Discussion Carried Forward

The discussion moved from a possible Prepare Task, through a proposed temporary
modifier category, to treating relevant preparation as Resonance under the
existing architecture. No new Task or Lens category was adopted. Preparation
must support the specific declared response, not every later action merely
because the same Field applies. Delaying alone does not establish that support.

Subsequent user-approved working default, 2026-09-08: assess the actual fictional
benefit using ten-percent contextual increments. Resolve genuinely uncertain
preparatory activities normally, without adding a roll merely to generate a
bonus. A better result can establish a better assessed advantage. The automatic
exchange of one generated Effect for +0.10 Resonance remains an unadopted
candidate. Preserve specific applicability and avoid duplicate contributions.

Persistence subsequently adopted, 2026-09-08: retain preparation while its
supporting facts remain, reassessing applicability and value when circumstances
change. Use or interruption alone does not erase it; actual consumption,
disruption, or invalidation can diminish or end it. Temporary inapplicability
does not necessarily destroy preparation. No universal duration, use limit, or
maintenance roll applies. See
`../tests/lens-preparation-persistence-test-v0.1.md`.

Action accounting subsequently adopted, 2026-09-08: distinct setup activities
use ordinary actions; ordinary execution within an Expression is not charged
again. A separate setup and later attack use separate actions. Prepared open
defenses retain existing reaction accounting. Delay relocates an available
action without refunding setup, granting extra attacks, or supplying Resonance.
Prepared open defensive reactions do not move initiative merely by benefiting
from preparation. Persistence remains unchanged. See
`../tests/lens-preparation-action-investment-test-v0.1.md`.

Held-response timing subsequently adopted, 2026-09-08: a previously declared,
perceptible trigger can release a feasible response using an available action
through Delay before an unfinished activity completes. Preserve ordinary
opposition and let actual Effects determine what can continue. No retroactive
prevention, extra action, or automatic preparation benefit follows. See
`../tests/lens-held-response-trigger-timing-test-v0.1.md`.

Multiple held responses subsequently adopted, 2026-09-08: existing relative
initiative at an event orders responses to that shared trigger. Reassess after
each outcome. A different valid trigger during a response can release another
held response; then resume unfinished activities where possible. Begun actions
are committed, not refunded or reusable; resuming them is not another action.
Unbegun responses may be declined, retaining ordinary Delay rather than granting
an unrelated interrupt. No new initiative roll or extra action is created. See
`../tests/lens-multiple-held-response-order-test-v0.1.md`.

User clarification, 2026-09-08: delaying or readying does not accumulate actions.
Continued preparation, such as aiming, may improve the actual bonus to a limited
useful extent. Preserve assessed benefit and reassess the same contribution,
without duplicate bonuses for rounds waited. Delay alone grants no Resonance.
No numerical ceiling or automatic bonus-per-round schedule was specified.

Further user clarification, 2026-09-08: initiative reorders where the Character
actually acts. Subsequent turns use that new position; the old position does
not also grant a turn. Continuous Delay handles waiting across a round boundary
without separate carried-action allocation or changes to multiple-action costs.

Further-preparation guidance adopted, 2026-09-08: additional benefit depends on
what the activity, available means, and circumstances can actually improve.
Equipment may change attainable benefit, required time, or both. No universal
three-round cap, automatic per-action bonus, or one-action-per-Lens rule applies.
Stop increasing the contribution when nothing relevant improves; retain an
already established benefit while its supporting facts remain. Removing an
obstruction changes its Dissonance; a distinct advantage despite it supplies
Resonance. Do not count the same improvement both ways. Make expected investment
and benefit clear when reasonably knowable, without guaranteeing uncertainty.
The preparation-improvement question is resolved as working guidance. Numerical
aiming stages remain illustrative, not equipment specifications. See
`../tests/lens-preparation-means-and-plateau-test-v0.1.md`.

## Adopted Working Terminology

Status: User working lock, 2026-09-03. Naming and layer relationships are
adopted; catalogue scopes and mathematical controls retain their prior status.

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Task | The practiced activity the Character is performing. |
| Field | The developed area of knowledge or practical expertise brought to that activity. |
| Specialty | A narrower concentration of expertise within a Field. |

Task naming convention, adopted by the user on 2026-09-04:

- every Task has a one-word mechanical name;
- every Task receives a formal definition establishing its scope;
- ordinary dictionary meanings do not expand a defined Task beyond that scope;
- natural familiarity or apparent boundedness creates no exception to the
  universal definition requirement.

Presentation clarification, 2026-09-05: formal definitions belong in the rules
reference and need not appear on the Character sheet. Task name and Rank are
sufficient there; a scope cue is not a required character-sheet element.

The current overlap and redundancy test below supersedes earlier reasoning
that treated shared applications as grounds for rejecting a Task. Exert and
Reconfigure retain provisional status with the definitions tested there.

Fields are portable across applicable Tasks. A Specialty belongs to its Field,
not to one Task, and travels with the Field when relevant. No layer grants
permission merely by being ranked or becomes a mandatory purchase for an
ordinary attempt.

Specialization describes developing narrower expertise. A Specialty is a
branch of its Field; branch describes that relationship rather than another
named layer. A technique can be the content of a Specialty without adding an
extra layer.

Earlier tests below retain their original vocabulary: portable Parent or
Parent Specialization now means Field; parent-bound Branch now means Specialty.
The earlier two-layer Skill/Specialization model is not a one-to-one rename.
These names do not change costs, budgets, multipliers, Rank horizons, Access,
compound rules, or the status of purchasing and cap tests.

## Earlier Test: Recover And Repair

Status update: the shared medical/mechanical Repair proposal below was
rejected by the user. It is preserved as the earlier test, not the current
Task recommendation. See the subsequent Treat / Repair / Recover direction.

### Question

Does Restore need one ranked Task or several? Does the user's proposed
internal/external distinction describe different activities, or merely change
who receives the same activity?

Test two candidate meanings:

```text
Recover
Practiced management of one's return toward function after strain,
disruption, injury, or loss of equilibrium.

Repair
Deliberately correct damage or a fault by working on the affected thing.
The relevant field supplies the particular treatment or technical practice.
```

These definitions are proposals. Repair includes living tissue in this test;
that wording and breadth have not been adopted. Healing remains part of the
earlier Anchor record and is not silently deleted or renamed.

### Scenario 1: Returning Toward Function

```text
Might x Resilience x Recover
Use a practiced recovery routine to return toward physical function after
exertion or disruption.

Psyche x Resolve x Recover
Use a practiced means of regaining composure and internal direction after
fright or mental pressure.
```

The body or mind's return toward function is what supplies the Expression.
The Task, if adopted, improves a relevant practiced response. It does not
become a prerequisite for ordinary recovery, manufacture regeneration, or
convert every interval of rest into a required roll.

The existing Resilience definition also permits recovery of equilibrium lost
through a physical effect. This test does not change that Derived Attribute.

Finding: Recover is a plausible candidate for self-restorative practice,
distinct from simply Withstanding pressure while it continues.

### Scenario 2: Same Treatment, Different Patient

A medic treats an injured arm. In one case it is another person's arm; in the
other it is the medic's own arm.

Candidate route for precise treatment:

```text
Mobility x Coordination x Repair x Medicine x applicable Branch
```

Changing the patient does not necessarily change the practiced treatment or
the capability that carries it. Real differences such as awkward access,
pain, tools, or available assistance may matter through the existing rules,
but no extra penalty is assumed merely to make this example harder.

The medic's own bodily recovery may separately be expressed through Resilience.
That does not require a second roll for every treatment or make the medic's
Resilience supply another person's biology.

Finding: self versus other is not sufficient by itself to split the Task.
The acting process and contribution determine routing, not ownership of the
affected arm.

### Scenario 3: Living And Mechanical Subjects

```text
Coordination x Repair x Medicine
Carry out a precise corrective treatment.

Coordination x Repair x Mechanics
Carry out a precise correction to a damaged mechanism.
```

The proposed Task describes corrective work; the Parent describes the field.
Mechanics does not apply to biological treatment merely because Repair does.
Medicine does not supply mechanical expertise.

An unranked ordinary attempt remains possible when the fiction supports it.
A ranked Task does not independently provide missing tools, knowledge-based
Access, extraordinary permissions, or every specialized procedure.

Finding: this is a plausible application of the portable-subject model. Whether
Repair is the right word and the right breadth remains a real user decision,
not a numerical conclusion.

### Scenario 4: A Mechanical Character Works On Itself

A mechanical Character uses tools to repair its own damaged joint. Another
Character could perform the same corrective procedure. Both are Repair under
the tested meaning, despite the difference in patient.

A built-in self-restoration capability could instead support Recover when the
Expression is the Character's own return to function. Its permission comes
from the relevant construction and Lenses, not from the Task label.

Finding: the distinction is active corrective intervention versus managing
return to function. It is not living versus mechanical, or strictly self
versus somebody else.

### Scenario 5: Restoring Everything Is Not One Automatic Application

Returning toward function, repairing machinery, retrieving stolen property,
rebuilding a damaged relationship, and regaining public trust can all be
described as restoration.

That shared desired result does not automatically make the same Task apply.
Some interpersonal remedial work might genuinely fit Repair; another case
might instead involve persuasion, restitution, investigation, or a different
practiced activity. These routes are not closed by a fixed whitelist, and they
are not settled by a shared word alone.

Finding: keep Restore as an organizing prompt for this comparison rather than
automatically purchasing one modifier for all restoration.

### One Scene, Different Activities

A shipment has disappeared. These are alternative illustrative Expressions,
not three prerequisites or compulsory sequential rolls:

| Activity | Declared attempt |
|---|---|
| Observation | Watch the unloading and notice that a seal or handling routine is unusual. |
| Research | Consult manifests, port records, and available testimony to establish the shipment's documented route. |
| Inference | Use the available inconsistencies to conclude where an unrecorded transfer probably occurred. |

The same relevant subject field can contribute across these activities. For
example, developed knowledge of freight operations may help identify the
unusual routine, interpret the records, or understand an inconsistency.
Possessing that field does not automatically make the three activities
identical or grant access to unavailable records and witnesses.

Existing lateral examples remain usable:

- Network can supply distributed attention for Observation of current events.
- Background can supply established context and access for Research.
- Background can supply cultural or formative context for Inference.

Finding: retain all three as distinct candidates in the Task map. They answer
different immediate questions: what is being noticed, what the sources
establish, and what follows beyond what is directly stated.

### Boundary Candidate: Search

The existing Index describes Search as systematically locating a concealed,
missing, or obscured subject within an available area or body of material.

Test case: locate the missing crate in a warehouse by deliberately covering
possible hiding places. That learned procedure is distinguishable from
watching normal unloading and noticing something unusual.

Countercase: an observant Character can still spot the crate or a revealing
clue without a Search Rank. A Search candidate must not monopolize ordinary
discovery or turn all attempts to find something into a new purchase gate.

Research also locates information. Finding a particular entry may overlap
Search, while comparing sources to answer a question more clearly demonstrates
Research. Overlap alone does not prove two Tasks must merge, nor does a change
in wording justify counting both for the same contribution.

Initial finding: Search was a credible separate candidate, but not adopted.
The subsequent user proposal that searching can be sustained or methodical
Observation is tested below. That test supplies the newer recommendation.

### Boundary Candidate: Investigation

The existing Index describes Investigation as systematically gathering,
preserving, testing, and connecting evidence concerning a specific matter.

Two readings need separating:

```text
Conduct an investigation
A broad undertaking that may involve Observation, Research, Inference,
interviews, and other activities.

Apply investigative procedure
Handle, preserve, and test evidence through a developed method.
```

The broad undertaking does not automatically warrant an additional universal
multiplier on every constituent activity. The narrower procedural reading may
identify genuine learned work that the three existing candidates do not fully
represent. It must not be dismissed merely because several activities can
appear within an investigation.

Forensics is an existing possible subject field, but placing it as a Parent
does not itself decide which Task carries a particular examination or test.

Initial finding: Investigation needs a scope decision. The user subsequently
proposed treating it as a complex undertaking: find clues, establish what they
are or study them, and determine what they mean. That proposal is tested below.

No roll chain is added. Scene scope, meaningful uncertainty, actual

## Investigation As A Complex Undertaking: Scenario Test

### Working Decomposition

```text
Find relevant evidence.
Establish what it is or study it.
Determine what it means for the question being investigated.
```

### Case 1: A Missing Shipment

A Character looks into a missing shipment at a warehouse. Possible declared
activities include:

| Function | Example contribution | What it does not automatically establish |
|---|---|---|
| Find evidence | Observation notices a disturbed seal, or Search locates a concealed dispatch tag. | What every mark on the seal or tag means. |
| Establish its identity | Research compares an unfamiliar marking with shipping records; an applicable field supplies relevant knowledge. | Who took the shipment or whether a witness is guilty. |
| Interpret significance | Inference combines the marking's meaning with the route and timing to identify a plausible earlier transfer. | Facts or certainty unsupported by the available evidence. |

These are illustrative options, not a prescribed solution to the scene.
The activity can move back and forth: a conclusion may suggest another place
to search or another source to consult.

Result: the user's decomposition supports distinct trained contributions

#### Qualified-Phrase Model Findings

The two-word model puts an immediate cue on the Character sheet. Physically
Force and Modify Device show why that can be useful when a bare verb has broad
ordinary or figurative meanings.

Across the full map, however, the second word does not perform one consistent
job. It variously identifies a subject, target, product, medium, or intended
result. Generic nouns such as target, subject, condition, action, and threat
often add little actual boundary. More specific nouns create accidental
exclusions: Treat Injury omits illness; Repair Device omits structures and
equipment; Discharge Projectile omits bursts and streams; Traverse Terrain may
mislead readers about water, air, or extraordinary environments.

Avoiding those exclusions requires either increasingly long Task phrases or a
formal definition. A two-word phrase therefore cannot replace definitions.
Applied rigidly, it also makes the Task list read like command clauses rather
than a compact set of practiced activities.

#### Defined-Term Model Findings

The one-word model produces a consistent action vocabulary: Force, Modify,
Repair, Treat, Recover, Observe, Research, Infer, Plan, Command, Teach,
Traverse, Pilot, Navigate, Convey, Discharge, Strike, Throw, Grapple, Block,
Dodge, and Withstand.

Under this model, every capitalized Task is a defined mechanical term. Its
ordinary dictionary senses do not expand its scope. Force can be formally
limited to applying physical pressure to move or hold a resisting load;
Modify can be formally limited to altering a constructed functional device.
The same rule applies to every Task, whether its everyday meaning appears
narrow or broad.

This model preserves lateral routing because the definition identifies the
practiced activity without embedding one preferred target into its name. It
also makes the rules text, rather than intuition about familiar words, the
common authority. Its cost is that a Character sheet containing only Task
names cannot teach their full boundaries; the Task reference must remain easy
to consult.

#### Definitions Control

A no-definition control fails under both naming models. Two-word labels still
cannot resolve whether Physically Force includes striking, whether Repair
Damage includes living injury, whether Modify Device includes software, or
whether Block Threat includes protecting another Character. These boundaries
affect purchasing and multiplication and cannot safely be left to dictionary
readings alone.

Test finding: all Tasks require formal definitions regardless of name length.
Of the two universal naming conventions, one-word defined activity terms are
more consistent and preserve more narrative portability. Universal two-word
phrases offer stronger immediate cues in a few cases but become artificial or
misleading across the full Task map and still require definitions.
