# Natural, Maintained, And Independent Persistence

Date: 2026-09-13

Status: the user confirmed the conceptual distinction. The two-Axis application
below checks the existing compound rule; numerical duration pricing and a
universal surplus-to-time conversion are not adopted.

## Confirmed Direction

Assess what the character's power establishes or continues supplying. Natural
combustion can continue without maintenance. Actively reinforcing a fire so it
keeps burning and spreads is a different contribution when that support actually
does work. Establishing persistence that continues without the character's
support places the intended duration in the challenge and its Difficulty.

The earlier general declared-duration proposal was not adopted wholesale.
The user's correction emphasizes imposed independent persistence, not a charge
for everything that happens to last. Neither the fire illustration's three
rounds nor the phantasm illustration's two rounds establishes a time band.

## Compound Application Check

An established fire burns normally while the character uses their power again:
there is no required maintenance contribution merely because the fire remains.

Alternatively, the character continuously reinforces one part of a fire while
directing its advancing edge as one coherent permitted manifestation. If this
genuinely requires two higher-order Axes, use the approved mean. Independent
attacks remain independent activities; unrelated actions do not merge merely
because their owner calls them concentration.

Illustrative arithmetic holds the common pre-Axis, pre-Difficulty core at 100,
uses D1 x0.98 and ordinary roll 40, and floors only the Final Rating. It is not
a complete character build or an assignment of universal duration Difficulty.

| Case | Axis contribution | Final Rating | Generated Effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Natural continuation; new use alone | x1.8 | 176 | 14 |
| Required active support x1.4 and new use x1.8 | Mean x1.6 | 156 | 12 |
| Both required Axes at x1.8 | Mean x1.8 | 176 | 14 |
| Required support Axis Open but unranked, x1.0; new use x1.8 | Mean x1.4 | 137 | 10 |

The compound case earns twelve Effects total, not twelve for maintaining plus
twelve for the other function. Allocation follows the actual functions and
their requirements; no universal one-Effect maintenance price is inferred.
Equal development does not incur an extra penalty merely to make splitting
concentration costly. Closed required Access prevents the combined use rather
than becoming a small numerical modifier.

Do not apply a second copy of the same Axis merely because one power use has
two described purposes. Whether a second Axis is actually required must come
from the Expression. Mixed non-HOLT and larger compound formulas remain open.

## Conclusion

The maintained-plus-new-use case fits the existing two-Axis compound procedure
where its requirements genuinely apply. No separate concentration resource,
automatic second action, or universal maintenance roll is needed.

The next duration comparison should hold the manifestation constant and vary
only its required independent persistence: brief, extended, and indefinite.
Use active support as a control, not as another independently persisting effect.
That isolates the remaining Difficulty question without making natural lifetime
an automatic charge or confusing persistence with a different kind of creation.
