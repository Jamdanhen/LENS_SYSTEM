The extra acquisition establishes a productive asset rather than merely a
larger reserve of spendable money. It does not, from its name alone, prove
the recurring output needed for every manor and household.

Pathfinder's room Earnings account for ordinary unskilled employees doing
the basic work. Its Farmland can generate money or Goods. This is evidence

accounted for, rather than charging for a bare site and all workers twice.

That convention does not establish a named staff family's duties, its entire

Result: the table prices physical and productive provisions. It does NOT

## Test 3: Established Operation Versus Narrative Disruption

For this conceptual probe only, stipulate that the purchased provision really
does include adequate ordinary household support and agreed caretaker duties.
This tests how that provision behaves; it does not prove its acquisition price.

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Another ordinary month passes | Continue the established operation. No sustaining roll is introduced solely because time passed. |
| The owner is away within the arrangement's normal expectations | Resident caretakers continue their agreed duties. Absence alone does not erase the support that was acquired. |
| Harvest or another supporting resource is materially disrupted | Reassess the actual support. Existing resources might absorb the loss; a consequential uncertain shortfall can become play. |
| The owner redirects support away from the household | The original self-supporting premise may no longer hold. Do not keep the same support and its diverted value simultaneously. |
| The household is asked to maintain a substantially larger operation | This exceeds the original promise unless additional support exists. No automatic free expansion follows from ownership. |

No automatic Condition levels or fixed monthly Difficulty are assigned.
Disruption creates a question about the situation, not an obligatory roll
for every change in the fiction.

## Test 4: Reserve Is Not Sustaining Support

The 750 left after the mansion-and-farm comparison is a finite reserve.
It can help bridge a shortfall, but it cannot by itself establish indefinite
maintenance. A productive holding, a supporting income relationship, and a
cash reserve have different functions. The same provision may include them
together when explicitly defined, without creating separate mandatory tracks.

## Findings

The external cost comparison supports the plausibility of meaningful
residential-plus-productive holdings below the individual starting ceiling,
while other combinations exceed it. No target Rank was needed to obtain
those results, and no single manor price follows from them.

The conceptual operation test supports honoring ordinary maintenance when
it is explicitly part of the acquired provision. A recurring roll is not
necessary merely to justify continued ownership or prevent automatic decay.

The unresolved input is a valued support arrangement sufficient for the
specified household and property. The peer prices above do not establish
that sufficiency. Defining it is needed before calling any combined price
a fully maintained holding; it does not require simulating every wage or meal.

## Approximate Valuation and Stretch Acquisition Outcome Test

The user's subsequent clarification changes how the monetary comparisons are
used: these are approximate valuations of starting means, not exact spending
limits. A plausible provision slightly beyond the reference may warrant an
Assets Expression, rather than rejection over a precise monetary shortfall.
Clearly fitting provisions do not require a roll merely because they have value.

Control: Assets Rank 10, a 10,000 additional-means reference, and a roughly
11,000 maintained holding. The requested holding includes ordinary support for
the residence and a small resident staff household, but no promised owner surplus.
Difficulty 5 is the user's illustrative resistance fixture, not an adopted manor
Difficulty or a conversion from price, Assets Rank, or percentage over budget.

This is an outcome-branch test. Success and failure are stipulated; no dice were
simulated and no success probability is claimed. An actual roll would use the
complete applicable Leverage / Assets Expression, not Assets Rank alone.

| Outcome | Starting provision | What the result establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Success | The described functioning, ordinarily supported holding | The stretch is secured without adding an automatic debt, Condition, or monthly upkeep roll. |
| Failure; player accepts reduced accommodation | The same ordinarily supported household, but its guest wing is unusable until restored | A useful home remains, while hosting capacity is genuinely unavailable. This is not merely a cosmetic description of peeling paint. Restoration is future work, not an extra compulsory construction roll. |
| Failure; player instead accepts conditional tenure | Use of the functioning supported holding depends on an explicitly agreed patron relationship; the Character cannot freely sell or transfer it | A distinct alternative concession changes control and Access. It is not automatically imposed alongside the damaged-wing option. |
| Failure; player declines the offered concession | The proposed stretch acquisition is not secured | No unwanted bargain, involuntary debt, or imposed Condition follows from declining. Retry and fallback-selection procedures are not established by this test. |

The concessions above are test candidates, not fixed discounts or mandatory
outcomes. Their significance depends on the intended use and campaign; an unusable
guest wing is a poor concession if the agreed provision never included meaningful
hosting capacity. The approximate difference does not become a mandatory 1,000 debt.

Finding: the outcome structure can preserve both useful starting provisions and
meaningful consequences without exact-price accounting or another resolution
system. It does not establish numerical odds, universal concession values, or
permission to attempt acquisitions far outside the Character's plausible means.

Recommendation: use this as the provisional procedure in the next complete
starting-character comparison. Return to the character package; further manor
pricing is not needed for that test. Keep the number of stretch attempts and
post-failure selection handling unresolved until that comparison requires them.
