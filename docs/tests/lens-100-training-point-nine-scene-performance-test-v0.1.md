## Sources And Limits

Use the three exact Training portfolios and uniform 25/50 Attribute/Derived
allocations in `lens-100-training-point-character-profile-test-v0.1.md`.
All Attributes are 5 and all Derived Ranks 2, giving a core of 6. The reference
named these intended concepts, not proven veteran/expert performance levels.

Difficulty verified against the baseline's Difficulty Curve and the earlier
Training-pool test: D1 x0.98; D2 x0.588; D3 x0.3528. Each additional step
multiplies by 0.6. D0 is not labeled Standard or assigned to these scenes.

These are nine fictional, unopposed performance probes, not combat encounters
or claims about real clinical outcomes. Their specific difficulty placements
are test assumptions, not adopted anchors. D1 is deliberately used as the
most favorable benchmark within the rolled ladder; D2/D3 sensitivity is shown.
If a scene is automatic in actual play, it succeeds without this roll instead.

Ordinary acts such as preparing a familiar meal without pressure, reading a
known language, or driving a familiar unobstructed route are not assigned a
failure chance merely to test them. The probes assume meaningful uncertainty
and a consequential limited opportunity. This does not create a deadline
Dissonance on top of Difficulty for the same problem.

Tool controls: x1.10 for the shopkeeper's useful ordinary tools, x1.20 for the
specified firearms and clinical equipment. These lie within the approved
ordinary range, but the item placements remain provisional. Equipment is
assumed available for testing; starting acquisition is not settled here.
The guide's unfamiliar document task has no additional equipment multiplier.

Scale is M1 x1 for character and task. All are at full current functioning;
no condition Dissonance, HOLT, additional active Resonance, action division,
or mitigation is applied. No preparation bonus is invented. Presence of tools
does not earn both equipment and an identical extra Resonance contribution.

## Expected Fiction Before Calculating

- Everyday adult: expected to do better in practiced shop work than an
  unfamiliar technical subject. No narrow Specialty was purchased, so a
  related trained shop application need not show a sharp drop.
- Guide: intended veteran concept should be strongest with the practiced
  revolver, retain meaningful other-handgun competence, and have weaker
  unfamiliar scholarly performance. Whether the strongest rolled result is
  sufficiently reliable to feel veteran remains a campaign judgment.
- Clinician: intended expert concept should benefit strongly from the deepest
  medical route while retaining useful broader surgical competence. Neither
  the concept nor narrow Rank 10 guarantees overall expert performance.

These expectations are qualitative, not newly adopted minimum percentages.
The question is both whether the relative pattern holds and whether the
absolute results support those intended labels.

## Findings And Next Proposal

1. Optional layers and fallback work consistently in all nine arithmetic cases.
2. Original labels were concept descriptions, not established performance tiers.
   Several original broadly spread portfolios have low rolled reliability even
   at D1. The existence of an affordable list does not settle scene competence.
3. The same 25/50/100 budget can materially improve a signature route by
   reallocating capability and Training, with visible losses elsewhere.
4. This does not prove 100 is sufficient for broad veteran competence, nor
   prove it insufficient. A desired breadth-and-performance target is needed.
5. No curve, mandatory Precision purchase, or budget increase follows from
   these results. Do not fix a perceived problem by inventing scene Resonance.

Next proposal: build the three concepts with intentional, concept-led
Attribute/Derived/Training allocations at the same 25/50/100 totals, instead
of treating uniform diagnostic allocations as finished characters. State
their intended strengths and acceptable gaps before allocating, then rerun
these same probes. Keep difficulties and equipment controls visible and
provisional; test Automatic cases as Automatic rather than low-probability
rolls. Only a remaining shortfall across the intended capabilities can support
a responsible proposal to change the Campaign Profile's point budget.
