# LENS Derivative Attribute Survey

Version: 0.1
Status: Research record; Derived Attribute decision promoted to system baseline
Date: 2026-08-21
Updated: 2026-08-22
Authority: C:\LENS-SYSTEM

---

## Purpose

This document surveys how a broad selection of tabletop role-playing games
divide broad human capability into primary Attributes, secondary
characteristics, derived statistics, skills, defenses, and resources.

The immediate LENS question is:

```text
What stable, narrower capability categories could sit beneath Might,
Mobility, Psyche, and Influence without becoming Skills, Specializations,
resources, or a catalogue of powers?
```

This document originally compared possible derivative-Attribute structures.

On 2026-08-22, the term `Derived Attribute`, a five-per-Attribute chassis, and
the twenty selected Derived Attributes were promoted into the active LENS
system baseline.

Earlier candidate lists remain preserved as development history.

Might, Mobility, Psyche, and Influence remain the current four-Attribute
working candidate inherited from MOTURPG. Classifying this survey through
those four categories tests the candidate chassis; it does not promote the
chassis to universal LENS doctrine.

## Existing LENS Boundary

The current working Base Expression architecture is:

```text
Character
↓
Classification
↓
Attribute
↓
Scale
↓
Derived Attribute
↓
Skill
↓
Specialization
=
Base Expression
```

Derived Attributes formalize the layer previously called Attribute Expression.

Examples already used for Attribute Expression include:

```text
Might
→ Strength
→ Endurance

Mobility
→ Balance

Psyche
→ Awareness

Influence
→ Leadership
```

This is a terminology refinement, not a new layer:

```text
Derivative Attribute
=
a fixed, consistently named form of Attribute Expression
```

`Expression` remains the broader term for the action or capability ultimately
produced through the full Lens stack.

## Research Definition

For this survey, a useful derivative-Attribute candidate must be:

```text
1. Narrower than one of the four broad Attributes.

2. Broader than a learned field, task, technique, or specialization.

3. Meaningfully improvable as a general capability.

4. Applicable across multiple actions and settings.

5. Distinct enough that increasing it does not automatically increase every
   other expression of the parent Attribute.

6. Capable of receiving permission or magnification from a Higher-Order Lens.

7. Comparable in breadth to the other derivative Attributes in the final
   chassis.
```

Examples:

```text
Strength
Likely derivative-Attribute candidate.

Weightlifting
Likely Skill or Specialization.

Carrying Capacity
Likely calculated output or fictional result of Strength.

Hit Points
Likely resource or harm capacity, not a derivative Attribute.
```

## Source and Sample Controls

The survey prioritizes publisher-hosted rules, official system reference
documents, official character sheets, and official rules databases.

The sample was selected for architectural diversity rather than market share.

It includes systems that:

- use three to ten broad Attributes;
- make secondary characteristics separately purchasable;
- calculate defenses from several Attributes;
- derive broad resistance traits from narrower actions;
- use Skills without an Attribute layer;
- combine capability and resource pools;
- support ordinary, heroic, horror, science-fiction, and superhero play.

Repeated descendants of the same six-score d20 structure are not treated as
independent proof merely because they use different setting material.

## Surveyed Systems

### 1. Dungeons & Dragons 2024

Primary abilities:

```text
Strength
Dexterity
Constitution
Intelligence
Wisdom
Charisma
```

Relevant derived statistics:

```text
Passive Perception
Initiative
Armor Class
Hit Points
Saving throw modifiers
```

LENS evidence:

- Strength and Constitution separate power from bodily resilience.
- Dexterity combines agility, reflexes, balance, and much precision.
- Intelligence separates reasoning and memory from Wisdom's perception and
  mental fortitude.
- Charisma combines presence and social effectiveness.
- Passive Perception is an Attribute-Skill composite.
- Initiative, Armor Class, and Hit Points are outputs, defenses, or resources,
  not clean derivative Attributes.

Official sources:

- https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game
- https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/creating-a-character

### 8. Blades in the Dark

Narrow action ratings:

```text
Insight:
Hunt, Study, Survey, Tinker

Prowess:
Finesse, Prowl, Skirmish, Wreck

Resolve:
Attune, Command, Consort, Sway
```

Broad Attribute ratings are derived upward from investment across the action
ratings beneath them.

LENS evidence:

- This reverses the common direction: broad resistance capacity emerges from
  narrower development.
- The model rewards breadth within an Attribute family.
- Its action list mixes inherent capability, learned practice, and approach,
  so the exact categories should not be copied into LENS.
- It demonstrates that the relationship between broad and narrow ratings can
  be mechanically meaningful in both directions.

Official source:

- https://bladesinthedark.com/actions-attributes

### 9. Cypher System

Core stat families:

```text
Might
Speed
Intellect
```

```text

```

Related character measures include Effort, recovery rolls, damage track,
Skills, and special abilities.

LENS evidence:

- Might, Speed, and Intellect are intentionally broad action and resource

- Pool and Edge separate capacity from efficiency inside the same stat family.
- The system absorbs social action into Intellect rather than preserving a
  separate external nonphysical Attribute.
- Pool depletion and the damage track are resources and conditions, not
  derivative Attributes.

Official source:

- https://www.montecookgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Cypher-Tools-Sample-Character-2024-02-27.pdf

### 10. Open Legend

Ordinary Attributes:

```text
Physical:
Agility, Fortitude, Might

Mental:
Learning, Logic, Perception, Will

Social:
Deception, Persuasion, Presence
```

The system also has separate extraordinary Attributes such as Alteration,
Creation, Energy, Entropy, Influence, Movement, Prescience, and Protection.

LENS evidence:

- The ordinary list is the strongest surveyed direct candidate bank for LENS
  derivative Attributes.
- Learning, Logic, Perception, and Will demonstrate a clean four-way division
  of internal nonphysical capacity.
- Deception, Persuasion, and Presence show that social capability can be split
  without immediately becoming individual Skills.
- Its extraordinary Attributes solve permissions by adding a separate power
  vocabulary. LENS is specifically testing whether Higher-Order Lenses can
  provide those permissions without importing that catalogue.

Official sources:

- https://openlegendrpg.com/core-rules/character-creation/
- https://openlegendrpg.com/core-rules/running-the-game/

## Architecture Families Found

The twenty systems do not merely offer different names. They use at least five
different architectures.

### Architecture A: Broad Primary Attributes

Examples:

```text
Cairn
Cypher System
Year Zero Engine
The One Ring
```

Strength:

```text
Fast character comprehension.
Few values to maintain.
Broad applicability.
```

Weakness:

```text
Cannot easily distinguish strength from toughness, perception from reasoning,
or presence from empathy without relying on Skills or exceptions.
```

### Architecture B: Broad Attributes with Derived Outputs

Examples:

```text
Dungeons & Dragons
Pathfinder
Basic Roleplaying
Savage Worlds
Dragonbane
13th Age
```

Strength:

```text
Familiar and readable.
Important combat and resource values can be calculated consistently.
```

Weakness:

```text
Calculated defenses and resources are often mistaken for independently
improvable capabilities even when they contain equipment, training, level, or
several Attributes.
```

### Architecture C: Broad Attributes with Adjustable Secondaries

Primary example:

```text
GURPS
```

Strength:

```text
A character can be more perceptive than their general Intelligence, possess
more Will than their Intelligence suggests, or move faster than their broad
physical traits imply.
```

Weakness:

```text
Many linked values and individual point costs increase construction and
character-sheet density.
```

### Architecture D: Many Peer-Level Capability Attributes

Examples:

```text
Open Legend
Adventure Game Engine
Cyberpunk RED
Eclipse Phase
Shadowrun
Mutants & Masterminds
```

Strength:

```text
Characters differentiate without requiring every distinction to become a
Skill.
```

Weakness:

```text
The system can blur inherent capacity, learned performance, permission,
defense, and supernatural power into one long Attribute list.
```

### Architecture E: Narrow Ratings Build Broad Capacity

Primary example:

```text
Blades in the Dark
```

Strength:

```text
Broad resistance rewards breadth across narrower development.
```

Weakness:

```text
The narrow action list mixes capacity, method, and learned practice.
```

### Architecture F: No Mandatory Attribute Layer

Primary example:

```text
Fate Core
```

Strength:

```text
Only table-relevant competencies receive ratings.
```

Weakness:

```text
Broad natural capability and narrower training are intentionally collapsed.
That does not answer the current LENS need for layered focus.
```

## Normalized Candidate Inventory

The following inventory translates recurring concepts into the current LENS
four-Attribute frame.

It does not select final names.

### Might Candidate Family

Might is the internal physical locus.

```text
Strength / Brawn / Power
Raw force, lifting, pushing, striking, gripping, or bodily output.

Toughness / Constitution / Fortitude / Durability
Resistance to injury, poison, disease, pressure, pain, and bodily disruption.

Stamina / Endurance / Vigor
Sustained physical exertion and resistance to fatigue or deprivation.

Recovery / Healing Rate
Restoration of ordinary physical function after strain or injury.

Adaptation / Hardiness
Functioning under hostile bodily or environmental demands.
```

Boundary notes:

- Toughness and Stamina are frequently bundled but answer different questions.
- Recovery is a rate or capacity; extraordinary regeneration still requires
  appropriate permission.
- Adaptation may be part of Toughness unless the final chassis needs to
  distinguish resisting harm from functioning in alien conditions.
- Hit Points, Wounds, and Fatigue Points are resources, not members of this
  family.

#### Might Longlist

Status:

```text
EXPLORATORY WORD AND FUNCTION BANK
NOT A FINAL LIST OF PEER CATEGORIES
```

Everything below remains available through ordinary Might.

These words identify possible narrower places where a character might choose
additional focus. Some are broad candidates, some are synonyms, some are
borderline concepts, and some are likely too narrow. They are retained here to
support comparison and lateral thinking before reduction.

```text
Strength
General production of bodily force.

Force
The direct physical output generated by the body.

Power
Force expressed rapidly or explosively.
Potentially distinct from sustained or controlled Strength, but "Power" has
other major meanings in LENS and its settings.

Brawn
An ordinary-language container for muscular force and physical heft.

Might
The parent Attribute itself and therefore too broad to serve as its own
derivative.

Leverage
Efficient application of force through position and mechanics.
Likely depends heavily on Skill, technique, tools, and situation.

Lifting
Force applied to raising or supporting weight.
Likely a Skill, Specialization, or application of Strength.

Load-Bearing
Supporting or carrying sustained weight.
Potentially combines Strength, Stamina, structure, and Scale.

Grip
Localized holding, crushing, or retention force.
Probably too narrow for a core derivative, but a useful Specialization.

Toughness
Remaining functional when struck, cut, crushed, burned, or otherwise injured.

Durability
Resistance to wear, damage, and physical breakdown over time.
Potentially a more construct-friendly word than Toughness.

Integrity
The body's ability to remain structurally coherent under stress or damage.
Potentially applicable to flesh, constructs, vehicles, and other entities.

Hardness
Resistance to penetration, deformation, or surface damage.
Often a material or equipment property rather than a whole-character focus.

Resilience
The ability to withstand disruption and continue functioning.
Potentially too broad because it may include Toughness, Stamina, Recovery, and
mental resistance.

Constitution
Systemic physical soundness and resistance to poison, disease, deprivation,
pressure, shock, and bodily disruption.

Health
General bodily condition.
Often describes current state more than specialized capability.

Vitality
The body's active aliveness, energy, and capacity to maintain function.
Potentially overlaps Constitution, Stamina, and Recovery.

Vigor
Energetic physical function and readiness.
Potentially overlaps Strength, Stamina, and Vitality.

Stamina
Sustaining physical effort before fatigue degrades performance.

Endurance
Continuing through exertion, deprivation, discomfort, or prolonged hardship.
Potentially broader than Stamina and may overlap Constitution or Will.

Conditioning
Developed capacity for sustained physical performance.
The word strongly implies training and may therefore sit near Skill.

Fatigue Resistance
Resistance to exhaustion during sustained activity.
A precise but narrow statement of part of Stamina.

Reserve
Stored physical capacity available for prolonged or repeated exertion.
May describe a resource rather than a derivative Attribute.

Recovery
Returning toward ordinary function after exertion, injury, or bodily stress.

Recuperation
Restoration through rest, treatment, nourishment, or time.
A possible narrower or more naturalistic label for Recovery.

Healing
Repairing physical injury.
Ordinary healing may be governed through Recovery; extraordinary healing still
requires permission.

Regeneration
Rapid, extensive, or otherwise extraordinary restoration.
Best treated as permission expressed through Recovery rather than as an
ordinary derivative by itself.

Regrowth
Replacing lost bodily structures.
An extraordinary permission or narrow expression of Regeneration.

Adaptation
Remaining functional in an environment or bodily condition outside ordinary
expectations.

Hardiness
Functioning despite climate, deprivation, exposure, illness, or hardship.
Potentially overlaps Constitution, Stamina, and Adaptation.

Tolerance
The amount of a specific substance, pressure, temperature, pain, or condition
the body can endure.
Likely a specialization unless retained as a broad systemic category.

Acclimation
Developing tolerance through exposure and time.
Potentially an application of Adaptation or Recovery.

Homeostasis
Maintaining stable internal function despite external or internal change.
Potentially a precise function beneath Constitution.

Metabolism
Processing fuel, toxins, medicine, heat, and other bodily inputs.
Likely narrower than a core derivative but useful for identifying the scope of
Constitution or Adaptation.

Resistance
Opposition to a named physical influence.
Too generic without specifying what is resisted.

Immunity
Complete or exceptional non-susceptibility to a named influence.
Normally a permission or effect, not a derivative Attribute.

Pain Tolerance
Continuing to act despite pain.
Potentially crosses Toughness, Stamina, and Psyche → Will.

Shock Resistance
Maintaining function after acute trauma.
Likely a narrow application of Constitution or Toughness.

Stability
Remaining physically set, braced, or difficult to displace.
Potentially sits at the Might/Mobility boundary.

Rootedness
Resisting forced movement through stance, mass, grip, or connection.
Likely a specialized expression combining Might, Mobility, and situation.

Density
Physical concentration of mass or material.
Usually Classification, Scale, bodily construction, or Higher-Order Lens
permission rather than ordinary specialization.

Mass
Amount of physical matter.
Usually Classification or Scale information rather than focused capability.

Flexibility
Range of bodily deformation or articulation without injury.
Potentially belongs under Mobility unless granted as an extraordinary bodily
permission.

Elasticity
Stretching and returning to form.
Usually an extraordinary permission rather than ordinary Might focus.

Breath
Holding, controlling, or efficiently using respiration.
Likely a narrow application of Stamina, Constitution, or Adaptation.

Physical Control
General command of internal bodily function.
Potentially too broad and may cross into Mobility or Psyche.
```

#### Current Recommended Might Functions

The strongest current recommendation is not five final words.

It is five functional territories worth testing against the longlist:

```text
1. Force Production

Candidate labels:
Strength
Force
Brawn

Question answered:
How much physical force can the Character generate?

2. Structural Integrity

Candidate labels:
Toughness
Durability
Integrity

Question answered:
How well does the Character remain physically coherent and functional under
direct damage or deformation?

3. Systemic Resistance

Candidate labels:
Constitution
Hardiness
Vitality

Question answered:
How well does the Character maintain bodily function against poison, disease,
exposure, deprivation, pressure, shock, and internal disruption?

4. Sustained Output

Candidate labels:
Stamina
Endurance
Vigor

Question answered:
How long can the Character sustain physical effort before fatigue degrades the
expression?

5. Restoration

Candidate labels:
Recovery
Recuperation
Healing

Question answered:
How effectively does the Character return toward ordinary physical function
after exertion, injury, or bodily stress?
```

This recommendation deliberately separates direct damage from systemic
resistance and separates sustaining function from restoring function.

Current strongest challengers to the five-function recommendation:

```text
Power
May deserve separation from Strength if explosive force produces meaningfully
different characters from sustained force.

Adaptation
May deserve separation from Constitution if functioning in alien environments
is common enough to support independent investment.

Stability
May deserve a place if resisting displacement is too important to leave as a
combined Might/Mobility expression.

Vitality
May replace Constitution or Stamina if LENS needs a more setting-neutral term
for active bodily function.
```

No candidate is excluded from ordinary Might when it lacks a separate rating.

The longlist is a vocabulary and function bank, not a permission list.

### Mobility Candidate Family

Mobility is the external physical locus and the Character's physical
relationship to the world.

```text
Agility / Balance / Poise
Whole-body control, stability, repositioning, and graceful movement.

Precision / Dexterity / Coordination
Fine motor control, aim, manipulation, and exact physical placement.

Reflex / Reaction
Physical response to sudden change.

Speed / Movement
Rate of physical relocation.

Propulsion
The physical capacity to generate or redirect movement, including running,
jumping, launching, swimming, or other permitted movement forms.
```

Boundary notes:

- Many systems bundle all five into Dexterity or Agility.
- Speed is often a calculated output rather than a purchased capability.
- Propulsion is a LENS-specific candidate prompted by the need to route
  strength-driven leaps, wave-riding, flight, and other permitted movement
  without increasing every form of Agility.
- Initiative is a procedural output involving Reflex, awareness, and the
  situation; it is not automatically a derivative Attribute.
- Weapon accuracy normally also requires Skill.

### Psyche Candidate Family

Psyche is the internal locus of will, thought, intelligence, awareness,
discipline, or energy.

```text
Reason / Logic / Cognition
Analysis, problem-solving, inference, and deliberate thought.

Learning / Memory / Knowledge Capacity
Acquiring, retaining, and recalling information.

Awareness / Perception
Receiving and identifying relevant information.

Intuition / Insight / Wits
Rapid synthesis, instinctive judgment, and pattern recognition.

Will / Resolve / Spirit / Composure
Maintaining internal direction against fear, pain, temptation, confusion, or
mental interference.
```

Boundary notes:

- Subject knowledge remains a Skill or learned Lens even if Learning or Memory
  becomes a derivative Attribute.
- Awareness and Intuition are frequently bundled. Separating them may be useful
  only if one means detection and the other means interpretation.
- Sanity, Stress, Lucidity, and Hope are resources or condition measures.
- Luck and metacurrency should not be hidden inside Psyche without a separate
  doctrine decision.

### Influence Candidate Family

Influence is the external locus of projected, social, intellectual, or
nonphysical control.

```text
Presence / Charisma / Cool
The force, salience, confidence, or emotional weight of the Character's
outward expression.

Rapport / Empathy / Savvy
Understanding, connecting with, and aligning with another actor.

Persuasion / Sway / Communication
Changing another actor's judgment through reasons, appeal, exchange, or
negotiation.

Command / Authority / Intimidation
Directing behavior through leadership, pressure, hierarchy, or threat.

Guile / Deception / Manipulation
Changing another actor's understanding through concealment, misdirection, or
false presentation.

Projection
Extending will, thought, energy, or a Higher-Order Lens outward into the world.
```

Boundary notes:

- Most surveyed games treat Influence primarily as social capability.
- Current LENS doctrine also asks Influence to carry projected nonphysical
  control. Projection therefore appears as a real LENS-specific pressure that
  the RPG survey alone cannot settle.
- Persuasion, Command, and Guile may be Skills if the system treats their
  general capacities as Rapport, Presence, or Direction.
- Social status, reputation, authority granted by office, followers, and
  wealth are persistent Lenses or Infrastructure, not inherent derivative
  Attributes.

## Higher-Order Lens Routing Test

The derivative chassis must support the current Higher-Order Lens problem
without turning each derivative into a power.

Working test structure:

```text
Higher-Order Lens
grants thematic permission.

Derivative Attribute rating
represents general focus in that narrower human capability.

Higher-Order Lens channel investment
determines how strongly that concept magnifies through that derivative.
```

These may be separate ratings.

Example:

```text
Character:
Ordinary guard

Might:
High

Strength derivative:
Trained

Higher-Order Strength permission:
Absent

Result:
The guard may be exceptionally strong by ordinary standards.
The guard does not gain permission to catch a mountain or clap out a
shockwave.
```

Example:

```text
Character:
Strength-focused extraordinary character

Higher-Order Lens:
Strength

Might → Strength channel:
Highly magnified

Mobility → Propulsion channel:
Moderately magnified

Result:
The character may perform impossible lifting and strength-driven leaps.
The character does not automatically gain Toughness, Stamina, Agility,
Precision, or Reflex magnification.
```

Example:

```text
Character:
Sea-focused extraordinary character

Higher-Order Lens:
Sea

Might channel:
Toughness or Adaptation

Mobility channel:
Propulsion

Psyche channel:
Awareness

Influence channel:
Rapport or Direction

Result:
One concept may coherently magnify several different derivative channels.
The character's point distribution determines which expressions are strong.
```

The examples above preserve the routing problem as it existed during the
survey. Their older candidate names do not supersede the promoted chassis.

They do not lock Higher-Order Lens arithmetic.

## Latent Derivative Clarification

Status:

```text
LOCKED FOR THE CURRENT SYSTEM BASELINE
```

Derivative Attributes are not mandatory separate ratings on every character.

```text
An unspecialized derivative remains part of its parent Attribute.

The character uses the parent Attribute normally.

A derivative receives a separate rating only when the character deliberately
specializes in it or another Lens specifically modifies it.
```

The existence of a derivative category therefore does not create a deficiency
for a character who has not purchased it.

It creates an optional point of narrower focus.

Possible presentation:

```text
MIGHT 6

Strength        +3
Recovery        +1

Unlisted Might derivatives continue to use Might without an additional
modifier.
```

This preserves a fixed vocabulary without requiring every character sheet to
display sixteen or twenty unchanged values.

### Derivative Locus Inheritance Rule

Status:

```text
LOCKED IN THE ACTIVE SYSTEM DRAFT
```

```text
A derivative inherits the locus of its parent Attribute.

Its name does not extend its applicability beyond that locus.
```

A derivative is not a free-standing keyword.

Shared wording does not move it between Attributes.

Therefore:

```text
Might → Resistance

applies to internal physical resistance.

It does not answer fear, telepathy, coercion, deception, or another mental or
social influence merely because a player can describe the action as
"resisting."
```

Another applicable Lens may explicitly establish a cross-locus relationship.

The derivative's name, metaphor, or wordplay cannot establish that
relationship by itself.

## Primary Findings

### Finding 1: The Layer Is Real

The survey strongly supports a capability layer between very broad Attributes
and learned Skills.

The recurring distinctions are too persistent to dismiss as setting detail:

```text
Strength versus Toughness
Agility versus Reflex
Reason versus Perception
Presence versus Empathy
```

### Finding 2: Derived Attribute Formalizes Attribute Expression

The current LENS stack already contained the layer under the earlier name
Attribute Expression.

`Derived Attribute` is now the adopted name for its fixed current chassis.

### Finding 3: Latent Derivatives Remove the Density Objection

Three, four, and five derivatives do not require every character to maintain
three, four, or five separate ratings beneath every Attribute.

An unspecialized derivative remains covered by the parent Attribute.

Only a selected specialization or specific modifier creates a separate entry.

Therefore, the final count should not be reduced merely to avoid character-
sheet density or fear that distinctions will collapse during ordinary play.

The count should be selected by asking:

```text
How many consistently useful and comparably broad specialization points exist
beneath each Attribute?
```

### Finding 4: Influence Was the Diagnostic Attribute

Might, Mobility, and Psyche produce relatively stable candidate families.

Influence exposed the decisive scope question:

```text
Is Influence primarily social relationship and direction?

Or does it also contain all projected nonphysical expression?
```

The selected Presence, Projection, Connection, Manipulation, and Control set
recognizes both social application and projected nonphysical expression.

### Finding 5: Permission and Magnification Must Stay Separate

No ordinary derivative rating should silently grant:

```text
regeneration;
mountain-catching strength;
flight;
telepathy;
water control;
or similar extraordinary permissions.
```

The derivative identifies where capability is focused.

Classification, Higher-Order Lens, Artifact, or another permission-bearing
Lens identifies what extraordinary expression is possible.

### Finding 6: Five Per Attribute Is the Current Baseline

A consistent final number supports:

- the same broad-to-narrow relationship beneath every Attribute;
- a symmetric Higher-Order Lens routing structure;
- predictable optional specialization;
- a unified learn-once rule.

Five useful in-game applications were identified beneath each current
Attribute without requiring a category that exists only to complete the count.

This decision does not establish a percentage cost.
