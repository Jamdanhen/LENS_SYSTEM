## Objective

The user's "Tested" follows the offered test and is interpreted as "test it."
This authorizes the test, not adoption of its assumptions or recommendations.

## Controls

Two Sources each commit one action and earn four and three applicable Effects.
One uncertain defense earns the stated output through its own complete Expression.
All equipment and contextual modifiers are already included. There is no added
team roll, second equipment application, or second success baseline.

Examples operate within a compatible frame with established permissions.
Fiction determines what a response can address, including the target, timing,
means, and any required perception. Choosing the smallest number is not itself
a valid method of targeting a contribution or connection.

The tests isolate generated Effects and cancellation. They do not estimate
encounter pacing or settle mitigation frequency.

## 1. Oppose the Combined Result

Two compatible contributions form one actual seven-Effect attack. A defense
capable of opposing that whole result uses ordinary cancellation against seven.

| Earned defense | Remaining attack | Potential defensive-surplus counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 7 | 0 |
| 3 | 4 | 0 |
| 7 | 0 | 0 |
| 9 | 0 | At most 1, if fictionally supported |

One three-Effect defense is not copied into three against A plus another three
against B. Four possible allocations of that same three-Effect budget across
the compatible four/three contributions all leave four total Effects.
Allocation is an accounting check, not a mandatory extra player procedure.

Two unrelated attacks remain two exchanges. If each is met by a separately
resolved three-Effect defense, their remainders are one and zero. The fact that
two people coordinate does not automatically transform separate attacks into
one favorable exchange. Conversely, one genuine combined result is not split
into separate defenses merely because two Sources created it.

This is not a universal limit on defenses per round or against a team. Any
further distinct response must have its own ordinary fictional and timing basis;
the compound label grants no extra response and removes no existing permission.

## 2. Oppose One Contribution

A permitted response can address B's three Effects but cannot address A's four.
The contributions reinforce the result, but A can still function without B.

| Defense applicable to B | B remaining | A remaining | Combined remaining |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| 3 | 0 | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 0 | 4 | 4 |

Five defense Effects do not erase five from the shared seven when the method
can only answer B. The two surplus Effects do not automatically spill onto A.
An eligible defensive-surplus counter remains at most one in that exchange;
neither a second target nor damage to either Source is automatic.

If the response can genuinely answer both contributions, assess it as such
before resolution. Do not choose targets retrospectively after seeing rolls.

## 3. Oppose Necessary Delivery

Fire supplies four Burning Effects. Wind supplies three delivery Effects.
The distant target can only be reached through this Wind route in the declared
situation. The Fire can remain locally viable without Wind.

Cancel all three delivery Effects:

```text
Wind delivery: 0 remains.
Burning delivered to the distant target: 0.
Independently viable local Fire: its four Effects remain applicable locally.
```

This prevents the remote result without numerically cancelling the Fire.
It does not award a free redirected attack against a new local target. Only
the established viable residual remains, with normal allocation and applicability.

Cancel one delivery Effect instead:

```text
Wind delivery: 2 remain.
Fire generation: still 4.
Remote delivery: reassess what the surviving Wind can actually accomplish.
```

Partial cancellation is neither automatic total disruption nor a guarantee
that the original full delivery still works. Exact delivery distance/output
valuation is unresolved; no threshold or meters-per-Effect is invented here.

The weaker dependency can be an effective point of attack. That is a real
exposure of the chosen method, not grounds for imposing a universal combined
penalty. Reaching or affecting that dependency still requires an applicable
Expression and opportunity.

## 4. Break the Connecting Lens

An established conduit, timing relationship, formation, or alignment may be
necessary to bring otherwise viable contributions together.

Stipulate that an ordinarily resolved action has actually severed the necessary
connection. The combination ends; independent Fire and Wind may remain. Neither
Source is automatically suppressed. Dependent output cannot continue without
another already established viable route or a legitimately restored connection.

This test checks consequences of actual severance. It does not establish that
one Effect always breaks a connection, that every connection is weaker than its
Sources, or that the lowest contributor supplies its resistance.

Where a device or rated manifestation already supplies relevant resistance,
use the applicable existing rules. Bare coordination without an established
rating remains a numerical design question. Do not create a disposable generic
connection track to manufacture a convenient weak point.

## 5. Timing and Action Costs

| Intervention | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Actually disable a necessary launcher before release | The launch cannot complete; dependent delivery fails |
| Disable that launcher after an ordinary ballistic release | Flight is not retroactively undone |
| Disable a Source sustaining an ongoing permitted transport effect | Continued transport depends on the established maintenance requirement |
| Merely hit the launcher without preventing its remaining activity | Apply actual Conditions and reassess; no automatic cancellation |

An attack on the launcher uses an available action, such as a feasible held
response released by its declared perceptible trigger. It is not a free attack
granted by the open-defense rule. A genuinely defensive cancellation remains
an open defensive response where ordinarily permitted.

Once participation begins and its action is committed, interruption does not
refund that action. A partner whose action has not begun is not automatically
charged merely because they intended to participate. Apply the existing held-
response reassessment and Delay rules rather than inventing a team-wide refund
or forfeiture. Resuming an interrupted but still possible activity is not a
second charge. No new initiative roll or free replacement attack is introduced.

## Verification and Recommendation

`lens-multiple-source-opposition-test-v0.1.cjs` verifies seven numeric opposition
fixtures, four same-budget allocation checks, the separate-exchange control,
and stipulated delivery/timing cases. Passing assertions verify the candidate
arithmetic and encoded assumptions; they do not prove encounter balance or
solve the unresolved connection and delivery valuations.

Recommendation:

Resolve opposition against what the response actually addresses. Cancel
applicable output there, then follow established dependencies to determine the
combined result and viable residuals. Preserve ordinary action accounting and
the one-counter-effect-per-resolved-exchange limit. Do not duplicate one
defense across contributors or treat every interruption as total disruption.

This supports a useful tradeoff: coordinated output may concentrate against
one defense, while a genuinely necessary contribution or connection can offer
a different point of opposition. Neither is automatically available merely
because the action is called a compound Expression.

The meaningful adoption decision is this targeted-opposition procedure.
Remaining valuation work concerns unrated connections and partial complementary
output, not a need to re-test the arithmetic of compatible Effect addition.

## Follow-On Review: What Resists Disruption?

Status: Application guidance subsequently explicitly approved, 2026-09-11.
This is a fictional-routing comparison, not an additional numerical simulation.

The baseline already distinguishes active opposition (another actor deliberately
contests the action) from passive opposition (conditions, objects, hazards,
systems, and other non-acting resistance). Test whether that distinction can
answer the connection question without adding an intrinsic connection Rank.

| Attempt | What actually resists it? | Consequence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distract a character maintaining a coordinated activity | That character's applicable defensive Expression, when they actively resist | Earned Conditions affect the activity where relevant; distraction is not automatic total cancellation |
| Cut a cable required to transmit a combined effect | The actual cable, tools, access, and circumstances through ordinary passive opposition, plus any legitimate intervention | Actual severance ends transmission; it does not erase independently viable Sources |
| Obscure the visual signals required for coordination | The activity creating the obstruction and the resulting actual visibility | Partial obscurity supplies applicable interference; complete loss of a necessary signal may prevent coordination unless an established alternative answers it |

The first case does not introduce a free-standing coordination defense roll
into every team action. Resolve an actual hostile activity normally, reusing
an already-resolved relevant contribution when it is the very output opposed.
Do not demand another roll for the same resistance solely because the activity
is collaborative. Distinct interventions retain their ordinary costs and
opposition; no free offensive interruption is added.

The cable need not receive a new team-connection rating. Its actual resistance
may still require ordinary case-specific assessment; absence of an equipment
entry does not justify averaging the users' Attributes or substituting the
weaker participant's Rating.

Obscuring a signal must actually interfere with the participants' means. Smoke
does not defeat a nonvisual link simply because the objective was disruption.
Likewise, noise is not a generic penalty to every form of communication.

Recommendation: use the existing resistance of the actual actor, effect,
object, or circumstance that maintains the connection. When nothing distinct
is being attacked beyond an already-resolved contribution, do not invent an
additional target or resistance roll. This removes the need for a universal
connection-stat formula, but does not automatically settle every device's
durability, partial-delivery threshold, or environmental valuation.
