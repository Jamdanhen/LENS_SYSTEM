# LENS Stable Personal Lens Architecture Test

Version: 0.1  
Status: Working Architecture Test  
Purpose: Compare ranked, fixed-count, and campaign-constrained procedures for
bringing persistent personal truths into character creation.

Nothing in this record adopts a final stable-Lens category, point cost,
starting count, scope rule, or acquisition procedure.

---

## Question Under Test

```text
How should persistent character truths enter character creation?
```

Examples include:

```text
profession
institutional role
social status
relationship
responsibility
oath
reputation
resource
credential
affiliation
```

The test compares three models:

```text
Model A
Ranked and purchased stable personal Lenses

Model B
A fixed number of unranked stable personal Lenses

Model C
Campaign-constrained narrative stable personal Lenses
```

---

## Shared Controls

Campaign Profile:

```text
Grounded Urban Investigation
```

All three Characters use the same numerical construction resources:

```text
Classification: Human
Scale: 1
Attribute Points: 20
Derived Attribute Points: 40
Training Points: 60
Physical Capacity: 10
Mental Capacity: 10
```

The exact Attribute, Derived Attribute, Skill, and Specialization allocations
are held equal and excluded from the comparison.

The test therefore isolates stable personal truths rather than competence.

No stable personal Lens receives an automatic numerical modifier during the
scene tests.

Possible functions are evaluated through the existing LENS grammar:

```text
permission
Access
contextual Resonance
contextual Dissonance
obligation
vulnerability
counterplay
resource relationship
```

## Test Characters

### Character A: Municipal Investigator

Proposed stable truth:

```text
Municipal Investigator
```

Possible content:

```text
recognized professional identity
investigative responsibility
institutional affiliation
limited legal authority
ordinary professional credentials
accountability to the municipality
```

### Character B: Independent Journalist

Proposed stable truth:

```text
Independent Journalist
```

Possible content:

```text
recognized press identity when credentials or reputation support it
professional interest in public information
public-facing reputation
responsibility to sources or publication standards
exposure to retaliation, legal pressure, and credibility loss
```

The phrase does not automatically create a source network, employer, staff,
wealth, or institutional protection. Those are separate truths if present.

### Character C: Wealthy Civic Official

Initial proposed phrase:

```text
Wealthy Civic Official
```

The phrase contains at least two independently functioning truths:

```text
Civic Official

Personal Wealth
```

Office may be lost while wealth remains.

Wealth may be lost while office remains.

They provide different permissions, resources, obligations, vulnerabilities,
and counterplay.

The phrase must therefore be separated for mechanical use even if it remains
one convenient narrative description.

This becomes the principal scope and packing stress test.

## Shared Scenario 1: Entering a Restricted Incident Scene

### Municipal Investigator

```text
Municipal Investigator may establish Open or Conditional Access when:

the incident falls within jurisdiction;
the Character is assigned or recognized;
credentials are valid;
and another authority has not lawfully restricted entry.
```

The Lens does not guarantee cooperation or success once inside.

### Independent Journalist

```text
Independent Journalist may establish press standing at a public perimeter.

It does not automatically grant entry into a secured inner scene.

The Character may still seek permission, persuade an official, use a source,
investigate from outside, or attempt another narratively supported approach.
```

### Civic Official

```text
Civic Official may establish Access when the office possesses relevant
authority over the location, agency, or emergency.

Office outside its jurisdiction does not become universal authority.
```

### Personal Wealth

```text
Personal Wealth does not create legal entry.

It may support transportation, counsel, hired expertise, or another available
resource, but it does not purchase permission that the fiction denies.
```

Finding:

```text
The same situation produces different Access relationships without requiring
a ranked profession modifier.
```

## Shared Scenario 2: Obtaining Protected Information

### Municipal Investigator

```text
Institutional authority may permit access to records relevant to an authorized
case.

Unrelated, sealed, restricted, or privately controlled information remains
Conditional or Closed.
```

### Independent Journalist

```text
Public-information rights may permit ordinary requests.

Protected records still require a source, consent, investigation, legal
process, or another applicable relationship.
```

### Civic Official

```text
Office may permit records access within its lawful function.

Using office for unrelated personal curiosity may create Dissonance,
disciplinary exposure, or an unlawful action rather than additional Access.
```

### Personal Wealth

```text
Wealth may purchase research, counsel, or publicly available information.

It does not automatically open protected records.
```

Finding:

```text
Stable personal Lenses create specific relationships to information. They do
not replace Research, Investigation, Security, Difficulty, or opposition.
```

## Shared Scenario 3: Calling Upon Institutional Resources

### Municipal Investigator

```text
The role may permit ordinary departmental tools, records, communications, or
backup when the request serves an authorized function.

The institution may deny, delay, monitor, or condition that support.
```

### Independent Journalist

```text
Independent status supplies no employer resources by itself.

A publication relationship, source network, personal equipment, or financial
resource must be established separately if it is expected to carry mechanical
force.
```

### Civic Official

```text
The office may provide staff, records, facilities, or an official budget for
authorized purposes.

Those resources remain attached to the office rather than becoming unrestricted
personal property.
```

### Personal Wealth

```text
Wealth may provide personally purchasable goods and services within the
campaign's grounded scope.

It does not create unavailable technology, institutional command, or unlimited
immediate liquidity.
```

Finding:

```text
Resource permission is functional, but exact resource magnitude remains
underdefined without Campaign Profile expectations or another resource
procedure.
```

## Model A Test: Ranked and Purchased

Model:

```text
Each stable personal Lens receives a Rank and costs character-construction
points.
```

Questions immediately produced:

```text
What does Municipal Investigator Rank measure?

Does a higher Rank increase legal authority, professional competence,
institutional support, reputation, or all of them?

How is Personal Wealth priced against Civic Official?

Does obligation reduce cost?

Must every relationship, credential, reputation, and resource receive a
catalogue entry and enhancement or limitation procedure?
```

Result:

```text
MODEL A DOES NOT PASS THE FIRST ARCHITECTURE TEST.
```

Reason:

The Rank does not answer one stable conceptual question. It risks combining
scope, authority, reliability, resources, reputation, and competence inside
one number.

The model also begins producing the advantages-and-disadvantages catalogue
that the broader LENS architecture is trying to avoid.

This does not prove that no stable personal Lens may ever be ranked. It shows
that universal ranking and pricing should not be the default without a more
specific function.

## Model B Test: Fixed Number of Unranked Lenses

Model:

```text
Every Character receives the same fixed number of unranked stable personal
Lenses.
```

Packing test:

```text
Municipal Investigator

Independent Journalist

Wealthy Civic Official
```

If each phrase counts as one selection, the third phrase contains at least two
independently functioning truths while using the same slot.

Additional examples expose the same issue:

```text
Decorated Military Commander

Beloved Heir to an Industrial Fortune

Exiled Royal Sorcerer
```

Result:

```text
MODEL B DOES NOT PASS WITHOUT A SCOPE AND DECOMPOSITION RULE.
```

A fixed count alone rewards load-bearing labels and does not establish
equivalent fictional reach.

The count may still become a presentation tool after scope is established, but
it cannot perform the balancing work by itself.

## Model C Test: Campaign-Constrained Narrative Lenses

Model:

```text
The Campaign Profile establishes acceptable starting scope.

The player proposes persistent truths appropriate to the Character and
campaign.

Each truth is recorded separately when it performs mechanically distinct work.

No stable truth provides an automatic universal modifier merely for existing.

Its permission, Access, Resonance, Dissonance, obligation, vulnerability, and
counterplay follow its relevance to the fiction.
```

Scenario result:

```text
MODEL C PASSES ONLY THE APPLICABILITY TEST.

It does not yet pass acquisition, balance, starting-scope, or construction
tests.
```

It distinguishes all three Characters across the same scenes without:

```text
changing their competence values;
creating bespoke profession rules;
granting universal bonuses;
requiring a separate resource subsystem for every identity;
or preventing the same stable Lens from helping in one context and exposing
the Character in another.
```

Model C still requires explicit acquisition and scope controls. Campaign
agreement by itself does not establish comparable starting value.

## Scope Control Produced by Test

The test produced two necessary candidate controls.

### Distinct Truth Test

```text
If parts of a proposed stable personal Lens can be independently granted,
removed, countered, lost, transferred, or invoked, they perform distinct
mechanical work and should be recorded separately.
```

Example:

```text
Wealthy Civic Official

becomes

Civic Official
+ Personal Wealth
```

This is the stable-Lens application of the existing distinct-contribution
principle. It is not a new subsystem.

### Campaign Scope Test

```text
A proposed starting truth must fit the Campaign Profile's established range of
authority, resources, relationships, and fictional reach.

A broader proposal must be narrowed, separated, or explicitly accepted as an
asymmetrical campaign premise.
```

The Campaign Scope Test does not require exact point equivalence. It requires
the table to recognize when two concepts do not occupy comparable fictional
space.

## Invocation and Ownership Test

Once recorded, a stable personal Lens is part of the shared fiction.

```text
The player may invoke what it permits or supports.

The GM may apply its obligations, vulnerabilities, opposition, or limits.

Other players may rely upon or interact with the relationships it establishes.

No participant may honor only the favorable half while ignoring established
constraints.
```

This follows the existing principle:

```text
The fiction must honor what your Lenses can do, and you must honor what the
fiction says they cannot currently do.
```

No separate advantage-disadvantage accounting is required for this reciprocal
use.

---

## Resource Magnitude Stress Test

Model C clearly identifies when a resource relationship exists, but not always
its exact magnitude.

Examples:

```text
ordinary departmental equipment
office staff
emergency transportation
personal legal counsel
expensive specialized instruments
large liquid financial expenditure
```

Current finding:

```text
The Campaign Profile can establish ordinary expectations.

An Expression, Difficulty, opposition, time, availability, or another relevant
Lens can resolve uncertain requests.

Extremely large or campaign-altering resources may still require a more
specific setting implementation.
```

The test does not yet prove that LENS needs a universal wealth or resource
rating.

---

## Acquisition During Play

The test establishes starting use but exposes a later question:

```text
When a relationship, office, reputation, obligation, or resource becomes a
persistent truth during play, when is it added to the Character record?
```

No advancement cost or acquisition procedure is adopted here.

The likely trigger is fictional stability rather than temporary scene
advantage, but that requires later testing.

---

## Findings

```text
1. Stable personal Lenses can distinguish ordinary Characters without changing
   their Attributes, Derived Attributes, Skills, or Specializations.

2. Universal Rank and point pricing does not currently identify one coherent
   quantity for profession, relationship, reputation, resource, and status.

3. A fixed number of selections fails when broad phrases package several
   independently functioning truths into one label.

4. Campaign-constrained narrative Lenses survive all six first-pass
   applicability scenes, but their acquisition and starting value remain
   unresolved.

5. Stable personal Lenses do not need automatic universal modifiers.

6. They may establish permission, Access, Resonance, Dissonance, obligation,
   vulnerability, resources, or counterplay according to relevance.

7. Distinct truths should be separated when they can be independently invoked,
   lost, transferred, removed, or countered.

8. The Campaign Profile must establish acceptable starting fictional scope.

9. Once recorded, both favorable and unfavorable implications belong to the
   shared fiction.

10. Exact resource magnitude and acquisition during play remain unresolved.
```

---

## Revised Test Status

```text
INCONCLUSIVE.

Model A demonstrated the complexity and category problems created by universal
Rank and point pricing.

Model B demonstrated that a fixed count alone does not control conceptual
breadth.

Model C demonstrated that campaign-constrained narrative truths can be applied
consistently in scenes, but did not establish a fair acquisition procedure or
comparable starting value.

No model currently passes the complete architecture test.
```

The peer survey and functional decomposition test must occur before another
architecture is selected.
