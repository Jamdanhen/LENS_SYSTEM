## Additional Story Development

The story-on fixture adds the following without deducting from regular awards:

- Session 2: defined dependable support, assigned a provisional value of 1.
- Session 3: one optional point earmarked toward Survival Field development.
- Session 4: language Access after established prior study, provisional value 1.
- Session 6: one narratively justified Assets Rank, development value 20 at
  the adopted Derived Attribute price, assuming the parent cap permits it.

The two one-point provision values are experimental placeholders, not validated
purchase prices. No relationship or language is charged twice, no free expertise
Rank accompanies language Access, and no treasure size automatically grants an
Assets Rank. Naming someone who supplies existing support is not a new benefit.

Total delivered development value is 22, plus 1 earmarked credit. Only the credit
is progress toward a future purchase; it is not a fractional Rank. Ordinary
unspent points could complete that eligible purchase. The already-delivered
support, language, and Assets development do not also create spendable points.

The story-off control delivers none of these extras. No recurring quantity of
story awards is guaranteed, and no minimum story-value target is imposed.

## Immediate Spending Sensitivity

Spending cases use 0, 2, or 4 points during each session. These are expenditure
budgets, not a claim that any particular declaration or modifier costs one point.
Each character begins with the same four previously earned spendable points,
so first-session expenditure does not borrow its end-of-session award. This is
not a starting grant or a decision about creation-point carryover.

For the near-conversion example spending 2/session, the ending cash bank is 82:

Those additional benefits do not appear in the cash bank.

Saving all cash toward a hypothetical eligible 50-point purchase first funds it

common opening bank and describes first affordability, not completed purchases

The script also tests an optional 12.5 milestone at the finale. It adds 12.5
spendable points independently; it neither replaces the Assets award nor treats
the award as cash a second time. The retained milestone recommendation is still
fractional: whole-point session cards do not by themselves eliminate every
half-point from the economy.

## Findings and Recommendation

Recommend Near-conversion as the next working award card: Participation 5 and
discretionary 0/3/5. It changes one amount, eliminates half-points in ordinary
session awards, retains the dependable floor and exceptional total, and gives
meaningful narrative contributions appreciable weight. The cost is somewhat
faster funding in sessions with meaningful awards and a smaller relative step
from meaningful to exceptional (3 to 5 rather than 2.5 to 5).

Keep story-delivered development and optional earmarked credit as additions
shown alongside the session award. Their value is already received or committed,
not automatically another spendable award and not an automatic subtraction from
Participation or the other categories. A short award summary is sufficient; no
compulsory ledger of every friendship or minor event follows.

Example under the recommended candidate:

```text
Session award: 14 spendable points.
Story award: dependable caravan backing, provisionally valued at 1,
already received. No additional spendable point for the same benefit.
```

Story awards can substantially increase actual development even while spending
for the present reduces saved cash. That supports evaluating both sides without
automatically reimbursing immediate spending through larger awards. However,
these calculations do not establish whether immediate spending is attractive,
necessary, or overpowered: its permitted benefits and prices still require
comparison. The one-point stun-equipment example belongs in that next valuation,
alongside social context and the candidate numerical Resonance benefit, with
lasting provision purchases distinguished from those uses.

Matching Framework recovery remains available for consideration under the
existing recovery grammar, but no point-to-Condition exchange was assumed here.
It is restoration, not automatically an additional Assets Rank or greater Capacity.
