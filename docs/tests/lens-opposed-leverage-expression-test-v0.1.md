## Purpose

This test follows the Leverage Derived Attribute boundary test.

It evaluates whether institutional, reputational, relational, and resource
conflict can use the current LENS resolution language without creating a
separate social-combat subsystem.

The test uses:

```text
active opposition;
separate percentile rolls;
Successful Margin;
Net Margin;
Effect Levels;
Conditions;
Load;
Function Bands;
Access;
and matching recovery.
```

The exact final opposed-roll procedure remains subject to later confirmation.
This record uses the current working definitions preserved in the terminology
reference.

---

## Working Resolution Controls

```text
Successful Margin
= Final Rating minus a successful roll

Net Margin
= higher Successful Margin minus lower Successful Margin

Winning opposed Expression
= 1 baseline Effect Level
+ 1 additional Effect Level for each complete 10 points of Net Margin

Successful unopposed Expression
= 1 baseline Effect Level
+ 1 additional Effect Level for each complete 10 points of Successful Margin
```

An Effect Level does not automatically become a Condition Level.

The declared effect determines whether generated levels:

```text
secure an outcome;
increase amount;
increase duration;
increase scope;
create or alter Access;
impose a Condition;
remove a matching Condition;
or perform another coherent function.
```

Each generated Effect Level follows one traceable path.

## Test 1: Authority Versus Authority

Declarations:

```text
Noble:
Under the emergency charter, provide the district levy.

Local Ruler:
My local defense authority permits me to refuse an external requisition while
my own territory remains threatened.
```

Both Expressions are fictionally applicable. The conflict is not personal
persuasion. It is competing established authority.

### Outcome A: Narrow Noble Victory

```text
Noble Final Rating: 25
Noble roll:          8
Noble Margin:       17

Ruler Final Rating: 28
Ruler roll:         20
Ruler Margin:        8

Net Margin:          9

Generated Effect Levels:
1 baseline
+ 0 for complete 10-point Net Margins
= 1 Effect Level
```

Possible declared result:

```text
The ruler provides the minimum levy required by the charter.
```

The Effect Level secures the requested outcome. It does not automatically
inflict a Condition on the ruler.

### Outcome B: Strong Noble Victory

```text
Noble Final Rating: 25
Noble roll:          4
Noble Margin:       21

Ruler Final Rating: 28
Ruler roll:         25
Ruler Margin:        3

Net Margin:         18

Generated Effect Levels:
1 baseline
+ 1 for one complete 10-point Net Margin
= 2 Effect Levels
```

Possible allocations:

```text
Option 1:
More soldiers are committed.

Option 2:
The minimum soldiers are committed for a longer duration.

Option 3:
Soldiers and supplies are both committed.

Option 4:
The aid is secured with less political cost or fewer conditions.
```

The two levels do not permit four simultaneous benefits. The player assigns
the generated levels according to the declared effect and any adopted effect
distribution procedure.

### Outcome C: One Success and One Failure

```text
Noble Final Rating: 25
Noble roll:         23
Noble Margin:        2

Ruler Final Rating: 28
Ruler roll:         29
Ruler result:       Failure

Working Net Margin control:
2 minus 0 = 2

Generated Effect Levels:
1 baseline
= 1 Effect Level
```

The Noble wins because the Noble succeeds and the Ruler does not. The small
margin produces only the baseline effect.

### Outcome D: Both Fail

```text
Noble roll:  31
Ruler roll:  44

Both exceed their Final Ratings.
```

No successful Expression wins. The demand remains unresolved, is delayed, or
creates a consequence according to the scene. Failure does not automatically
award Effect Levels to the actor who failed by less.

Finding:

```text
PASS

Competing Authority uses ordinary active opposition.

The generated Effect Levels can describe the strength, scope, duration, or
cost of institutional compliance without becoming damage.
```

## Test 2: Reputation Attack Versus Network Defense

Scenario:

```text
A rival attempts to discredit the Noble before the local court.

The Noble's allies use their established relationships to defend the Noble's
credibility and preserve cooperation.
```

Diagnostic Ratings:

```text
Rival Reputation attack: 22
Noble Network defense:    22
```

Rolls:

```text
Rival roll:          2
Rival Margin:       20

Noble roll:         22
Noble Margin:        0

Net Margin:         20

Generated Effect Levels:
1 baseline
+ 2 for two complete 10-point Net Margins
= 3 Effect Levels
```

Declared effect:

```text
Publicly Discredited 3

Capacity: Leverage
Primary scope: Reputation
```

Resulting state:

```text
Leverage Load: 3
Leverage Function Band: 75%
```

The specific Condition may make a Reputation route Condition or Closed where
public credibility is required.

It does not automatically close Authority, Assets, Network, or Heritage.

The accumulated Leverage Load nevertheless reduces Leverage-carried
Expressions through the shared 75% Function Band.

Finding:

```text
PASS

A Reputation attack can impose named Leverage Conditions through the ordinary
Effect procedure.

Network can actively defend because the relationship itself is contesting the
attack.
```

## Test 4: Access Change Is Not Automatic

Compare two different attacks.

### Attack A: Publicly Discredited

```text
Targeted truth:
Public belief about the Noble.

Primary Derived Attribute affected:
Reputation.

Possible Access result:
Reputation becomes Condition in contexts requiring credibility.

Authority result:
Still Open unless the fiction says the office depends directly upon public
standing.
```

### Attack B: Legitimacy Contested

```text
Targeted truth:
The Noble's legal or inherited claim to issue commands.

Primary Derived Attributes affected:
Authority and possibly Heritage according to the declared attack.

Possible Access result:
House Valerin / Authority becomes Condition until the Noble proves the charter,
restores recognition, or resolves the dispute.
```

Both attacks may produce Leverage Load. They do not create the same specific
Access changes.

Finding:

```text
PASS

Condition form and target determine specific Access consequences.

Load alone does not partially close Access, and every Leverage Condition does
not disable every Leverage Derived Attribute.
```

## Test 5: No Double Counting

At Leverage Load 3, the Noble's Leverage Function Band is already 75%.

Incorrect calculation:

```text
Authority Expression
x 0.75 Function Band
/ Publicly Discredited 3 again as numerical Dissonance
```

Correct control:

```text
Authority Expression
x 0.75 Function Band

Publicly Discredited identifies:
the source of Load;
its narrative scope;
specific Access consequences;
matching recovery;
and possible vulnerabilities.
```

Independent opposition or a separate relevant Dissonance may still apply. The
same three Condition Levels do not reduce the Expression twice.

Finding:

```text
PASS

The existing double-counting control survives Leverage conflict.
```

## Test 6: Recovery

The Noble attempts to remove `Publicly Discredited 3` by presenting verified
records and testimony from trusted allies.

Possible carrying route:

```text
Leverage / Network / Investigation
```

The exact Skill depends on the declared method. Network supplies the witnesses
and relationships. Investigation may organize or verify the evidence.

Diagnostic successful recovery:

```text
Final Rating:       30
Roll:                9
Successful Margin: 21

Generated matching recovery Effect Levels:
1 baseline
+ 2 for two complete 10-point Successful Margins
= 3 recovery Effect Levels
```

All three levels remove the matching `Publicly Discredited 3` Condition.

Result:

```text
Leverage Load returns from 3 to 0.
Leverage Function Band returns from 75% to 100%.
Any Reputation Access restriction created only by that Condition is restored.
```

If the recovery only repaired reputation but did not answer a separate
`Authority Revoked` Condition, Authority Access would remain restricted.

Finding:

```text
PASS

Leverage recovery uses the same matching-Lens and Effect-Level procedure as
Physical and Mental recovery.
```

## Test 7: Outcome Effect Versus Condition Effect

Two successful Authority Expressions may generate the same number of Effect
Levels while doing different things.

```text
Expression A:
Secure troops.

Effect Levels become amount, duration, scope, or reliability of aid.


Expression B:
Strip the rival's command legitimacy.

Effect Levels become matching Leverage Condition Levels and may alter Access.
```

The action declaration determines the effect form and target before the roll.

Finding:

```text
STRONG PASS

An Expression can succeed and matter without causing a Condition.

Leverage conflict does not turn every negotiation, command, favor, purchase,
or reputation action into damage.
```

## Test 8: HOLT Partial Counterplay

House Valerin is not one indivisible bonus.

The rival may target:

```text
House accounts
= Assets Axis Access

House allies
= Network Axis Access

House charter
= Authority Axis Access

House name
= Reputation Axis Access

House lineage or compact
= Heritage Axis Access
```

Successfully attacking one relationship does not automatically close the
other four.

Total Leverage Load still represents cascading pressure on the Character's
ability to mobilize worldly position as a whole.

Finding:

```text
PASS

The five Derived Attributes create partial counterplay without five separate
Capacity tracks.
```

## Failure Search

```text
FAILURE CANDIDATE                         RESULT

Social conflict needs social hit points  Survives. Leverage uses Capacity,
                                          Load, Conditions, and Access.

Every success causes a Condition          Survives. Effect form follows the
                                          declared objective.

Authority is resolved by static Rank      Survives. Both actors roll and compare
                                          Successful Margins.

Reputation damage erases Authority        Survives. Specific Access follows the
                                          Condition's form and target.

Load partially closes every route         Survives. Load changes Function Band;
                                          Conditions change specific Access.

Worldly pressure weakens all competence   Survives. Only Leverage-carried
                                          Expressions use this Function Band.

One Condition is counted twice            Survives. Function Band is the numerical
                                          consequence of accumulated Load.

HOLT failure erases the whole Character   Survives. Individual Axis relationships
                                          remain separately targetable.
```

## Overall Finding

```text
STRONG PASS

Opposed Leverage Expressions use the same resolution language as every other
LENS conflict.

No social-combat subsystem is required.

Separate rolls and Net Margin determine the winning Expression and generated
Effect Levels.

Effect Levels may secure outcomes, change scope or duration, alter Access, or
impose Conditions according to the declaration.

Conditions create Leverage Load.

Leverage Load changes the Function Band for Leverage-carried Expressions.

Specific Conditions determine specific Access consequences.

Matching recovery removes matching Conditions.

The architecture survived institutional conflict, reputation attack, partial
HOLT counterplay, Function Band pressure, and recovery.
```

---

## Design Finding Produced

```text
Leverage is not merely a fifth character-creation category.

It supplies a complete worldly conflict locus using the existing LENS engine.

The world can pressure, damage, deny, restore, and transform a Character's
material, relational, institutional, reputational, and historical position
without converting those events into physical or mental harm.
```
