Date: 2026-09-05

Status: Approved diagnostic rebuild and same-probe retest. No new budget,
Rank curve, Difficulty anchor, mandatory depth, or final character tier adopted.

Interpretation correction after user review: the arithmetic below remains

## Objective And Fixed Controls

Rebuild the three concepts with deliberate allocations, then rerun the exact
nine probes in `lens-100-training-point-nine-scene-performance-test-v0.1.md`.
Original builds remain unchanged; these are separately identified variants.

All three spend exactly 25 Attribute Points, 50 Derived Attribute Points,
and 100 shared Training Points. Every individual rank is at most 10; every
Derived Attribute is capped by its parent. No Attribute gives free Derived
or Training ranks. Unranked Training contributes x1 rather than denying Access.

The existing curve is x(1 + Rank/10); Attributes enter directly. Precision is
optional and can be bought directly under Field without a Specialty under the
user's working decision. These examples do not require every layer: A buys
neither Specialty nor Precision. B and C retain their named deeper expertise.
One point per Precision Rank remains the inherited test cost.

Keep all previous routes, scene scopes, equipment assumptions, and D1 x0.98
unchanged. D2 x0.588 and D3 x0.3528 remain sensitivity checks, not reassigned
scene difficulties. Equipment remains x1.10 for A, x1.20 for firearm/clinical
probes, and x1.00 for B3. The particular item placements and scene Difficulty
anchors remain provisional. M1 Scale, full current functioning, and no extra
Resonance, Dissonance, HOLT, mitigation, or multi-action factors are unchanged.

Routine automatic activities remain automatic. These are conditional results
for the same uncertain rolled probes, not probabilities of living ordinary
life successfully, real clinical outcomes, or opposed combat results.
Original labels described intended concepts, not measured population tiers.

## Design Intent

- Experienced local shopkeeper: An experienced local shopkeeper with capable accounts/planning, social communication, and ordinary hobbies. Preserve the no-Specialty/no-Precision control. This is not an untrained novice: high learned ranks can reflect long practice without formal specialist schooling.
- Expedition security guide: An expedition security guide with reliable firearm use, useful travel/survival competence, observation, and a functioning handgun fallback. Keep some medical, logistics, command, and vehicle expertise rather than maximizing a single attack.
- Medical specialist: A technically focused clinician with surgical depth, an especially strong neurological route, developed reasoning/learning, and retained communication/teaching. Do not give medical expertise any shooting benefit.

These intents precede the numerical verdict. No success target was established
by the user, so the test reports achieved reliability rather than declaring an
arbitrary required percentage. No outcome-driven free equipment or Resonance
has been added to make a build pass.

## What Changed And What Did Not

- A improves both practiced business probes from 13% to 37% and the unfamiliar
  observation probe from 9% to 12%. Broader learned practice improves without
  purchasing either narrower layer. The intended comparative pattern holds,
  but 37% is still modest reliability for a rolled D1 benchmark.
- B improves the signature probe from 37% to the test's 98% ordinary-roll
  ceiling; other handguns improve from 23% to 68%. Learning/Research remains
  weak: the unrounded unfamiliar probe falls from 5.88 to 5.39, both displaying
  5%. This is not an all-purpose upgrade.
- C improves the neurological route from 58% to 98% and broader surgery from
  29% to 73%. Untrained shooting rises only from 7% to 11% through Mobility and
  Coordination, not through borrowed medical expertise.
- Relative to the original equal allocations, some untouched probe subjects
  conceal meaningful losses elsewhere; the complete spending lists expose
  those costs. The nine probes do not certify every aspect of the concepts.
- All three still fit exactly 25/50/100. No cost or budget change was needed
  to obtain the guide's or clinician's displayed signature/fallback results.

## Does A Budget Shortfall Remain?

No general shortage of 100 Training Points is demonstrated for these three
portfolios. In particular, B and C can support substantial signature depth
and a strong related fallback within the current budget. Whether their less
developed competencies are acceptable is a Campaign Profile/concept judgment.

A was initially treated as an unresolved calibration case. Subsequent review
found that its ordinary-work probes had not justified rolling; the ceiling
below is arithmetic only, not evidence of a competence defect. Hold its
Psyche 6 / Cognition 6 and all scene/tool controls
fixed, then maximize its applicable Task and Field at Rank 10:

    6 x 1.6 x 2 x 2 x 1.10 x 0.98 = 41.3952

Only four additional ranks would maximize both tested shop routes (Inference,
Planning, Bookkeeping, Retail commerce from 9 to 10), but the result would
still be 41%, not a large improvement. Even unlimited additional Training
Points cannot exceed that two-layer ceiling at the fixed capability and
current Rank horizon. Different Attributes, narrower expertise, justified
circumstances, or rules calibration would be different choices, not a simple
demonstration that the shared Training pool is too small.

This does not prove Specialty or Precision must be purchased. Whether the
activity actually requires resolution must be established before using the
rolled ceiling as evidence for a design change. Automatic classification is
not a device for improving a bad test result: it is an existing rule that the
test must apply before selecting a numerical probe.

## Routine Versus Contested Reassessment

The user reaffirmed that routine work is generally Automatic when its outcome
is not meaningfully in doubt. A specialized adventurer attempting to obtain an
advantage from the shopkeeper is a different situation. The merchant need not
match that specialist merely to qualify as competent at ordinary trade.
Further experience can develop relevant countermeasures through existing
character construction; no new merchant subsystem or mandatory layer follows.

| Original probe | Reassessment | Consequence for the prior result |
|---|---|---|
| A1: explain a shop-account discrepancy before submission | A familiar correction using accessible records can be routine. No concealed fraud, inaccessible evidence, unusually complex discrepancy, or consequential time constraint beyond ordinary work was actually established. Importance or a deadline alone is insufficient. | Default to ordinary competent handling on the supplied facts; do not use 37% as the probability of successfully maintaining the accounts. A genuinely uncertain investigation would need its own stated facts. |
| A2: revise a delivery plan after a supplier change | Ordinary stock replanning is part of the declared developed practice. The probe supplied no exceptional scarcity, incompatible demands, or active resistance. | Routine adjustment can be Automatic. A meaningful contested or uncertain objective must be established before assigning Difficulty. |
| A3: notice a relevant fault sign in unfamiliar equipment | Unfamiliarity may matter, but an obvious sign can still be noticed. Subtle or ambiguous evidence could justify a roll. The original wording did not establish which. | Keep the numerical result as conditional only; neither a definite Automatic outcome nor a validated D1 anchor follows from the label. |

An adversarial discount negotiation is not a replacement D1 shop-work probe.
When the desired concession is genuinely contested, compare the applicable
Expressions under existing active-opposition rules. Relevant expertise,
Specialty/Precision, actual Leverage, and established countermeasures matter.
Do not add a generic Difficulty for the same resistance already supplied by
the other participant. Adventurer and shopkeeper are concepts, not automatic
bonuses or predetermined winners. Ordinary refusal or an uncontested agreement
need not become a roll solely because a demand was spoken.

No bargaining contest has been calculated in this reassessment, and no new
values or merchant countermeasure bonuses have been invented.

## Recommendation And Next Decision

Keep 25/50/100 as the working test budget. It supports the tested developed
profiles without a demonstrated general shortfall. Do not final-lock it from
nine probes or assume these test builds define citizen/veteran categories.

Withdraw the prior proposal to require majority success for every strong
Task/Field-only D1 route: it was not approved, and the shop examples did not
establish the premise used to recommend it. Preserve 25/50/100 as the working
budget; no demonstrated shortfall follows from these routine-work cases.

The next proposed performance case is one explicit opposed bargain between
the current merchant and a negotiation specialist, with complete relevant
stacks and stated stakes. That would test actual asymmetric competition,
not ordinary livelihood. It remains a proposal, not a completed test or
permission to invent new experience tiers or purchasing requirements.
