# LENS Authority Whole-Provision Test

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: Conceptual construction test; numerical examples are new candidates.

## Objective

The user agreed to applying whole-provision construction to Authority after
the Network comparisons. Test one broader office against several narrower
permissions and check upper-end scope. Do not create a new authority subsystem.

Sources: the current terminology definition of Authority, the Task map's
Command and enforcement examples, and the approved Network construction lane.

Authority includes office, title, credentials, license, jurisdiction,
clearance, delegated power, and recognized rights to direct and decide. It is
not confined to command over people. Ordinary autonomy and ordinary rights
already supplied by the Profile are not purchased again.

Proposed allocation uses purchased Authority Rank without spending that Rank
down. Each selected provision is valued as a whole. Reach, discretion,
recognition, responsibility, and restrictions are descriptive considerations,
not separate numerical dimensions. Costs below are test proposals; neither
their equality nor the acquisition Difficulty mapping is numerically validated.

## Two-Point Equal-Expenditure Comparison

### Two Narrow Provisions: Candidate Costs 1 + 1

- Gate warden: recognized discretion to admit or refuse entry at one named
  gate under the settlement's rules. This is more than simply carrying a key.
- Archive clearance: recognized permission to inspect a specified restricted
  collection. It provides no authority to alter, remove, or publish everything
  in it and no automatic expertise in reading the material.

The fictional Profile permits this combination, such as an off-duty watch
member who also has a scholarly credential. Usable schedules and compatibility
must be established; contradictory offices are not treated as simultaneously
available merely because their costs add correctly.

### One Broader Office: Candidate Cost 2

Watch officer: authority to direct an assigned patrol, allocate its ordinary
duties and issued equipment, and make specified enforcement decisions within
the named watch jurisdiction. The office does not include the independent
archive's special clearance. This is one concrete office package, not a price
assigned to every person with an officer title.

| Situation | Two narrow provisions | Watch office |
|---|---|---|
| Decide admission at the named gate | Gate duty supplies recognized discretion | Only if this gate is within the office's established watch remit |
| Inspect the restricted archive | Clearance supplies permission | No automatic permission from watch rank |
| Redirect the patrol during a routine incident | Neither narrow provision grants command over the patrol | Within the specified office powers |
| Read an obscure document after admission | Clearance allows access; actual interpretation uses applicable capability and Training | Would first need a valid route to the document, then applicable expertise |
| Archive suspends the clearance | Gate appointment remains if independently unaffected | Watch authority is not affected merely by that archive decision |
| An opponent refuses a legitimate instruction | Authority establishes the recognized basis, not automatic compliance | The same; use the actual Expression and opposition when needed |

Finding: breadth and depth have distinct uses. The broader office may include
some narrower permissions; it must not be made artificially incapable of them.
If it includes every narrow provision being compared plus substantial extras,
the supposed equal-cost comparison needs revision rather than invented barriers.

## Upper-End Scope Checks

### Candidate Cost 5: Substantial Municipal Office

A magistrate has recognized authority to issue binding judgments in a defined
class of cases, authorize specified official actions, and call on the relevant
municipal apparatus to implement those decisions. The office carries procedure,
review, and a defined jurisdiction. It does not automatically grant control of
all municipal decisions, external jurisdictions, or unrelated institutions.

Probe: settling an ordinary case within the remit has an established procedural
route. Knowing the relevant facts or law, managing a disputed case, and securing
compliance can still involve applicable Expressions. The office is neither
guaranteed expertise nor a title that must be socially re-earned at every use.

### Candidate Cost 10: Broad Sovereign or Delegated Governing Authority

Within the fictional realm, a ruler or fully empowered regent can make broad
governing decisions and direct the institutions subject to that recognized
mandate. State the actual powers and constraints. A figurehead does not receive
this package merely by possessing a royal title.

Probes: direct an official mobilization or authorize a major public undertaking.
Where these powers exist, the office gives a real route to institutional action.
Opposition, unavailable supplies, legal or customary constraints within the
fiction, and contested recognition remain relevant. The office does not itself
produce resources, obedience, or jurisdiction over another sovereign polity.

The Character need not separately own every institutional asset to direct its
authorized use. Conversely, the permission to allocate state resources is not
unrestricted private ownership. Equipment issued or facilities made available
through an office retain their source, authorized use, and possible recall.
No automatic second charge or second multiplier is imposed for the same grant.
Detailed starting resource-package translation is not settled by this test.

Upper finding: greater Authority can enable actions that smaller offices cannot
authorize. Several small offices do not add arithmetically into sovereignty.
They may cooperate or aggregate support through real relationships and shared
procedures, not a newly invented prohibition on combining them.

## Cross-Layer and Limitation Checks

- Knowing the magistrate through Network is not holding the magistracy.
- Holding office does not automatically buy personal loyalty, a favorable
  Reputation, or a private estate. The fiction may establish overlap; count
  each actual contribution only once rather than prohibiting overlap.
- The Authority Rank improves a relevant Authority-carried Expression through
  the existing multiplier. A provision's cost is not an additional multiplier.
- Routine legitimate acts can be automatic. A resisted order or difficult
  coordination can use the actual applicable Task; not every official act
  is automatically Command and no second enforcement Task is created.
- Appointment-specific restrictions are included in the whole value. Do not
  discount an office again for the same limits already used to define it.
- A nominal title with substantially weaker actual powers can have lower
  provision value without changing the universal Attribute or Training rules.
- Jurisdictional reach does not automatically multiply personal Scale or
  every action. An institutional function may operate at its own Scale when
  actual resources and routing supply that function.

## Result and Next Decision

Conceptual pass for whole-provision Authority construction and for combining
several narrower permissions instead of one broader office. Upper examples
also fit without reducing Authority to guaranteed obedience or private Assets.

Proposed reference costs are 1 for either defined narrow credential, 2 for the
specified watch office, 5 for the municipal office, and 10 for broad governing
authority. These values need user calibration judgment; the described scopes
matter more than their illustrative titles. No full price ladder or final
Difficulty equivalence is adopted.

Next: decide whether to retain these as provisional Authority anchors. If so,
continue to Reputation using recognition, audience, and expectations as the
provision being valued. Background provisions remain deferred.

## Subsequent User Decision

The user approved retaining the tested 1 / 2 / 5 / 10 Authority provisions
as provisional construction references and instructed continuation to Reputation
("Yes, let's move on"). Their described powers and restrictions remain part
of the references. This does not final-lock a universal title/price ladder or
numerically validate acquisition Difficulty equivalence.
