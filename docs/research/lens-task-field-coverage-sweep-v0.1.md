# LENS Task and Field Coverage Sweep v0.1

Status: Bounded comparison and accepted provisional Field-selection headings. Not adopted
Field names, scope, pricing, or a final Task catalogue.

User accepted the eight guide headings with expanded examples for everyday
livelihoods, recreation, and ordinary religion and culture. These are additions
within the eight headings, not three additional groups. Individual examples
are not automatically adopted as equal-cost Fields.

## Objective And Endpoint

Compare the current 27-entry Task map with the existing illustrative Skill
Index and a bounded peer sample before returning to individual cases. Provide
grouped examples that help players select or propose Fields without enumerating
every subject. Resolve content before revisiting the word Field.

This pass ends with a coverage crosswalk, proposed browsing groupings, and a
finite set of issues. It does not open one approval request per legacy Skill.
Further cases should address those issues, not expand the catalogue merely
because another word can be found.

## Reading The Crosswalk

Task names identify available activity coverage, not an exclusive route, a
required roll sequence, or a demand that every example have ranked Task support.
Field candidate means possible portable subject expertise, not an adopted
purchase. A broad undertaking can draw on several Tasks while a coherent body
of expertise remains relevant across them. Neither label settles Field breadth.

No knowledge entry grants equipment, credentials, contacts, or extraordinary
permission. Conditions, preparation Resonance, and established position remain
in their existing layers. All current Task statuses remain as recorded.

## Accepted Provisional Field Browsing Groups

These are non-mechanical headings for a book, appendix, or selection guide.
They have no Ranks, costs, prerequisites, caps, slots, or required purchases.
They are not a new layer above Field. A candidate may be cross-referenced under
several headings. They are not Attribute families or exclusive routing rules.

| Browsing group | Illustrative Field candidates, not adopted equal-cost selections |
|---|---|
| Body and Health | Medicine, Acrobatics, bodily training practices, animal care, recreational sports. |
| Travel and Environments | Survival, Seamanship, aviation knowledge, local-area knowledge, agriculture. |
| Weapons and Conflict | Firearms, Archery, martial traditions, Tactics, Demolitions. |
| Trades and Technology | Carpentry, Cooking, Tailoring, Smithing, Mechanics, Electronics, Computing, animal husbandry. |
| Arts and Expression | Music, Painting, dramatic traditions, written media, Appearance, recreational needlework and other expressive hobbies. |
| People and Institutions | Emotion, Protocol, Law, Commerce, Politics, languages, cultural customs, religious practices, card and board games. |
| Sciences and Histories | Biology, Geology, History, Archaeology, Theology, cultural history, defined investigative disciplines. |
| Extraordinary Traditions | A named magical school, Alchemy, a psychic discipline, a defined ritual tradition. |

Eight headings are accepted provisionally for readability, not because LENS needs eight
categories. Agriculture and games/recreation should not disappear simply
because early tests emphasized adventurers. Games can be cross-referenced
under cultural, strategic, physical, or expressive practices as appropriate.
These are coverage reminders, not additional compulsory Fields.

### Whole-Set Grouping Review

Finding: the eight headings provide workable browsing coverage for
the surveyed material. No additional mechanical category is indicated. This
is an editorial assessment, not a finding of equal Field purchase value.

Accepted example expansion covers three areas within those headings:

- Everyday livelihoods: agriculture, animal husbandry, food preparation, and
  ordinary trades should remain visible alongside adventurous occupations.
- Recreation: games, sports, and hobbies should be findable through the
  relevant physical, cultural, strategic, or expressive headings. They are
  not automatically combat expertise or audience performance.
- Ordinary religion and culture: theology, customs, and learned cultural
  knowledge can appear under People and Institutions or Sciences and Histories.
  Do not imply that religious knowledge requires extraordinary permission.

Cross-reference rather than force exclusive placement: Medicine can appear
under Body and Health and Sciences and Histories; Seamanship under Travel and
Environments and relevant Trades and Technology examples. Repeated placement
does not duplicate a purchase. Learned cultural knowledge remains distinct
from Background's established personal history and belonging.

Decision: retain these eight as provisional guide headings, with the
coverage additions above. This is one accepted editorial set, not eight
independent mechanical decisions or approval of every example as a Field.
The next substantive comparison concerns Field breadth using a contrasting
sample, not how many examples each heading is permitted to contain.

### How A Player Could Use This Guide

1. Start with a developed subject, tool family, practice, or tradition that
   belongs in the Character's story.
2. Find nearby examples in the relevant heading; propose an addition when
   the examples do not cover the concept.
3. State what the Field covers and a meaningful adjacent thing it does not.
4. Show its use with more than one appropriate Task where natural. This is
   explanatory evidence, not an arbitrary minimum number of routes.
5. Identify a narrower Specialty when desired; it remains within its parent.
6. Compare usable scope with the campaign's other same-cost Fields.

Example structure, still illustrative:

    Browsing heading: Travel and Environments (not purchased)
    Field candidate: Survival
    Specialty candidate: Arctic
    Uses: Withstand exposure, infer environmental signs, create shelter
    Boundary: not every historical or cultural fact about Arctic regions

The guide should show a few complete examples per heading rather than every
science, weapon, craft, language, and physical practice. Campaign Profiles
select and contextualize examples; players can propose comparable additions.

## Findings And Bounded Remaining Work

The crosswalk does not establish a new required Task. Most legacy action labels
have existing coverage; many remaining labels identify subject expertise,
broader undertakings, or character truths. This is conceptual coverage, not
proof of equal purchase value or of every possible fictional route.

The ten-candidate starter Field list undersampled practical trades, animal
work, institutions, local knowledge, scholarly subjects, and extraordinary
traditions. That is a presentation/Field-coverage gap, not an automatic Task gap.

Three grouped decisions should replace serial approval of every example:

1. Guide structure: completed provisionally. User accepted eight browsing
   headings with the three example expansions; headings are not purchased or rated.
2. Field breadth: initial five-candidate comparison completed below; its
   provisional selection standard accepted after that comparison, not adopted pricing.
   This set tests breadth; it is not the whole catalogue. Languages,
   broad Science, and general Combat need explicit scope treatment, not hidden
   subcategory multipliers or free universal expertise.
3. Guide usability: three conceptual Character walkthroughs completed below.
   These are designer checks, not independent reader trials or costed builds.
   The subsequent user approval confirms Field terminology and closes this pass.

Two route examples remain flagged, not new Task proposals: directing/riding a
living mount, and executing a solitary ritual whose practice is neither a
message nor an audience performance. Test them only when their Field examples
are developed or a Character needs them. Ordinary unranked/default routes and
Field contributions remain possible; the catalogue need not name every act.

Preparation Resonance, held actions, advancement through study, and numerical
purchase balance are separate work. They are not prerequisites for identifying
these Field-selection headings. No additional peer survey is needed to finish
this pass; another source is warranted only for a named remaining gap.

## Five-Field Breadth Test

Status: Conceptual test completed at user direction. Candidate scopes and
recommended selection guidance are not adopted doctrine. No numerical combat,
probability, construction-budget, or price changes are inferred.

### Controls

Use one ordinary modern adventure campaign involving travel, a settlement,
environmental hazards, conflict, and community life. Compare Medicine,
Firearms, Survival, Music, and Geology at equal Rank and purchase cost, holding
other applicable ratings and circumstances equal when comparing alternatives.
Equal cost is a test assumption, not a result. No Specialty is required or
added for this comparison; narrower examples below only demonstrate nesting.

Ask what learned contribution each Field supplies, whether it supports more
than one natural Task, whether overlaps are legitimate, and whether one Field
replaces another across the other's scope. A nonapplicable Field means no
contribution from that Field, not that the Character is forbidden to attempt
the action. Equipment, Access, and extraordinary permission remain separate.

### Working Scopes And Portability

| Field | Test scope | Natural Task examples | Possible narrower Specialty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medicine | Knowledge and practice concerning health, injury, diagnosis, and treatment. | Inference about symptoms; Treat an injury; Research a medical question. | Emergency medicine. |
| Firearms | Knowledge and practice concerning guns, their use, characteristics, and care. | Discharge a gun; Observation of firearm wear; Repair a familiar mechanism. | Handguns. |
| Survival | Knowledge and practice for sustaining life without ordinary support. | Planning supplies; Create shelter; Withstand exposure through learned protective practices. | Arctic survival. |
| Music | Knowledge and practice concerning musical structures and expression. | Create a composition; Perform music; Inference about a musical pattern. | A particular instrument or tradition. |
| Geology | Knowledge and practice concerning earth materials and processes. | Observation of strata; Inference about ground hazards; Research a mineral deposit. | Volcanology. |

These examples do not assign an Attribute family to a Field. Medicine can also
support Teaching or Convey; Music can support Research or Teaching. Additional
Task routes are neither additional purchases nor proof of greater power.
Familiarity across instruments, unfamiliar technology, or radically different
biology remains subject to the agreed Field scope and fictional applicability;
this test does not establish universal expertise or compulsory new purchases.

### Shared-Scene Cases

| Case | Applicable contribution and comparison | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Treat an injured expedition member | Medicine supplies clinical knowledge through Treat. Firearms may identify the weapon, but weapon knowledge alone does not supply clinical treatment. | A related cause is not automatically treatment expertise. |
| Direct gunfire at a threat | Firearms contributes through Discharge. Medicine does not replace gun handling merely because anatomy is relevant to injury. | Anatomy could matter in a separately justified Expression; relevance is not automatic substitution. |
| Sustain the party through an exposed overnight stop | Survival informs supplies, shelter, and protective practices. These may involve several Tasks; the Field does not make the entire undertaking one action. | Broad subject applicability does not erase action accounting. |
| Compose music for a local memorial | Music contributes through Create. Medicine's connection to death does not supply musical expertise. | Narrative association alone does not establish the practiced contribution. |
| Interpret mineral-bearing strata | Geology contributes through Observation or Inference according to the declared action. Survival's outdoor relevance does not establish knowledge of ore formation. | Environment is not ownership of every subject encountered there. |
| Care for someone suffering from cold exposure | Medicine supports treatment; Survival can also support familiar exposure care when that knowledge is within its scope. The same warming measure does not earn duplicate contributions merely under two labels. | Genuine overlap is allowed; Survival does not thereby become all Medicine. |
| Choose a safer campsite | Survival can assess practical shelter/exposure needs; Geology can assess unstable ground. Either may answer a particular danger. | Distinct contributions may combine under existing Compound rules; two names for the same assessment do not double it. |
| Repair a damaged gun | Firearms can supply relevant maintenance knowledge through Repair. Designing a new firing mechanism is not automatically the same expertise. | Care/repair has legitimate reach without granting all engineering. |
| Use a firearm to obtain food | Survival can inform the hunt; Firearms informs shooting. Tracking, planning, and firing are not collapsed into one interchangeable contribution. | A common objective does not make Fields synonymous. |
| Raise local support through music | Music supports the actual musical activity. It does not replace Persuade training, guarantee cooperation, or grant Authority. | A Field can travel into social scenes without purchasing every social capability. |

### Replacement And Campaign-Sensitivity Findings

No complete replacement was found among the five test scopes. Each has clear
applications the other four do not supply, and each supports multiple natural
Tasks. This supports keeping all five as usable guide examples. It does not
prove equivalent payoff or decide every Field/Specialty boundary.

Survival needs the clearest wording: sustaining life is its subject, not a
license to claim every activity that might help a Character survive. That
reading would swallow medicine, weapons, negotiation, transport, and more.
The same check applies to other candidates: Music is not every audience-facing
activity, Medicine is not all biology, and Firearms is not all conflict.

Campaign emphasis changes opportunity without changing the Field definition:
a wilderness-heavy story may favor Survival, an armed-conflict story Firearms,
a community-performance story Music, and a geological expedition Geology.
No encounter frequencies were measured. Consequently this test cannot certify
equal numerical value, require identical spotlight, or justify variable prices.
The guide should make likely campaign relevance visible during selection.

### Accepted Provisional Selection Standard

User accepted the following provisional selection standard: a Field names a coherent body of
developed knowledge or practice that can support relevant Tasks. Describe its
positive scope, demonstrate natural uses, and identify a meaningful neighboring
area it does not automatically replace. Shared applications are acceptable;
one candidate routinely encompassing another at equal cost merits revision.
These are review questions, not a fixed minimum Task count or an exhaustive
list of permitted actions.

This acceptance does not adopt every candidate scope or certify equal purchase
value. The following walkthrough applies the standard without expanding into
serial approval of all possible subjects or reopening the Task catalogue.

## Character Selection Walkthrough

Status: Designer-led conceptual test, not observed reader behavior. No actual
players were recruited, no full Character budgets were allocated, and no
numerical outcomes were simulated. Test whether a Character concept can find
or propose relevant expertise using the eight headings and current Tasks.

### Remote-Settlement Medic

- Browse Body and Health for Medicine; Travel and Environments for Survival.
- Medicine supports Treat an injury, Inference about symptoms, and Teaching
  first aid. Survival supports Planning an unsupported journey and Create shelter.
- Emergency medicine is a possible Medicine Specialty, not a mandatory purchase.
- The concept does not automatically supply licenses, patients, supplies, or
  local Authority. Those truths remain elsewhere in ordinary construction.
- Finding: both Fields serve the concept without a mandatory Medic Field,
  profession package, fixed Attribute pairing, or new Task. Shared exposure-care
  knowledge remains legitimate overlap, not a duplicate multiplier.

### Touring Musician

- Browse Arts and Expression for Music. A chosen instrument can be a Specialty.
- Music supports Create a song, Perform for an audience, and Teaching music.
- Seeking a booking can use Persuade; music-industry knowledge may contribute
  when the actual case depends on it, but Music does not grant all negotiation.
- Knowing music does not automatically provide fame, venue contacts, income,
  or instruments. These remain distinct character truths and resources.
- Finding: one Field can support several Tasks and a complete area of the
  concept. No minimum number of Fields or representation of every heading is
  needed. This does not settle expertise across every instrument or tradition.

### Ceramic Artisan And Restorer

- Browse Trades and Technology and Arts and Expression. Neither list currently
  names Ceramics; propose it as one Field cross-referenced in both headings.
- Test scope: developed knowledge and practice concerning ceramic materials,
  forming, firing, finishes, and the resulting objects.
- Create a vessel, Repair a damaged ceramic object, Inference about a firing
  defect, and Teaching the craft all use existing Tasks.
- A particular ceramic tradition or porcelain restoration can be a Specialty.
- It does not automatically supply kiln engineering, every art medium, general
  geology, or the history of every culture that produced pottery.
- Finding: the proposal has a coherent subject, several natural applications,
  and meaningful boundaries. It qualifies for consideration under the accepted
  standard without requiring a ninth heading or a new Task. Its final scope
  and placement in a particular campaign remain candidate decisions.

### Results And Endpoint

All three concepts could select or propose their central expertise. The test
required no new Task, no mandatory profession purchase, no ranked heading, and
no catalogue expansion before the proposed Field could be considered.
The Field supplies expertise; the Task supplies practiced activity. Neither
replaces the rest of character construction or manufactures missing Access.

The small test also demonstrates why the headings should permit cross-references:
the ceramic practice can be utilitarian, artistic, or both without two purchases
for the same expertise. A Field's absence from the examples is not a rejection.

The bounded sequence is complete at the conceptual level: coverage sweep,
accepted guide structure, comparative breadth test, and Character walkthrough.
Independent reader usability and numerical purchase balance remain untested.

Terminology decision: user confirmed Field as the portable expertise layer.
It accommodates learned subjects, tools, practices, and traditions;
it does not imply only academic study or a profession, and it fits the optical
vocabulary. Task / Field / Specialty is confirmed terminology. The catalogue
remains open, the eight headings remain non-mechanical browsing aids, and the
accepted provisional selection guidance remains in force. This does not adopt
every illustrative Field, settle purchase balance, or change the mathematics.
