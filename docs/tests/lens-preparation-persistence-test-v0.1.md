# Preparation Persistence Test

Status: User-approved working persistence rule, adopted 2026-09-08.
Date: 2026-09-08.

## Objective and Controls

Test preparation against use, interruption, and changed circumstances without
imposing a universal duration. This is a qualitative rules comparison with an
arithmetic illustration, not an empirical playtest or an action-economy test.

The adopted default assesses the actual fictional benefit in ten-percent
increments. Genuine preparatory uncertainty uses normal resolution. Preparation
is Resonance, not a new Task or Lens category. Automatic Effect-to-Resonance
conversion remains unadopted. Access and distinct-contribution rules remain.

Authority: baseline draft, Preparation and contextual combination sections;
`lens-preparation-assessment-versus-effects-test-v0.1.md`.

## Tested Rule

Preparation persists while the facts supporting its benefit remain true.
Apply it only where it still helps the particular Expression. Reassess its
applicability and value when those facts change. Use alone neither consumes
nor preserves the benefit: what happened to its actual basis decides.

Remaining, changing, temporarily inapplicable, and ended are descriptions of
the cases below, not new tracked states or Condition categories.

## Case Comparison

| Case | Result under the candidate | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rehearsed testimony is used to answer several relevant questions. | Remains useful where the rehearsal applies. | Answering once does not erase practiced material. Repeating the same resolved claim does not create another roll. |
| The questioning moves to an unrelated incident. | Original preparation is currently inapplicable. | It still exists but does not support that answer. Returning to the rehearsed subject can restore applicability without rehearsing again. |
| A prepared speaker faces a different audience. | Reassess, not automatic loss or full transfer. | Knowledge of the subject may remain useful; tailoring to the first audience may not. |
| A Character misses a carefully lined-up shot. | The miss alone does not decide persistence. | Firing, recoil, movement, and the target's response may change the setup. Examine the resulting opportunity rather than treating failure as a consumption rule. |
| The shooter changes targets. | Target-specific alignment does not automatically carry. | A supported firing position may still help, but the old target-specific advantage cannot follow merely because the Field is unchanged. |
| Someone speaks while the Character waits in readiness. | No automatic loss. | Interruption is not itself proof that relevant readiness changed. A genuinely distracting exchange may change it. |
| A shove breaks the Character's stance and line of sight. | Benefits requiring those facts end or diminish. | Previously learned timing or other still-valid preparation is not automatically erased with the stance. |
| A target briefly passes behind a screen. | Benefits are unavailable or lost according to their basis. | Knowledge of the target's route may remain; continuous visual alignment has been broken. Reappearance does not automatically restore alignment or require repeating unrelated preparation. |
| An ambusher is discovered by one opponent. | Reassess against the actual opposition. | Concealment from that opponent is gone. Others may or may not know; an advantageous position may remain. No universal surprise procedure is inferred. |
| An ambusher fires and is now visibly exposed to everyone involved. | The concealed-approach benefit ends. | A still-useful position can remain, but it is not the same concealment bonus renamed. |
| A single-use prepared distraction is activated. | Its one-use setup is consumed; assess any resulting advantage. | The distraction may continue to matter. Do not count both the consumed setup and its resulting distraction as duplicate benefit. |
| Preparation relied on a guard changing shifts at a time that proves wrong. | The benefit founded on that schedule ends. | False information is not useful preparation. Any resulting exposure must be established in the fiction, not imposed as an automatic reversed bonus. |
| A Character waits several rounds with no relevant change. | No automatic increase or expiry. | Elapsed rounds alone are neither improvement nor disruption. Fatigue, a changing patrol, or an expiring opportunity can matter when actually present. |
| An opponent learns a previously effective prepared response. | Reassess what advantage remains. | Anticipated surprise may be lost while rehearsed execution remains useful. Neither a universal use counter nor a blanket persistence guarantee fits. |
| Preparation becomes genuinely better through new information. | Replace the assessed value of the same contribution. | Do not retain the old amount and add the improved version as a second source. Genuinely distinct benefits follow ordinary pooling. |

## Alternative Rules

Universal next-roll consumption handles expendable setups, but incorrectly
erases rehearsal, information, or durable position merely because they helped.
It also makes an irrelevant intervening roll a potential expiry unless more
exceptions are added.

A fixed number of rounds handles an actually timed opportunity only by
coincidence. It cannot represent enduring rehearsal and fragile visual alignment
with the same expiry. Fictionally established time limits remain valid without
a universal preparation timer.

Unconditional persistence fails too: concealment, stale intelligence, consumed
resources, and lost alignment cannot remain numerical benefits after their
supporting facts cease to exist.

The candidate handles all these cases through existing applicability and
distinct-contribution rules. Its cost is situational judgment about whether an
advantage changed, rather than a mechanically guaranteed number of uses.

## Arithmetic Illustration

Fixed Expression before contextual adjustment: 60.
Separate applicable Dissonance: 0.20.
Illustrative preparation assessments, not universal prices:

| Preparation basis | Current Resonance | Calculation | Rating |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fully applicable | 0.30 | 60 x (1 + 0.30 - 0.20) | 66 |
| Reduced benefit after circumstances change | 0.10 | 60 x (1 + 0.10 - 0.20) | 54 |
| No applicable benefit | 0 | 60 x (1 - 0.20) | 48 |

The reduced case uses 0.10 instead of 0.30, not 0.30 plus 0.10. The zero case
alone cannot tell whether preparation was destroyed or merely inapplicable;
that distinction remains in the fiction and matters on a later Expression.

## Recommendation and Remaining Boundary

The user adopted the tested rule. Neither use nor interruption is a universal expiry
event. The relevant change is consumption, disruption, invalidation, or loss of
applicability of the actual advantage. Retain a benefit unchanged when its
supporting facts and usefulness have not changed; do not require reassessment
or a maintenance roll merely because another round passed.

Where maintaining or reestablishing an advantage is genuinely uncertain, use
normal resolution of that activity. This creates no mandatory maintenance roll,
universal renewal cost, additional Capacity track, or preparation-use counter.

This comparison does not settle action investment, trigger declaration, held
responses, initiative repositioning, or stacking repeated preparation attempts.
Persistence of an advantage is not permission to take a later action for free.
The persistence decision is resolved; action and held-response accounting
remain the subsequent design question.
