# LENS Combat Action Peer Sweep v0.1

Date: 2026-09-03

Status: Requested research and candidate comparison. No new Tasks, costs,
combat procedures, permissions, or catalogue boundaries are adopted here.
Grab / Grapple / Catch naming remains parked at the user's request.

## Objective

Compare combat activities in peer games with the current LENS Task map,
identify missing examples and genuine scope questions, and examine Shooting
with equipment versus a projectile supplied through higher-order Access.

This is a selective sweep of six game systems, not a complete inventory of
their supplements. Peer actions, maneuvers, moves, skills, and power effects
are not equivalent construction layers. Their inclusion does not imply that
LENS needs a separately purchased Task for every item.

## Existing LENS Work

The earlier [defense-method test](../tests/lens-defense-method-task-test-v0.1.md)
already compared a rifle shot through Coordination and a Fire Bolt through
Projection, both with Shooting. Those were candidate routes. The user
separately established that Shooting and Throwing are distinct Tasks.

The active [Task map](../design/lens-task-landscape-working-map-v0.1.md)
subsequently adds Block regardless of recipient, Parry within Block, and
Toughness-based bodily interception. Traversing, Piloting, Navigation, Creation,
Conceal, and Convey provide current scope comparisons.

Different techniques are not independently sufficient grounds to split Tasks.
Fields and Specialties carry relevant expertise. Internal/external locus is
a useful diagnostic, not a compulsory division or fixed Task ownership.

## Peer Findings

### Fate Core And Venture City

Fate distinguishes broad action purposes from the skill used. Its Shoot
examples include precision effects, suppressive pressure, and cinematic
disarming. These show that a shot need not aim solely at bodily injury.
Sources: [four actions](https://fate-srd.com/fate-core/four-actions),
[Shoot](https://fate-srd.com/fate-core/shoot).

Venture City's Energy Blast explicitly uses Shoot for power-generated
projectiles, including emitted energy. It is direct precedent for grouping
ordinary shooting and extraordinary directed fire under shared trained
delivery. Its particular power enhancements and exceptions are not adopted
into LENS. Source: [Energy Blast](https://fate-srd.com/venture-city/energy-blast).

### Blades In The Dark

Hunt, Skirmish, Finesse, Prowl, and Wreck can all participate in violence with
different fictional approaches. Survey and Study expose opportunities;
Command and Sway can also matter during conflict. Action overlap is explicit.
Its teamwork rules include setup, assistance, coordinated effort, and
protecting another participant. These are useful coverage examples, not a
reason to import its ratings or position/effect procedure.
Sources: [actions](https://bladesinthedark.com/actions-attributes),
[teamwork](https://bladesinthedark.com/teamwork).

### Dungeon World, Original Edition

The original moves distinguish close fighting, shooting, responding to danger,
defending someone or something, reading a situation, and helping or hindering
another participant. Defy Danger routes through different attributes according
to the fictional response. Its moves resolve situations rather than provide
a list of equally priced learned activities. This sweep uses the original
edition source, not the separate Dungeon World 2 playtest.
Source: [author's basic-move text](https://github.com/Sagelt/Dungeon-World/blob/master/text/Moves.xml).

### GURPS Basic Set, Fourth Edition Revised

Local primary source: [Basic Set, Revised](<C:/ROLE_PLAYING GAMES/GURPS/GURPS_Basic_Set_Fourth_Edition_Revised.pdf>),
version 1.1, July 2026. Inspected printed pages 201, 240, and 551
(PDF pages 211, 250, and 561).

The maneuver table separates attacks from preparation, evaluation, deceptive
openings, posture changes, item readiness, movement, concentration, and waiting.
Those entries are turn choices, not thirteen equivalent skill purchases.

Innate Attack is trained direction of built-in ranged capabilities, distinct
from activating them. Its specialties distinguish forms of emission. Missile
spells separately use spell creation and an Innate Attack roll to hit. This
offers a contrasting architecture to Fate's shared Shoot approach. LENS does
not adopt that separate casting roll, its skill taxonomy, or a second effect
contest merely because the source uses them.

### Pathfinder Second Edition

The basic actions cover movement, attacking, observing, preparing, helping,
using objects, and taking cover. Athletics includes forcing movement, toppling,
gripping, escaping, and removing an opponent's held item. Deception supplies
combat misdirection. These are useful tests of intended effects and
preparation as well as activities. PF2's action costs, prerequisites, and
critical-result tables are not LENS rules.
Sources: [basic actions](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx),
[Athletics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=36),
[Deception](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=38),
[Aid](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2292).

### Mutants & Masterminds Third Edition

The publisher's Quick-Start describes ordinary weapons and amazing powers
within the same attack-check framework, with close and ranged attack
competence. It separately rates the effect and its resistance. That supports
the conceptual distinction between delivering an attack and possessing its
effect, but is not a recommendation to restore the rejected second LENS
damage/soak contest. Only the Quick-Start was inspected, not the full maneuver
catalogue. Source: [publisher's Quick-Start](https://freeronin.com/3e_files/MnM3_Quickstart.pdf).

### Access Limit

Pinnacle's current Savage Worlds reference-chart download could not be fetched.
Its marketing page and third-party discussions were not used as authority for
specific mechanics. Savage Worlds is not counted among the six reviewed systems.

## Coverage Bank And LENS Assessment

The first column is an inventory of things to demonstrate, not a proposed
list of purchases. The classifications are this sweep's design inferences.

| Activity or situation to cover | Current LENS position | Remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Deliver a direct blow | Strike candidate | Preserve varied capability routes; no mandatory weapon split. |
| Fire a directed projectile or beam | Shooting already on the map | Settle its ordinary/extraordinary scope; see next section. |
| Hurl a weapon, object, or manifested substance | Throwing already distinguished from Shooting | Test delivery, not the substance's origin. |
| Seize, catch, restrain, pin, or escape a hold | Grab / Grapple / Catch discussion | Park naming; retain contact and restraint cases for later. |
| Intercept with body, shield, weapon, or permitted barrier | Block working direction | Parry and protection of another target do not automatically add Tasks. |
| Evade or receive pressure while maintaining function | Dodge and Withstanding candidates | Use established narrative routes; no defensive matching chart. |
| Push, pull, shove, drag, or force something aside | Genuine scope gap | Compare sustained displacement with Lift, Strike, and managing a grip. |
| Trip, sweep, tackle, or otherwise put someone down | Missing effect examples | Test existing Tasks first; toppling is not automatically a new purchase. |
| Disarm or deny use of a held object | Missing effect examples | Compare knocking it away, taking it, and disabling it. |
| Break an object, disable equipment, or sabotage infrastructure | Genuine scope question | Compare destructive impact with deliberate technical interference. |
| Feint, distract, bait, or misdirect | Underdeveloped Task boundary | Compare Conceal, Convey, Presentation, and the unresolved Sway group; do not assign all deception to one Attribute. |
| Aim, assess an opponent, or find an opening | Preparation and information coverage | Use existing scene-support and information ideas; no automatic extra targeting multiplier. |
| Suppress movement, cover an ally, or control an approach | Missing purpose/effect examples | A shot, barrier, threat, or positional action may supply the actual Task. |
| Advance, withdraw, pursue, charge, or take cover | Movement and exposure examples | Apply Traversing or Piloting as appropriate; timing and combined effects remain separate questions. |
| Draw, ready, reload, swap, release, or retrieve equipment | Turn and equipment procedure coverage | An action cost does not imply a new ranked Task; test genuinely uncertain skilled handling when relevant. |
| Assist, set up, coordinate, or communicate during combat | Existing cooperation rules; Command and Convey | Demonstrate the contributor's actual activity and intended benefit; no automatic universal Help multiplier. |
| Wait, delay, interrupt, or counterattack | Timing or compound-procedure lane | Do not settle these through the Task inventory. |
| Maintain, end, or disrupt an ongoing manifestation | Duration, Access, and counter-expression examples | Identify the actual activity; do not import a universal Cast or Activate purchase. |

## Shooting And Extraordinary Projectiles

The origin of an effect and the practiced delivery of that effect are distinct
questions. An ordinary weapon, HOLT, or other permission-bearing Lens supplies
what can be attempted. It does not by its name determine every applicable Task.

Candidate scope for discussion, not adoption:

> Shooting directs a discharged projectile or emitted beam toward a target.

This wording includes an aimed energy discharge without requiring a physical
bullet. It does not mean that every harmful effect at a distance is Shooting.

| Stipulated fiction | Candidate Task | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fire a rifle at a target. | Shooting | Direct the weapon's discharge. |
| Emit an aimed energy bolt from the hand or eyes. | Shooting | Direct an emitted shot; power Access supplies the emission. |
| Manifest a solid ice spear and hurl it with a bodily throw. | Throwing | Creation of the object does not change the subsequent throwing activity. |
| Manifest an ice spear and launch it as an aimed discharge without a bodily throw. | Shooting | Same material, different delivery. |
| Ignite something directly at its location. | Unassigned in this test | No projectile travels there; do not force the example into Shooting. |
| Form a surrounding aura or interpose an elemental barrier. | Actual expressed activity; Block when intercepting | The material and potential for harm do not make it a shot. |

Possible rating fragments, not complete stacks:

```text
Mobility x Coordination x Shooting x Firearms x Rifles

Influence x Projection x Shooting x applicable Field x applicable Specialty
```

Equipment, extraordinary permission, applicable Axis Rank, Scale, Difficulty,
and current conditions are not represented by those fragments. The second
route does not require one universal Fire Magic Field or a Fire Bolt Specialty.
Their naming and purchase scope remain separate construction questions.

The first shot is not automatically Piloting because it uses equipment; that
Task concerns guiding a vehicle or craft. Nor does the second automatically
need Creation, Cast, and Shooting rolls. Whether a manifestation genuinely
contains independent activities follows the established Expression rules.

The backpack test remains intact: a flyer expressing personal locomotion and
a non-flyer directing a flight apparatus can perform different activities.
Here, both actors may be performing directed shooting despite different
sources of propulsion or substance. Internal/external origin is useful
evidence, not a compulsory split.

## Recommendation And Next Decision

Keep Shooting as the leading shared candidate for ordinary and extraordinary
directed shots. Peer practice supports considering it; it does not decide
LENS's vocabulary or prove purchase balance. If its everyday wording still
feels too equipment-bound, compare alternative names for that same activity
before inventing a separate power-only Task.

The immediate user-facing decision is its scope, not numerical testing.
After that, examine push/pull/displacement with concrete ordinary and
extraordinary examples. Then examine misdirection and disabling. Retain
disarming, toppling, suppression, and preparation as an example bank while
checking whether existing Tasks already produce those outcomes.

Do not resume Grab naming, adopt every peer maneuver as a Task, add a second
resolution stage, or reopen budgets in order to complete this sweep.
