## User's Idea

These are possibilities, not a single approved model:

- A character may identify, select, or come to be recognized for a meaningful
  aspect of their character. They may have more than one.
- It could be purchased, or earned through consequential actions aligned with
  it, perhaps awarded by the GM as non-paid story development.
- Such development might strengthen powers, perseverance, drive, or other
  applicable Expressions. Powers-only versus broader applicability is open.
- It might be an enhancement related to the existing idea of limitations, but
  no mandatory paired drawback or compensation formula has been proposed.
- It could contribute to existing Resonance or be an independent mechanical
  multiplier. Neither representation has been selected.
- It need not be limited to Heralds of the Source. Core option, Profile option,
  or setting-specific implementation remain possible.
- Focus, Aspect, Nature, and Aspect Resonance are candidate descriptions only.
  Focus is already general optical language in the Progressive Focus Principle,
  but is not an adopted formal construction category, rank, or multiplier. That
  existing usage does not automatically disqualify it as a candidate name.

The user explicitly wants to explore meanings before terminology or structure.
Do not constrain the idea to actions such as Protect, Create, Destroy, or Deceive.
Good/Evil/Balance is not the organizing model for this exploration.

## Breadth To Preserve

User examples: family-focused devotion, protector, destruction, creation,
deception, and an environmentalist whose convictions supply will, drive, or
strength to continue or enhance their powers.

Brainstorming examples, not adopted categories:

- Relationships: family devotion, loyalty to companions, community belonging,
  hospitality, solidarity with outsiders.
- Places and living things: environmental stewardship, homeland, animals,
  wilderness, attachment to one place.
- Convictions: freedom, justice, mercy, truth, duty, tradition, progress.
- Temperament: defiance, curiosity, patience, audacity, perseverance, pride.
- Calling: protector, healer, creator, destroyer, explorer, teacher, liberator,
  keeper of memory.
- Drives: ambition, vengeance, redemption, discovery, mastery, recognition.
- Approaches and commitments: deception, candor, ingenuity, improvisation,
  keeping promises, honoring ancestors, preserving a craft, rebuilding loss.
- Tensions: loyalty against conscience, mercy against vengeance, belonging
  against independence. Whether tensions themselves qualify is exploratory.

Family devotion may support negotiation, construction, endurance, deception,
or extraordinary protection without becoming six separate activity purchases.
Environmental stewardship may support restoration, exposing pollution,
sabotaging destructive equipment, or enduring hardship. A creator and a keeper
of traditional craft can perform similar actions for different personal reasons.
Defiance can oppose domination without necessarily being altruistic.

## First Exploratory Pass

Begin with family devotion, environmental stewardship, and defiance. Follow
each through several activities, including ordinary and extraordinary use.
Compare clear applicability, an unrelated action, a meaningful costly choice,
and overlapping aspects. In an eviction confrontation, for example, devotion
to family and defiance may both matter: examine distinct support versus two
descriptions of the same commitment without deciding stacking in advance.

Then contrast with an action-adjacent example such as creation or deception.
Do not force every candidate through every Attribute or invent strained routes.
Allow counterexamples to show where a proposed interpretation is inadequate.

Separate three questions:
1. What supports applicability: identity, stakes, intentions, actual conduct?
2. How does it arise/develop: selection, purchase, GM recognition, or story award?
3. How does it enhance: existing pooled Resonance or an independent multiplier?

Do not treat these choices as inseparable. Compare purchase versus earned
development at an otherwise equal benefit before changing all variables.
Use a few illustrative numerical controls only when the conceptual comparison
supports them. Clearly label all trial values, not as user proposals or locks.

## Current Core Controls

Use the adopted additive foundation A + D, with D <= A and no free first
Derived Rank; multiply applicable Task, Field, Specialty, Precision, and Axis
factors as defined in the current baseline. HOLT acquisition grants defined
Access only, not a parent Rank. Axis remains purchased and ranked, with no
replacement Derived-to-Axis cap.

Flat prices: Attribute 60; HOLT acquisition 40; Derived 20; Axis 15; Task/Field
10 each; Specialty/Precision 5 each. Session Participation 10, discretionary
categories 0/3/5. Do not recalibrate these or protected starting allocations.

Distinct challenge Difficulty bands now add; apply 0.98 x 0.6^(D-1) once.
Do not duplicate a demand through both Difficulty and context. Distance and
area thresholds, starting duration increment, and area-entry/surplus rules
remain unresolved. Current context is max(0, 1 + Resonance - Dissonance).

The combined-scope test held ordinary ratings fixed while varying Axis. Axis
0 to 10 doubles the pre-floor Expression: more than offsetting one Difficulty
step, not two. This supports asking about conditional enhancement; it does not
prove another multiplier is necessary. The user expects farther/wider/longer
to be harder. Do not create a mandatory enhancer to cancel that Difficulty.

## Critical Questions, Not Preselected Objections

- Does the benefit express character meaning rather than reward convenient
  post-hoc descriptions of any action?
- Can quiet or less theatrical players earn recognition without GM personality
  judgments, morality grading, or pressure to repeat a defining trait endlessly?
- What distinguishes a lasting aspect from momentary motivation or ordinary
  context? Does contradiction, change, or growth affect it? Do not presume loss.
- Can multiple aspects coexist without automatic unrestricted multiplication,
  or an arbitrary one-aspect restriction imposed before comparison?
- Does a broadly applicable option dominate more selective ones?
- Does the same underlying contribution receive duplicate mechanical credit?
  A story-earned benefit and session recognition are not automatically forbidden
  together; current doctrine does not require subtracting every story reward.
- Non-paid development still increases capability. Does its expected pace make
  sense alongside purchased development without requiring a new award currency?
- What value does a separate multiplier add beyond existing Resonance?
- Preserve permission versus enhancement: protective intent alone grants no
  otherwise unavailable manifestation. No compulsory compound averaging merely
  because the enhancer supports another capability.
