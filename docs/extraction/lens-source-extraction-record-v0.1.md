## Extraction Standard

A source proposition may enter LENS only when it is:

1. mechanically or doctrinally relevant to the generic engine;
2. separable from licensed, campaign, or setting-specific assumptions;
3. consistent with current LENS authority, or clearly labeled as a conflict;
4. assigned an explicit status;
5. accompanied by enough provenance to recover the reasoning later.

### Extractable Propositions

#### Capability and Pressure Are Separate

Status:

```text
CONFIRMED EXISTING DOCTRINE
```

A rating describes built capability.

Requirements, resistance, and adverse conditions describe pressure acting on
that capability.

This independently supports the LENS separation between Base Expression and
Difficulty, Dissonance, or opposition.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, lines 92-94
```

#### Objective Environmental Pressure

Status:

```text
CONFIRMED EXISTING DOCTRINE
```

Persistent environmental pressure is recorded once and changes only when the
environment materially changes.

It does not increase or rewrite an actor's stable capability.

This supports the Counter-Lens doctrine that a scene remains objective while
characters answer it through their own Lenses.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, lines 130-153
```

#### Applicability Is Required

Status:

```text
SUPPORTED CANDIDATE
HIGH CONFIDENCE
```

Possessing a Lens does not make it applicable to every action.

A Lens contributes only when its fictional identity genuinely applies to the
Expression being attempted.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, lines 214-222
```

#### No Synonym Stacking

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
HIGH CONFIDENCE
```

One underlying contribution cannot be counted repeatedly under synonymous
descriptions.

Several names do not establish several contributions when they perform the
same fictional work.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, line 224
```

#### Fictional Identity Without Bespoke Packages

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
HIGH PRIORITY
```

Named fictional advantages can remain descriptive rather than becoming fixed
rules packages.

Their identity determines:

- applicability;
- permissions;
- boundaries;
- persistence;
- vulnerabilities.

Their mechanical effect uses the common system grammar.

This is directly relevant to the LENS goal of supporting regeneration,
extraordinary toughness, fire, ice, aquatic capability, and similar concepts
without creating a catalogue of bespoke powers.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, lines 351-401
```

#### Stable Lenses Persist

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
HIGH CONFIDENCE
```

A persistent advantage remains available until the fiction specifically
disables, destroys, removes, or makes it inapplicable.

Ordinary pressure does not automatically erase a stable Lens.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, lines 417-421
```

#### Persistent Dissonance Names Cause and Scope

Status:

```text
CONFIRMED EXISTING DOCTRINE
```

A persistent disadvantage identifies both its fictional cause and what it
affects.

Recovery must address that cause rather than merely pay a generic mechanical
cost.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, lines 450-475
```

#### Fiction Determines Consequence

Status:

```text
SUPPORTED CANDIDATE
```

The mechanical shortfall may identify what failed without universally fixing
the consequence.

The fiction determines the resulting consequence unless the cause has already
established one.

Source:

```text
Asset Operations System - Working Rules.md, lines 205-212
```

#### Organization-Scale Continuity

Status:

```text
SUPPORTED CANDIDATE
LOWER PRIORITY
```

Large-scale actors should use the same broad action grammar and create
campaign-facing consequences rather than requiring a separate accounting
game.

This may eventually support factions, organizations, and Infrastructure, but
no generic procedure is adopted.

Source:

```text
Faction_Downtime_Activity_Rules_DRAFT.md, lines 17-55
```

### Already Extracted and Supported

Status:

```text
CONFIRMED EXISTING DOCTRINE
```

The source and playtests support these existing LENS propositions:

- Everything changing an action is a Lens.
- Stable Lenses build a precomputed Expression Rating.
- Active Lenses modify that rating during play.
- Active opposition is Expression against Expression.
- Passive opposition is Expression against Dissonance.
- Harm may impose typed Dissonance instead of reducing hit points.
- Recovery must answer matching Dissonance.
- Strained is a useful middle state between Available and Suppressed.
- Infrastructure requires usable access, control, compatibility, repair, or
  routing; location alone grants no benefit.

The playtests provide evidence for these propositions, but do not final-lock
their numerical implementations.

### Distinct Contribution Principle

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
HIGH PRIORITY
EXPLORATORY SOURCE
```

Every Lens added to a compound Expression must contribute something
fictionally distinct.

Whatever a Lens contributes becomes an element that may be supported,
amplified, disrupted, stripped, redirected, or otherwise countered.

This reinforces the anti-synonym-stacking proposition found independently in
the general RPG sweep.

Source:

```text
compound-expressions-focus-and-counterplay-working-record-2026-08-18.md,
lines 217-257
```

### Partial Counter and Residual Expression

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
EXPLORATORY SOURCE
```

A counter may remove or weaken one contributor without erasing the entire
Expression.

Working descriptive vocabulary:

```text
Core Expression
What remains structurally central.

Contributed Effect
What another distinct Lens adds.

Partial Counter
Removal or weakening of one contributed element.

Residual Expression
What remains after the partial counter.
```

These terms are not adopted mechanical categories.

### Countering and Continuing Dissonance

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
HIGH CONFIDENCE
```

Countering is the immediate action or force opposing an Expression.

Dissonance is continuing interference that limits, distorts, or destabilizes
an Expression after or during that opposition.

A momentary counter need not automatically create continuing Dissonance.

Source:

```text
compound-expressions-focus-and-counterplay-working-record-2026-08-18.md,
lines 408-435
```

### Capability and Exposure

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
```

Compound Expressions may gain capability and exposure together.

Each distinct contributor creates another relationship that may be countered
when the fiction supports it.

Exposure is not automatically Dissonance.

Source:

```text
compound-expressions-focus-and-counterplay-working-record-2026-08-18.md,
lines 437-480
```

### Expression Constraints

Status:

```text
NEW WORKING CANDIDATE
HIGH PRIORITY
NOT ADOPTED
```

A broad permission may be bounded by an Expression Constraint without making
the boundary a flaw or Dissonance.

A Constraint may define the channel through which a broad capability normally
manifests.

Possible roles:

- define applicability;
- deny broad amplification outside the channel;
- support precision or potency inside the channel;
- identify relevant counters;
- create paths for growth, broadening, corruption, or restoration.

No point value, multiplier, bonus, penalty, or rebate is established.

Source:

```text
domain-expression-constraints-theory-v0.1.md, lines 7-25 and 148-178
```

### Connection and Authority

Status:

```text
UNRESOLVED
HIGH PRIORITY
```

A rated high-order Lens may be conflating several different properties:

- permission or access;
- connection;
- authority;
- concentration;
- magnitude;
- multiplier value.

Before a high-order rating is adopted, the system must identify which of these
jobs it performs and avoid duplicating Classification, Scale, Attribute,
Skill, or Specialization.

Source:

```text
master-character-generation-framework-v0.1.md, lines 145-166
```

## Conflicts Requiring Tests

### Expression Rank vs. Expression Rating

Status:

```text
CONFLICT
PRIMARY MECHANICAL PRIORITY
```

The MOTURPG prototype compared Load with a separate 1-10 Expression Rank.

The current LENS reference compares Load directly with Expression Rating.

Current specialized Expression Ratings can reach 16-80 or more, while the
tested Load values were built for the earlier rank scale.

The old Load procedure cannot be imported unchanged.

### Relevant Dissonance vs. Total Dissonance Pool

Status:

```text
CONFLICT
PRIMARY MECHANICAL PRIORITY
```

The tested prototype applied one most-relevant Dissonance as roll pressure.

The current LENS reference combines all relevant Dissonance Lenses into one
total Dissonance modifier.

The distinction between relevant aggregation and unrelated accumulated Load
must be tested explicitly.

### Rounding

Status:

```text
RESOLVED IN FAVOR OF CURRENT LENS
```

The older prototype rounded to the nearest whole number.

Current LENS carries full precision and floors only the final displayed value.

The current LENS rule supersedes the prototype.

### Scale Entry and Progression

Status:

```text
HISTORICAL CONFLICT
RESOLVED FOR CURRENT TESTING
```

Older MOTURPG records used Magnitude 1 = x3.

Current LENS uses Magnitude 1 = x1 and tests exponential x1.6.

The old values are historical only.

### Focus Terminology

Status:

```text
CONFLICT
UNRESOLVED
```

Older records used Focus as a synonym or translation for Scale.

Later records used Focus for thematic or functional concentration.

Neither usage should become a formal baseline term until the collision is
resolved.

### Attribute and Domain Collision

Status:

```text
CONFLICT
ACTIVE DESIGN PROBLEM
```

Might already includes physical strength, endurance, toughness, durability,
and recovery-related capacity.

MOTURPG also considered Strength as a rated Domain.

Strength multiplied through Might risks repeating the same concept at two
different layers.

This is the central diagnostic case for the unnamed generic high-order Lens.

### Overflow

Status:

```text
HISTORICAL PROCEDURE SUPERSEDED
CURRENT ROLE UNRESOLVED
```

The MOTURPG prototype used fixed Overflow bands.

Current LENS treats Overflow as optional and under reconsideration.

No old Overflow band is imported as current procedure.

## Current Discussion Extraction

The current discussion adds the following system-neutral design questions and
candidates.

### Candidate Four-Attribute Chassis

Status:

```text
WORKING CANDIDATE
UNIVERSAL STATUS UNRESOLVED
```

```text
Might
Internal physical locus of control.

Mobility
External physical locus of control.

Psyche
Internal locus of will, thought, intelligence, awareness, discipline,
or energy.

Influence
External projected, social, or nonphysical locus of control.
```

The chassis is inherited from MOTURPG but may prove generic.

It is not yet adopted as the universal Attribute set for all LENS games.

### High-Order Lens Models Under Test

Status:

```text
UNRESOLVED
```

```text
Model A
Separate high-order Lens rating through each Attribute.

Model B
One rated high-order Lens multiplier across all compatible Attributes,
with narrower channels beneath it.

Model C
Bottom-up expressions share tags and produce an emergent thematic identity.

Model D
Bespoke power packages.
```

Model D is the least compatible with the current design goal because it risks
creating a book of powers.

Model B has been the strongest early candidate, but it does not yet solve the
Attribute collision exemplified by Strength and Might.

No model is adopted.

### Domain Is Not Renamed Theme

Status:

```text
BOUNDARY CLARIFICATION
```

Domain remains MOTURPG-specific.

Theme has been used only as temporary neutral discussion language.

Theme is not adopted as the generic replacement term.

The generic high-order Lens slot remains unnamed and unresolved.

### Permission Cannot Be Manufactured Below the Point of Access

Status:

```text
WORKING HYPOTHESIS
HIGH PRIORITY TEST
```

A narrower Lens may improve, focus, or specialize an available permission.

It may not create a qualitatively extraordinary permission that is absent
from the preceding stack.

Example diagnostic:

```text
A trained guard specializing in ordinary recovery does not thereby gain
supernatural regeneration.
```

The unresolved question is where the extraordinary permission enters without
requiring a bespoke power package.

### Extraordinary Capability Diagnostic Cases

Status:

```text
ACTIVE TEST SET
```

The generic engine must distinguish at least these cases:

- ordinary physical strength;
- extraordinary strength capable of extreme feats;
- exceptional toughness or damage resistance;
- rapid or supernatural regeneration;
- ordinary medical recovery expertise;
- high Attribute values without extraordinary permission;
- the same extraordinary permission expressed differently by two characters.

No Paragon layer, Theme layer, Domain replacement, or power catalogue has
been adopted to solve these cases.
