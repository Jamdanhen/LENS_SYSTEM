# LENS Weaken Cross-Context Task Test v0.1

Date: 2026-09-05

Status: Conceptual comparison at user direction. Subsequently, the user
accepted provisional Weaken with further testing as needed. Compromise remains
an unselected alternative. No pricing,
probability, damage procedure, compulsory purchase, or Task count is changed.

## Purpose And Controls

Test the candidate outside the Sea/weathering case supplied by the Heralds
of the Source handoff. All cases below are system-neutral fictional situations.
The full handoff, including the user's corrections, was reviewed. Earlier
claims that weathering either requires a unique Task or cannot justify a new
Task are not inherited as rules.

Candidate definition:

    Weaken: Deliberately reduce the integrity, strength, or functional
    capacity of something.

Reference: `../design/lens-task-landscape-working-map-v0.1.md`, current
consolidated definitions of Exert, Strike, Discharge, Reconfigure, Create,
Research, Inference, Convey, Persuade, Deceive, and the other working Tasks.

Review dimensions: recognizable practiced contribution; ordinary and
extraordinary portability; overlap versus wholesale replacement; neutral or
beneficial use; scope across short actions and larger undertakings; and
continued separation of permission, expertise, and effect resolution.

Verdicts concern conceptual fit, not success probability. Fields mentioned
are illustrative expertise, not newly adopted purchases. No fixed Attribute
routing is imposed. The comparison does not require a Task to serve all five
Attribute families or exclude it because another Task could also apply.

## Twelve Cases

### 1. Release A Fragile Object From An Adhesive Bond

Ordinary work, with appropriate tools and relevant material expertise. The
practiced contribution is reducing the bond without ruining the object.

Finding: strong candidate fit, with a beneficial purpose. Repair may overlap
when this is part of restoration, but weakening the bond is not itself
necessarily restoration of damaged function. Weaken need not mean attack.
No real-world chemical procedure is specified or assumed.

### 2. Degrade An Obsolete Structure Before Its Removal

Ordinary controlled material degradation supported by construction or material
expertise. Exert, Strike, or Reconfigure may also describe the actual work.

Finding: strong candidate fit. The transferable practice is managing a loss
of integrity, not automatically knowing how every structure works. Detailed
demolition is potentially an undertaking, not a single roll simply because
the intended outcome can be stated in one sentence.

### 3. Attenuate A Transmitted Signal To Reduce Interference

Ordinary technical adjustment with equipment and relevant electronics expertise.
The signal becomes weaker without necessarily damaging the transmitter.

Finding: coherent overlap with Reconfigure when settings or arrangements are
changed. Weaken describes the reduction; Reconfigure describes adjustment of
operation. Do not require both multipliers for the same adjustment. A task
that can reduce a signal does not thereby cover every change of settings.

### 4. Undermine An Argument By Challenging Its Supporting Premise

An intellectual application: the character constructs a rebuttal that reduces
the argument's support. The appropriate subject expertise still matters.

Finding: plausible, but overlaps established activities. Inference may supply
recognition of the flaw; Convey its explanation; Persuade may address an
audience's belief. These are not mandatory successive rolls. Identifying an
error privately is not automatically the same action as undermining a claim
before an audience. Weaken should not replace all reasoning merely because
reasoning can refute something.

### 5. Disrupt A Performer's Concentration

An ordinary mental-pressure example in a fictional contest. A character uses
practiced disruption to make concentration harder; the description must say
what is actually being done rather than merely announce reduced Resolve.

Finding: plausible conditional fit. A message can involve Convey; a misleading
cue can involve Deceive. A practiced attempt to undermine concentration could
support Weaken without changing beliefs or communicating false information.
Neither the Task name nor the goal guarantees a Mental Condition or bypasses
the target's resistance. This is not physical harm by default.

### 6. Erode Cooperation Within A Coalition

A social/institutional objective. The character might contest a particular
commitment, expose an incompatibility, or interrupt an actual support relation.

Finding: possible Weaken applications, but the broad objective is not enough
to define one Expression. A campaign against an entire coalition may require
several activities and participants. Persuade, Convey, Deceive, or other Tasks
may describe particular approaches. This does not create a universal ability
to weaken all relationships or make every political activity one Task.

### 7. Withdraw An Existing Funding Commitment

The withdrawal could weaken an organization, but the character may simply
possess the authority or control to stop supplying their own resources.

Finding: consequence alone does not establish a practiced Weaken contribution
or require a roll. Assets, Authority, agreements, and current Access determine
what can actually be withdrawn. A contested effort to sever someone else's
support relationship would be a different declared activity.

### 8. Apply A Fictional Curse That Diminishes Physical Strength

Extraordinary, non-Sea example. Assume explicit permission for this capability;
Weaken supplies only relevant practiced application, with any appropriate
magical expertise. The curse is not automatically Strike or Discharge.

Finding: strong candidate fit. No newly invented delivery method or secondary
effect contest is needed. The actual consequences use the existing applicable
effect and condition rules; no direct Attribute subtraction is adopted here.
Its speed, duration, and counters belong to the actual expression and rules,
not to automatic benefits of the Task label.

### 9. Psychically Dampen An Overwhelming Fear

Extraordinary, beneficial mental example. Assume the character has permission
to influence that emotional state. Relevant practice aims to diminish the
fear rather than impose obedience or communicate a persuasive argument.

Finding: strong candidate fit, potentially overlapping Treat when addressing
a Condition. Weaken does not automatically remove Load: the actual supported
recovery or effect procedure remains authoritative. This is not permission
for every character with Weaken training to alter minds.

### 10. Hit An Opponent And Leave Them Less Capable

The description establishes Strike and its consequences. It does not by itself
identify a second practiced contribution of weakening.

Finding: Weaken is not automatically added and does not automatically replace
Strike. A different description could establish a legitimate Weaken approach;
the test does not ban overlap in combat. Intent to cause harm alone must not
turn Weaken into all damaging activities.

### 11. Reveal True Information That Damages A Reputation

The character communicates information; reduced standing may follow. Convey
or another applicable Task may already capture the practiced contribution.

Finding: reputational damage alone is insufficient to infer Weaken training.
A deliberate practiced effort to erode credibility could make it relevant,
but the player must identify that actual contribution. The same disclosure
does not earn two multipliers merely through two descriptions of its effect.

### 12. Reconfigure A Machine Into A Different Effective Mode

The machine changes function without reduced integrity or intended capacity.
One previous output might cease while the selected output becomes available.

Finding: not automatically Weaken. Reconfigure covers useful alterations that
are not degradation. Describing any ceased function as weakness would erase
the intended distinction. Deliberately impairing the machine is a different
case and may legitimately overlap.

## Comparative Findings

- Strongest examples: bond reduction, material degradation, strength-diminishing
  curse, and psychic dampening. These establish possible ordinary, extraordinary,
  harmful, and beneficial uses without dependence on Sea.
- Signal adjustment is a clear overlap rather than a reason to prohibit either
  Weaken or Reconfigure. Reconfigure still covers non-weakening changes.
- Intellectual, mental-pressure, and social applications are plausible, but
  need concrete activity descriptions. These are not rejected merely because
  they concern something other than physical material.
- Funding withdrawal, incidental harm, and reputational consequences show
  where reduction alone is not evidence of a trained contribution.
- A coalition campaign or demolition project does not become one action
  because Weaken can describe its overarching purpose.

The tested definition is promising but broad. These examples support a useful
identity; they cannot prove that every player will distinguish it consistently
from generic harm. If play permits every damaging action to substitute Weaken
without a change in practiced contribution, its scope would need revision.

Do not repair that risk with unsupported requirements for slow action, permanent
damage, a preliminary analysis roll, a specific weak point, or exclusively
physical targets. None follows from the accepted general Task architecture.

## Recommendation

Retain the recommendation for provisional Weaken, with the existing definition
and these examples. Its instructional center is practiced reduction of a
target's integrity, strength, or functional capacity, not every activity that
has an adverse consequence. This applies the existing relevance and distinct-
contribution principles rather than introducing another mechanical condition.

Compromise remains a naming alternative but adds ambiguity between negotiation,
exposure, and impairment; this test gives no reason to prefer it to Weaken.

Subsequent decision: after this broader test, the user accepted provisional
retention and instructed return to the main lane. The Task map records that
decision. Further tests should arise from actual needs rather than an exhaustive
attempt to settle every possible application now.

After the side request is settled, the queued main-lane proposal remains the
nine scene-based performance cases for the three 100-point Training builds.
The instruction to return to the main lane resumes that proposed comparison.
