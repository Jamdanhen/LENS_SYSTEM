## Question

The user expects an effect that reaches farther, covers more, and persists
longer to be harder. Accumulating distinct demands is not itself a defect.
Test the resulting capability requirements rather than presume that they
should be reduced.

## Controls

- Add the distinct distance, area, and independent-duration Difficulty bands,
  then apply 0.98 x 0.6^(D-1) once, with final-only flooring.
- Test distance 100 increments as D3 and coverage 25/100 units as D1/D3,
  continuing the existing 25/50/100 candidates. Coverage is footprint, not
  radius. Single-target and instantaneous cases add no area/duration band.
- Finite independent duration uses the adopted tenfold progression: one,
  ten, and one hundred applicable time increments at D1/D2/D3.
- No extra generic manifestation D1 is added on top of the specified demands.
  Additional distinct challenges would require separate assessment.
- All manifestations have the necessary Access, matched Scale, full Function,
  applicable training and one Axis. No equipment, context, spending, active
  opposition, or mitigation. These are uncertain required checks, not routine
  actions being forced to roll.
- Controlled routes have A, D, Task, Field, Specialty, Precision, and Axis all
  at the indicated rank. Raw Expressions are 22.27758 at Rank 3, 75.9375 at
  Rank 5, 302.33088 at Rank 8, and 640 at Rank 10. These are developed routes,
  not complete equal-budget characters or claims about typical citizens.
- No HOLT parent Rank is purchased or multiplied. Axis remains multiplicative.

The comparison measures check success and generated Effects. It does not settle
the outstanding area-entry expenditure or surplus-growth procedure. In
particular, success percentages are not promises of harm to every target.

## Output And Alternative Means

At ordinary roll 40, the all-10 route produces 19, 10, 5, 1, 0, and 0 successful
Effects down the table. The all-8 route produces 7, 3, 0, 0, 0, and 0.
These are generated Effects before any required allocation, opposition, or
protection; they cannot be spent twice or assumed to be all damage.

An actively maintained version does not purchase independent persistence merely
because elapsed time passes. Where its actual means permit it, it retains the
distance-plus-area challenge (D4 here), with substantial sustaining paid through
existing action accounting. The all-8 route therefore has a 63% initial check
instead of 38% for one autonomous increment, but the character commits ongoing
activity. No automatic maintenance roll or universal free action is introduced.
This comparison does not guarantee unchanged Difficulty under interruption or
other changed facts.

Likewise, ordinary natural continuation is not charged as autonomous supernatural
duration. If the result persists because its ordinary supporting conditions
remain, use those facts and the existing persistence rules.

## Assessment

Every added Difficulty step retains 60% of the pre-floor Rating, a 40% reduction.
The effect on success chance can be hidden above 99%, but successful output still
falls. This is the existing curve operating consistently, not a newly introduced
penalty exception.

The severe D3 + D3 + D3 case remains a 10% check even at the fully developed
one-Axis route without further support. This is the important calibration fact:
three individually manageable demands need not be jointly reliable. It is not
proof of a balance defect. Whether that combination should be a rare unsupported
feat is an intended-capability judgment.

Recommend keeping cumulative Difficulty and advancing the sum-of-distinct-bands
candidate, without changing the curve to compensate for this result. Do not
also charge a contextual penalty for the same distance, area, or duration merely
under another label. Actual separate interference remains relevant.

Before adopting aggregation, the substantive decision is whether adding the
component bands is the intended combined challenge. This test does not adopt
the separate lookup thresholds, introduce a Difficulty ceiling, or authorize
unbounded Effect-based expansion past a cheaper declaration.

### Interpretation And Cost

- Axis 0 to 10 doubles the pre-floor Expression. At one harder Difficulty step,
  the resulting factor relative to the original Axis-0 challenge is 2 x 0.6 =
  1.2; at two harder steps it is 2 x 0.36 = 0.72. Full Axis development more
  than offsets one step, not two. These are comparisons, not Difficulty refunds.
- Axis 5 to 10 adds one third to the pre-floor Expression, not another doubling.
- Floor rounding and special-result bounds can conceal an individual purchase's
  numerical improvement on a particular check. No claim that every Axis rank
  changes every displayed success chance is warranted.
- Additional Axis development costs 0, 15, 75, and 150 at the tested ranks.
  Including one unrestricted acquisition, totals are 40, 55, 115, and 190.
  The test does not compare optimal alternative spending or change pricing.

challenge's intended severity, not whether HOLT development was omitted.
