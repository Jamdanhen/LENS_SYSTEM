## Additive Foundation

```text
A = Attribute Rating, 1-10
D = Derived Attribute Rank, 0-A
T, F, S, P = applicable Task, Field, Specialty, Precision ranks, 0-10

Ordinary six-layer Expression =
(A + D) x (1 + T/10) x (1 + F/10) x (1 + S/10) x (1 + P/10)
```

Replace A x (1 + D/10) with A + D in all five Attribute families. Add established
broad and focused capability; multiply practiced activity and expertise afterward.
Established includes acquired Leverage, not only innate characteristics.

Derived ranks remain separately purchased and parent-capped. Rank 0 adds zero;
no free first Derived rank or compensating rank reduction is introduced.
The ordinary maximum remains 320 before additional applicable factors, not a
universal Final Rating cap. Scale, Difficulty, Axis development, equipment,
context, Function, Access, final-only flooring, and independent Effects retain
their rules. Higher-than-10 horizons remain unadopted.

## Access Only HOLTs

A HOLT supplies defined thematic Access without a numerical parent Rank. Purchase
Axis development directly at 15 per rank, from Rank 0 at x1.0 through Rank 10
at x2.0. No HOLT-parent or replacement Derived-rank cap applies to Axes.
The ordinary Derived-to-Attribute cap remains.

Acquisition costs a working 40 once, with no free Axis ranks and no second
initial charge. It does not open every possible Axis, change Condition or
Closed Access to Open, grant incoherent manifestations, raise Scale, or guarantee
competence. Identity, scope, limitations, counters, and narrative eligibility
remain meaningful. An Axis can cover several manifestations, not necessarily
one named power. Existing two-required-Axis averaging is unchanged.

No later parent-rank purchases remain. Creation and advancement share flat

Training leftovers remain spent-or-lost at creation finalization; unrestricted
carryover, existing expertise parent relationships, and priced provisions remain.

## Limitations

Retain acquisition estimates 40 unrestricted, 30 Modest, 20 Substantial, and
10 Severe. These are 25/50/75-percent acquisition reductions, not development
discounts. Assess combined actual burden without duplicate stacking. No recurring
award follows. Eligible permanent buyoff pays the acquisition-price difference
without repricing past advancement. Narrative eligibility remains necessary.

Partial-Axis compensation remains conceptually available but numerically
unresolved, including its replacement reinvestment boundary. Do not double-count
the same loss, invent hypothetical refunded ranks, or impose a new Derived cap.

## Awards And Spending

Participation is 10. Progress, Discovery, Character Expression, and Narrative
Engagement each remain 0/3/5: not demonstrated, meaningful, exceptional.
Exceptional replaces meaningful. Distinct contributions can qualify separately;
renaming one contribution does not earn it twice.

Keep Participation for participating players, Progress/Discovery for the
participating group, and Character Expression/Narrative Engagement individually.
Do not grade acting, airtime, morality, or plot compliance. Quiet contributions
and opportunity remain relevant; awards are not single-winner competitions.

The three-meaningful reference is 19; all meaningful is 22; the card spans
10-30 before optional milestones. No compulsory total is imposed. Optional
milestones remain 12.5 without a new frequency, rounding, or recipient rule.

Boost, paid reroll, and defined narrative help retain their one-point prices
and existing usage limits in the shared development currency. Spending competes
with advancement; no reimbursement, second pool, or spending quota is introduced.
Story development is value already received, without automatic deductions or
duplicate spendable grants. Narrative eligibility still governs purchases and
their activation, preferably between scenes, chapters, or stories.

## Reserved Decisions

- Existing paid parent HOLT ranks require a separate conversion decision;
  this adoption authorizes no refunds, balance changes, or free Axis ranks.
- Legacy grants of parent Rank N do not become N Axis ranks, N developed
  Axes, or a point grant automatically. Each package needs explicit translation.
- Partial-Axis compensation amounts and replacement reinvestment boundaries
  remain unresolved, as do setting-specific parent-potential requirements.
- Higher rank horizons, final theme valuations, unfinished Profile packages,
  and small-provision prices remain open.
- Absent-player handling and milestone recipients remain open.
