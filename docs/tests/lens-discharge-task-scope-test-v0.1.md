# LENS Discharge Task Scope Test v0.1

Date: 2026-09-03

Status: User-requested conceptual test of the user's Discharge candidate.
This record recommends continued use as the candidate replacement for
Shooting; it does not adopt a catalogue entry or alter baseline doctrine.
Subsequent user clarification: launching a projectile with a sling is
Discharge. That application is resolved for the working map.

## Question And Controls

Can Discharge name the practiced activity shared by firing a weapon and
releasing an extraordinary projectile without absorbing every ranged action,
Throwing, or Projection itself?

This is a narrative applicability and scope test, not a numerical simulation
or proof of equal purchase value. Existing Task, Field, and Specialty rules,
Access, Scale, Difficulty, Effect, and opposition remain unchanged. No second
aiming, activation, damage, or resistance roll is introduced.

Different equipment, media, or techniques do not by themselves justify new
Tasks. Fields and Specialties remain the expertise layers. The examples are
illustrative routes, not ownership assignments or a compulsory action list.
The user's prior distinction between Shooting and Throwing remains a control;
renaming Shooting does not silently merge the two purchases.

## Candidate Meaning

Original discussion definition:

> Release and direct a projectile or outward flow through a weapon, device,
> or personal capability.

Working wording for this test:

> Deliberately release and direct an outgoing projectile, burst, or stream.

The practiced contribution is managing the release and its direction. The
source can be a device or a personal capability. Discharge is not restricted
to harm, a distant target, a physical projectile, or a brief burst.

The comparisons below distinguish the candidate from Throwing through the
activity being performed. Sling use is resolved as Discharge by the user.

## Core Cases

| Case | Candidate route | What the Task contributes | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire a handgun toward a chosen mark. | Mobility x Coordination x Discharge | Place and time the outgoing shot through the weapon. | Strong ordinary fit. Firearms expertise can contribute through a Field. |
| Release a permitted force bolt from the hand. | Influence x Projection x Discharge | Direct the outward release of the Character's capability. | Strong extraordinary fit. The permission-bearing Lens supplies the bolt, not Task Rank. |
| Place an eye beam through a small opening. | Mobility x Coordination x Discharge, when precise alignment supplies the attempt | Precisely direct an already permitted output. | A power does not automatically force a Projection route or require Coordination and Projection together. |
| Direct a hose stream into a difficult opening. | Mobility x Coordination x Discharge | Place an outgoing stream without making an attack. | Strong noncombat fit. Pressure, reach, and supply remain properties of the actual equipment and circumstances. |
| Maintain a permitted energy stream against a moving target. | Influence x Projection x Discharge, for a stipulated power-output-led attempt | Sustain the directed outward flow. | The Task need not become a different purchase merely because the release continues. Existing duration and action rules still apply. |
| Send an electrical pulse into a compatible receiver. | Influence x Projection x Discharge, given the stipulated capability | Deliver an output for a constructive purpose. | Damage is not part of the Task's definition. This does not grant compatibility or electrical power. |
| Shoot an arrow from a bow. | Mobility x Coordination x Discharge, for this placement-led attempt | Manage the release and direction of a launched projectile. | The activity fits. The name is less idiomatic here than Shooting; retain an explicit bow example if adopted. |

These routes identify the capability doing the work in each stipulated scene.
They are not rules that all gunshots use Coordination or all bolts use
Projection. Other routes remain subject to the ordinary applicability rule.

## Throwing Boundary

| Comparison | Finding |
|---|---|
| Hurl an ordinary stone by hand. | Throwing is the clearer activity. The Character performs the throw rather than directing a separate emission. |
| Conjure a solid projectile and then physically hurl it. | Throwing remains the clearer activity. Extraordinary origin does not relabel the throw. Nor does this sentence automatically require separate creation and attack rolls. |
| Emit a bolt directly from the palm. | Discharge is the clearer activity. A hand gesture need not mean the Character is physically throwing an object. |
| Throw an object through a permitted telekinetic extension. | Throwing can remain applicable. Personal muscular propulsion is not a mandatory boundary, and supernatural permission does not automatically mean Discharge. |
| Launch a stone using a sling. | Discharge. User-confirmed application; the sling releases the outgoing projectile. Its preparatory motion does not require a separate Throwing contribution or a Sling Task. |

The distinction is not object versus energy, body versus equipment, or
ordinary versus extraordinary. The actual activity matters. Hurling a stone
and discharging it through a sling remain distinct applications in this
working map. Neither requires multiplying Discharge and Throwing together.

The initial assistant test treated the sling as an unresolved overlap. The
user explicitly corrected that finding: sling use is clearly Discharge.
This resolves the example without adding a special rule.

## Boundaries With Other Tasks And Layers

### Strike And Target

Hitting a sword with another sword to disarm its bearer remains a Strike
candidate, not Discharge merely because the sword is the target. Shooting an
object from someone's hand can instead be Discharge. The intended effect
does not determine the activity, and this test adds no called-shot procedure.

Target describes selecting or directing toward a mark more broadly. Discharge
does not absorb Strike or Throwing merely because all three can be aimed.
There is no additional Target multiplier or compulsory aiming roll.

### Convey And Projection

An intelligible speech uses Convey's message-carrying activity. It does not
become Discharge just because speech physically emits sound. A stipulated
sonic pulse used as force can instead fit Discharge: the trained contribution
is controlling an output, not composing or conveying meaning.

Projection remains the Derived Attribute for deliberate outward transmission
of internal content or capability. Discharge is one practiced activity that
can use it, and can also use other Derived Attributes. The Task neither
replaces Projection nor encompasses every one of its applications.

### Creation And Effects At A Distance

Forming a wall at a distant location, igniting something in place, or holding
an object remotely is not automatically Discharge. Establish what actually
happens. Range and extraordinary Access do not demonstrate outgoing release.
This test does not assign those cases to new purchases.

Creating a projectile as an incidental part of an already permitted attack
does not automatically create a separate Creation Task or roll. Actual
compound contributions remain governed by the existing compound rules.

### Mere Activation

An obvious button press or ordinary emptying of a container does not require
a Discharge roll simply because the verb can describe it. Resolve the
meaningfully uncertain activity; preserve ordinary automatic actions.

## Training Transfer And Breadth

A broadly trained Discharge Character can receive the Task multiplier across
applicable weapon, device, and personal-output uses. Relevant Fields and
Specialties supply additional expertise. Their absence does not automatically
close an otherwise available ordinary attempt, and they must not be made
mandatory simply to conceal the breadth of the Task.

For a familiar handgun Expression, replacing the Shooting label with
Discharge at the same Rank leaves the numerical stack unchanged. The issue
being tested is which additional Expressions can legitimately use that Rank,
not whether the new name increases a particular multiplier.

The generalist gains broad directed-release competence, not mastery of every
weapon's repair, every power's possible manifestations, or every use of a
device. Rifle repair still calls for a different activity even if Firearms
expertise can travel with it. A fire barrier does not become an application
of Discharge solely because the same HOLT permits a bolt.

Finding: the scope is more bounded than Attack, Operate, or unrestricted
Target. It has recurring ordinary and extraordinary applications without
requiring separate rules by source. Relative point value remains unproven
until the broader Task landscape is compared; this test changes no budget.

## Recommendation And Remaining Decision

Use Discharge as the leading candidate replacement for Shooting, not as an
additional required Task beside it. Retain Throwing as a separate candidate
scope and show the ordinary-stone / conjured-and-hurled-projectile comparison
to prevent magical source from deciding the Task.

The core examples pass the conceptual scope test, with the sling application
subsequently confirmed by the user. The remaining wording observation is that
bow use fits the proposed activity better than the ordinary phrase
"discharge a bow" fits everyday speech. This is not a separate mechanical
problem and requires no subsystem or list of exceptions.
The user decision is whether the name and directed-release scope are strong
enough to carry forward as the working replacement for Shooting. Final
purchase balance and a final Task catalogue are outside this test.
