# Declared Duration And Successful Output Comparison v0.1

Date: 2026-09-12

Status: user-authorized comparison complete. Recommendations are not adopted.
No universal time ladder, Effects-to-time conversion, or duration price is set.
This is a scenario and accounting comparison, not a numerical balance test.

## Question

Compare resolving an effect for its declared duration with spending additional
Effects to extend that duration. Preserve the actual means, Access, sustaining
activity, exposure scope, opposition, and Effect allocation already established.

## Existing Rules Used

- The actual declared activity defines the resolution scope.
- Ordinary uncertainty and Difficulty concern the challenge being attempted;
  a routine permitted activity need not roll.
- Active sustaining uses ordinary action accounting; mere persistence or
  dependency does not automatically consume an action.
- Real interruption and loss of necessary means can prevent continuation.
  Success does not cancel future changes in circumstances.
- Compatible contributions add; different functions retain their identity.
  Effects assigned to one improvement are not also available for another.
- Hazardous exposure can require further applications beyond a prior resolved
  scope, without automatic harm each round or once-per-target immunity.

## Model Comparison

### Declared Duration Alone

The intended duration forms part of what is being attempted. If successful,
the Expression accomplishes that duration while its necessary means remain.
Do not add a second duration roll or Effect charge solely to obtain the span
already included in the successfully resolved objective.

This gives a clear success condition. Its limitation as an exclusive model is
that it leaves no duration improvement from exceptional execution even when
the action could actually improve persistence.

### Additional Effects As Universal Time Purchases

Every effect receives some starting duration and each additional Effect buys
time. Without a conversion, this is not numerically executable. More importantly,
as a universal rule it can imply that good activation extends a fixed timer,
that continuing an already available sustained action needs an additional
Effect payment, or that enough output removes a real sustaining dependency.

Reject as a universal requirement. Access and the actual controllable property
must first support duration improvement.

### Declared Duration With Applicable Surplus Improvement

Recommend declared duration as the default resolved objective. Permit additional
Effects to improve duration where the actual Expression can do so, using an
explicit valuation agreed before the roll or already supplied by the relevant
capability or Campaign Profile. The extension amount is not invented after
seeing how many Effects happened to be rolled.

This is not a separate subsystem for each means. The same applicability question
determines whether duration is an available output improvement, just as other
contributions require a relevant fictional relationship.

## Cases

| Case | Declared-duration result | Role of additional Effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activate a device with a fixed operating timer | It operates for the established span, subject to its ordinary requirements | Good activation does not extend that fixed timer; redesign or actual efficiency control would be different work |
| Hold a gate while the group crosses | Resolve the agreed holding challenge; provide its required ongoing activity | No extra Effect payment is automatically required to keep holding where the means already allow it; longer or harder work can create new uncertainty |
| Create a temporary ward whose persistence the character can influence | Assess the requested duration as part of the actual challenge; successful output supplies that span | Surplus may extend persistence at a previously established, applicable valuation |
| Produce a brief cold burst with no means to leave a continuing hazard | Resolve the burst | Surplus does not manufacture lingering-hazard Access |
| Released mist normally persists while local conditions support it | Its real persistence and the declared objective govern; no universal timer is imposed | Manipulating its stability could improve duration if that was an actual permitted contribution; bare extra harm output does not automatically do so |
| A collaborator specifically contributes stabilization or persistence | Their successful contribution answers its declared duration function and dependencies | Its additional Effects may improve duration under the same agreed valuation, not automatically become the other contributor's harm |
| A power supply fails or a successful counter removes the sustaining means | Apply the actual disruption and preserve viable residuals | Purchased extension is not immunity to interruption or restoration of missing Access |

The held-gate example does not waive the ordinary action cost. Success for the
crossing is not success at every future duration, and agreeing a longer span
does not make an unsupported feat possible. Nor does the expiration of a
resolved scope automatically force a roll when continuation remains routine.

## How Duration Enters The Challenge

Establish the intended span before resolution in the language that makes the
objective clear: a concrete interval, a crossing, or a defined operation can
serve where appropriate. Narrative wording is not permission to conceal an
arbitrarily long requirement; the scope must be understood before the roll.

Longer duration changes Difficulty or relevant Lenses when it actually makes
the attempted result harder: sustained strain, unstable conditions, maintaining
coherence, limited reserves, or similar relevant facts. Duration can instead
meet an Access limit or be routine within the means. Merely lasting longer is
not an automatic Difficulty increase for every kind of effect.

Do not assess the same obstacle twice under different names. Distinct burdens
still matter: maintaining a demanding effect can have both genuine performance
uncertainty and an action commitment. Accounting for one does not erase the other.

No table mapping seconds or minutes to Difficulty is adopted in this comparison.

## Output Accounting

If duration is already part of the declared successful objective, that success
does not need a second generic duration payment. This is not a new free baseline
Effect for each declared function and does not replace existing area or other
output-allocation procedures.

As a ledger illustration, a successful duration contribution with four Effects
can have its successful baseline and three additional Effects. If two of those
additional Effects are legitimately assigned to a valued extension, only one
additional Effect remains for another applicable improvement. The two extension
Effects are not automatically two rounds, two minutes, or two extra attacks.
The extension's actual amount must already be established to use that allocation.

Longer duration does not increase resolved intensity, waive necessary sustaining
actions, or automatically inflict damage once per time unit. Any further harm
uses the approved exposure scope. If no duration improvement is applicable or
valued, there is no established time purchase merely because Effects remain.

## Short Declaration Versus Extension

The proposed applicability and pre-roll valuation requirements prevent a
post-roll invention of whatever extension is convenient. They do not prove
that extension will be numerically balanced against declaring a longer span.
That comparison requires a concrete extension valuation and duration-sensitive
challenge. The prior area tests show why this relationship matters, but their
radius conversion must not be imported into time without a separate decision.

No duration-specific ceiling or prohibition on crossing a Difficulty boundary
is inferred. This comparison establishes the recommended procedure, not a
universal mathematical solution to extension pricing.

## Recommendation And Next Decision

Use declared duration as part of the resolved objective. Successful output
accomplishes that span while the means remain; routine continuation remains
routine and substantial active support retains its ordinary action cost.
Additional Effects may improve duration only when that is a permitted,
relevant output with an explicit pre-roll valuation. Do not adopt a universal
Effects-to-time conversion.

The substantive decision is this default-and-extension procedure. If accepted,
universal duration arithmetic need not block returning to the final area-growth
choice and then the queued advancement, HOLT pricing, and award review. Specific
time valuations can remain tied to the concrete capabilities that require them.
