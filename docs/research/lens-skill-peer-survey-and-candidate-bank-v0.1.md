# LENS Skill Peer Survey and Candidate Bank

Version: 0.1  
Status: Candidate Accumulation  
Purpose: Build a broad, system-neutral bank of possible LENS Skills by
comparing peer role-playing games and then applying LENS's existing layer
boundaries.

Nothing in this document is an adopted standard Skill list.

Current post-survey construction direction:

```text
15 Core Skills
+ 5 Campaign Profile Skills
= 20 Active Skills
```

The thirty candidates in this research record are retained as a source bank,
not as the current active Skill list.

---

## Current LENS Controls

The current working direction is:

```text
LENS provides a standard Skill list.

Settings and players may adapt that list within a common scope standard.

A Skill is a learned field of action.

A Specialization is a narrower mastered application of a parent Skill.

A profession or character identity is normally a stable Lens rather than a
Skill.

A Skill may apply through several Derived Attributes across its different
uses.
```

The current Skill equivalence questions are:

- Does the candidate represent learned capability?
- Does it support several related recurring actions?
- Can it route through several Derived Attributes across its uses?
- Can it support several recognizable Specializations?
- Is it comparable in breadth to its peer Skills?
- Does it avoid replacing an Attribute, Derived Attribute, permission, identity,
  resource, or equipment Lens?

### GURPS Basic Set, Fourth Edition Revised

Local primary source:

```text
C:\ROLE_PLAYING GAMES\GURPS\GURPS_Basic_Set_Fourth_Edition_Revised.pdf
```

GURPS supplies the maximal-catalogue control for this survey.

Its Basic Set separates a very large number of learned practices, including:

- individual weapon families;
- individual athletic practices;
- sciences and academic disciplines;
- crafts and technical professions;
- vehicle classes and crew functions;
- investigative methods;
- social methods;
- artistic practices;
- medical fields;
- survival environments;
- organizational practices;
- supernatural and cinematic practices.

GURPS also distinguishes several structural tools:

```text
Controlling Attribute
The Attribute normally used to calculate the Skill.

Using Skills With Other Attributes
The same Skill may be rolled through another Attribute when the task calls for
a different capability.

Defaults
Some untrained uses remain possible through an Attribute or neighboring Skill.

Required and Optional Specialties
Some fields must or may be narrowed to a particular subject.

Grouped Skills
One heading may collect mechanically related but independently purchased
Skills.

Familiarity
Training in a Skill does not automatically remove unfamiliarity with every
piece of equipment in its field.

Techniques
Narrow applications may be improved from a parent Skill.

Wildcard Skills
Several ordinary Skills may be compressed into one cinematic broad Skill at a
higher cost.
```

The strongest direct validation for LENS is GURPS's explicit use of one Skill
with different Attributes. Its examples include using a repair Skill through
physical capability when manipulating components and using a combat or vehicle
Skill through cognition when recalling procedures or analyzing action.

That supports the LENS direction:

```text
Skill
= learned method

Derived Attribute
= capability used to perform that method in the current Expression
```

The strongest caution is breadth.

GURPS charges extra for wildcard Skills because one broad purchase replaces
many narrower Skills. LENS currently gives every Skill Rank the same Training
cost. LENS must therefore make its standard Skill families reasonably
equivalent in scope rather than mixing a wildcard-sized Skill with a narrowly
defined practice at the same price.

GURPS also exposes several candidates that the first peer pass did not capture
cleanly:

```text
Administration
Teaching
Strategy
Protocol or formal social procedure
Meditative or trained self-regulation practice
```

It also supplies useful controls that do not automatically become new LENS
Skills:

```text
Area Knowledge
May be stable location familiarity combined with Research, Scholarship,
Streetwise, Survival, or another applicable Skill.

Current Affairs
May be current Research rather than a separate method.

Professional Skill
May be a stable profession Lens expressed through several Skills.

Soldier and Crewman
May be setting-facing profession or operational Skills rather than universal
core entries.

Observation and Search
Must be tested against Awareness and Investigation rather than copied as
automatic Skills.

Body Language and Detect Lies
Must be tested against Insight plus applicable learned social or investigative
methods.
```

GURPS does not decide the LENS list. It provides the most detailed available
map of distinctions that LENS may preserve, combine, route through another
layer, or leave to a Campaign Profile.

## LENS Layer Filter

Peer placement does not determine LENS placement.

### Likely Derived Attributes Rather Than Core Skills

```text
Force or Physique
Stamina or Endurance
Mobility
Finesse
Perception, Notice, or Observation
Insight when it means general interpretation
Empathy when it means general relational sensitivity
Will
Evasion or Dodge when it means raw responsiveness
```

LENS already represents these through Might, Mobility, Psyche, Influence, and
their twenty Derived Attributes. Learned methods related to them may still
become Skills or Specializations.

Examples:

```text
Awareness
= Derived Attribute

Investigation
= possible learned Skill using Awareness, Cognition, Insight, or another
applicable Derived Attribute

Resolve
= Derived Attribute

Meditation or Interrogation Resistance
= possible Skill or Specialization when learned training genuinely matters
```

### Likely Stable Lenses Rather Than Core Skills

```text
Profession
Character identity
Contacts
Resources or Wealth
Status
Reputation
Institutional membership
Legal authority
```

These may grant recognition, relationship, resources, obligations, or Access.
Associated learned performance still uses Skills.

### Likely Permission-Bearing Lenses or Setting Skills

```text
Attune
Arcana
Religion
Occultism
Spellcasting
Psionics
Hacking in settings where network Access is restricted
```

Knowledge about these subjects may be an ordinary Skill. Performing an
extraordinary act still requires Classification, HOLT, equipment, Access, or
another permission-bearing Lens.

## First Normalized Candidate Bank

These candidates are deliberately broader than a final recommendation.

### Physical and Field Skills

```text
Athletics
Acrobatics
Stealth
Survival
Animal Handling
Navigation
Vehicle Operation
```

Questions:

- Does Acrobatics remain a peer Skill, or become an Athletics Specialization
  expressed through Maneuverability, Stability, and Coordination?
- Does Vehicle Operation cover land, sea, air, and space with vehicle types as
  Specializations?
- Does Riding belong beneath Animal Handling, Vehicle Operation, or remain a
  setting-facing Skill?

### Combat Skills

```text
Unarmed Combat
Melee
Ranged Combat
Tactics
Demolitions
```

Questions:

- Are Unarmed Combat, Melee, and Ranged Combat equivalent in breadth?
- Are particular weapons always Specializations?
- Is Demolitions a combat field, technical field, or setting Skill?
- Does active defense use the applicable combat Skill rather than a separate
  Dodge or Evasion Skill?

### Investigative and Knowledge Skills

```text
Investigation
Research
Scholarship
Medicine
Languages
```

Questions:

- Are Investigation and Research distinct peer Skills or different methods
  within one Skill?
- Is Scholarship too broad if History, Law, Science, Religion, and Culture are
  Specializations beneath it?
- Is Language a ranked Skill, an Access state, or a campaign-specific module?
- Does Streetwise describe learned navigation of informal social systems, or
  does it duplicate stable contacts and local identity?

### Technical and Creative Skills

```text
Craft
Engineering
Technology
Security
Performance
Art
```

Questions:

- Are Craft, Engineering, and Technology three equivalent fields or a broad
  Skill with setting-specific Specializations?
- Does Security coherently include locks, alarms, surveillance, intrusion, and
  countermeasures without becoming a profession package?
- Is Art distinct from Performance, or should artistic media become
  Specializations beneath one Creative Practice Skill?

### Social Skills

```text
Persuasion
Deception
Command
Intimidation
Commerce
Streetwise
```

Questions:

- Are Persuasion, Command, and Intimidation distinct learned methods with
  comparable breadth?
- Does ordinary rapport use social Skills, Connection, or both when the Skill
  represents learned practice?
- Does Commerce cover bargaining, trade, appraisal, and business practice, or
  should some of those become Specializations?

## Strongest Initial Findings

1. The earlier possibility of a standard list between twelve and eighteen is
   only a comparison point. The GURPS maximal-catalogue control demonstrates
   that no target count should be selected before the boundary tests are
   complete.

2. LENS probably needs fewer inherent-capability Skills than many peers because
   its Derived Attribute layer already handles perception, endurance, force,
   bodily control, resolve, and general social sensitivity.

3. The core list should concentrate on learned fields that can route through
   several Derived Attributes.

4. Settings should be able to add or replace Skills when a recurring activity
   deserves more mechanical resolution. Cyberpunk technology and fantasy
   magical traditions are leading examples.

5. A profession can remain a stable Lens while its work is resolved through
   one or more Skills. `Doctor`, `Soldier`, and `Engineer` do not need to become
   universal umbrella Skills.

6. The next useful pass is not numerical. It is to test each normalized
   candidate for recurring uses, Derived Attribute routing, available
   Specializations, and peer-level coverage.

7. GURPS directly validates routing one learned Skill through different
   Attributes while warning that broad and narrow Skills cannot receive equal
   cost without a deliberate scope standard.

8. Administration and Teaching are the strongest omissions exposed by the
   GURPS pass.

9. The subsequent Skill Boundary Test provisionally retained Tactics and
   Strategy as peer Skills, separated Protocol from Access, and separated
   learned Meditation from the Resolve Derived Attribute. Those are test
   results, not adopted doctrine.
