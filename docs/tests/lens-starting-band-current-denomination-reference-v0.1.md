## Reading the Comparison

Preserving the accepted first 125 for ordinary development, a Profile could
make the next 125 eligible for HOLT/Axis or ordinary purchases. At that same
larger starting amount, a more grounded Profile could keep it ordinary-only.
The later-eligibility rows compare larger ordinary reservations, not new costs.

Previously reserved funding does not become HOLT-eligible retroactively. These
are starting-profile alternatives, not levels or automatic advancement unlocks.
Extra funding alone does not specify permitted Themes, mandatory shared
provisions, Scope, Scale, or number of powers.
