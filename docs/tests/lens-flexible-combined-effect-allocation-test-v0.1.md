# Flexible Allocation Within A Combined Contribution

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: user-authorized worked test. No new growth, Condition-price, duration,
or distance conversion is adopted. Follow Narrative Possibilities Before
Mechanical Categories in AGENTS.md.

## Question And Fiction

Two characters coordinate a wave carrying stinging aquatic creatures into a
group of guards. This neutral example continues the user's supplied wave-and-
creatures illustration without importing setting canon.

The wave-maker says, "I will carry them into the guards with a wave." That
states the immediate purpose; it does not turn the entire contribution into
an area-only or delivery-only purchase. A forceful wave can strike, sweep,
and throw spray into faces as well as carry something. The partner supplies
the creatures and their stinging contact.

For this fixture the wave-maker has the necessary permission and means to
produce and direct the wave. The partner has the necessary creature-related
permission; the creatures need this wave to reach these targets at this time.
Neither water nor creatures are assumed automatically available in every scene.
The water is not freezing and has no special temperature-changing permission
in this fixture. That limits this instance, not every possible wave Lens.

The declared combined scope, feasibility, coordinated timing, and applicable
Difficulty are resolved before rolling. No exhaustive list of every supported
refinement is required before the roll: elaboration may identify another real
use of this same contribution. A genuinely new independent activity still uses
its own normal action accounting and required construction.

## Rolls And Existing Successful Scope

Use controlled complete Final Ratings, not a new character build:

| Contributor | Final Rating | Ordinary roll | Margin | Generated Effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wave-maker | 96 | 43 | 53 | 6 |
| Creature-handler | 78 | 43 | 35 | 4 |

Each has committed one actual action. Ratings stand for completed Expressions
after applicable factors and final flooring; they are deliberately supplied
test inputs, not assertions about default difficulty or a particular build.
There is no extra coordination roll or second roll to make the wave travel.

The successful wave establishes the declared footprint without an area-entry
charge. Its travel and carrying function are inherent in the manifested wave,
like the travel of an ordinary projectile is inherent in its attack. This does
not add a delivery Effect on top of the six, nor require purchasing delivery
again out of those six merely because the wave also hits.

The rule for a separately resolved delivery contribution is not abolished.
Its necessary surviving output and means still matter. In this example the
wave itself is the carrying means, and disrupting that means changes what can
reach the guards. Six Effects are the entire wave output budget, not six for
impact plus a second free budget for another function.

## One Supported Allocation

| Source | Effects | Application and explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wave | 3 | Impact: the mass of water strikes the guards. |
| Wave | 2 | Sweeping force: the surge destabilizes their footing. |
| Wave | 1 | Obscuring spray: water in their faces interferes with sight. |
| Creatures | 4 | Stinging contact through creatures carried into the occupied area. |

Total: six wave Effects plus four creature Effects. No ninth, eleventh, or
extra success bonus appears. The initial successful Effect is already included.

Sweeping and obscuring are supported outcome allocations. The fixture assumes
those Effects can produce matching Condition consequences for the exposed
guards. It does not establish a universal rule that one Effect fully blinds
any target or that two Effects always move someone a particular distance.
Use applicable existing Condition treatment, actual susceptibility, opposition,
and protection. This test does not price selective shaping.

## Alternatives Using The Same Six Wave Effects

- Six impact Effects: use the successful output to make the wave hit harder.
- Four impact and two sweeping Effects: emphasize impact and loss of footing.
- Three impact, two sweeping, and one obscuring Effect: the worked allocation.
- Three impact, one obscuring, and two area-growth Effects: devote part of
  the output to spreading wider, subject to whatever growth conversion is
  adopted. Under the tested 20% candidate those two would add 40% of original
  coverage, but neither that percentage nor any expansion ceiling is adopted
  by this allocation test.

The wave can support these applications despite its first description as
delivery. Six impact plus two sweeping plus one obscuring from the same six
Effects is not valid: that is nine expenditures from six, not creative routing.
Nor does calling this fixture's water "freezing" create missing temperature
permission or actual cold after the roll. Conversely, an appropriately cold
or temperature-capable wave might legitimately support a chilling outcome.

Distance, duration, and more intricate shaping can likewise be legitimate
directions for supported output; their numerical conversions remain separate
questions. Do not infer that the absence of a conversion makes them inherently
unavailable, or that a narrative description supplies a free arbitrary amount.

## Opposition And Protection

This is one genuinely coordinated incoming event, not two unrelated attacks
declared simultaneous after rolling. Each guard resolves the feasible response
they actually make, within its scope. Use one cancellation pool for that event,
not a full copy against each participant.

For one guard, assume the declared response knocks the creatures away and
earns two matching defensive Effects. Those cancel two of the four stinging
Effects. A distinct, already-defined protection then mitigates one physical
impact Effect. It is not the equipment multiplier being reapplied after the roll.

Remaining for that guard:

- Two impact Effects.
- Two sweeping Effects.
- One obscuring Effect.
- Two stinging Effects.

Ten incoming applications = two cancelled + one mitigated + seven remaining.
This is a trace of distinct outcomes, not seven interchangeable damage points.
Protection against stings, footing loss, or obscured sight would be assessed
according to its actual scope; physical impact protection does not automatically
cancel all of them. Another exposed guard uses their own feasible opposition
and protection without consuming this guard's or the whole area's output.

## Failure And Disruption

| Change | What remains |
| --- | --- |
| The creature-handler fails | The successful wave retains its six supported Effects. It can still strike, sweep, and obscure; it was not only a carrier. |
| The wave fails to form or reach the guards | In this fixture the creatures cannot make the intended contact there. Their successful contribution may remain locally useful, not vanish or automatically receive a new remote attack. |
| An intervention stops the wave before arrival | Reassess both wave applications and dependent creature delivery. It is not merely cancellation of an abstract delivery label while all dependent harm arrives unchanged. |
| A guard avoids only the stinging contact | The wave's other effects remain to the extent the guard is exposed to them. |

These dependencies follow actual means and timing, not an imposed requirement
that both participants must succeed for any result to survive. If the creatures
had another viable route already established, reassess that different fiction.

## Wind-And-Fire Cross-Check

Initial purpose: "My wind spreads your fire." Fire earns four Effects and the
wind earns three (controlled Final Rating 72, roll43, margin29).

Suppose actual fuel and combustion support the wind feeding the fire, and
actual smoke or embers support interference with sight. A supported allocation
of the wind's three Effects is one to area growth, one to enhancing Burning,
and one to obscuring sight. The resulting allocation is five Burning, one
area-growth expenditure, and one obscuring expenditure: seven Effects from
four plus three, not nine. Applicable growth and Condition valuations govern.

The wind did not become a second fire-generating Domain. Its actual interaction
with the existing fire supports the enhancement. The relevant fuel, flame,
smoke, and ember dependencies remain. If the fire never manifests and there
are no other such sources here, those particular applications lose their
necessary means. The wind may still have independently viable wind effects;
that is assessed, not forbidden by its initial area-helping role.

If the wind can only transport this particular manifestation without feeding
it, a Burning enhancement needs another actual explanation or capability.
Do not generalize that narrower case into a prohibition applying to all wind.

## Result And Recommended Wording

Flexible allocation works in these examples without duplicating output or
requiring a new universal control roll. The restriction belongs to what the
actual contribution can support, not the first label used to describe its job.

Proposed core clarification:

> A contribution's initially stated purpose does not restrict all of its
> remaining Effects to that purpose. Remaining Effects may enhance any aspect
> of the combined result that its actual nature, means, and applicable Lenses
> narratively and mechanically support. Explain that contribution and allocate
> each Effect once, preserving relevant dependencies, opposition, and protection.

This clarifies the difference between initial allocation and converting an
Effect already applied or committed elsewhere. It does not authorize changing
the entire attempted action, importing unrelated permissions, or claiming free
duration, distance, growth, or selective exclusion after seeing a roll.

The high-output area-growth comparison remains arithmetically valid: it already
counted remaining strength after expansion. Its narrow area-only warnings must
not become a general inability to allocate supported output. The containment
experiments remain unadopted and are not required to resolve this allocation
question. A volatile-power limitation remains a separate possible application.
