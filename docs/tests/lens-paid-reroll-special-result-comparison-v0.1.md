# Paid Reroll and Special Results

Status: Completed decision comparison. The user adopted full replacement for
special results and consequence timing, preserving one paid reroll per
Expression. The baseline and terminology reference now record that decision.
Alternatives and pre-decision reasoning below remain historical comparisons.

## Recovered Earlier Proposal

A subsequent source review recovered an explicit proposal in
`../system/lens-system-working-reference-v0.2.md`, under Percentile Edge Rules
(section begins at line 1104; edge outcomes at line 1195). Its status is
WORKING DRAFT, not a newly adopted rule:

- Natural 99: automatic failure regardless of Final Resonance.
- Natural 00 (100): automatic failure with a meaningful Complication arising
  from the attempted Expression and moving the fiction forward.
- Examples include equipment damage, temporary Dissonance, new opposition,
  lost opportunity, worse position, consumed resources, or environmental change.
- If no meaningful complication is available, treat 00 as ordinary failure.

The newer baseline leaves the exact complication procedure unresolved. Thus
there is an earlier substantive proposal, not an absence of prior design.
The initial comparison below did not recover this passage and should not be
read as evidence that no definitions had previously been drafted.

The conversation's repeated-01/repeated-00 idea was deferred. This source sweep
did not locate a settled sequential-result procedure in the project's Markdown
records. Do not turn recollection of that idea into mandatory extra rolls,
an automatic catastrophe, or a new exceptional-success rule.

Recommended next decision: carry forward the recovered 99/00 distinction as
the working outcome rule, with its existing no-meaningful-complication fallback.
Use the adopted paid-reroll timing before consequences: only the retained result
supplies the outcome. A complication is not automatically a fixed Load award,
permanent injury, equipment destruction, or terminal defeat. Its form must fit
the actual Expression and use existing mechanisms where relevant. Severity
details and the separate sequential-result idea are not decided by recovery
of this draft. Baseline adoption awaits the user's choice.

## Existing Authority at the Time of Comparison

The baseline reserves 99 and 100 around the ordinary certainty boundary but
leaves the exact complication procedure unresolved. Do not label these faces
automatic catastrophes or invent an exceptional-success face. Earlier repeat-face
or sequential exceptional-result ideas remain outside this comparison.

The adopted paid reroll replaces the original result even if worse, preserves
the purchased boost, costs one point, and is permitted at most once per
Expression. The open question is whether special results qualify for that
replacement and whether their attached consequences persist.

## Candidates

| Treatment | First result | Replacement | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full replacement | Any eligible initial result, including a special result | New result governs, including any special result of its own | Luck can rescue an extreme result, but also produce one. No mixed outcome to track. |
| Special results locked | A special initial result cannot be rerolled | Ordinary results can still be replaced | Preserves initial exceptional events regardless of the player's available points. Requires a special-result exclusion. |
| Retain initial special consequence | Numerical outcome may be rerolled, but the original special consequence survives | New ordinary outcome can coexist with the old consequence | Splits the resolved outcome across two rolls and requires a rule for two special consequences if the replacement is also special. |

Recommend full replacement. It best preserves the existing replacement rule
and the user's luck/determination rationale. The reservation is intentional:
when points are available, an initial adverse special result becomes escapable.
If the design goal is instead that special results are irrevocable, the locked
candidate is coherent but represents a different experience, not a mathematical
necessity. Do not introduce the mixed-result candidate merely as a compromise.

## Edge Cases

- Adverse special first result, ordinary successful replacement: use the new
  result; do not retain the discarded roll's attached special consequence.
- Ordinary first result, adverse special replacement: the new special result
  stands; the paid reroll has been used.
- Favorable special first result voluntarily replaced: its benefits are lost;
  the player cannot retain the favorable parts of the discarded roll.
- Special followed by special: apply the replacement result, not both results.
- A Condition or event established before this Expression: unaffected. Replacing
  a die result does not rewrite earlier scenes or independently existing facts.

If adopted, choose whether to reroll after seeing the result but before applying
its consequences to the fiction. This is a proposed resolution boundary, not
an opportunity to wait for subsequent actions and then revise history.

## Existing Numerical Evidence

The exhaustive prior boost/reroll test already brackets allowing and excluding
initial 99/100. At Rating 60, failure-only reroll gives 84% ordinary success
when all failed initial faces are eligible, versus 82.8% when 99/100 are locked.
With the tested x1.20 boost, the corresponding figures are 92.16% and 90.72%.

These figures use the existing test convention, not a finalized special-face
rule. They describe ordinary success, not the frequency or severity of actual
catastrophes. No additional pricing pass is needed: the meaningful distinction
is whether a special initial result can be escaped and whether replacement
remains complete.

## Decision Boundary

Recommend allowing special results to be replaced and allowing special results
on the replacement to take full effect, with the existing one-paid-reroll limit.
Leave definitions of special outcomes and any sequential-result procedure open.
The user approved this treatment. Choose after seeing the roll and before
applying its consequences. The baseline now records full replacement; no
special-result definitions or sequential-result procedure were adopted.
