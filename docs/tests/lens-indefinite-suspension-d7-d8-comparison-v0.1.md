# Indefinite Suspension: D7 And D8

Date: 2026-09-15
Status: exploratory comparison; neither placement adopted.

Disposition, 2026-09-15: after reviewing D10's one-billion-increment reference,
the user concluded that a separate permanence rule is not needed in this form.
The universal-band proposal is closed without adopting D7, D8, or a substitute.
The analysis below is historical, not the active next decision. A finite result
does not become literally permanent, and natural lasting consequences are not
charged for continued extraordinary support they do not need.

## Appropriate Fiction

Following the user's confirmed distinction, compare a permitted power that
leaves a stone suspended without active maintenance or inherent expiration.
Do not compare stable reshaped terrain as if the ground required ongoing
extraordinary support. Do not treat the lifetime of a binding as necessarily
the lifetime of the creature it binds. These application examples are now in
the baseline and terminology; their acceptance does not adopt a numerical band.

The suspension remains susceptible to actual disruption. Permission, strength,
area, distance, and countermeasures are held equal between the two candidates.
No supporting artifact, new limitation, perpetual energy source, or substitute
maintenance procedure is invented to justify a price.

## Placement Concern

The core finite ladder still contains D9 and D10. A uniform indefinite D7 or
D8 would therefore be easier than some finite declarations of the SAME
suspension, with other requirements and dependencies held equal. At raw 640,
indefinite D8 would give Rating 17 while finite D9 gives 10 and finite D10 gives 6.
This issue does not depend on what a Profile's increment represents.

No-expiration is not always more desirable in every story; an automatic ending
can itself matter. The finding is not that permanent outcomes must universally
cost more than temporary ones. It is that D7/D8 cannot be treated as the top of
the existing finite progression while retaining its higher finite bands.
If a different way of establishing the result genuinely changes the challenge,
assess that actual difference rather than inventing it to fix the table.

## Recommendation

Do not adopt either as a universal indefinite-duration component on this evidence.
First decide whether no-expiration is meant to be a separately assessed property
of the permitted manifestation, or a duration endpoint that should remain harder
than its finite counterparts. Those are different design commitments. There is
no justification here for adding a new pool, rank, maintenance tax, or D11 rule.
The suspended-stone case exposes the decision without reopening natural
continuation or the settled surplus conversions.
