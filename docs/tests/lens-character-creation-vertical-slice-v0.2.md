# LENS Character Creation Vertical Slice

Version: 0.2  
Status: Working Construction Test  
Purpose: Build one ordinary Scale 1 human through the current LENS architecture
after adoption of the fifteen-plus-five Active Skill structure.

This is not a final character, Campaign Profile, equipment catalogue,
advancement procedure, or completed character-creation chapter.

Version 0.1 remains the earlier control. This version does not overwrite it.

---

## Test Question

```text
Can the current LENS components produce a complete, playable ordinary
Character without inventing missing numerical rules during construction?
```

The test must distinguish:

```text
what can already be constructed;

what can already be calculated;

what can be recorded without a numerical value;

and what still requires a design decision.
```

## Step 6: Languages

The Language Skill is recorded at Rank 2.

The Character concept establishes ordinary fluency in the campaign's local
language. The exact procedure for recording additional known languages remains
unresolved.

Current application control:


## Step 7: Stable Character Lenses

The concept suggests two stable Lenses.

### Municipal Investigator

Possible functions:

```text
establishes professional identity;

may establish institutional Access when the Character's authority is
recognized;

may create Resonance when official standing materially supports an
Expression;

may create vulnerability, obligation, or Dissonance when institutional duty
conflicts with the attempted action.
```

### Community Responsibility

Possible functions:

```text
may supply Resonance when protecting the community genuinely supports the
Expression;

may create obligation or Dissonance when the Character abandons or betrays
that responsibility;

may expose the Character to coercion through threatened people or places.
```

No Rank, construction cost, compensation, or selection limit currently exists
for stable personal Lenses of this kind.

The concepts can be recorded and applied through relevance, but their
character-creation economy remains incomplete.

## Earlier Skill Gaps Resolved by Current Architecture

Version 0.1 used provisional Skills such as:

```text
Vigilance
Local Law
First Aid
```

The current architecture no longer requires those terms as universal Skills.

```text
Vigilance
Ordinary immediate notice may use Awareness alone.
Deliberate evidence gathering may add Investigation.
A recurring watch practice may become a justified Specialization or
player-proposed Skill.

Local Law
Legal knowledge or procedure may use Learning with Administration, Protocol,
Research, or another justified Skill according to the Expression.
A campaign centered on legal practice may add a player-proposed Law Skill.

First Aid
Ordinary trained treatment uses Medicine.
First Aid may become a Medicine Specialization when narrower mastery matters.
```

This reduces catalogue growth without erasing the underlying actions.

## Test Result

```text
The current 20/40/60 construction pools and fifteen-plus-five Active Skill
structure successfully produce the ordinary Character's central capability
stack.

The basic numerical character is constructible.

The test does not yet produce a completely finished playable record without
working assumptions for equipment, movement, stable personal Lenses, language
recording, and advancement.
```

The remaining gaps are no longer caused by Skill breadth or Training Point
allocation.

## Exact Next Decision

The first unresolved dependency encountered after the completed numerical
capability stack is:

```text
How are stable personal Lenses selected, limited, and valued during character
creation?
```

This question should be answered before equipment budgets because identity,
profession, relationships, obligations, resources, and credentials may
determine what equipment and institutional Access a Character begins with.
