## Objective and Authority

The user requests that range Difficulty bands be determined. Compare readable
distance ladders against existing Expressions without changing the Difficulty
curve or inventing a team-specific range procedure.

Source rules:

- Baseline: Difficulty is evaluated within the acting Scale's natural frame;
  different resistance profiles may exist by setting. Scale does not merely
  multiply success against an otherwise universal objective ladder.
- `lens-concrete-equipment-placement-opposed-play-test-v0.1.md`, Range

- `lens-low-rating-build-trace-v0.1.md`: the guide's complete, applicable routes

  final floor. Context remains one net multiplier.

## Controlled Frame

These candidates describe a personal-delivery, Scale-one reference frame.
They are not universal meter assignments for every Scale or setting, nor do
they automatically make any action at a given distance uncertain. Routine
activities remain automatic where appropriate.

For each comparison hold target, circumstances, and applicable means constant.
Permission to attempt the distance is stipulated solely to inspect arithmetic.
A mathematical Rating is not permission for a revolver, thrown object, voice,
or extraordinary manifestation to reach any listed distance.

The three main candidates share an illustrative D1 upper boundary of ten meters.
A separate five-meter anchor sensitivity avoids treating that choice as proven.
All positive distances use the first inclusive upper bound containing them.
Contact actions are not assigned range Difficulty by this test. Distances
beyond a table's last entry are outside that candidate's mapping, not an
automatic success, failure, or Closed Access ruling.

## Anchor and Support Sensitivity

Halving C's distance boundaries gives a D1 upper boundary of five meters.
At 50 m this changes the signature route from Rating 63 to 38; at 100 m from
38 to 22. The first-band anchor is a consequential choice, not decoration.

With C and a distinct, applicable +0.50 Resonance contribution, signature
Rating 63 at 50 m becomes 95, and Rating 38 at 100 m becomes 57. This is a
stipulated sensitivity, not a price or valuation for any scope, weapon, or
preparation period. Ordinary equipment x1.20 was already included and is not
applied again. Two declared actions instead give signature Ratings 31 and 19
at those distances; other penalties remain independently applicable.

## Boundaries and Limitations

- Range remains objective within the selected frame; this comparison does not
  introduce a different distance ladder for each character or weapon.
- Available means and Lenses can establish reach and answer difficulty. Actual
  weapon/ability-specific support still needs concrete testing before final lock.
- Ten meters as D1 is a proposed anchor, not a previously accepted distance.
- The inclusive cutoffs create discontinuities. Moving just beyond 20 m changes
  this signature route from Rating 106 to 63 in C. That is the existing discrete
  Difficulty curve interacting with hard bands. No smoothing or extra exception
  was introduced to hide it; practical table use needs judgment on this tradeoff.
- A successful combined delivery still needs a permitted route. Range enters
  its Expression before rolling; Effects do not pay off Difficulty afterward.
- This comparison does not set target-size rules, range maxima, engagement
  horizons at other Scales, or universal distances for every setting.

## Recommendation and Decision

Carry C, with ten meters as its first upper bound, as the leading provisional
personal-range reference. Its simple values and less compressed progression
are preferable to borrowing x1.6 merely because it appears elsewhere in LENS.
Do not call it final or physically validated.

The next meaningful comparison is application through contrasting means:
throwing, a bow or firearm, and extraordinary delivery. Check permission,
preparation, and genuine range-support Lenses against this common reference;
do not change the ladder per means without explicitly revisiting the existing
objective-range doctrine. Also expose the practical effect of the band edges.
