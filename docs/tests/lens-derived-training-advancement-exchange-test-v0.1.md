# Derived / Training Advancement Exchange Test

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: Approved comparison. Flat layer costs and Derived = 20% of Attribute
are working test controls following the user's instruction to proceed. No final
prices, currency, awards, or Training-layer price differences adopted.

## Scope

Compare one Derived Rank increase with two, three, or five Training Rank
increases for equal expenditure. The exchange counts are candidates, not XP
denominations. Attribute improvement remains five times a Derived improvement
as the ratio control, without assigning the user's illustrative prices.

For isolation only, one Task, Field, Specialty, or Precision Rank has equal
Training cost. This is not a finding that their advancement costs should remain
equal. Use current Rank 10 horizon, parent caps on Derived Attributes, and
the saved concept-led builds without redistributing their starting points.

Source: lens-concept-led-25-50-100-rebuild-test-v0.1.json and its narrative
record. Hold other Expression factors constant. No Difficulty is introduced;
relative gains apply to the existing routes wherever the added factor applies.
They are Rating gains, not success-probability or Effect-output percentages.

## Guide: Focused Development Versus Coordination

Saved guide: Mobility 7, Coordination 6, Discharge 8, Firearms 8,
Handguns 6, Revolvers 8. Coordination 6 -> 7 is legal and increases applicable
Coordination-carried Expressions by 6.25%.

For each Training exchange, enumerate all nonnegative allocations among the
four existing firing layers, spending exactly that number of ranks and never
exceeding Rank 10. Maximize the signature Expression. Among numerically tied
allocations, prefer Task, then Field, then Specialty over Precision as a
declared selection preference, not a proof of universal breadth ordering.

| Exchange | Selected Training changes | Revolver gain | Other handgun gain | Other firearm gain |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 Training ranks | Handguns 6 -> 8 | 12.50% | 12.50% | 0% |
| 3 Training ranks | Handguns 6 -> 8; Discharge 8 -> 9 | 18.75% | 18.75% | 5.56% |
| 5 Training ranks | Handguns 6 -> 9; Discharge 8 -> 9; Firearms 8 -> 9 | 32.31% | 32.31% | 11.42% |

Training portfolios have no effect on an unrelated Coordination Expression,
such as manipulating a delicate object without applicable firearm expertise.
Coordination provides its 6.25% there as well as on the tested shots. Conversely,
the Training purchases can carry across valid non-Coordination firearm routes;
Coordination does not. Neither category universally subsumes the other.

The comparison optimizes one existing trained line only. It is not a global
optimization across every legal new Field, Specialty, or Precision purchase.

## Shopkeeper: No Specialty or Precision Required

Saved shopkeeper: Awareness 3 under Psyche 6, Observation 5. Use the already
recorded unfamiliar-equipment observation route, without adding a Field.

| Equal-expenditure option | Observation-route Rating gain |
|---|---:|
| Awareness 3 -> 4 | 7.69% |
| Observation 5 -> 7 (2 Training ranks) | 13.33% |
| Observation 5 -> 8 (3 Training ranks) | 20.00% |
| Observation 5 -> 10 (5 Training ranks) | 33.33% |

Awareness improves other applicable Tasks through Awareness. Observation

The tradeoff exists without a deep Specialty stack. This conditional Rating
comparison does not turn routine observation into a mandatory roll.

## Cap Checks

The shopkeeper's Cognition is already 6 under Psyche 6. The clinician's
Coordination is already 6 under Mobility 6. Neither can purchase a further
Rank there without first raising the parent. Do not bypass those constraints
to create a symmetric comparison or interpret them as evidence of a new level.

Under the ratio control, buying the parent and then the capped Derived increase
costs six Derived-increase equivalents. The parent also improves its entire
family and raises all five child ceilings; it is not merely an unlock fee and
does not award free child Ranks. Whole-pool performance including such purchases
has not been optimized in this test.

## Method and Findings

JavaScript parsed the saved JSON, calculated both probes, and exhaustively
enumerated the bounded guide allocations. All selected ranks remain within
the current horizon. No dice simulation, XP award schedule, or peer pricing
was necessary.

- Five Training increases buy substantially greater Rating gain on the tested
  activities. This is a strong incentive to favor Training, not proof that
  Derived development is useless everywhere.
- Three is intermediate; two produces the smallest tested gap while still
  rewarding concentration.
- Portability prevents a simplistic claim that all Derived investments are
  always broader than all Tasks or Fields.
- No universal price balance is established by two numerical probes and the
  cap checks. Campaign use frequency and Training-tier costs remain important.

## Recommendation and Next Decision

Use one Derived increase = two Training increases as the next provisional
exchange control; retain Attribute = five Derived increases. These are relative
prices, not an adopted XP award rate or final advancement economy.

Next compare whether Task, Field, Specialty, and Precision should retain equal
advancement prices. Hold the above candidate exchange stable while examining
their actual applicability and opportunity cost. Leave new Lens acquisition,
Heralds domain prices, Background provisioning, and higher horizons unresolved.
