# Leverage as support: role comparison

Date: 2026-09-15

Status: exploratory, unadopted. User-authorized continuation of the comparison
between Leverage carrying an Expression and supporting another capability.
No sixth Attribute, removal of Leverage, new price, character conversion,
Capacity change, or new mandatory modifier is adopted.

## Finding

Established position can plausibly enhance personal performance. Requiring all
established position to work only that way is a substantially different claim.
The support-only candidate works best when the actual uncertainty concerns the
character's execution with help. It is incomplete where the uncertainty concerns
the reach, availability, or competing force of established position itself.

The strongest next candidate is role-sensitive: examine what the means actually
do, preserve their direct consequences, and test a numerical enhancement where
they genuinely support another performance. This is not a mandatory division
into exclusive fictional categories. One relationship can open a door, supply
trust, and bring another acting person into the scene when those functions are
actually present. Valuing the same contribution twice remains a separate issue.

Importantly, current Stable Character Lens doctrine already permits relevant
support through Resonance, Access, resources, and other functions. This pass
does not discover that the existing game forbids help. The new questions are
whether to assign a dependable ranked support factor, whether it should be
independent of contextual Resonance, and whether it replaces direct Leverage
Expressions. Those decisions need not travel together.

## Current controls

The current baseline uses Attribute + Derived as its foundation. Leverage is
the fifth Attribute; Assets, Network, Authority, Reputation, and Background are
its five Derived Attributes. The source of force selects the carrying Attribute.
Leverage + Assets also describes stable material position; its conversion to
resource bands remains unfinished. Framework Function currently affects whole
Leverage-carried Expressions, not unrelated personal competence.

Current HOLT Access and Axis ranks each cost 20 under the September 15 adoption.
The old 40/15 prices and acquisition-only discount schedule are superseded.
Neither that revision nor this comparison prices the proposed support layer.
Existing caps, protected allocations, and character balances remain unchanged.

Historical fifth-Attribute and opposed-Leverage tests provide relevant design
questions, not current arithmetic: their older multiplicative foundations,
parent-HOLT ranks, and Net-Margin procedure are not reused here.

## Three interpretations under comparison

1. **Current source-based approach.** Use the Attribute supplying the force of
   the declared action. Other distinct help may already contribute under current
   Lens rules. Direct personal persuasion and a demand backed by institutional
   authority are different possible routes.
2. **Support-only replacement.** Use a personal Attribute + Derived foundation;
   an applicable position rank supplies x(1 + rank/10). This test provisionally
   omits the Leverage parent from that expression. What becomes of the parent,
   its costs and caps is unresolved, not silently refunded or deleted.
3. **Role-sensitive support candidate.** Established facts still produce access,
   resources, and direct outcomes. Personal performance can receive an applicable
   support factor. Genuinely uncertain mobilization of position retains its
   existing Leverage route for comparison rather than being forced into personal
   persuasion. This is a conservative experimental option, not proof that a
   retained fifth Attribute is the only possible architecture.

## Narrative probes

### 1. The awkward patron pays for an agreed service

The provider is willing, the price is agreed, and the patron can pay. None of
the models needs an invented persuasion roll. Assets establishes the means and
the exchange occurs. If the service is performed by an expert, that expert's
actual capability governs their difficult work.

If the uncertainty instead concerns whether the patron can raise exceptional
funding before a deadline, it is not settled by calling the purchase automatic.
Current Leverage provides a resolution route. Support-only replacement needs
to explain how resource uncertainty is assessed without making wealth merely
a small bonus to charm or cognition. Resource-rating lookup is a possible
answer, but its unresolved translation cannot be claimed as a tested solution.

### 2. The persuasive outsider and the connected patron seek risky aid

The same contact is reachable by both; neither is barred from making a request.
The patron has a substantial existing relationship. The outsider must rely more
on their proposal and presentation. Here personal application plus relational
support is a natural description. The outsider can legitimately outperform the
patron if their offer or execution is better; whether the numeric balance is
convincing is a play expectation, not something determined by labels alone.

Do not grant the outsider access to the patron's private contacts because their
personal score is higher. Conversely, Network Rank 0 is not proof of having no
relationships. Established facts, including ordinary ones, still matter.

The current model also permits the outsider's direct Influence route. Comparing
only their low Leverage roll against the new calculation would falsely imply
that they previously lacked personal persuasion.

### 3. A recognized official gives an order

With valid jurisdiction and willing routine compliance, the order can be
executed without testing the official's charm. Under resistance, determine
what is contested: legitimacy, practical ability to compel, understanding,
loyalty, fear, or the requested decision. Several can matter in the actual scene.

Authority can strengthen Control or Manipulation when that describes the
official's performance. It can also supply institutional power independent of
eloquence. A rival charter is not automatically overcome by a beautiful speech;
neither must institutional conflict ignore an effective argument. Support-only
must preserve both possibilities rather than declare every order manipulation.

### 4. An ally performs the difficult work

A trusted engineer has agreed to repair a failing gate. Network explains why
the engineer is helping; the engineer supplies their own repair capability.
The patron's poor personal Coordination does not make the engineer incompetent.

If the patron actively contributes tools, planning, instructions, or physical
assistance, examine those contributions normally. Do not count the same
engineer's complete earned output and then award an additional generic Network
factor merely for having obtained it. Distinct further help can remain possible.
The current multiple-Source procedure applies when independent contributions
are actually uncertain; this is not a compulsory roll for every favor.

### 5. Worldly support assists a physical act

A network can supply people, local knowledge, shelter, transport, or coordinated
help. Assets can supply tools or facilities. These can genuinely improve a
physical result; their social origin does not restrict them to Influence.

Inspect what arrives in the scene. A purchased lifting machine contributes its
actual properties and permission; the owner's wealth does not automatically
supply another multiplier for the same machine. A separately useful facility
or crew is not prohibited merely because equipment is already involved.
The point is traceable contributions, not one predetermined use per category.

### 6. Reputation and Background

A rescuer's reputation may make witnesses listen or cooperate, while a known
fraud may encounter distrust. Shared formative experience may make an account
credible or provide an established relationship with a place or community.
These can support a present action without replacing it.

Neither is exclusively beneficial. Do not convert every reputation into a
positive factor. Nor should Background silently duplicate learned competence
already represented by applicable Training. The actual history can supply
additional distinct facts; the label alone does not award them.

### 7. Character-aligned enhancement alongside position

Devotion to family may sustain a rescue effort; a family contact may separately
provide transport. An official's office may supply recognized authority while
their oath supplies personal resolve. These are plausible distinct contributions,
not an automatic entitlement to two bonuses whenever two names are written.

The same title might merely restate its institutional power, or might also
express a genuinely motivating identity. Establish that meaning first. The
calculator cannot decide sincerity, narrative distinctness, or applicability.
Acquisition by purchase or story allocation remains separate from arithmetic.

## Controlled arithmetic

All fixtures below are analyst-selected, not campaign Difficulty assignments or
equal-price character rebuilds. Task 5 and Field 5 supply Training x2.25;
Specialty and Precision are zero. No other support is included in the current
route column: that isolates this relocation, not every option current doctrine
already permits. Raw values precede Difficulty. D3 is an arbitrary shared
permitted, required, unopposed check; natural 01 succeeds, 00 fails, no rerolls.

| Fixture: L / position; personal A / D | Current raw | Support-only raw | Current D3 chance | Support-only D3 chance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 / 8; 3 / 3 | 36 | 24.3 | 12% | 8% |
| 8 / 8; 8 / 8 | 36 | 64.8 | 12% | 22% |
| 2 / 2; 8 / 8 | 9 | 43.2 | 3% | 15% |
| 8 / 8; 1 / 0 | 36 | 4.05 | 12% | 1% |

At identical Training, the candidate exceeds the direct Leverage route when:

```text
(personal Attribute + Derived) x (1 + position rank / 10)
    > Leverage + position rank
```

For Leverage 8 and position 8, the crossing personal foundation is 16/1.8,
approximately 8.89. A legal integer foundation of 9 or more is higher; 8 or less
is lower. This is why the change can help one character and weaken another.

The grid checks all 65 currently legal L/position pairs against all 20 possible
positive personal foundations: 1,300 arithmetic cases. The candidate is higher
in 845, equal in 22, and lower in 433. These counts describe the selected grid,
not player-population frequencies, equal-budget advantages, or gameplay balance.

The weak-application fixture is a warning only when that personal capability
would be an inappropriate bottleneck. It is not evidence that a poor negotiator
must always match a good one. Distinguish performing badly from being unable to
use resources that already accomplish the requested result.

## Impairment: optional support versus necessary means

Applying Framework Function to an entire new personal Expression is not a
neutral transfer of the current rule. Control: personal output 40, full support
x2. Compare reducing the whole result with reducing only its optional bonus:

| Remaining support function | Whole result reduced | Optional bonus reduced |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 100% | 80 | 80 |
| 75% | 60 | 70 |
| 50% | 40 | 60 |
| 25% | 20 | 50 |
| 0% | 0 | 40 |

The candidate bonus-only formula is B x [1 + f x (m - 1)]. It is appropriate
as a test of disappearing optional help, not an adopted universal rule. If a
contact merely strengthens an otherwise possible appeal, losing that help need
not erase the character's own capability. Independent harmful consequences can
still impede them; this formula does not nullify real Dissonance.

If the contact is the only way into a closed institution, losing access cannot
be treated as simply falling back to an unassisted roll. Reassess the declared
means and available alternatives. A different viable route is a different
declaration, not automatic survival of the original one.

Framework therefore remains an actual design dependency. We have not decided
whether its present broad pressure scope, a revised contribution-level scope,
or another treatment should govern support. Do not add duplicate condition
tracks or charge the same condition through both reduction and Dissonance.

## Stacking and the character-alignment connection

For genuinely distinct +50% contributions, independent multiplication and
collecting their bonuses behave differently even before any impairment:

| Distinct contributions | Separate x1.5 factors | Collected bonuses |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | x1.5 | x1.5 |
| 2 | x2.25 | x2 |
| 3 | x3.375 | x2.5 |
| 5 | x7.59375 | x3.5 |

At pre-support output 160 and D5, three separate factors yield Final 68, versus
50 for collected bonuses; five yield Final 154 versus 71. The former has 99%
unopposed success, but its surplus still matters to Effects. This is not a
claim that all five Leverage categories should apply to an ordinary attempt.

One position factor x1.8 plus one character-aligned x1.5 gives x2.7 if separate,
or x2.3 if the +0.8 and +0.5 are collected. Thus the previously discussed
x1.1-to-x2 per-element range is not a bound on the combined benefit.

Collected bonuses in this neutral control are not automatically the adopted
Resonance pool. Whether they join that pool, form one independent support
factor, or remain separate factors is unresolved; their interaction with
Dissonance differs. No one-aspect restriction or blanket stacking prohibition
is adopted. Numerical cross terms are a consequence to judge, not proof of a
fictional impossibility or an automatic reason to reject the model.

## Recommendation and actual decision boundary

Continue with role-sensitive support as the leading test candidate, alongside
the existing model. Do not eliminate direct Leverage resolution solely to make
the sheet symmetrical. Do not require personal persuasion where actual means
already produce the result. This preserves the user's central proposal that
Network, Assets, and other established position can enhance the capability
actually being exercised.

The conceptual decision now exposed is whether established position must always
depend on a personal capability when its mobilization is genuinely uncertain,
or can sometimes supply the primary force itself. Retaining the latter leaves
a direct Leverage route available; rejecting it requires a substantive account
of resource and institutional uncertainty, not just a multiplier conversion.

Separately, numerical adoption requires decisions about contribution strength,
stacking, Framework pressure, and acquisition. Removing or repurposing Leverage
would also affect its parent cap, five Derived Attributes, protected allocations,
Assets translation, and HOLT Axis relationships. None is converted here.

This pass supplies scenario reasoning and reproducible arithmetic, not observed
table play, a finished replacement architecture, or a verdict on fair pricing.
