# LENS Reputation Whole-Provision Test

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: Approved provisional construction references; not a final price ladder.

## Scope

After accepting the Authority references, the user requested moving on.
Apply whole-provision construction to Reputation: what is believed about the
Character, who recognizes it, and what expectations it carries. Ordinary
familiarity is not purchased again. Background remains deferred.

Sources: the current Reputation definition in the terminology reference,
the baseline Stable Character Lenses doctrine, and the current Network and
Authority whole-provision tests. The baseline allows helpful, harmful, and
mixed reputations; current context determines applicability.

Use one starting allocation point per purchased Reputation Rank as the current
construction candidate. Selection spends the allocation, not persistent Rank.
The cost of a provision supplies no separate automatic Expression multiplier.
The cost/acquisition-Difficulty equivalence remains uncalibrated numerically.

## Proposed Reference Packages

| Candidate cost | Established recognition and expectation |
|---:|---|
| 1 | A defined local circle knows the Character for one consequential quality, such as fair dealing among the local market traders. |
| 2 | The Character is well known across a settlement as a dependable defender, with meaningful credibility when discussing local safety and protection. |
| 5 | The Character's name and record as an expedition leader carry substantial weight through relevant communities and institutions across a region. |
| 10 | The Character is a realm-wide symbol of a particular cause or achievement; identification with them can meaningfully affect public or institutional responses to that cause. |

These are package candidates in the fantasy Profile, not automatic prices for
geographic labels. Recognition's reach, strength, and actual relevance must
be stated. A small elite audience may be more consequential than widespread
but shallow recognition. A name can be famous while its bearer goes unrecognized
in person; specify the identity actually associated with the reputation.

## Scenario Checks

| Probe | Result |
|---|---|
| The fair dealer requests ordinary consideration from a trader who recognizes them | Established credibility is relevant. Routine consideration need not be rolled; a disputed price or exceptional request still has its own resolution. |
| The fair dealer addresses an unrelated audience that has never heard of them | No automatic recognition or modifier merely because the provision was purchased. A real introduction or evidence can change the fiction. |
| The known defender warns of a local threat | The reputation supplies a reason to listen and take the warning seriously. It does not guarantee that the warning is accurate or that every listener agrees. |
| The regional expedition leader seeks a hearing from an organizer who knows their record | Recognition can establish consideration or support an applicable Expression; it does not guarantee funding or a standing patron relationship. |
| The realm-wide symbol endorses a cause or urges a public response | Established public expectations can carry significant force. The declaration does not grant sovereign powers, universal compliance, or a personally loyal army. |
| Any reputation is used to perform the activity for which the Character is famous | Fame does not supply unpurchased Task, Field, Specialty, or Precision Ranks. Being reputed capable and actually executing the activity are different contributions. |
| The Character needs anonymity | Recognition can create exposure or relevant Dissonance. No automatic numerical penalty or starting Load is assigned merely for having a reputation. |

The purchased provision establishes consequential recognition in its stated
audience. Do not turn every encounter with that audience into a roll to learn
whether the reputation exists. Actual identification, changed beliefs, or
contested claims can still require resolution when the fiction makes them
uncertain. Reputation Rank contributes through the established applicable
Expression architecture, not an added fame multiplier per provision.

## Two Narrow Reputations Versus One Broader Reputation

At proposed expenditure two:

```text
Split: fair dealing among local traders (1)
     + discretion among an independent circle of couriers (1)

Concentrated: dependable defender recognized across the settlement (2)
```

The split portfolio gives established credibility in two different kinds of
interaction: trade and confidential information. The broader defender reputation
reaches more local people but carries a different expectation, especially about
safety and protection. It is not automatically a reputation for fair prices or
keeping secrets. Neither description replaces the other.

If two selections are merely the same reputation restated for the same audience,
do not generate duplicate contributions. Conversely, a truly broader purchased
reputation may subsume narrower examples; re-evaluate the packages rather than
inventing artificial audience exclusions.

## Notoriety and Mixed Reception

Alternative regional example: the Character is known for defying a powerful
regime. Their record supplies credibility among dissidents, suspicion among
officials, and attention from people who have heard the story.

This uses the same Reputation architecture. It does not require positive and
negative reputation meters or an alignment multiplier. Whether its whole value
matches the proposed five-point expedition-leader example depends on the actual
campaign, audience, and available leverage; bad publicity is not intrinsically
cheap or valuable. A wholly unwanted stigma need not be priced as a purchased
advantage, and disadvantage compensation is not settled here.

Naturally connected exposure already considered in the whole package does not
earn an automatic second point refund. A real hostile attack on reputation may
create Conditions under the existing rules; no new Condition values are adopted.

## Result and Next Decision

Conceptual pass: Reputation provisions can establish consequential expectations
without replacing Network support, Authority permissions, or Training. Multiple
narrower reputations and a broader single reputation offer different uses.
Favorable and notorious reputations both fit, but need contextual valuation.

Recommend the described 1 / 2 / 5 / 10 examples as provisional references,
subject to user judgment about scope and price. This test is not live play or
a numerical validation of the Difficulty curve as a construction price ladder.

After those references are accepted, return to the complete starting-package
status: Assets translation and shared Difficulty pricing still need completion;
Background provisions remain explicitly deferred pending Lens access. Do not
mistake initial examples for a finished universal character-creation system.
