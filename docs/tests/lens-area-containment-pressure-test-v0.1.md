# Area Containment Pressure Test

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: requested exploratory test. No containment limit, power-density rating,
growth conversion, or mandatory spill rule adopted.

## User's Correction And Objective

This is not a character voluntarily buying expansion beyond a safely controlled
area. The user proposes that declaring too small an area to obtain the easier
roll and preserve output can produce more power than that area can contain.
The resulting excess may not be neatly controllable. A larger declaration
could therefore buy the ability to manage the manifestation, not only coverage.

Test that relationship without turning uncontrolled spill into a selectable
extra area benefit or assuming a universal cap on generated Effects.

## Numerical Sensitivity Test

Current rules contain no adopted conversion between footprint and containable
power. To examine the proposed relationship, try three explicitly invented
comparison allowances: 5, 10, or 20 emitted-power Effects per 25 covered-area
units. These are assistant-selected sensitivity controls, not numbers supplied
by the user, proposed prices, or a recommendation to add another rating.

For this numerical probe only, interpret every generated Effect as emitted
power. Flag containment pressure when that output exceeds the trial allowance.
The flagged excess is not automatically assigned a radius, direction, strength
against bystanders, or controlled destination. The test does not determine how
much original strength remains after actual spilling or dispersal.

Reuse the exact Final Ratings from the covered-area comparison: candidate area
bands 25/D1, 50/D2, 100/D3; shared bands 0, 3, or 4 for otherwise identical
distance/duration demands; raw Expressions 75.9375, 302.33088, and 640. This
produces 81 sensitivity probes. Open permission, matched Scale, full Function,
and no context/equipment/opposition/spending are held fixed. Existing natural-01
continuations are included; natural 00 is not successful emitted output here.

### Fully Developed Route, Ordinary Roll 40

Raw Expression 640, with no shared additional demands:

| Declaration | Generated Effects | Trial allowance: 10 per 25 area | Excess over that allowance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 area units | 59 | 10 | 49 |
| 50 area units | 34 | 20 | 14 |
| 100 area units | 19 | 40 | 0 |

The small declaration is no longer guaranteed to deliver all 59 Effects neatly
within its requested footprint. The larger declaration can be a means of
maintaining control, rather than an inefficient way to produce the same blast.

At allowance 5 per 25 area, excess is 54/24/0 across the three declarations.
At allowance 20 per 25 area, excess is 39/0/0. Thus the broad relationship is
consistent in this fixture, but the point at which control is sufficient
depends on the invented allowance. The data cannot identify a final threshold.

With the middle allowance, the raw640 small and medium declarations flag
overload on every successful check; the 100-area declaration flags it only
through a sufficiently long natural-01 continuation (approximately 0.0023%
of all checks). This is materially stronger than a rare-complication rule.

## Narrative Cases

| Case | What the candidate reveals |
| --- | --- |
| An indiscriminate blast centered in an empty battlefield | Uncontrolled spill may be acceptable or useful. The candidate intentionally does not make every small declaration undesirable. |
| The same blast inside a courtyard with allies nearby | Inability to contain output becomes a real cost even if the intended targets are hit. The player cannot designate only desirable spill directions. |
| A larger declared blast with genuinely supported selective shaping | The harder scope can support deliberate placement; a larger declaration alone does not grant selective-exclusion permission. |
| A narrow, intense cutting effect whose Lens supports concentration | Small footprint is not by itself defective containment. A universal footprint-to-output rule would erase a supported capability. |
| A precise protective ward or controlled illusion with many successful Effects | High output need not be literal released destructive energy. A universal spill rule may be meaningless or can create an unintended free extension. |
| Containment disrupted by an actual intervention | Loss of control can follow the dependency that was disrupted. Existing interference rules can address that event without treating every good roll as disruptive. |

Uncontrolled does not mean automatically harmful in every scene. Conversely,
it is not an entitlement to full original strength throughout a newly selected
larger footprint. Actual spread, dissipation, exposure, and protection govern.
If the effect genuinely becomes an area hazard, use existing area application
and hazardous-exposure rules rather than inventing free per-round harm.

## Failure Of The Simplest Universal Trigger

### Better Rolls Can Cause Spill

At Final Rating 74, ordinary rolls 70, 40, and 02 generate 1, 4, and 8 Effects.
Under the five-Effect allowance, the first two stay below the threshold and the
best roll crosses it. Natural 01 can likewise produce spill through its extra
successful output, without rolling 00 or suffering a separate interruption.

This need not be invalid for an inherently volatile manifestation, but it is
a substantive success-with-overload rule, not merely a consequence of failed
control. It should not be introduced silently to every well-executed Expression.

### Output Does Not Identify Its Source

These permitted routes have identical raw Expression 205.2864 and D1 Final
Rating 201, producing 17 Effects at ordinary roll 40:

- A8, D8, Task8, Field8, Specialty8, Precision1, Axis10.
- A8, D8, Task8, Field8, Specialty8, Precision10, Axis1.

The numerical total cannot distinguish which contribution supplied the result.
Precision is not automatically a containment modifier, and Axis is not
automatically a physical energy gauge. A universal trigger based only on the
final Effect count cannot make those distinctions without another interpretive
rule. Additional Effects can represent effective execution or supported
refinement, not necessarily more material or energy forced into a fixed space.

## Comparison Of Interpretations

1. **All generated Effects are emitted power.** The numerical probe above makes
   this executable, but it can punish better execution and makes a new
   footprint/output allowance necessary. It is not ready as universal doctrine.
2. **The declared manifestation and its means establish actual output and
   containment.** Overload follows a genuinely containment-limited undertaking,
   not the Effect total alone. This can preserve precise or stable manifestations,
   but needs an identifiable pre-resolution boundary rather than GM surprise.

The second is the stronger candidate for further development. It is not a
claim that a character must voluntarily buy expansion or deliberately push.
They may have committed to more output than their chosen means can contain,
with involuntary consequences even though the manifestation succeeds.

It also does not solve the tradeoff by simply allowing a player to erase
unwanted output after seeing the roll. Whether output is committed before the
roll, limited by the declared effect, or freely underused afterward requires
clarification if containment is intended to constrain underdeclaration.

## Recommendation And Decision Boundary

Retain the user's containment idea as a serious candidate. It supplies a
fictionally meaningful reason to choose an appropriately large declaration
without a universal maximum area. Do not adopt the trial density numbers or
equate all successful Effects with unstable energy.

The next real decision is what the containment trigger measures: actual power
committed through the manifestation, or all successful output produced by the
roll. Recommend the former, with the source of containment identified in the
declared means, relevant Lens, and circumstances before resolution. No new
numerical attribute or extra mandatory control roll is implied by that proposal.

If the intended design instead embraces good rolls releasing unexpectedly
large output, retain that explicitly as an alternative and evaluate its
consequences. It cannot be described as merely preserving the existing meaning
of success. No main rule or prior adopted successful-area entry is changed here.

## Verification

The accompanying CJS reuses prior exact Final Ratings and the established
Effect-distribution helper, with omitted continuation probability 1e-12. All
81 probes pass assertions for accounting, probability bounds, comparative
examples, and the equal-output/different-source counterexample. Displayed zero
after rounding does not grant or deny permission. This establishes behavior
under stated test assumptions, not a validated physical simulation or final
containment formula.
