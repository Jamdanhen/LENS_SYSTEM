# Defensive 00 Complications Within Independent Effects

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: Bounded test completed; user approved the consequence-handling boundary
within the independent-Effects candidate on 2026-09-10.

## Subsequent Decision

Resolve 00 complications separately from opposed successful Effects. Require
a fictionally supported additional consequence rather than duplicate accounting.
Preserve potentially cumulative consequences, matching mitigation, and the
distinction between own-failure complications and capped defensive counters.
The user's agreement adopts this boundary within the candidate, not the entire
independent-Effects model. The test and recommendation below retain the reasoning
presented before approval.

## Objective

Test a failed defense's own 00 complication alongside offensive remainder.
Do not add failure Margin to attacking output, re-add equipment, or apply the
one-Effect defensive-surplus counter limit to the defender's own complication.
The independent-Effects model remains a candidate.

## Arithmetic Fixtures

Successful output is one baseline Effect plus complete ten-point successful
Margin bands. Failed defense produces zero successful defensive Effects.
00 severity is floor(max(0, completed roll - Final Rating) / 10), without a
baseline complication Effect. Complete continuations before evaluating output.

| Case | Attack Rating / roll | Defense Rating / completed 00 roll | Offensive remainder | Own complication severity |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Modest complication | 90 / 60 | 80 / 106 | 4 | 2 |
| Severe complication | 90 / 60 | 80 / 150 | 4 | 7 |
| High-rated automatic failure | 90 / 60 | 160 / 150 | 4 | 0 |
| Both attempts fail | 40 / 60 | 80 / 106 | 0 | 2 |
| Repeated 00 | 90 / 60 | 80 / 206 | 4 | 12 |

All five fixtures were calculated and checked against expected results. Defense
and defensive counter output are zero in every fixture. Equipment is already
inside Final Ratings. These fixtures do not estimate encounter probabilities.
Fictional consequence adjudication is not established by the arithmetic checks.

## Fictional Application Tests

1. Hit plus lost footing: the attack creates Wounded 4; a disastrous evasive
   step creates Staggered 2 where terrain and the attempted movement support
   that result. If both Conditions apply to Physical Capacity without matching
   mitigation, Load is 6. They represent distinct consequences, not a six-Effect
   attack. Values illustrate the existing Condition model, not fixed penalties.
2. Duplicate hit: narrating the same blade contact as both the four-Effect hit
   and a second charge merely because the parry failed does not identify any
   additional consequence. A second label alone cannot justify another charge.
3. Same Condition, distinct harm: an attack wounds the defender while a failed
   dodge carries them onto an established hazard. Both may legitimately cause
   Wounded. Identical Condition names do not by themselves establish duplication.
   The hazard's applicable resolution and mitigation still govern its injury;
   this test does not create an automatic severity-to-hazard-damage conversion.
4. Miss plus failed dodge: no successful attack Effects arise, but the defender
   may still suffer a fictionally supported complication from the attempted
   defense. The complication cannot retroactively make the missed attack hit.
5. High-rated 00: defense still fails with zero calculated additional severity.
   The attack is unopposed by successful defensive Effects, but no minimum
   numerical complication is manufactured.

## Severity and Pacing

Four attacking Effects plus seven complication levels could produce Load 11
if both genuinely apply as Conditions in the same Capacity and no matching
mitigation prevents them. That reaches loss of function under existing bands;
it is not automatic death. This is a conditional consequence, not a requirement
to translate every 00 severity level into Load. Repeated 00 remains potentially
more severe. The one-counter limit does not suppress either result: these are
own-failure complications, not successful defensive surplus.

## Recommendation and Decision Boundary

Keep attacking output and own-failure complication severity separate. Adjudicate
the complication from the failed activity and supporting fiction, requiring
an additional consequence rather than charging the already-resolved outcome
twice. An actual additional consequence may affect the same Capacity or Condition
as the attack; no mandatory different-track rule follows.

Do not automatically add complication severity to offensive output, force all
severity into Wounded or Load, subtract the attack's Effects from complication
severity, or invent a universal combined ceiling. Preserve ordinary consequence
handling, applicability, mitigation, and paid-reroll consequence timing.

The subsequently approved boundary does not adopt an exhaustive complication
catalogue or a numerical equivalence for every fictional consequence.
