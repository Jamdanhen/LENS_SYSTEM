# Mixed Attribute-Derived foundations: controlled comparison

Date: 2026-09-20

Status: completed test; mixed foundations parked as exploratory by user decision
2026-09-20. The baseline retains parent-paired foundations. No prices, character
records, original development parents, caps, or capability meanings changed.

## Subsequent user decision

Approved: retain parent-paired foundations and park mixed foundations as
exploratory. Preserve Task, Field, and Lens flexibility without adding
cross-parent foundation rules. The additional foundational flexibility did not
justify the extra complexity for current core work.

The former recommendation below is preserved as test history, not the active
next step. Cross-Capacity Function testing and broader mixed-route testing are
not required follow-ups; do not resume them without new user direction. This
does not prohibit fiction-supported applications through existing parent-paired
routes or established supporting Lens functions. No universal extra-roll or
new support-multiplier rule was adopted.

## Historical test recommendation

The candidate has credible narrative uses and a bounded foundation, but is not
ready for unrestricted core adoption. It changes the breadth of Attribute
investment and exposes a real Function-accounting question. Neither issue is
solved simply by retaining the original parent cap.

Keep mixed foundations as an exploratory candidate. The strongest examples are
Influence + Insight and Influence + Network: a broad outward capability working
through an actual interpretive or relational contribution. Mobility + Strength
also describes a literal physical contribution without redefining Strength.
These are plausible judgments to test at a table, not facts proved by arithmetic.

Do not reject a route merely because another route could reach the same outcome.
Conversely, mentioning a capability somewhere in the story is not enough to
establish that it supplies the uncertainty being resolved.

The next necessary decision is how impairment follows a mixed foundation when
the selected Attribute and Derived's original parent use different Capacities.
Recommended direction: preserve the consequences of the actual impaired means;
changing the carrying Attribute must not wash them away. This does not yet
specify whether a Function factor affects the whole Expression, a contribution,
or some other existing dependency handling. No double-Function tax is adopted.

## Candidate and controls

Current foundation: original parent Attribute + Derived Rank.

Candidate foundation: applicable selected Attribute + applicable Derived Rank.

Only one Attribute and one Derived contribute. The Derived keeps its literal
meaning, original development parent, original-parent cap, and existing price.
The candidate does not allow selecting the highest numbers without fictional
support, establish new Access, or add an extra foundational multiplier.

Current Attribute ranks cost 60; Derived ranks cost 20. Every numerical fixture
has one equally applicable Task 5 / Field 5 / Specialty 5 / Precision 5 training
line, costing 50 + 50 + 25 + 25 = 150, and multiplying by 81/16. These are
synthetic purchase comparisons, not complete characters built from protected
creation allocations. Rank-1 Attribute bases are free; omitted Attributes are
1 and omitted Derived ranks are 0. No HOLT, Aspect, equipment, or contextual
bonus is included. Scale is matched and Function is full except where stated.

The training line is a controlled numerical profile for each declared activity,
not a claim that one purchased Task or Specialty covers every activity in this
record. Alternative rows that change the activity show capability comparisons;
their training applicability must be checked independently in actual play.

Difficulty uses 0.98 x 0.6^(band - 1), with final-only flooring. Chance figures
assume permitted, required, unopposed checks, including natural 01 success and
00 failure, with no paid spending. Routine outcomes still need no roll.
D1-D3 here are shared test inputs, not assigned difficulties for these scenarios.

Existing doctrine already permits appropriate Access, Resonance, equipment,
relationships, and other support. Neutral context isolates the foundation
change; it does not claim that current characters receive no other help.

## Narrative comparisons

### Interpretive persuasion

The character hears a commander's objections, recognizes the underlying fear,
and shapes an appeal around it. Influence supplies outward effectiveness;
Insight still means interpretation and recognition of meaning, not a renamed
Manipulation score.

| Build and route | Foundation | D1 / D2 / D3 Final Ratings |
|---|---:|---|
| Outward: Influence 8 + Manipulation 2 | 10 | 49 / 29 / 17 |
| Outward: Psyche 4 + Insight 4 | 8 | 39 / 23 / 14 |
| Outward: mixed Influence 8 + Insight 4 | 12 | 59 / 35 / 21 |
| Inward: Influence 4 + Manipulation 2 | 6 | 29 / 17 / 10 |
| Inward: Psyche 8 + Insight 4 | 12 | 59 / 35 / 21 |
| Inward: mixed Influence 4 + Insight 4 | 8 | 39 / 23 / 14 |

The first character gains a meaningful interpretive way of persuading. The
second remains the stronger inward interpreter despite the identical budgets.
If the task is only to understand the commander, outward Influence has not
become the source simply because the subject is another person.

This legitimately reduces the need to buy Manipulation for this particular
approach if the candidate is accepted. That is not automatically an exploit:
alternative approaches are part of the purpose. The risk is allowing every
appeal to become Insight merely by saying 'I understand what they want.' Actual
interpretive work must carry the attempt. Deceit, emotional pressure, and other
changes do not become interpretation solely because they share an outcome.
No mandatory extra diagnostic roll or compound Expression is introduced.

### Defiance through actual durability

The character takes a blow and their unbroken physical bearing intimidates the
aggressor. Current Influence 8 + Presence 2 gives foundation 10; candidate
Influence 8 + Toughness 4 gives 12. At D1 these are 49 and 59 respectively.
Their bodily coherence remains Might 4 + Toughness 4, foundation 8, not 12.

This is a literal use of physical Toughness, not mental fortitude renamed.
However, intimidating someone does not establish that the blow caused no harm.
When the injury itself is uncertain, preserve that opposition and its actual
consequences. If a prior resolved feat already supplied a bonus, do not award
another bonus for the identical contribution under a second description.
Distinct contributions can still coexist under existing doctrine.

This example is promising but exposes cross-Capacity impairment; see below.

### Propelling a leap

Mobility supplies physical relocation and Strength supplies bodily propulsion.
Both remain physical; no metaphor or new permission is needed.

| Build | Mobility + Movement | Might + Strength | Mixed Mobility + Strength |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mobile leaper, foundation | 10 | 8 | 12 |
| Strong leaper, foundation | 6 | 12 | 8 |

At D1, foundations 6/8/10/12 yield 29/39/49/59. The mixed route benefits the
mobile build, not automatically the strong build. Where raw propulsion is the
actual means, the strong build already has a plausible Might + Strength route.
Do not compare only its poor Movement route and claim mixing rescued it.
Landing, changing course, or staying airborne do not automatically become
Strength-based because force started the motion. Both original parents use
Physical Function, so this case does not create the cross-Capacity question.

### Network brought into personal influence

The connected advocate appeals to an established ally, drawing on real mutual
history. Current Leverage 4 + Network 4 gives foundation 8; personal Influence
8 + Connection 2 gives 10; mixed Influence 8 + Network 4 gives 12. At D1 those
are 39%, 49%, and 59% respectively.

The mixed route retains paid relational development rather than erasing it.
Leverage 4 still caps Network at 4. The character cannot obtain Network 8 by
pointing to Influence 8. A stranger does not become an old friend, a contact
does not gain unavailable resources, and an agreed routine favor needs no
invented test of charm. Network 0 also does not mean literally no acquaintances.

If the ally is already performing an independently resolved action, the mixed
foundation does not replace that ally's own competence or duplicate their output.

This supports the earlier intuition that Network can matter to another kind of
performance. It is not the same formula as the September 15 support-multiplier
candidate, and does not adopt that candidate's extra numerical layer.

### Leverage plus Control: valid coordination or erased Network?

Two organizers have the same Leverage 8 and Influence 4 / Control 4. One bought
Network 6; the other spent the same 120 points on Assets 6. Both can contact
some relevant people; no unavailable contact is invented for the comparison.

| Check | Network organizer D1 | Asset organizer D1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current Leverage + Network | 69 | 39 |
| Current Influence + Control | 39 | 39 |
| Candidate Leverage + Control | 59 | 59 |

If the helpers are present and committed, equal direction capability can make
sense. Leverage + Control is a candidate where actual established arrangements
give force to the direction. Merely owning unrelated assets does not establish
that source. Current Influence + Control already handles personal direction;
the mixed route is not required merely to let an organizer issue instructions.

If the uncertainty is securing help through standing relationships, substituting
Control deletes the paid Network distinction. Saying 'I control my network'
does not itself justify the substitution. The numerical problem occurs even
when both characters have basic access: permission checks alone do not solve it.

Different real means may still work. The asset-holder can offer paid transport
or hire a crew where available; that is an actual Assets approach, not a free
claim to the other character's contacts. No universal rule requires a Network
roll before every use of help or forces the whole scene into separate actions.

## Attribute-shopping and equal-budget stress

With the same Derived and all later factors held constant:

```text
Mixed foundation - current foundation = selected Attribute - original parent
```

All 650 legal numerical triples were checked: 210 increase, 65 are unchanged,
375 decrease. The possible difference is -9 to +9. These are grid counts, not
frequencies of valid fictional applications. If players retain ordinary routes
and may choose between equally valid ones, a lower mixed number is not a
counterbalancing penalty: they can use the ordinary route instead.

The foundation remains at most 20; the six-layer ordinary maximum remains 320.
Foreign Derived Rank 10 still requires its original parent at 10. That
preserves a substantial cost, but does not fully protect intermediate breadth.

### One route bought from scratch

Minimum costs for the foundation alone, starting from free Attribute-1 bases:

| Target foundation | Own-parent route | Two different parents, mixed route |
|---|---:|---:|
| 4 | 100 | 140 |
| 8 | 260 | 380 |
| 12 | 420 | 640 |
| 16 | 580 | 960 |
| 20 | 740 | 1,280 |

No mixed-only discount appears here. Mixed foundations require developing two
parents, even though only one contributes its rating to a given roll.

### Reusing a developed Attribute

At an equal 680-point foundation budget, the concentrated stress build has
Influence 10, Psyche 2, Manipulation 2, Insight 2. Its ordinary foundations are
12 and 4. The balanced build has Influence/Psyche 5 and Manipulation/Insight 5:
ordinary foundations 10 and 10.

If Influence is allowed to carry both routes indiscriminately, the concentrated
build gets 12 and 12. With identical 150-point training profiles, the equal
830-point builds produce D1 ratings of 59/59 versus 49/49. Exhaustive search of
legal two-parent/two-Derived purchases within 680 confirms maximum minimum
foundations of 10 under own-parent use and 12 under unrestricted highest-parent
reuse. Training for multiple distinct activities would add the same applicable
costs to both fixtures; the optimized foundation result does not depend on it.

That is an abuse-bound comparison, not permission to use Influence for inward
analysis. Enforcing its outward meaning keeps the concentrated character's
ordinary inward rating at 19 rather than 59. Nevertheless, frequent genuinely
outward interpretive situations can make this concentration attractive. How
often the campaign supports those situations determines its practical breadth.

Even the extreme Influence 10 / Leverage 1 / Network 1 example needs an honest
baseline: mixed foundation 11 versus relational foundation 2 looks enormous,
but that character already has Influence 10 before any personal Derived rank.
Do not label the full difference a newly obtained persuasion capability.

## Effect and opposition check

A fixed ordinary-roll rehearsal isolates changed output without estimating
whole-encounter odds. Both sides roll 45; the defender has Final Rating 49,
producing one Effect. Current offense at 49 also produces one Effect, which
cancels. Mixed offense at 59 produces two Effects, leaving one offensive Effect.

The gain changes successful output, not just success chance. If both contestants
gain the same amount, cancellation can remain unchanged; unequal gains can
change it. No special-result, mitigation, defensive-counter, or equipment rule
is altered. This is not a Monte Carlo encounter or observed playtest.

## Impairment is a genuine unresolved dependency

Current Function follows the carrying Attribute: Physical for Might/Mobility,
Mental for Psyche/Influence, Framework for Leverage. Own-parent foundations
therefore do not split their foundational ratings across Function categories.

For Influence 8 + Toughness 4 at D1 with the common training profile:

- Full Function gives Final Rating 59.
- Following only unimpaired Influence still gives 59 when Physical Function is
  50%, even if the performance depends on currently impaired bodily coherence.
- Applying that necessary Physical Function once to the entire mixed result
  would give 29. This is a sensitivity calculation, not an adopted solution.

Physical exhaustion cannot be evaded merely by calling the same bodily act a
social display. But an old scar or a reputation for durability may continue to
impress while the person is injured; those are not necessarily an exercise of
current Toughness. The actual fictional contribution must be identified first.

Influence + Network similarly exposes whether Framework impairment affects a
relationship's present contribution when Mental Function carries the roll.
Closed access remains closed, but partial numerical impairment is not settled
by an Access check. Applying both whole-Expression Function factors by default
would introduce another unadopted rule. No repair is silently installed here.

## What this does and does not establish

- Mixed foundations can represent real, non-metaphorical combinations without
  increasing the ordinary maximum or removing original caps.
- They change purchase breadth, not just notation. High-Attribute reuse is the
  principal economic risk; buying one isolated mixed route is not cheaper.
- Omission of a relevant paid capability can be legitimate alternative means
  or a bypass, depending on what actually supplies the attempted result.
- Existing support remains available; a failed case for mixed foundations is
  not proof that the narrative contribution must be excluded from the game.
- Cross-Capacity Function handling needs a substantive decision before general
  adoption. Axis routing and extraordinary applicability also need confirmation
  before claiming that this ordinary test validates all HOLT applications.

No price adjustment, new cap, mandatory two-roll procedure, replacement support
multiplier, or universal approval of mixed pairings is recommended by this test.
