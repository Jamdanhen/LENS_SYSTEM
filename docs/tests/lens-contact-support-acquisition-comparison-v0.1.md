# Contact Support: Existing, Introduced, and Purchased

Status: Completed qualitative comparison; no new contact price or acquisition
rule adopted.

## Objective and Sources

Test what a lasting contact purchase adds beyond an existing Network
relationship or one-point narrative help. Preserve current permissions, normal
fictional persistence, Automatic actions, Conditions, and story development.

Sources:

- `lens-one-point-network-contact-test-v0.1.md`
- `lens-two-point-network-relationship-test-v0.1.md`
- `lens-narrative-help-scope-test-v0.1.md`
- Current baseline Network definition and narrative-help procedure.

The earlier one- and two-point contact comparisons used a starting Network
allocation and candidate acquisition Difficulty. Their descriptive packages
remain useful references. Those point labels do not independently establish
prices in the current unrestricted creation/advancement currency.

## Controlled Cases

Use the same local innkeeper and capabilities in all three cases. The Character's
Network Rank, expertise, and other ratings remain unchanged.

1. Existing support: the established relationship already includes holding
   ordinary messages and sharing local information the innkeeper possesses.
2. Narrative introduction: one point establishes plausible familiarity and an
   introduction to the innkeeper. It does not stipulate recurring services.
3. Purchased support candidate: an eligible lasting purchase establishes the
   same recurring message-holding and information-sharing as case 1. Price is
   deliberately unspecified.

The third case adds no benefit over the first when their established facts are
identical. Its proposed purpose is obtaining a provision not already present,
not upgrading purchased friends above story-established friends.

## Scenario Results

| Situation | Existing support | Narrative introduction | Purchased support candidate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collect an ordinary message already entrusted to the innkeeper | Hand it over normally; no point or cooperation roll | An introduction alone does not establish a message service; the Character can ask | Same as existing support |
| Ask for local information the innkeeper knows and can freely share | Within agreed support; normally Automatic | Conversation may provide it freely; introduction does not require a roll or guarantee an answer | Same as existing support |
| Return next week for the same ordinary help | Relationship persists; no new charge | Previous meeting remains true, but recurring commitment was not automatically established | Relationship persists; no new charge |
| Ask for information the innkeeper could not know | No invented knowledge | No invented knowledge | No invented knowledge |
| Ask for dangerous concealment outside agreed support | Resolve the actual request and circumstances; neither automatic refusal nor a mandatory roll | Same principle, with whatever relationship now exists | Purchase does not guarantee obedience or willingness to face any danger |
| Innkeeper becomes unreachable because the inn is closed by an established event | Apply actual Access change | Apply actual Access change | Same Access change; payment is not immunity, and the GM should not invent routine denial to erase the provision |
| Introduction develops into dependable support through play | Not applicable to the acquisition route | Record the relationship earned in the story; no retroactive purchase tax | Paying is not the only route to the same established benefit |
| Proposed purchase repeats support already granted by Network construction or story | Do not charge for it again | Check the relationship's current facts, not only its original introduction | Reject duplicate purchase; identify genuinely additional support before pricing |
| Character has high Network Rank but no established tie to this innkeeper | Rank alone does not identify this person or promise this help | A plausible introduction can supply the missing specific route | A purchase may establish an eligible route; neither creates a contact-count entitlement |

## Finding

The useful distinction is the content of the established relationship:

- Familiarity or an introduction provides a route to interaction.
- Defined recurring support establishes what help is ordinarily available.
- Existing grants, story development, and a possible purchase are different
  acquisition routes, not different resolution systems.

Do not reduce Network to permission to approach people. It already includes
relationships capable of real support. Conversely, merely recording someone's
name does not promise every useful service they could possibly provide.

Within agreed support, cooperation normally needs no new contest. Actual
obstacles or expanded requests use existing resolution. No new loyalty score,
favor meter, per-use point toll, automatic Framework Load, or mandatory daily
limit is necessary. No extra multiplier follows merely from purchasing support.

Narrative help can lead to lasting benefits through normal play. Do not make its
relationships expire artificially to protect a separate purchase price.

## Recommendation and Next Decision

Retain the old limited innkeeper support package as the concrete reference for
any new purchase comparison. A purchase should establish specifically described
support not already supplied, without increasing Network Rank or guaranteeing
all requests. Match relationships with identical facts regardless of their
acquisition route.

The three-way distinction passes conceptually, but it does not determine a
price. Next compare funding that same limited provision with one unrestricted
point against the already adopted one-point ordinary-language provision and
one-point narrative help. That is a new price candidate, not conversion of the
old Network-allocation cost. Do not extend it to powerful patrons or all contacts.
