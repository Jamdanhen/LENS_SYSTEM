# LENS One-Point Network Contact Test

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: User-approved candidate tested conceptually; no final price ladder adopted.

## Candidate and Scope

The user agreed with the proposed one-point contact and requested a test.
One point of the starting Network allocation establishes a dependable, limited
relationship. Network Rank is retained, not spent down. Candidate acquisition
Difficulty is 1, following the proposed Difficulty-as-construction-cost model.

Contact: a local innkeeper, normally reachable during business, who will hold
messages and share useful local information they possess. Free lodging,
substantial financial support, and dangerous concealment are not established
by this package. These are boundaries of promised support, not prohibitions
on future generosity or attempts to secure additional help.

Reach, Accessibility, and Loyalty are descriptive valuation considerations,
not three separately purchased ratings. The prior three-points-per-Network-point
construction and category cap remain alternatives, not adopted rules.

This is a scenario and boundary test, not a dice simulation or numerical proof
that acquisition Difficulty 1 is calibrated. Background provisions remain
deferred. No character budget, equipment package, or baseline definition changes.

Sources: current terminology reference definitions of Network and Access;
baseline Automatic/Trivial and Difficulty rules; the user's current construction
proposal and approved contact description.

## Cases

| Case | Stated facts | Result under the candidate |
|---|---|---|
| Collect a message | An ordinary message was left; the innkeeper is present and willing | The message is handed over. No roll to re-establish cooperation. |
| Learn something useful | The innkeeper witnessed a relevant traveler's arrival and can share it without unusual risk | They share what they know. The contact provides useful established support, not merely permission to approach a stranger. |
| Ask about an unknown event | The innkeeper neither saw it nor has a credible source | They cannot supply the answer. Dependability does not create knowledge. |
| Return next week | Another ordinary message or local inquiry, no changed conditions | Support remains available. The construction point is not a single-use favor token. |
| Request dangerous shelter | Concealing the Character would expose the innkeeper to serious retaliation | Not guaranteed by this relationship. The Character may ask, negotiate, establish another basis, or face refusal. No automatic Difficulty or mandatory roll follows solely from exceeding the package. |
| Request distant intervention | The problem is in a town where this innkeeper has no established reach | This contact does not acquire a regional network by implication. An actual introduction may help if established; otherwise the request needs another route. |
| Disrupt ordinary availability | The inn is closed by a specific event and the innkeeper cannot be reached there | Existing Access changes apply. The contact's purchased reliability remains the ordinary baseline, not immunity to the fiction or permission for arbitrary repeated GM denial. |
| Turn message-holding into a major operation | The Character asks for constant deliveries, significant storage, or dedicated labor | The demand is materially different from ordinary message-holding. No unlimited service follows from repeated use; no universal daily-use cap is needed to identify this change. |

## Construction and Resolution Checks

```text
Starting Network allocation available: 1
Contact cost:                         1
Allocation remaining:                 0
Persistent Network Rank:              unchanged
```

The contact's cost does not add another multiplier to every Network Expression.
It establishes a relationship and available support. Ordinary fulfillment need
not be rolled. When genuine uncertainty or opposition exists, use the current
applicable Expression and resolution rules.

The candidate acquisition Difficulty describes obtaining this relationship;
it is not a recurring D1 toll on each use. A later exceptional request has its
own fiction and resistance, not automatically the contact's construction cost.

Ordinary social acquaintance and previously established relationships are not
erased because the Character has not bought this provision. This test prices
an explicitly selected starting support package, not the existence of friends.
Developing relationships in play remains possible through the shared fiction
and rules, rather than compulsory retroactive construction expenditure.

The Character cannot claim two multipliers merely because the same innkeeper
was described twice. Several separately purchased contacts could provide
different routes or corroboration, but applicability and distinct contribution
still govern any combined numerical support.

## Finding

Conceptual pass: the package offers dependable, recurring, bounded help.
It is useful without guaranteeing all information, all favors, unlimited labor,
or protection from changes in the fiction. The existing rules can express its
limits without a new loyalty roll, favor meter, or mandatory use quota.

This supports retaining it as the one-point reference candidate. It does not
prove the point-to-Difficulty conversion, equal-cost alternatives, or the
balance of the whole Network allocation against other Derived Attributes.

## Next Pricing Test

Compare equal expenditure: two one-point local contacts versus one two-point
relationship. Give the latter a concrete expansion of support, not another
automatic multiplier. Test breadth versus depth before extrapolating a full
ladder or assuming each extra point doubles resources or geographic reach.
