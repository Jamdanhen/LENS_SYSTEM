## Approved Playtest Ceiling

The user approved **40 points maximum in total starting drawback compensation**.
Retain **30 and 60** as comparison candidates. Forty is a ceiling on compensation,
not a grant, a required expenditure, or a count of drawbacks.

The earlier assistant suggestion of a three-drawback count limit was rejected.
The earlier assistant statement adopting a sixty-point ceiling was corrected;
sixty was a consideration, not an adopted decision.

For example, thirty-five points of agreed, distinct burdens receive thirty-five
points under the forty-point ceiling, not forty. A larger assessed burden does
not automatically permit compensation beyond the ceiling.

## Approved Working Spending Rule

The user explicitly approved ordinary flexible starting points and awarded
drawback compensation funding **ordinary development or approved benefits**.
Retain setting-specific HOLT/Axis eligibility and count each drawback offset
only once. This branch-approved rule was expressly adopted into working core
rules on 2026-09-17.

Ordinary development includes Attributes, Derived Attributes, Tasks, Fields,
Specialties, Precision, and eligible ordinary languages at their established
prices. Preserve normal prerequisites, Rank ceilings, parent caps, and narrative
eligibility. The approval does not turn compensation into mandatory benefit
spending or make all extraordinary purchases eligible in every setting.

The ordinary flexible allowance remains 125 points; this decision changes
eligible purchases, not that base allowance. Actual awarded compensation can
add spending capacity, subject to the forty-point total ceiling. Compensation
already consumed by benefits inside a package is not also available outside it.

For individual creation, 125 flexible points plus forty points of actual awarded
compensation can provide up to 165 points of flexible spending capacity. This is
not an automatic 165-point grant. No additional species-package point pool is
created. The deferred HOLD work must preserve the same no-double-credit rule.

## Approved Acquisition and Removal Principle

On 2026-09-17 the user explicitly approved:

- During play, purchase narratively eligible, GM-approved benefits at their
  creation prices, without separate benefit ranks.
- Explicitly designated GM story grants do not require an additional point
  charge. Encountering an opportunity is not automatically a free grant.

The user then clarified that drawback removal does not need a separate system:
if the narrative and story fit, the drawback can be bought off. Apply the same
narrative eligibility and GM approval principle, using existing buyoff logic
rather than creating another acquisition currency, surcharge, or procedure.

For ordinary paid removal, use the compensation being bought off as the price
basis. Where a partial easing is narratively supported and agreed, use the
difference rather than charging the entire original value repeatedly. Preserve
actual compensation and prior payments; do not manufacture a charge for credit
the character never received. Existing buyoff reference:
`lens-uniform-holt-pricing-adoption-v0.1.md`.

Main adoption clarification: record actual compensation attributed to the
drawback or agreed combined burden, with GM agreement on attribution if the
ceiling reduces the total award. Where burdens cannot be valued separately,
retain their agreed relationship for later adjudication. Identify the credited
burden relieved by partial easing and preserve prior payments. Do not silently
redistribute credit after removal or treat a theoretical value as received points.

For example, two thirty-point assessed burdens can receive only forty total.
If the agreed record credits twenty to each, full ordinary buyoff of one uses
twenty, not thirty. This illustrates actual-credit accounting, not a required
equal split or a new removal procedure.

An explicit GM story award can provide the change under the same grant principle;
this does not automatically make every narratively possible removal free.
Points alone do not authorize a fictional change, and temporarily countering a
drawback is not the same as permanently removing it.

This package does not create a new automatic point award for suffering a later
injury, losing a benefit, or acquiring an unwanted limitation. It does not settle
legacy conversion or introduce automatic point debt. Normal GM adjudication and
existing acquisition/buyoff rules remain the basis for unusual cases.

## Working Valuation Direction

- Five points is the working anchor for a narrow, genuine drawback, not a
  universal price for every numerical penalty or minor inconvenience.
- Assess meaningful adventuring consequences, not merely the name of a trait
  or a theoretical list of activities it could affect.
- Consider severity, breadth, frequency, and actual means of countering the
  limitation. No formula multiplying these factors has been adopted.
- The player identifies meaningful Tasks or Fields in which the drawback is
  guaranteed to apply under its stated circumstances; the GM agrees on the
  applications and compensation.
- These are guaranteed applications, **not exclusive boundaries**. Other
  applications remain possible where the fiction supports the same drawback.
- The user suggested two or three identified areas depending on compensation.
  No exact count-to-price schedule is adopted. Naming overlapping activities
  or irrelevant Fields does not itself establish additional value.
- An identified Field is not penalized in every use: a fine-hand-control
  limitation can interfere with delicate repair without affecting purely
  observational diagnosis.
- Ordinary lack of purchased training is not itself a compensated drawback.

Review the finished character, including equipment and powers. A limitation
effectively answered by the actual build does not retain its unmitigated
valuation merely because the description remains on the sheet. Legitimate
preparation or a costly alternate route is not automatically permanent removal.
Do not invent additional burdens to preserve a discount.

Compensate distinct consequences once. Inability to run already encompassed
by inability to walk is not a separate award. Fear of heights and poor night
sight can be distinct even when both interfere in one scene. A restriction
already reflected in a discounted ability cannot generate another offset for
the same burden.

## Compensation-Ceiling Arithmetic

These are spending-side stress tests, not complete balance tests. They originally
assumed compensation could fund ordinary development; that eligibility is now
approved in the working spending rule above. Optimize one
applicable Expression using current flat costs, the Rank 10 ceiling, and the
Derived-to-parent cap. These values precede Difficulty and final flooring.

Formula: `(A + D) * (1 + T/10) * (1 + F/10) * (1 + S/10) * (1 + P/10)`.

| Additional points available | Initial A5 D5 T5 F3 S2 P1 | Initial A5 D5 T5 F5 S8 P8 |

| 0 | 25.74 | 72.90 |

These gains depend on the purchased expertise being applicable; they do not
extend to all Expressions. Context is applied afterward through normal pooled

but does not prove whole-character imbalance: other actions, unavailable means,

30, 35, and 35. For the first Expression above, the optimized outputs are

## Benefits and Existing Provisions

Benefits have defined provisions, not separately purchased
advantage ranks. Personal flight, for example, uses the character's applicable
Movement rather than a new Flight-rank track. Fixed Resonance benefits and
permissions are not interchangeable and need their own valuations.

Current ordinary language provision already prices an additional narratively
justified language at one unrestricted point, without charging again for
languages granted by Background or the Campaign Profile. This supplies an
existing small provision for comparisons; it does not establish a new language
knack price. Prior species-package illustrations are deferred under Current Scope.

The current 125 ordinary flexible points are not HOLT-only points. They fund
ordinary development and approved benefits under the adopted spending rule.
HOLT/Axis eligibility remains setting-specific,
not automatically granted by this change.

### Construction and Pricing Rationale

Start with what the benefit actually does. Distinguish a permission to do
something otherwise unavailable from a fixed improvement to an already possible
Expression. Not every advantage is permission-only, and not every permission
automatically provides Resonance or an Axis bonus.

Compare against ordinary development and, where appropriate, a narrowly
restricted HOLT permission. Current comparison prices are Attribute 60,
Derived Attribute 20, HOLT Access 20 once, Axis rank 20, Task or Field rank 10,
and Specialty or Precision rank 5. A five-point benefit competes with a Precision
rank; evaluate useful adventuring frequency, consequence, breadth, and interaction
with existing expertise rather than the attractiveness of its name.

Five, ten, twenty, thirty, and sixty were discussed as useful candidate price
points, not an exclusive denomination system or adopted tier schedule. One-point
languages and calculated four-point restricted permissions remain possible
comparison inputs. There is no blanket rule that 20% Resonance costs five points,
or that all useful benefits must cost ten or less. The forty-point drawback
compensation ceiling is not a ceiling on the price of a benefit.

Green thumb and black thumb illustrated natural aptitude or difficulty in working
with plants, not powers that grow or wilt nearby vegetation. The user treated
a rarely useful green-thumb example as at most a five-point candidate; a
theoretical specialized campaign was not justification for inflating its ordinary
adventuring value. These are illustrations, not required catalogue entries.

full broad HOLT Access and the quoted advantage cost for the same permission.
Awareness, Momentum, and Sea refer to the setting examples used in the discussion,
not universal LENS requirements that every setting use those HOLTs. A suitable
Sea permission in this fixture permits biological aquatic adaptation; that is
not a declaration that all Sea HOLTs must do so. HOLD/species-package balancing
remains deferred; existing restricted Access pricing is used only as a value comparison.

Calling a provision a benefit does not bypass Profile permission or funding
restrictions, grant unrelated HOLT manifestations, or open further Axes. Narrower
thematic scope alone does not automatically justify a discount. Preserve actual
defined permissions and avoid charging for the same permission twice.

Existing limitation rules assess the combined actual burden, apply a reduction
once to affected purchases, and leave extreme reductions subject to review.
Do not infer automatic approval of every 80% restriction or stack discounts
simply because several restrictions can be named. There are no advantage ranks
to buy in these fixtures; capability grows through the character's applicable
existing development and means.

### Fixed Resonance Comparisons

All prices in this table remain provisional. They illustrate different breadths,
not completed balance findings.

| Benefit | Proposed fixed provision | Candidate price |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Sea Legs | +0.20 relevant Resonance to maintaining balance on a pitching vessel | 5 |
| Natural Balance | +0.20 relevant Resonance across defined unstable-surface balance situations | 10 |
| Mechanical Aptitude | +0.20 relevant Resonance to applicable mechanical diagnosis, repair, understanding, or interference | 20 |

Use normal contextual pooling: `max(0, 1 + total Resonance - total Dissonance)`.
A +0.20 contribution is not a guaranteed twenty-percent relative increase in
every context, nor is it an independent x1.20 Axis multiplier. Do not change
existing context rules or grant missing Access through a numerical benefit.

Preserve the distinct-contribution rule. Sea Legs and Natural Balance do not
automatically provide +0.40 for the same balance contribution on a pitching
vessel. Genuinely distinct contributions may still combine; no universal
one-benefit limit is introduced.

Development comparisons must include already-specialized characters. With an
Expression value of 40 before Precision and neutral context, Precision 5 gives
60. Spending ten points on two Precision ranks gives 68; adding a hypothetical
ten-point +0.20 Resonance benefit instead gives 72 when applicable. The benefit
can grow in value alongside expertise, so a neutral, untrained comparison alone
does not establish its price. Conversely, a purportedly broad mechanical benefit
must be compared with actual applicable Field development, not only a narrowly
chosen Precision comparator.

## Core Adoption and Deferred Work

The framework is adopted as working core rules: spending eligibility, the
compensation ceiling, agreed-impact valuation, defined benefit provisions, and
ordinary acquisition/removal with traceable actual compensation. Permission and
Resonance comparisons remain illustrative. Catalogue valuations stay playtest
candidates where marked; adoption does not promote them to universal prices.

HOLD/species balancing, human provisions, package variants, and comparative
targets are parked at the user's request. A complete benefit/flaw catalogue
and new legacy-conversion rules are not adopted by this record.

development. Core adoption of lasting Aspects was recorded on 2026-09-17;
this framework does not reopen its optional character selection, rank awards,
or deferred temporary-enhancement extensions. Knacks and natural Dissonance
remain part of this benefits/flaws discussion, not automatic new Aspects.

Do not interpret this record as a completed universal catalogue, adopted human
package, final species balance, or authorization to alter protected creation
