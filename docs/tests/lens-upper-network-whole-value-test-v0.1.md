# LENS Upper Network Whole-Relationship Value Test

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: Conceptual stress test completed; upper prices remain provisional.

## Approved Objective

The user asked to continue above the one- and two-point examples before
moving to Authority, then approved five- and ten-point comparisons and the
accessible but uncommitted king. The earlier recommendation to move directly
to Authority is therefore superseded for this lane.

Test whole-relationship pricing. Reach, Accessibility, and Loyalty inform the
description and valuation; no three-category point pool, ratings, or cap is
adopted. Background provisions remain deferred.

Sources: `lens-one-point-network-contact-test-v0.1.md`,
`lens-two-point-network-relationship-test-v0.1.md`, current Network/Authority/
Access definitions, and the working reference's Scale Scope and Reference
Frame sections. The local project and Registry inheritance guidance were read.

## Controls

Use a fantasy campaign with recurring local, regional-travel, and court-facing
play. The examples have usable relevance, not nominal connections to places
the campaign never visits. Prices are proposed construction controls for whole
packages, not automatic prices for titles or geographic labels.

Within each equal-cost comparison, keep the Character's Attributes, Derived
Ranks, and Training unchanged. Network 5 supplies five allocation points;
Network 10 supplies ten. The Rank itself is retained after allocation. The
existing parent cap requires Leverage at least 5 or 10 respectively. These
are not full rebuilt Characters or a comparison of unaccounted free Ranks.

When applicable, Network's normal multiplier is x1.5 or x2.0 respectively.
No contact price adds another multiplier. Allocation cost, acquisition
Difficulty, persistent Rank, and functional Scale remain distinct roles.

This is a scenario comparison, not rolled probability testing. Correspondence
between construction cost and the acquisition Difficulty curve remains a
candidate; no calibrated D5/D10 acquisition success claim follows here.

## Five-Point Portfolios

### 5C: One Regional Patron, Cost 5

A regional merchant patron provides a reliable liaison, introductions through
their actual trade relationships, use of established routes and facilities,
and material backing for an agreed kind of expedition. That backing includes
ordinary travel support and meaningful supply or escort arrangements within
the patron's established resources. Some cost and inconvenience to the patron
are part of the commitment, not fresh favors to win each time.

This relationship is commercial and regional. Royal orders, unlimited personal
gifts, and control of the patron's business are not included. Requests outside
the commitment remain possible, but are not pre-established support.

### 5D: Three Independent Relationships, Costs 2 + 2 + 1

Two dependable local innkeeper allies in different settlements, using the
previous two-point package, plus an independent local information contact in
a third settlement using the one-point package. These are genuinely separate
support relationships, not three employees dependent on the same patron.

| Probe | 5C: Regional patron | 5D: Distributed locals |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare an expedition inside the agreed undertaking | Can bring connected logistics and material backing to bear | Individual shelter and information help, but no equivalent coordinated funding or escort was established |
| Seek immediate news from one of the named neighborhoods | Patron can use actual local sources if present; no automatic omniscience | Relevant local contact supplies what they know directly |
| Need help outside commercial business, such as lodging during a personal setback | Depends on the patron's stated support; no assumption it includes every personal need | Local allies' temporary personal support is already established |
| Patron's organization withdraws its participation after a specific conflict | The connected support route may be broadly affected | Independently established locals do not automatically withdraw |
| Ask for royal authorization | Patron may make a useful introduction if they possess that route | A local may also have a relevant route if established; no portfolio grants royal authority automatically |

Finding: concentration is materially stronger for coordinated expeditions.
Distribution preserves independent, personally useful relationships. It is
not necessarily geographically wider: a regional patron already has reach.

## Ten-Point Portfolios

### 10C: One Substantial Royal Relationship, Cost 10

A ruler is an established committed supporter of the Character and an agreed
undertaking. There is a dependable route to their attention or authorized
representatives. Available support includes introductions to officials,
access to royal facilities appropriate to that undertaking, significant
expedition support, and political backing that accepts meaningful expense or
controversy within the agreed commitment.

This must be actual backing, not a prestigious audience followed by an
unrelated loyalty contest for every ordinary request. The ruler retains agency
and control of their resources. This package does not grant the Character a
royal office, unrestricted treasury, command of all forces, or ownership of
the kingdom. Additional authority can arise through an actual decision or
delegation in play; the contact price does not silently pre-grant it.

### 10D: Two Independent Regional Patrons, Costs 5 + 5

Two patrons of the 5C type, operating through different organizations and
regions. Each has its own agreed support and actual resources. This uses the
same five-point candidate twice rather than artificially restricting a
distributed ten-point build to small local contacts.

### Additional Ten-Point Shape: Five Local Allies, Costs 2 x 5

Five existing two-point packages in five relevant settlements. This supplies
more independent personal fallbacks, not collective sovereign authority.

| Probe | 10C: Royal supporter | 10D: Two patrons | Five local allies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Obtain political backing for the agreed undertaking | Directly supported where the ruler can act | Patrons can advocate or introduce, but do not themselves possess sovereign power | Personal advocacy only, according to their actual position |
| Arrange substantial travel and supplies | Royal arrangements within established support | Either or both patrons can contribute; aggregation is allowed when fictionally feasible | Local aid at several stops, not an invented expedition budget |
| Act outside one patron's region or interests | Depends on royal reach and the agreed relationship | The other independent patron may provide a route | A relevant local ally may offer personal support |
| One supporter becomes unavailable | Its effects depend on the source: losing one liaison need not erase delegated royal support | Another organization remains if unaffected | Other independent allies remain if unaffected |
| Seek neighborhood-level information | Court resources can investigate through real sources | Trade contacts can help within their actual knowledge | Immediate local knowledge may be directly available |
| Ask for an unrelated war or the entire treasury | Not supplied by this package | Not supplied by these packages | Not supplied by these packages |

Finding: royal backing opens kinds of action that independent commercial
contacts may not directly provide. Two patrons can nevertheless equal or
exceed a royal connection for some logistical needs, especially across their
own operating regions. No universal rule prevents combining their help.

## Accessible King Without Loyalty

Hold the ruler's actual resources constant. Replace the committed royal
relationship with a reliable route to an audience or delivery of a proposal,
but no personal commitment to provide material or political backing.

| Request | Access without commitment | Committed royal relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Present a proposal | Within the established Access, subject to its actual timing/conditions | Also available through the agreed route |
| Obtain ordinary support already covered by the undertaking | Not promised; must establish a reason for the ruler to help | Support is established; do not require re-winning loyalty |
| Make a genuinely exceptional request | Resolve the actual interaction and resistance if uncertain | Likewise; prior commitment remains relevant where it applies |
| Ask for help that would undermine the ruler's position beyond the commitment | No automatic agreement | No unlimited compliance merely because the relationship is expensive |

Access alone remains useful. The Character may be highly persuasive or bring
an excellent proposal. Their success would be earned through the actual
Expression; it is not proof that the starting relationship included loyalty.

Under identical circumstances, the access-only relationship supplies less
established support than the same access plus substantial commitment. It is
therefore a candidate for a lower whole cost. This test does not calculate a
specific discount or carry the earlier 10/5/0 distribution into the new model.
The player's former five-point king example was not a locked whole-value price.

## Failure Searches

- Unlimited patron: a description promising any resources or any favor lets
  one high-cost relationship swallow Assets, Authority, and other Network
  provisions. That description fails; state the actual support relationship.
- Hollow patron: if even the purchased ordinary backing requires repeated
  persuasion, the expensive package collapses into access-only. That also fails.
- Manufactured independence: splitting contacts only creates independent routes
  when their actual relationships are independent. Do not invent immunity to
  a regional crisis or shared dependency to make distribution look better.
- Manufactured restriction: a concentrated patron may supply excellent local
  information and personal assistance if established. Do not forbid these
  merely to preserve a theoretical advantage for cheaper contacts. Reassess
  the whole package if it wholly subsumes its equal-cost alternatives.
- Double compensation: a narrow mandate or restricted availability already
  used to lower the whole value cannot earn another automatic point refund.
- Scale leakage: realm-wide reach or access to a powerful institution does not
  automatically increase the Character's personal Scale or every Expression.
  Higher-Scale functions require an applicable actual source and route.

## Conclusion and Decision Boundary

The whole-value model survives the upper-end conceptual tests without separate
Reach, Accessibility, or Loyalty ratings. Concentration supplies stronger or
more coordinated backing; distribution can supply independent routes and
different kinds of personal support. Neither is guaranteed superior in every
campaign, and a high-value relationship can contain many subordinate contacts.

The test supports using the 1/2/5/10 packages as provisional comparison anchors,
not a completed universal price ladder. Values still require calibration against
the Campaign Profile and other starting provisions. The point-to-acquisition-
Difficulty relationship has not been numerically validated by these scenarios.

The next substantive choice is whether the specified five- and ten-point
commitments feel appropriate for those investments. Once their scope is accepted,
compare limited versions to establish whole-value reductions. Do not move on
to Authority merely because the low end passed, and do not introduce a
three-stat contact construction system to price the remaining uncertainty.

## Continuation: Limited Whole-Relationship Values

The user said "Okay, keep going" after the upper-end findings. Retain the
five- and ten-point descriptions as provisional comparison anchors and continue
the proposed limitations comparison. The following reduced values are new
test candidates, not independently approved prices.

### Regional Facilitator: Candidate Cost 3

Keep the five-point patron's established regional contacts, reliable liaison,
introductions, and capacity to arrange trade logistics. Remove the commitment
to provide material backing: the facilitator can arrange supplies, passage,
facilities, or an escort through actual contacts, but the Character must meet
the relevant terms and provide payment or another basis for obtaining them.
The facilitator's agreed arranging help itself remains dependable; this is
not an extra fee or a persuasion roll for every introduction.

Equal-cost comparison at five allocation points:

```text
Full regional patron:                   5
Regional facilitator + local ally: 3 + 2 = 5
```

| Need | Full patron | Facilitator plus local ally |
|---|---|---|
| Arrange a relevant shipment | Agreed logistics and relationships available | Facilitator can arrange it through their actual network |
| Undertake the agreed expedition without sufficient supplies or funds | Material backing is part of the patron package | Introductions do not erase acquisition requirements; the local ally supplies only their established modest aid |
| Need temporary personal lodging outside the patron's undertaking | Not included unless the actual patron relationship provides it | Local ally's support is explicitly established |
| Regional route becomes unavailable | That source's support is affected | Independently established local aid may remain available |

The three-point candidate therefore retains meaningful regional assistance
without silently preserving the five-point material backing. The mixed
portfolio trades supplied expedition resources for a separate personal fallback.
It does not prove that the exact numerical difference must always be two.

### Privileged Royal Access: Candidate Cost 5

Keep a reliable privileged route to the ruler's attention, through a direct
audience or a representative empowered to put the proposal before them.
Remove the ten-point package's promised material and political backing.
The ruler has no special personal loyalty or standing commitment to help.

This is not merely the ordinary public right to petition. The distinctive
provision is a dependable route to serious consideration, not a guaranteed
favorable answer or an automatic persuasion modifier.

At equal proposed cost five, compare this relationship with the regional
patron. The royal route enables a direct appeal for decisions the patron
cannot make. The patron supplies dependable regional assistance that the
royal route does not promise. Neither wholly replaces the other. Successful
persuasion can yield substantial aid from the king, but that result belongs
to the actual interaction, not a concealed construction entitlement.

The ten-point relationship still differs: support within its agreed undertaking
is already established. It must not be reduced to this access-only behavior
by repeatedly asking the Character to win the same commitment again.

### Limitation Value Controls

- Existing scope is already included in a package's value. Calling the
  five-point patron "commercial" again does not earn another discount.
- A genuine reduction in obtainable support can justify a lower whole value;
  there is no automatic one-point refund per limitation label.
- A contact present only during an annual fair has reduced practical value
  in a year-round campaign if no substitute communication route exists.
  If the whole campaign takes place at that fair, the same fact supplies no
  demonstrated reduction in usable support. No fixed availability discount
  is assigned from the wording alone.
- Delayed personal access does not negate a patron's support if authorized
  representatives can still provide the purchased assistance.
- A limitation incorporated into the lower price remains part of the actual
  relationship; it is not then charged as automatic Framework Load or used
  to claim a second construction refund.

### Continuation Finding

Conceptual pass for revaluing the remaining whole relationship. Candidate
costs 3 for the facilitator and 5 for privileged royal access occupy useful
positions relative to the existing anchors. These are proposed calibration
values, not calculations derived from the Difficulty curve.

The substantive decision now is whether to retain those two reduced packages
at those trial values. This is not a need for another contact statistic or
a universal limitation refund table. Once their trial scope and costs are
accepted, the same whole-provision approach can be applied to Authority.

### Subsequent User Decision

The user accepted retaining the regional facilitator at cost 3 and privileged
royal access at cost 5 as provisional Network construction examples ("Yeah,
it's okay"). Their stated support and limitations remain part of the examples.
This approval does not final-lock a universal price ladder, validate the
acquisition Difficulty curve, or adopt the discarded three-rating allocation.
Use these examples alongside the earlier anchors. The next discussion is
whole-provision construction for Authority.
