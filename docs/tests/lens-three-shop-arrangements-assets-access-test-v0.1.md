# LENS Three Shop Arrangements: Assets and Access

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: Conceptual comparison completed; numerical starting-Assets bands unresolved.

## Approved Lane

Compare a tenant proprietor, a premises-owning proprietor, and an employed
manager using existing Assets and Access rules. Identify starting possessions
and permissions before proposing numerical Assets bands.

Sources: the shopkeeper completeness check, the opposed bargain test, the
ordinary equipment construction test's Starting Equipment section, and the
current terminology reference definitions of Assets, Authority, and Access.

No original Character allocation or baseline definition is changed. No dice
simulation is necessary: these probes concern established possession and
permission, not uncertain success. This does not prove numerical balance.

## Controls

Use the same shop, stock quality, ordinary tools, and learned competence.
All three may conduct ordinary sales and negotiate the same modest discount.
Do not infer additional Authority, wealth, expertise, or cash from a job title.

The owner comparison explicitly adds an unencumbered building while keeping
the tenant's stock, tools, and available business funds otherwise equal. This
isolates additional property; it does not claim all real owners are wealthier
than all tenants. These are stipulated fictional arrangements, not legal advice.

The employed manager has delegated permission for ordinary trading and routine
reordering within an employer-provided budget. Personal use, disposal of the
premises, and pledging employer property are outside that permission.

The existing shopkeeper's stable Assets Rating is 7.2. These alternative
arrangements are not three certified packages of equal value at that rating.
Hold learned competence fixed without pretending starting material position
has already been priced. Personal holdings outside the business are not inferred
from employment status.

## Starting Arrangements

| Arrangement | Personally held or controlled | Access supplied by another party | Not granted merely by the concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tenant proprietor | Stock, tools, business funds, and the established right to use rented premises | Premises use under the agreed tenancy | Ownership or disposal of the building |
| Premises-owning proprietor | Same stock, tools, and funds, plus the building | Any separately established services or permissions | Extra ready cash, superior tools, or stronger bargaining expertise |
| Employed manager | Personal belongings and whatever personal resources are separately established | Employer's premises, stock, tools, and approved operating budget through delegated Authority | Personal ownership of those resources or unrestricted disposition of them |

Assets includes controlled means, not ownership alone. A tenancy is a real
material relationship, and delegated resources can be materially useful.
Authority explains why the manager may direct a particular use; it does not
make the employer's entire estate the manager's personal wealth. No universal
owner=Assets/employee=Authority routing rule follows. Select the relationship
actually carrying the attempted Expression, and count distinct contributions
under existing rules rather than multiplying labels automatically.

## Six Probes

### 1. Sell an Ordinary Item or Negotiate Its Price

All three can sell from the shop under the stated permissions. Routine sales
remain routine. If the prior contested bargain is repeated with the same
reasoning, expertise, stakes, and circumstances, the relevant stack remains:

```text
Psyche 6 x Cognition 1.6 x Persuade 1.6 x Retail commerce 1.9
= 29.184
```

Ownership alone adds no modifier. The owner's willingness to accept a price
and the manager's permitted discount may differ in another scene, but those
facts were held equal here. A demand outside the manager's discretion requires
another source of permission; a better roll does not invent it.

### 2. Replace Routine Stock

Both proprietors can commit their established business resources. The manager
can order within the delegated budget. Routine replenishment does not require
an acquisition roll simply to demonstrate these different sources.

If a later order exceeds available resources or approved discretion, use the
existing relevant acquisition route. No numerical deficit or Difficulty is
invented in this probe. None may spend an unspecified unlimited reserve.

### 3. Raise a Substantial Sum or Offer Security

The tenant can offer personally controlled stock or other established holdings,
but not the landlord's building. The owner can offer the building if the setting
provides a valid transfer or financing route. The manager cannot offer employer
property for a personal transaction under the stated permissions.

The building provides an additional possibility, not automatic cash or a
guaranteed buyer/lender. Availability, Access, and genuine acquisition uncertainty
remain separate. No fixed credit bonus or currency conversion is assigned.

### 4. Equip an Expedition from Shop Stock

The proprietors can choose to divert their own suitable goods from sale to
personal use. This is a real change in what remains available for the business,
not an inexhaustible equipment source. The manager needs permission for that
use, a personal purchase, or another valid route.

Ordinary ownership or purchase permission is not permission for every use of
every item. Unauthorized taking would be a different declared action with its
own fiction, not proof that the material can never physically be taken.

No automatic Load amount follows merely from removing an item. Whether the
depletion creates meaningful strain is evaluated through existing Conditions.

### 5. End the Arrangement

Under the stipulated terms, after ending the tenancy the tenant retains their
stock and tools but needs somewhere else to operate. Ending the manager's
employment removes employment-based Access, not their learned competence or
unrelated personal possessions. An owner who stops trading still holds the
building, stock, and tools unless another event changes that position.

This identifies different recovery needs after disruption: find premises,
restore delegated Access, or repair/redeploy an owned holding. Loss of one
relationship does not automatically create Physical or Mental Load. Meaningful
Framework pressure can arise without adding another ownership track.

### 6. Use the Same Tool

With valid Access, the same tool has the same relevant equipment function and
modifier regardless of who owns it. A higher Assets Rating does not improve
its workmanship. No modifier placement is adopted by this test.

Similarly, a building may support Infrastructure functions where established;
ownership alone does not automatically raise personal Scale, Capacity, or every
Expression. Functional Scale is not assigned just because it is a building.

## Findings

The three arrangements are distinguishable through existing rules. They do not
require separate commercial occupations, a property subsystem, or a merchant
bonus. Training, material position, delegated authority, current permission,
and equipment function remain different contributions.

The owner control has additional material capability relative to the tenant
control because it explicitly adds a usable holding with nothing removed.
That difference must be accounted for in construction, but the test cannot
calculate its Rank cost or prove it crosses a whole Assets band. Equivalent
packages may differ in liquidity, holdings, obligations, and permitted use.

The manager's work Access is valuable, not a free all-purpose estate. The
comparison does not establish how much starting delegated Authority a Profile
permits at a given rating. That parallel translation remains open; do not solve
it with a mandatory HOLT, an invented exposure refund, or an automatic quota.

The clearest teaching result:

```text
They may all run the same shop equally well.
They do not necessarily control the same things outside that job.
```

## Next Decision

For the first numerical translation trial, propose the tenant-proprietor package
as a working anchor for the current shopkeeper's Assets Rating 7.2 within the
grounded urban Profile: modest stock, ordinary tools and fixtures, rented
premises Access, and ordinary personal possessions. It does not include a
building, substantial surplus cash, or unrestricted institutional resources.

This is a calibration choice for user judgment, not a result derived from the

against that anchor and equivalent non-shop packages. Do not assign a universal
income or currency value, equate asset value to equipment modifier, or label
all ordinary business owners as the same construction tier.

## Deferred Aside: Equipment and Asset Conditions

The user raised this for future planning after the arrangement comparison.
Record the idea without running tests, choosing track names, or adopting a
new universal equipment procedure. The Assets 7.2 reference-package proposal
above remains unapproved; this aside does not accept it.

Candidate: equipment, vehicles, businesses, and other assets may use the
existing Capacity / Load / named Condition / Function Band architecture,
with ten Condition Levels as a possible starting control. An entity need not
have all three tracks simply because Characters do.

Illustrative possibilities supplied by the discussion, not fixed assignments:

- A simple sword might need one track.
- A vehicle might need two, distinguishing physical integrity from internal
  or mechanical functioning if that distinction earns separate tracking.
- A complex asset or business might support a third locus describing its
  established commercial/social position and functioning, beyond damage to
  its premises or equipment.

Physical, internal/mechanical, and business/social descriptions are scope
ideas, not adopted Capacity names. Do not rename the Character's Framework
Capacity or Background Derived Attribute through this aside. Ten levels is
not yet a mandatory capacity for every object, and equal track length would
not by itself establish equal durability across Scale.

Existing related groundwork: `lens-infrastructure-capacity-unification-test-v0.1.md`,
especially Tests 7-11, explored Conditions with explicit external subjects,
their effects on Access, and matching repair. Its proposed Infrastructure
Capacity name was a historical naming candidate, not the current name.

Preserve the subject distinction: damage to a vehicle or business does not
automatically copy the same Condition Levels into its owner's personal Load.
Consequences for the owner require their own applicable relationship/effect.

Future questions, deliberately not resolved here:

1. Which functional distinctions justify separate tracks rather than named
   Conditions sharing a track?
2. Which assets need active tracking in the campaign?
3. How do their Function Bands affect the functions they supply?
4. What does exhaustion mean for each subject, and what restores it?

The user mentioned Shadowrun as an inspiration. No claim about Shadowrun's
actual rules was researched or adopted. Resume starting-Assets development
after the aside; do not expand this note into a vehicle or business subsystem.
