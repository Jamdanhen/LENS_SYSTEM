# LENS Character Creation Vertical Slice

Version: 0.1  
Status: Construction Test  
Purpose: Build one ordinary Scale 1 human through the current LENS architecture
and expose the next actual character-creation decisions.

This is not a final character, Skill list, advancement currency, or equipment
catalogue.

---

## Test Control

The first specimen is an ordinary town guard.

Reasons:

- Human Classification does not require extraordinary permission.
- Scale 1 keeps the test inside ordinary human scope.
- Familiar actions expose missing rules clearly.
- Equipment, active opposition, Conditions, and stable character Lenses remain
  relevant.
- No HOLT can conceal a gap in the ordinary construction procedure.

## Stable Character Lens Tests

### Sworn Local Guard

Possible functions:

- grants Access to exercise local legal authority;
- may supply Resonance when recognized authority supports the Expression;
- may create Dissonance when acting outside that authority;
- creates obligations to the community and governing institution.

### Protective Duty

Possible functions:

- may supply Resonance when protecting assigned people or places;
- may create Dissonance when abandoning or betraying that responsibility;
- may expose the guard to coercion through threatened dependents.

No numerical value or compensation is assigned.

## Allocation Stress-Test Findings

The 20/40/60 economy was tested against broad and concentrated construction.

### Broad Generalist

```text
Attributes: all four at Rank 5
Derived Attributes: all twenty at Rank 2
Training: twelve Skills at Rank 5
Specializations: none

Representative Expression:
5 x 1.2 x 1.5 = 9
```

This produces broad competence without exceptional narrow Expressions.

### Equal Training Cost

Ten Training Points may produce either:

```text
Skill Rank 10, no Specialization
Training multiplier: 2.0
```

or:

```text
Skill Rank 5, Specialization Rank 5
Training multiplier when the Specialization applies: 2.25
Parent Skill multiplier outside the Specialization: 1.5
```

Specialization therefore creates a measurable narrow advantage while general
Skill development remains more broadly applicable.

### Maximum Concentration

The current pools legally permit:

```text
Attributes: 10 / 4 / 3 / 3
Derived Attributes: four children of the Rank 10 Attribute at Rank 10
Training: three Skill Rank 10 / Specialization Rank 10 pairs
```

Each of three fully aligned narrow Expressions may reach:

```text
10 x 2.0 x 2.0 x 2.0 = 80
```

This does not break the current construction economy. The character has spent
its development on three narrow Expressions and remains comparatively
undeveloped elsewhere. Rank 80 is not automatic success and does not grant
extraordinary permission or a higher Scale.

The test therefore supports allowing Rank 10 during character creation rather
than adding a lower universal starting ceiling, escalating Rank prices, or a
level-based unlock.

## Exact Next Decision

```text
What counts as one Skill, and how broad may one Skill be?
```

The construction economy cannot be evaluated completely until Skills have
comparable scope. Broad and narrow Skill labels must not receive the same cost
without performing equivalent mechanical work.
