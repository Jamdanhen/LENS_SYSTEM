## Higher-Order Lens Architecture

Conceptually locked:

```text
HOLD
Higher-Order Lens Definition

HOLT
Higher-Order Lens Theme

Axis
The relationship through which a HOLT expresses through one Derived Attribute

Rank
An Axis is ranked or unranked

Access
Open
Condition
Closed
```

A HOLD establishes setting-facing access, construction, Rank limits,
limitations, weaknesses, and advancement. It is mechanical but does not
multiply an Expression.

A HOLT grants coherent thematic permission. Its Rank sets the maximum Rank of
every Axis beneath it. The parent HOLT Rank is not a universal multiplier.

A ranked Axis supplies the HOLT-derived modifier when Access permits. An Open,
unranked Axis grants thematic permission without a HOLT-derived modifier. A
Closed Axis removes thematic permission through that Derived Attribute.

No separate Focused, Unfocused, Invested, Developed, or other binary investment
field is used.

## Setting-Calibrated Construction

Conceptually resolved:

- the applicable HOLD and campaign profile determine starting HOLT Ranks;
- they determine Axis allocation at acquisition and advancement;
- they determine advancement cost and speed;
- they determine compensation for Closed and Condition Access;
- limitation compensation remains inside the same HOLT;
- reinvestment may not exceed the parent HOLT Rank;
- limitation values require setting-specific tests;
- high-powered starting allocations must be tested against larger limitation
  returns to prevent double scaling.

One limitation procedure may target one Axis, several Axes, or an entire HOLT.

Removing, replacing, or transforming a limitation requires both mechanical
cost and narrative change permitted by the HOLD and setting.

## Combined Axes Within One HOLT

Conceptually locked:

```text
A single manifestation uses its applicable Axis.

A combined manifestation requiring several Axes from the same HOLT uses the
lowest required Axis Rank as the controlling HOLT contribution.

Required Axis Ranks do not stack.
```

This preserves each Derived Attribute's relevance. A combined manifestation
may accomplish more, but its weakest required Axis limits the integrated use.

## Compound HOLT State

Preserved distinctions:

```text
Primary function
The principal objective supplied by one HOLT Axis.

Secondary function
An independently rated effect supplied by another HOLT Axis.

Contextual Resonance
A contribution that meaningfully agrees with the current objective,
opposition, or resistance.

Compound exposure
The separately counterable relationships created by each contributor and by
their combination.
```

A secondary HOLT does not automatically improve the primary function merely
because it supplies the manifestation's medium.

Example:

```text
A protective fire barrier against ordinary arrows:
Protection supplies defense.
Fire may supply heat, ignition, threat, or another secondary effect.

The same barrier against a frost attack:
Fire may also become contextual Resonance because it directly answers the
current resistance.
```

Compound capability and compound exposure may increase together. The exact
increase remains unresolved.
