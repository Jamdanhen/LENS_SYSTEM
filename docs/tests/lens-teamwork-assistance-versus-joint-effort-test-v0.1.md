# Teamwork: Assistance Versus Joint Effort

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: Approved test completed. Recommendations remain candidates; no team
formula, automatic helper bonus, or organizational system adopted.

Reassessment, 2026-09-11: The assistant withdraws the recommendation below
to make lead-Expression assistance the default starting architecture for

Expression principles. The original analysis is retained as a historical

extension of the two-Axis mean to complete character Expressions is adopted.

Further source recovery, 2026-09-11: Example Library Part V, Examples 11-13,

it omitted separately viable successful contributions and did not compare
the output earned by two committed actions. General contribution valuation
remains open. No further lead-helper or averaged-team default is recommended.

## Result

One lead Expression with assessed assistance is a workable default for ordinary
cooperation when it fairly represents the activity being resolved. It is not
a universal substitute for every participant's independently consequential
action. No tested ordinary case requires adding or averaging full character
Expressions merely because several people participate.

This is a fictional-scope and arithmetic consistency test, not an empirical
playtest of table speed or a quantitative model of combined physical strength.
It does not establish a universal numerical formula for crews, crowds, or
cross-Scale collective capability.

## Existing Rules Used

- Applicability and distinct contribution determine what actually helps.
- Context is max(0, 1 + Resonance - Dissonance), with otherwise unassigned
  contributions assessed in ten-percent steps; no universal helper ceiling.
- Routine contributions need not be rolled. Genuinely uncertain preparation
  resolves normally, with no automatic Effect-to-Resonance conversion.
- Actual scope determines the number of Expressions, not the number of people
  or the number of sentences used to describe their work.
- Existing action accounting, Delay, and held-response timing continue to apply.
- Access and Scale are not manufactured by numerical bonuses.
- Effects retain one traceable path. A shared roll does not create a separate
  full copy of its Effects for every participant.
- The adopted mean for two HOLT Axis modifiers is not an adopted formula for
  averaging different characters' complete Expressions.

Authority: current baseline sections Applicability, Distinct Contribution,
Current Roll Budget, Preparation, Resolution Scope, Compound Expressions, and
Organization and Team Continuity. The last section explicitly leaves team
procedure unadopted.

## Cases

| Situation | Candidate handling | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One character forces a gate; another supplies and positions a useful lever. | The actual means may make the attempt possible or easier. Assess the specific advantage on the acting character's Expression if uncertainty remains. | Assistance works. Do not also add a generic helper bonus for that same lever. |
| An ally removes debris that was obstructing the gate. | Resolve debris removal if uncertain. Reassess the now-absent obstruction for the later attempt. | Removing Dissonance is not automatically an additional Resonance award. |
| One forces the gate while another holds off pursuit. | Each resolves the actual activity and opposition relevant to their action. | A common objective does not merge distinct actions or grant free defensive/offensive Effects. |
| Two people lift a beam that neither can handle alone, but which their combined means can manage. | Establish collective physical feasibility first. If routine, lift it without a roll. If uncertainty remains and one person's handling fairly anchors the joint execution, use that Expression with assessed assistance. | More participants can change what is possible. The bonus itself does not create supernatural Access or quantify lifting capacity. Both lifters remain necessary. |
| One necessary lifter lets go or loses the ability to support their end. | Reassess the beam's actual support, feasibility, and consequences. | An essential contributor is not merely a removable numerical bonus if their absence makes the activity impossible. No universal automatic drop is introduced. |
| One operator resets a local control while another must cross a hazardous shaft to reach a second control. | Resolve the independently uncertain crossing and control activities at their actual scope. Routine button presses need no extra roll. | A strong lead roll cannot erase the other character's consequential hazard. |
| A researcher assembles a reliable dossier for a spokesperson's presentation. | The established information may support the presentation. Research uncertainty, if real, resolves when assembling it. | Portable assistance does not transfer the researcher's Attribute or entire expertise stack into the spokesperson. |
| An interpreter is needed for a conversation. | Establish actual language access and faithful interpretation. Routine translation can enable communication; genuinely uncertain interpretation can require its own Expression. | An essential enabling contribution need not always give a numerical bonus or another roll. The speaker does not permanently acquire the language. |
| Several people repeat the same directions to one navigator. | Count the actual informational benefit once; assess additional distinct help only if it exists. | Headcount and repeated descriptions do not determine the multiplier. |
| A coordinated group contributes more genuine lifting or hauling effort. | Assess the increased useful contribution and actual feasibility. | Do not misread distinct contribution as requiring every helper to perform a different Task. Similar effort from different people can genuinely add physical help. |
| Two people use separately permitted treatment methods on separate matching Conditions. | Resolve each treatment at its actual scope and action cost. Apply its Effects once. | Independent work can both help without becoming a single multiplied team roll. This does not grant instantaneous medical recovery. |
| Mundane helpers assist a practitioner who alone has extraordinary Access. | Helpers may contribute ordinary support they can actually perform. Required extraordinary functions still need a permitted performer. | Assistance neither shares a HOLT nor opens an unavailable extraordinary function. |

The beam case does not prove that any chosen lead rating represents every
collective task. Where no individual Expression fairly represents the joint
activity, do not conceal that gap by selecting the largest available number.
Collective capability construction remains a separate unresolved question.
Nothing in these examples requires resolving that entire question before
ordinary assistance can work.

## Numerical Comparison

Use complete illustrative lead Ratings of 20 and 80 at neutral context. These
are controls, not new character allocations. Assessed help of +0.20 or +0.50
is a stipulated fictional benefit, not a value generated from helper ranks.

| Lead Rating | Assessed help | Adjusted Rating | Individual success chance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | None | 20 | 20% |
| 20 | +0.20 | 24 | 24% |
| 20 | +0.50 | 30 | 30% |
| 80 | None | 80 | 80% |
| 80 | +0.20 | 96 | 96% |
| 80 | +0.50 | 120 | 99% |

These include guaranteed individual01 success and initial00 failure. They are
not opposed-win probabilities. Continuation magnitude affects Effects but
does not reverse either special result's individual outcome.

With a lead of 80 and another character rated 40:

- Adding complete Ratings gives 120. This automatically counts the second
  stack rather than measuring its actual contribution to the declared activity.
- Averaging gives 60. For optional assistance to the same already-feasible
  activity, it makes accepting a weaker helper harmful by construction.
- Requiring both independent attempts to succeed gives 80% x 40% = 32%.
  That is appropriate only when those successes really are both required.
- Reliable established assistance assessed at +0.20 gives Rating 96 and 96%
  success without a manufactured helper roll.
- If uncertain preparation succeeds at 40%, supplies that same +0.20 when
  successful, and on failure merely supplies no help, the later lead attempt
  succeeds 40% x 96% + 60% x 80% = 86.4% of the time.

The last case's harmless preparation failure is a fixture, not a general
failure rule. Actual complications and their timing still apply. The numbers
compare different scopes, not alternative formulas proven equally appropriate
for one event. Averaging may be meaningful for a different combined capability;
this test only rejects importing it automatically into ordinary assistance.

Ordinary roll 50 gives the unaided Rating 80 four Effects, and aided Rating 96
five Effects. The helper receives no additional five-Effect copy. If a resulting
hazard affects several participants, resolve that actual hazard and targets;
do not duplicate the original generated pool by headcount.

## Accounting and Boundaries

If two characters lift together during action timing, both commit the actual
effort. One roll does not mean the helper retains a free action to do unrelated
work. Ordinary incidental communication is not automatically a separate action;
use the existing distinction between execution and distinct setup. Simultaneous
requirements must respect current timing rather than granting out-of-turn acts.

Select the lead because their applicable capability carries the chosen
resolution, not simply because they own the group's largest Rating. A speaker
does not make a physical lift an Influence Expression merely by giving orders.
When instructions actually solve a coordination problem, that specific help
may be assessed without replacing the lifters' contribution.

The means permitting teamwork and their numerical benefit are separate
questions. A second pair of hands can make a task feasible. That fact does
not grant flight, teleportation, or a numerical Scale promotion. Conversely,
do not pretend an already feasible cooperative task is impossible solely
because no special team formula has been adopted.

No fixed +0.10 per helper, helper-Effect conversion, forced minimum helper
Rank, universal contributor cap, pooled character Load, or best-roll selection
is introduced. More help can matter when it actually improves the undertaking.
Loss of support changes the relevant facts; established information or already
completed assistance may persist without the helper remaining present.

## Recommendation

Use a lead Expression with assessed, fictionally relevant assistance as the
working default where one Expression fairly represents the cooperative
activity. Preserve separately resolved activities when their own uncertainty
or opposition matters. Determine actual collective feasibility before
calculating the numerical benefit, and retain real dependencies on helpers.

Do not adopt a universal group-rating formula from this test. Crew/crowd
construction and joint activities with no adequate individual anchor remain
open; neither full-Rating addition nor automatic averaging is established.
