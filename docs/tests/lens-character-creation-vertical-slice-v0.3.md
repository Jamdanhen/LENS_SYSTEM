# LENS Character Creation Vertical Slice

Version: 0.3  
Status: Working Construction Test  
Date: 2026-09-01

Purpose: Build one ordinary Scale 1 human through the current 25 / 50 / 75
LENS architecture after adoption of Leverage, Framework Capacity, and the
working HOLT boundary.

This is not a final Character, Campaign Profile, equipment catalogue,
advancement procedure, or completed character-creation chapter.

Versions 0.1 and 0.2 remain earlier controls. This version does not overwrite
them.

---

## Test Question

```text
Can the current LENS components produce a complete, playable ordinary
Character without a HOLT and without inventing missing numerical rules during
construction?
```

The test distinguishes:

```text
what can already be constructed;

what can already be calculated;

what can be recorded without a separate numerical value;

and what still requires a design decision.
```

## Step 7: Stable Character Lenses

The Character records two stable Lenses.

### Municipal Investigator

Possible functions:

```text
establishes professional identity and the applicability of relevant Authority;

may establish institutional Access when credentials are recognized;

may expose the Character to jurisdiction, procedure, supervision, discipline,
and public accountability;

may create Resonance or Dissonance only through its relationship with the
current Expression and fiction.
```

### Community Responsibility

Possible functions:

```text
may support action that genuinely protects the community;

may create obligation or conflict when the Character abandons that
responsibility;

may expose the Character to coercion through threatened people or places.
```

Neither Lens is a HOLT. Neither receives a universal multiplier merely for
being recorded.

Leverage now owns the numerical strength of established worldly position.
These stable Lenses identify why particular Leverage Expressions apply and
what relationships, obligations, Access, vulnerabilities, or consequences are
present.

No final selection count, construction cost, decomposition rule, or
acquisition procedure exists for stable character Lenses.

still requires focused testing.

## Step 14: Multiple Actions and Reactions

The Character has one ordinary action and Open Reactions under the current
candidate procedure.

If the Character declares two independent actions, each Expression's Final
Rating is divided by two.

Example using two identical pistol shots before equipment modifiers:

```text
Prepared Service Pistol Rating: 11.830

Two-action divisor: 2

Each shot: 11.830 / 2 = 5.915

Displayed Rating for each shot: 5
```

This can be calculated. The action economy remains an active working candidate
rather than final-locked doctrine.

PASS:

personal capability stack without a HOLT.

Attribute, Expression, Condition, Load, and Function-Band grammar.
