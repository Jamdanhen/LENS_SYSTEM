# One Unrestricted Point: Limited Recurring Support

Status: Completed conceptual comparison; user accepted the limited price
provisionally with "Okay, for now." The reasoning below records the tested
candidate, not a universal contact price. Baseline and terminology updated.

## Controls

Compare the existing one-point ordinary-language provision, adopted one-point
narrative help, and a candidate one-unrestricted-point recurring-support purchase.
Sources are the current baseline, `lens-language-access-one-point-test-v0.1.md`,
`lens-contact-support-acquisition-comparison-v0.1.md`, and the older
`lens-one-point-network-contact-test-v0.1.md`.

Reuse the older contact's scope, not its former Network-allocation currency:
a local innkeeper, normally reachable during business, holds ordinary messages
and shares local information they possess. Free lodging, major funding,
dedicated labor, dangerous concealment, and regional intervention are not
established by this purchase. Additional help remains possible through play.

Candidate funding is unrestricted points, including ordinary flexible creation
funds, not protected Training. Network Rank is unchanged. Already established
support is not charged again. Normal narrative eligibility applies to acquiring
lasting development; no new mandatory training or waiting period is proposed.

## Equal-Price Comparison

| Probe | Ordinary language | Narrative introduction | Limited recurring support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Communicate with unfamiliar speakers of the selected language | Ordinary fluency travels with the Character | Can establish a plausible social route, not general fluency | Only the stated innkeeper's help; no portable fluency |
| Reach an otherwise inaccessible host through a plausible acquaintance now | Language alone does not supply an introduction | Can establish this specific route with GM agreement | Innkeeper support supplies it only if their actual relationships provide the route |
| Collect messages and local information over repeated visits | Permits communication, not anyone's commitment | Familiarity persists; recurring services are not automatically promised | Agreed ordinary support persists without another payment |
| Campaign leaves the town | Useful where the language is used | The meeting remains real; future usefulness depends on the relationship | No automatic remote service or replacement contact in the next town |
| Innkeeper lacks the needed information | Fluency cannot create it | Introduction cannot create it | Dependability cannot create it |
| Someone encountered through an introduction becomes a dependable ally in play | Not an acquisition claim | Record earned support without a retroactive charge | Payment is not the exclusive way to establish this benefit |

All three can have recurring value. Number of uses alone does not distinguish
their prices. Their coverage, supplied permissions, actual support, and
eligibility differ. None universally replaces the others.

## Same-Person Dominance Check

If an introduction and the lasting-support purchase are both eligible at the
same moment, concern the same innkeeper, and provide the same immediate access,
the lasting purchase includes more benefit for the same price. This is a real
local advantage, not mathematical equivalence.

Do not invent expiring introductions, a compulsory scene boundary, an extra
charge, or automatic refusal of a purchase to conceal that advantage. Select
the eligible purchase establishing the intended benefit. An arbitrary request
for lasting loyalty still does not satisfy narrative eligibility merely because
the player has a point. Narrative help remains broader in the kinds of useful
details it can establish and does not require that recurring support be justified.

## Breadth and Spending Check

From the current 125 ordinary flexible points:

| Selected package | Cost | Remaining |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| One limited support relationship | 1 | 124 |
| One language, one support relationship, one later narrative introduction | 3 | 122 |
| Five independently justified limited support relationships | 5 | 120 |
| Twenty independently justified limited support relationships | 20 | 105 |

Five such relationships cost the same as one Specialty Rank; twenty cost the
same as one Derived Attribute Rank. Thus the candidate makes a broad set of
minor support relationships inexpensive. It does not meaningfully ration them.
In an urban campaign these relationships could be highly useful. That is the
principal balance exposure, not repeated message collection from one innkeeper.

Several purchases do not automatically multiply Network Expressions, grant
shared coordination, invent information, or supply a regional institution.
Actual distinct contributions still count; real broad coverage must not be
denied simply to protect the price. No arbitrary contact-count cap is proposed.

## Finding and Recommendation

Conditional conceptual pass for this limited package at one unrestricted point.
It fits the inexpensive ordinary-provision approach adopted for languages and
has distinct coverage from the two comparison purchases. No numerical outcome
simulation can establish their equal narrative value; no live-play evidence is
claimed here.

Recommend adopting one point as the working reference for specifically defined
minor recurring support, using the innkeeper package as the example. This is
not a universal price for a contact, any service a contact can perform, a powerful
patron, or an organized network. Preserve existing grants, story acquisition,
normal Access, and no charge for routine fulfillment of established support.

The recommendation accepts cheap breadth and occasional same-price overlap with
narrative help. If that is not the desired campaign economy, the one-point
price should be revised rather than obscured with new usage restrictions.

Decision resolved provisionally: use the bounded one-unrestricted-point price,
preserving existing grants and excluding protected Training. Broader support
pricing remains unresolved; no full relationship ladder follows from this test.
