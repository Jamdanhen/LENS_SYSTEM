# Aspect package

Date: 2026-09-15; revised 2026-09-17.
Origin: LENS system tests, thread 01a09cd1-7d0f-74c0-90a6-0059d86fed1b.
Status: USER-APPROVED CORE ADOPTION, 2026-09-17, for the revised lasting-Aspect
package with optional character selection and the deferred extensions below.
Integrated into the baseline, terminology, and protected construction references.
No prices, session awards, protected point allocations, or existing character
records are changed. Attribute structure and Leverage remain intact.

Decision history: temporary Aspect spending was removed on 2026-09-16 and
reserved, including a GM-offer variant, for optional advanced expansion. NPC
treatment was approved the same day. Recognition of new Access and Rank 1
together when justified was approved on 2026-09-17 before core adoption.

## Purpose

A Character Aspect expresses something about who the character chooses to be:
a nature they embrace, a bond, conviction, or drive. It can strengthen a relevant
Expression and develop through choices made in play. Character Aspect remains a
working name, not a final terminology requirement.

This is not exclusively a last-ditch reserve, moral alignment, or a reward for
suffering. An Aspect can enhance an ordinary aligned action without desperation,
danger, sacrifice, or a dramatic speech. It can apply to ordinary or extraordinary
Expressions, subject to their actual means and permissions.

The feature has two distinct functions:

1. Access establishes an Aspect the character can draw upon.
2. Awarded ranks provide lasting enhancement when applicable.

There is no temporary Aspect purchase in the core rules. Existing ordinary
boosts and rerolls remain available under their own rules.

Narrative alignment is not itself necessarily an Expression. Accepting restitution,
honoring a promise, or choosing between commitments can inform development
without needing a check. Investigating the offer or negotiating its terms can
involve an Expression the Aspect meaningfully enhances.

## Working prompts, not mandatory slots

| Prompt | Illustrative Aspects |
| --- | --- |
| Nature | Protector, creator, destroyer, healer, explorer |
| Bonds | Family devotion, companions, community, loyalty, promises, oaths |
| Convictions and Drives | Justice, freedom, duty, discovery, mastery, redemption, vengeance |

Categories may overlap. They do not dictate exclusive activities or require a
character to choose one from each. Protector of the Realm might represent a
nature, oath, or commitment rather than merely the Authority of holding office.

Natural knacks and jinxes, such as a green thumb, mechanical aptitude, or computers
misbehaving around someone, were separated into the potential benefits/flaws and
natural Resonance/Dissonance discussion. Approaches and Temperament are not part
of this initial set. They were not declared universally impossible.

## Starting allocation

Adopted starting allocation; selection is optional:

- Up to two developed Aspects, each at Rank 1, providing x1.1.
- Up to four potential Aspects with Access but no permanent numerical increase.
- Neither allocation must be filled. No primary designation or required category
  distribution. Which Aspects develop more strongly emerges through play.

These are separate selections, not point purchases or an increase to protected
funding. Unused selections do not convert to points. This adoption does not
automatically populate or change existing character records.

Potential Aspects identify possibilities for narrative development. At Rank 0
they contribute x1.0 and do not unlock a purchasable temporary Aspect increase.

The earlier three-rank starting-pool alternative was not selected. Flaw-funded
additional or stronger starting Aspects remain a separate unpriced candidate,
not an exception granted by this package.

The allocation concerns creation. This package does not adopt a lifetime cap on
Aspects. New Aspects can emerge through play under the recognition guidance below.

### Recognizing a New Aspect During Play

User-approved core guidance, 2026-09-17: a new Aspect's Access and first
rank may emerge together when the fiction justifies both. The character need
not first record an unranked potential or wait a prescribed number of sessions.
Recognition follows the existing GM-awarded development guidance: the player
authors the character, and the GM recognizes what the actual choices establish.

Access can also be recognized without a rank when the story establishes a
potential identity without yet supporting numerical development. Neither a new
label nor a dramatic declaration automatically earns Rank 1. Quiet conduct can
be sufficient evidence; dramatic performance, sacrifice, and success are not
prerequisites. A declaration may be meaningful in its actual circumstances,
but saying the words alone does not establish entitlement.

For example, a character's decisions to take responsibility for displaced
neighbors may establish a new community bond and its first rank together.
An expressed interest in serving that community may instead remain a potential.
These are illustrations of judgment, not a required sequence of deeds.

Use the existing narratively appropriate award timing and resolution rules.
This does not rewrite settled rolls or consequences, grant missing permissions,
or introduce a new purchase price or award schedule.

## Expression application

The working numerical range is x1.1 through x2.0, with each permanent rank adding
0.1 to the factor. Access without development has the neutral x1.0 factor.

Use one most-applicable Aspect for the Expression, not multiplication of several.
The player recommends an Aspect based on the character, intent, actual action,
means, and circumstances. The GM adjudicates applicability, including apparent
ties. A player does not obtain unilateral selection of the highest factor simply
by describing two Aspects as equally relevant.

Consider the fiction before narrowing it through labels. A protector can negotiate,
investigate, build, teach, or fight where the actual work serves that identity.
GM selection is neither an obligation to choose the strongest factor nor a reason
to deny it merely because the player would benefit.

The Aspect factor is independent of pooled contextual Resonance/Dissonance.
It does not alter the Attribute-plus-Derived foundation or replace any Attribute.
It grants no missing power permission, equipment, Scale, action, or automatic win.
The same Aspect contribution is not also credited again as ordinary contextual
Resonance. Distinct helpful circumstances
can still contribute through their existing rules.

## NPC Aspects

Guidance approved 2026-09-16 and adopted into core 2026-09-17: NPCs use the same
lasting Aspect factors and fictional applicability standards as player characters. Their ranks
reflect established characterization and history; the GM need not reconstruct
a sequence of past session awards to represent that development.

An NPC's identity does not make its Aspect relevant to every action. Use one
most-applicable Aspect, preserve permissions and ordinary resolution, and do
not count the same contribution again as contextual Resonance. This introduces
no NPC Aspect-spending pool and does not require every NPC to have Aspects.

## Permanent development

Development is GM-awarded story development, not point-purchased
permanent Aspect ranks. The player authors and plays the character; the GM
recognizes development grounded in the fiction, not conformity to a preferred plot.

The first question is: Is it narratively relevant to the fiction?

Meaningful evidence can include sustained alignment without an immediate mechanical
benefit, knowingly surrendering an advantage, accepting a harder path, or making a
sacrifice to follow through. Quiet choices matter. Sacrifice is not mandatory.
Success is not mandatory: the informed commitment and cost can exist even when
the attempted action fails.

Receiving an enhancement is already a benefit and does not automatically earn
further growth. Nevertheless, an enhanced action can also involve a meaningful
larger sacrifice, and its use does not disqualify narrative recognition.

Approximately three to four relevant sessions is a rough development reference,
not an automatic timer, mandatory waiting period, or counted-action schedule.
Significant events may support earlier development. Multiple Aspects can develop
in the same session; another session may develop only one or neither. There is
no global one-award quota, compulsory streak, or loss of prior progress merely
because the next session supplied no opportunity.

Awards take effect when narratively appropriate. Reflection can support development;
holding burning timber while children escape can also be the moment a protector
draws upon newly realized strength. Between scenes is not a binding restriction.
Existing resolution handling still governs results and established consequences;
this is not a separate Aspect timing restriction.

### Application and Development Examples

These examples explain the existing distinction, not automatic award triggers.

| Fiction | Application to an Expression | Evidence for development |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A protector holds a failing gate while others escape. | Protector can enhance the permitted effort to hold the gate. | The commitment may support growth; merely using the multiplier does not award a rank. |
| The same protector negotiates safe shelter for those people. | Protector can apply to the negotiation, not only physical defense. | Follow-through can matter without danger, sacrifice, or dramatic performance. |
| A character quietly keeps a promise when no check is needed. | There is no Expression to enhance. | The choice can still demonstrate an established bond or conviction. |
| A committed rescue attempt fails. | An applicable Aspect can have enhanced the attempt without guaranteeing success. | Failure does not erase the significance of the character's choices. |
| A protector makes an unrelated purchase for personal convenience. | The Aspect label alone supplies no reason for enhancement. | Lack of relevance here is not misconduct or a reason to remove prior progress. |
| An NPC has a long-established devotion to a community. | That devotion can enhance relevant efforts under the same standards. | An existing rank can represent that history without simulated past awards; it does not apply to unrelated conduct. |

An applicable Aspect expresses who the character already is. Development
recognizes how play establishes, deepens, or changes that identity. The same
event can do both, but neither a successful roll nor receiving the enhancement
automatically establishes growth.

## Tracking is a visual aid

The user's chosen paper aid is one eight-wedge circle per Aspect, behind the GM
screen, shaded approximately to remember development. The app idea is an adjustable
0-100% thumb slider. These are visual representations, not eight required acts,
objective percentages of devotion, a new currency, or automatic rank triggers.

Progress, Access, and current enhancement are distinct. GM award judgment remains
necessary. Post-award progress reset/carryover and player visibility are not fixed.
A provisional dashboard handoff was sent to LENS APP for planning only; that did
not authorize app implementation. Core adoption on 2026-09-17 does not authorize
app implementation either.

## Tensions, transfers, and fulfillment

Aspects can pull in different directions without prescribing one mandatory choice.
Choosing family over duty does not automatically damage duty, and choosing duty
does not prove family no longer matters. Conduct can support development in both
where the actual fiction warrants it, even though their Expression factors do
not stack.

Agreed transfer rule: when play supports a shift, the player may transfer one rank
from one established Aspect to another. This is optional redistribution, not a
penalty or a new award; neither Aspect must be completed or abandoned. Purely
convenient swapping for the next challenge is not by itself narrative development.
Narrative appropriateness governs the shift; existing rank limits still apply.

Both convictions and drives can have endpoints; scope matters more than category.
The user proposed promoting a potential or paying off a fulfilled Aspect. The
subsequent working direction favors promoting an established potential and
carrying earned enhancement forward where the fiction supports the transition,
without forcing the character to retain a concluded pursuit. Paying off an Aspect
remains an unvalued alternative. Combining fulfillment with an already-developed
successor has no adopted arithmetic; do not silently add both ratings.

## Lasting Enhancement and Ordinary Spending

User-approved candidate revision, 2026-09-16: remove temporary Aspect spending.
The previous one-point/+0.20 Aspect purchase, repeated pushes to x2.0, and final
partial-increment purchase are no longer part of this package. No replacement
GM-offer procedure or Aspect-specific NPC spending allowance is introduced.

For the one applicable Aspect's permanent rank r, from 0 through 10:

```text
Aspect factor = 1 + r/10
Expression = other applicable factors
             x max(0, 1 + Resonance - Dissonance)
             x Aspect factor
```

Use x1.0 when no developed Aspect applies. Retain full precision until final
flooring. An Aspect's contribution is not also credited as contextual Resonance.

The existing ordinary boost still costs one point and adds +0.20 Resonance,
once per Expression, before rolling. It changes the contextual contribution,
not the Aspect Rank. The existing paid reroll still costs one point and replaces
the complete dice result for the same Expression, retaining its applicable
Aspect factor and purchased ordinary boost. Neither may be purchased repeatedly.
No Aspect activation cost, new point pool, or change to ordinary spending follows.

## Sacrifice and Development

Meaningful choices can support lasting Aspect development under the narrative
guidance above. Spending through an existing core option may be part of such a
choice, but no spending is required and payment does not establish entitlement
to a rank or a refund. Assess the whole choice, including its actual sacrifice
and commitment; receiving an ordinary boost does not negate that significance.

There is no Aspect purchase price or spending-to-development exchange rate.
The earlier equivalent-value controls belong to the historical spending test,
not an active conversion procedure. Removing temporary Aspect spending does
not remove recognition of costly choices, quiet consistency, or failed attempts.

## Package boundary and deferred extensions

The revised lasting package was adopted into core on 2026-09-17: independent
Aspect multiplication, narratively appropriate story development, optional
starting selections, new recognition, NPC treatment, transfer, and fulfillment
guidance. Temporary Aspect spending remains removed. Existing prices, session
awards, protected funding, and character records are unchanged. Live-play
consistency of applicability and growth remains to be observed, not assumed.

### Optional Expansion: Temporary Aspect Enhancement

User direction, 2026-09-16: preserve temporary Aspect enhancement with the
optional advanced-expansion work, outside the adopted core and its completion
requirements. Retain both the previously tested player-spending model and the
possible GM-offer variant for later consideration. Their inclusion as expansion
candidates does not adopt either procedure.

The historical spending model used one point for up to +0.20 on the Aspect
factor, repeatable to x2.0, alongside the ordinary boost. Its complete procedure
and tests remain in `../tests/lens-aspect-spending-and-development-test-v0.1.md`
and the companion executable. The GM-offer variant has no adopted trigger,
price, duration, or recognition procedure. Do not infer those from the historical
player-spending model or resume either investigation as core work.

This deferral is independent of the separate higher-rank expansion candidate;
temporary enhancement does not require ranks above 10.

### Other Deferred Extensions

- Flaws funding additional or stronger starting Aspects.
- A valued payoff rather than promotion when an Aspect concludes.
- Moving fulfilled development into an already-developed successor.

Do not infer these extensions or automatically combine existing ratings. They
are not prerequisites for the adopted package and were not adopted with it.

GM recognition strength remains guided judgment unless the user chooses a formal
conversion. The slider, eight wedges, and economic proxies must not silently turn
into automatic advancement rules. App implementation remains separate from
this core adoption.
