# LENS Illustrative Skill Index

Version: 0.1  
Status: Working Design Index for Campaign Construction and Skill Testing  
Date: 2026-09-02

---

## Purpose

This document supplies a larger bank of possible Skills for LENS without
creating a closed universal catalogue.

It builds upon the current Anchor Skill teaching package:

```text
The Anchor list teaches.

The Skill Index suggests.

The Campaign Profile guides.

The Character selects and develops.
```

The index is intended to support:

- Campaign Profile construction;
- player-proposed Skills;
- Skill and Specialization boundary decisions;
- ordinary and extraordinary Character construction;
- examples in a future core book or appendix;
- and lateral thinking about how one learned field may route through different
  Derived Attributes.

Inclusion in this index does not mean that a Skill is mandatory, universal,
free, mechanically adopted, or equally broad in every campaign.

## Current Authority And Boundaries

The twenty-five Anchor Skills in this document are the current working
teaching roster adopted in:


The additional entries in this document are illustrative candidates. A
Campaign Profile must decide whether each selected entry functions as:

```text

or an unavailable field in that campaign.
```

This document does not independently change:

```text

Skill selections.
```

## Governing Skill Rules

### Skill

```text
Skill
= an independently developed field of action.
```

A Skill describes learned practice. It does not describe raw capability,
identity, possession, permission, Scale, or a desired result by itself.

### Specialization

```text
Specialization
= a narrower mastered application of a parent Skill.
```

A narrow practice may become a full Skill when a Campaign Profile gives it
enough recurring breadth, but it does not become a full Skill merely because a
player wants to purchase it separately.

### Routing

A Skill is not owned by one Attribute or Derived Attribute.

```text
What capability or established position is actually making this action
effective?
```

The answer identifies the applicable route. The same learned field may use a
different route when the fiction changes what is carrying the action.

### Permission

A Skill does not manufacture:

```text
Classification;
extraordinary capability;
a HOLT;
HOLT Axis Rank;
Open Access;
equipment;
recognized Authority;
an established Network;
or another permission-bearing Lens.
```

Knowledge about magic does not grant spellcasting. Ranged Combat does not
grant an energy blast. Vehicle Operation does not provide a vehicle. Command
does not create subordinates. The required permission must exist before the
Skill can improve its application.

### Applicability

The fiction must establish that the Skill genuinely contributes to the
declared Expression. A broad Skill Rank does not make every possible
Specialization, culture, technology, weapon, language, institution, or medium
equally applicable.

### Defaults

Omitting a Skill does not forbid ordinary action. The Character may attempt an
ordinary action through an applicable Attribute or Derived Attribute without
trained Skill support when the fiction permits it.

## Index Labels

```text
ANCHOR
One of the twenty-five current teaching Skills.

PORTABLE CANDIDATE
A learned field likely to recur across several kinds of campaigns.

PROFILE CANDIDATE
A field whose breadth, relevance, or terminology depends strongly upon the
Campaign Profile.

PERMISSION-SENSITIVE
The Skill may govern learned practice, but a separate Lens must permit some or
all of its possible actions.

BOUNDARY CANDIDATE
The field may be a Skill, Specialization, application, or merged practice
depending upon campaign scope.
```

These labels are design aids rather than additional game mechanics.

### Permission-Sensitive Fields

The following pattern applies to entries such as Arcana, Psionic Practice,
Ritual Practice, and Spellcasting:

```text
Knowledge may be ordinary.

Practice may be trained.

The extraordinary capability still requires permission.
```

A Character may possess Arcana without casting a spell. A Character may have
an extraordinary HOLT without formal Spellcasting training. When both are
applicable, they perform different jobs in the Expression.

## Labels That Are Not Skills By Default

Some useful Character truths should not be forced into the Skill layer.

| Label type | Examples | Default LENS treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Raw capability | Strength, Awareness, Resolve, Presence | Attribute or Derived Attribute. |
| Character concept | Crime fighter, noble wanderer, reluctant heir | Descriptive concept informing selected Lenses; not automatically a HOLT or Skill. |
| Profession or identity | Doctor, soldier, engineer, investigator, priest | Stable Lens when mechanically relevant; work is expressed through applicable Skills. |
| Established position | Wealth, contacts, office, fame, cultural formation | Leverage and its Derived Attributes, plus relevant stable Lenses. |
| Possession | Sword, vehicle, laboratory, spellbook | Equipment, Artifact, Infrastructure, or another possession Lens. |
| Permission | Flight, regeneration, fire projection, transformation | Classification, HOLT, Access, equipment, or another permission-bearing Lens. |
| Magnitude | Human, vehicle, fortress, planetary | Scale. |
| Resistance | Range, obstruction, scarcity, hostile conditions | Difficulty or relevant Dissonance according to function. |
| Desired result | Damage, healing, escape, obedience, discovery | Expression outcome; not a Skill merely because the Character wants it. |

An identity can still matter mechanically. It simply performs a different job
from the learned methods through which the Character acts.

## Campaign Profile Construction Procedure

A Campaign Profile should use the index in this order:

1. Identify the activities expected to recur during play.
2. Select candidate Skills that describe the learned methods used in those
   activities.
3. Define each selected Skill at comparable breadth within that campaign.
4. Merge entries when the campaign will not meaningfully distinguish them.
5. Split entries when the campaign repeatedly treats their methods as separate
   fields of mastery.
6. Rename entries when the setting has clearer, more natural language.
7. Identify expected Specializations.
8. State what ordinary fictional basis makes each field applicable.
9. State what equipment, institution, Classification, HOLT, or Access is
   required for permission-sensitive uses.
10. Preserve the shared Rank, Training Point, routing, applicability, and
    Specialization rules.

The Campaign Profile should not make every index entry active merely because
the entry exists.

### Campaign Profile Skill Equivalence Test

Before treating a candidate as a full Skill, test:

```text
Training
Does it describe learned practice rather than inherent capability?

Routing
Can different capabilities or established positions carry meaningful uses?

Recurrence
Will it support repeated decisions and actions in this campaign?

Specialization
Does it contain recognizable narrower practices?

Default
Can an untrained Character still attempt ordinary action without receiving
trained support?

Overlap
Does it perform a distinct job from neighboring Skills and Lenses?

Equal Cost
Is its usable breadth comparable to the other selected Skills?

Permission
Does it avoid manufacturing Access, equipment, Classification, or a HOLT?
```

If the field fails recurrence or equal-cost testing, it should normally become
a Specialization, application, or descriptive Lens instead of a full Skill.

## Player-Proposed Skill Review

A player proposing a Skill should explain:

```text
1. What learned field does the Skill represent?

2. Why will that field recur in this Campaign Profile?

3. What actions does it cover?

4. What actions does it not cover?

5. What narrower Specializations can it support?

6. What fictional basis makes it applicable to this Character?

7. Which current Skills are closest to it, and why is the proposal not merely
   one of their Specializations or applications?

8. Does any proposed use require separate permission, equipment, Authority,
   Network, Classification, or a HOLT?
```

Approval makes the Skill available. It does not grant free Ranks, additional
Training Points, broader applicability, or extraordinary permission.

### Review Outcomes

```text
APPROVE AS SKILL
The field is learned, recurring, bounded, and comparable in breadth.

APPROVE AS SPECIALIZATION
The field is meaningful but narrower than peer Skills.

TREAT AS APPLICATION
The term describes a particular use or result of an existing Skill.

TREAT AS STABLE LENS
The term describes identity, profession, position, relationship, possession,
or another persistent truth rather than learned practice.

REQUIRE PERMISSION
The learned method may exist, but the proposed action also needs Access,
equipment, Classification, a HOLT, or another permission-bearing Lens.

REVISE SCOPE
The proposal is too broad, overlaps several Skills, or lacks a usable boundary.
```

These outcomes organize an existing decision. They are not separate Skill
mechanics.

## Important Boundary Pairs

```text
Healing / Medicine
Healing emphasizes restoration and recovery practice. Medicine emphasizes
clinical diagnosis, prevention, and treatment. A Profile may distinguish or
merge them according to recurring play.

Observation / Search / Investigation / Research / Inference
Observation attends to present information.
Search locates a concealed or missing subject.
Investigation gathers and tests evidence about a matter.
Research locates and assembles information from sources.
Inference derives an unstated conclusion from available evidence and context.

Planning / Tactics / Strategy
Planning creates a usable course of action.
Tactics organizes action inside an engagement.
Strategy organizes objectives and resources across engagements.

Presentation / Oratory / Performance
Presentation shapes immediate impression.
Oratory composes and delivers organized spoken content.
Performance presents rehearsed expressive or entertainment material.

Empathy / Persuasion / Deception / Intimidation
Empathy establishes understanding or support.
Persuasion changes a position through reasons, appeals, incentives, or values.
Deception creates or maintains mistaken belief.
Intimidation changes behavior through credible fear or pressure.

Command / Leadership / Enforcement
Command directs available participants.
Leadership sustains coordinated commitment when retained as a distinct Skill.
Enforcement applies an established rule or official decision through procedure.

Trading / Finance / Negotiation
Trading exchanges or commits controlled resources.
Finance manages money, credit, investment, and financial risk.
Negotiation exchanges proposals and concessions between differing interests.

Craft / Engineering / Technology Fields
Craft makes and repairs through established practical methods.
Engineering designs and analyzes constructed systems.
Named technology fields identify bodies of practice selected by the Campaign
Profile. Technology itself should not become a timeless universal Skill.
```

## Working Findings

```text
PASS:
The Anchor roster can sit inside a substantially larger possibility bank
without becoming exclusive Skill ownership.

PASS:
The larger index can support grounded, fantasy, technological, social,
maritime, and extraordinary Campaign Profiles through one Skill grammar.

PASS:
Permission-sensitive Skills can describe learned practice without becoming a
book of powers when Classification, HOLT, Access, equipment, and Skill remain
separate.

PASS WITH PROFILE CONTROL:
Broad fields such as Athletics, Arts, Scholarship, Vehicle Operation, and
Natural Science require deliberate scope decisions before equal-cost use.

PASS WITH BOUNDARY CONTROL:
Healing and Medicine, Observation and Search, Command and Leadership, and
Craft and Engineering remain the strongest merge-or-separate decisions.

The index must not become an implied checklist of abilities every Character
needs or a universal catalogue every Campaign Profile must activate.
```
