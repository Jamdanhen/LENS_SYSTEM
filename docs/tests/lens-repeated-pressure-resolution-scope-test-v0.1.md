# Repeated Pressure and Resolution Scope

Status: Qualitative application test of the user-approved scope principle.
Date: 2026-09-10.

## Active Objective

Apply the approved distinction between narration within an already-resolved
activity and further activity that can warrant another Expression. Preserve
social agency, consequence weight, ordinary action costs and universal rules.
Movement remains parked. No opposed-roll formula or new numerical rule is
introduced. Read local guidance, inherited continuation guidance and the
current social boundary before updating the baseline and terminology reference.

## Cases

| Case | Scope and result |
| --- | --- |
| Repeat the threat inside the exchange already resolved | Saying the threat again supplies no additional roll or Effects for that same exchange. The established result remains. |
| Begin a further episode of pressure after a bounded exchange | Potentially another Expression. Continued intimidation can be meaningful further activity even with the same demand and argument. Apply ordinary accounting, Access and opposition. |
| A roll covered the whole sustained interrogation attempt | Do not subdivide the already-covered questioning into additional rolls after seeing its result. A genuinely further attempt needs further activity outside that scope, not merely another sentence. |
| Present evidence or change the offer after the exchange | The changed situation can support another attempt. Determine its actual scope and stakes; changed wording alone is not changed evidence or leverage. |
| Recipient leaves or ends communication | Check whether the aggressor still has an actual route to exert the attempted pressure. Repetition does not manufacture Access. Ending conversation is not universal immunity from consequences or reachable later attempts. |
| Narrate another movement within a resolved sword strike | No free reroll of that strike. A separately undertaken subsequent strike remains a normal candidate for another Expression. |
| Attempt a technical task again using the same technique | Apply the same scope question. One failed manipulation need not settle every later effort; a roll covering the whole current effort cannot be retried merely by describing an already-covered step. |
| Further pressure produces a valid new Effect of the same type | Resolve and account for it normally. The old Effect is not duplicated merely because it persists, but a genuinely new Effect is not disallowed merely because its Condition name matches. |

These cases are conditional applications, not new permission rules. A later
attempt still needs uncertainty worth resolving; declaring another activity
does not make an automatic or impossible act into a roll. No fixed number of
seconds, sentences, attempts or scene changes is required by this principle.

## Existing Boundaries Preserved

On a successful persuasion attempt, the player may comply or refuse while
bearing an appropriate result. The GM adjudicates consequence weight from the
resolved Effect and established stakes. Neither a fresh attempt nor refusal
resets existing Conditions, Load, Function Bands or Access.

The original roll's scope controls what it already resolved. A broad scope
does not authorize unlimited effects or permanently foreclose future activity.
There is no requirement to invent a new argument for every attempt, no free
roll per sentence, and no social-only prohibition on repeating a method.

## Outcome

The same principle handles social, physical and technical examples without an
additional retry subsystem. This resolves the current repetition question;
no wider rule audit or repeated approval is needed. Numerical outcome severity
and the final opposed-resolution formula remain governed by their own work.
