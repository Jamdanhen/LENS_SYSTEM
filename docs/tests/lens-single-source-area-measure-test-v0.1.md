# Single-Source Area: Coverage Versus Span

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: First area comparison completed. No measurement rule or allowance adopted.

## Authority and Scope

The user agreed to begin with one character affecting a small area versus a
larger area, before returning to multiple-Source assembly. Conversation supplies
the existing direction that intended area increases Difficulty. No concrete
area-to-Difficulty table was located in the current baseline, working reference,
terminology reference, or the relevant area search of tests and examples.

The baseline's Multiple-Source Output and Declared Delivery sections preserve
distinct functions, conservation, actual means, and ordinary opposition while
expressly leaving complementary area valuation unresolved. Example 11 in the
example library gives Fire the burning function and Wind expanded coverage;
it does not supply an area measurement formula.

This test does not promote the earlier declared-delivery rule into an adopted
area-success rule. It examines only a candidate coverage assessment and the
resulting ordinary successful output. Per-target distribution, intensity,
persistence, volumetric effects, and teamwork assembly are not decided here.

Range work preceding this test reached the user's frame-defined increment
proposal: 25 increments, doubling, with meters the personal-scale default and
another measurement appropriate to larger narrative frames. That framework was
proposed for adoption, but no explicit rule-adoption instruction was given before
the user continued into area. Preserve it as the working candidate discussion;
do not silently adopt an all-Scale distance conversion.

## Matched Test Baseline

One Source has permission to affect one contiguous marked surface. Hold
delivery reach, shape control, other context, and duration requirements constant
for square-patch comparisons. No targets or attacks are added. Use a raw Rating
of 100 for the main arithmetic; repeat 20, 80, 180.280512, and 320 as controls.

Two candidate measurements start with the same 5-by-5 patch at D1:

- Coverage: 25 area units at D1; double covered area for each next band.
- Span: 5 linear units across at D1; double the largest side for each next band.

These are normalized fixtures, not starting permissions or adopted area sizes.
The 25-area-unit number is not justified merely by the range table starting at
25 linear increments. A personal surface illustration may use square meters;
the broader frame translation and volume treatment remain open.

Use the existing 0.98 x 0.6^(D-1) curve and final-only flooring. A successful
ordinary roll earns baseline one Effect plus complete ten-point margin bands.
Do not copy that output per square, per occupant, or per recipient. Ordinary
automatic resolution remains available where uncertainty is absent.

## Square-Patch Results

| Patch | Covered area | Coverage D | Span D | Coverage Rating | Span Rating |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 x 5 | 25 | D1 | D1 | 98 | 98 |
| 10 x 10 | 100 | D3 | D2 | 35 | 58 |
| 20 x 20 | 400 | D5 | D3 | 12 | 35 |
| 40 x 40 | 1,600 | D7 | D4 | 4 | 21 |

Doubling both dimensions quadruples area. The coverage candidate therefore
advances two Difficulty steps per doubling of a square's width, while the span
candidate advances one. Neither result proves the appropriate balance by itself.
The difference is a substantive output choice, not a naming preference.

At ordinary roll 30, the raw-100 coverage outputs are 7/1/0/0 Effects; span
outputs are 7/3/1/0. These are not per-target harm or average output.
The larger requested coverage both reduces probability and reduces earned
output under the existing engine. This test adds no separate area divisor.

## Shape Probe

| 10 x 10 | 100 | D3 | D2 |
| 5 x 20 | 100 | D3 | D3 |

Coverage treats the same affected amount alike. Largest-span measurement makes
a long narrow patch much harder than a compact patch of equal area. That may
reflect reach or control in a particular fiction, but is not automatically more
coverage. These geometry rows isolate only the measurement; they do not declare
that shaping, reaching, or selecting every real patch has identical total
Difficulty. How multiple demands combine remains outside this test.

## Recommendation and Next Decision

Recommend covered area as the default comparison basis for area Difficulty,
rather than largest span. It distinguishes the amount affected from the range
needed to reach it and avoids treating thin and broad effects as identical
merely because they extend equally far.

Use approximate, table-facing coverage references rather than requiring exact
tile counts or geometric calculations during play. The square fixtures are a
design diagnostic, not mandatory effect shapes or grids. Equal coverage alone
does not erase distinct reach, control, permission, or contextual requirements.

The meaningful decision is coverage versus span as the default reference.
Do not adopt the 25-area-unit D1 anchor or double-area-per-Difficulty progression
merely by accepting coverage as the measure. Those remain calibration choices.
After the measurement choice, assess a small practical area reference and its
growth, then return to separately contributed coverage and effect output.
