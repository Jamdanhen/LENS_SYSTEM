Prices are the same at creation and advancement. Task/Field equality is a

no free first Derived rank or reduction of starting ranks is introduced.

## Protected Starting Reference

```text
25 total Attribute ranks, protected
50 allocated Derived Attribute ranks, protected
750 weighted Training Points, protected
125 ordinary flexible development points
```

The 25 and 50 are rank allocations, not priced currency amounts. The Attribute
total includes the five required Rank-1 bases. Protected Training buys Tasks,
Fields, Specialties, and Precision at the common prices. Players are not
required to purchase narrower expertise or distribute flexibility broadly.

Protected allocations cannot be withdrawn or converted. Ordinary flexibility
can purchase additional ordinary development across layers but is not
automatically HOLT-eligible. The 125 is the accepted branch 25 multiplied by
five, not a larger allowance. The former 100 unweighted Training ranks are not
preserved automatically: the rebuilt reference deliberately sacrifices some
secondary competence. It is not a universal citizen or superhero tier.

Derived parent caps and existing Rank horizons remain. Specialty and Precision
require an applicable parent Field without a Rank cap tied to that Field.
Precision does not require an intervening Specialty. Neutral unranked x1.0 is
not a free trained Rank 1, and development does not manufacture permission.

## Subsequent Lasting-Aspect Construction Adoption

Adopted 2026-09-17: Character Aspects are core rules with optional character
selection. A starting character may select up to two developed Aspects at
Rank 1 each, plus up to four potential Aspects with Access at Rank 0. Neither
allocation must be filled; no primary designation or category distribution
is required. These are separate selections, not point purchases, and unused
selections cannot be converted into points.

The protected construction allocations and priced reference remain unchanged.
Existing character records are not automatically revised. Later Aspect growth
is story-awarded, not purchased from any of the pools. New Access and Rank 1
can emerge together when narratively justified, with no mandatory delay or
automatic rank for declarations. The core ceiling is Rank 10.

One most-applicable Aspect supplies an independent x(1 + Rank/10) factor,
not stacked Aspects or duplicate contextual Resonance. Temporary enhancement,
GM offers, and the package's other deferred extensions remain unadopted.
See `lens-character-aspects-working-package-v0.1.md` and the current baseline
for application, development, NPCs, and narrative transition rules.

This adoption changes no development prices or session award amounts.

## Subsequent Benefits and Drawbacks Adoption

Adopted as working core rules on 2026-09-17. The 125 ordinary flexible starting
points and actual awarded drawback compensation may fund ordinary development
or approved benefits, preserving Profile-specific HOLT/Axis eligibility and
normal prerequisites. Protected allocations and existing rank prices are unchanged.

Starting drawback compensation has a forty-point total ceiling, not a grant,
required selection, or limit on drawback count. Thirty and sixty remain test
comparisons. Forty actually awarded gives 165 flexible points before purchases;
thirty-five gives 160. Compensation consumed inside a package is not also
spendable outside it. There is no extra species-package allowance.

Record actual credited compensation against the drawback or agreed combined
burden, including the agreed attribution of a capped award and prior buyoff
payments. Use that basis and existing narrative eligibility/difference-based
buyoff logic, not a theoretical catalogue amount or a new removal system.

Benefits provide defined provisions without separate advantage ranks. Eligible,
GM-approved benefits can be bought during play at creation prices; explicit
story grants need no extra payment. Individual catalogue valuations remain
provisional, including Personal Flight's ten-point working test price. HOLD/
species balancing and deferred Aspect extensions remain parked. Session awards
and existing character records are not changed by this adoption.

Full rules: `lens-benefits-and-drawbacks-working-package-v0.1.md` and the current
baseline's Benefits and Drawbacks section.

## HOLT Conversion

Current rule, revised 2026-09-13: unrestricted acquisition 40 supplies defined
Access with no parent HOLT Rank or Axis ranks. Initial-only overall limitation
estimates remain 30 / 20 / 10 for 25 / 50 / 75 percent reductions. Axes cost
15 per rank and are purchased directly, through the Rank-10 baseline horizon,
without a replacement Derived-rank cap. Open unranked Axes retain their
permission with x1.0. No later parent-rank purchases remain.

Legacy grants of parent Rank and existing paid parent development require a
separate conversion decision. Partial-Axis compensation and the replacement
for its former parent-rank reinvestment limit remain unresolved. Do not refund
or grant ranks automatically. Whole-HOLT acquisition reductions do not discount
Axis advancement or create recurring awards.

Eligible permanent limitation buyoff costs the difference between acquisition
estimates only: Severe 10 to Substantial 20 costs 10; Substantial 20 to
unrestricted 40 costs 20. No overlapping-discount stacking, automatic buyoff
eligibility, or area-count Access premium is introduced.
