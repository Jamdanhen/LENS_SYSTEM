## Domain Scope

The setting reference defines Domains as morally neutral fundamental concepts
through which differentiated Power becomes intelligible and active. The proposed
engine mapping is Domain system -> HOLD, individual Domain -> HOLT, and expression
through a Derived Attribute -> Axis. The setting reference still marks this
mapping as a candidate, not a newly adopted universal classification.

Sea, Strength, and Fire have concrete scope tests. The Sea component profile
examines wave travel, underwater breathing, marine creature command, cliff
erosion, and sandbar formation. These are illustrations of permission, not
separately priced components or extra multipliers.

The containment review distinguishes shared subjects, shared outcomes, causal
relationships, and actual permission containment. Overlap alone is not grounds
to split, merge, or price Domains. Its twenty-name roster is a candidate review,
not a closed or fully approved catalogue. Sorcery has subsequently developed
into the Actualization description below.

## Actualization and Heralds Sorcery

Actualization is the accepted working name for broadly bringing intended
possibilities into operative reality. Sorcery is its Heralds identity, not a
second purchased Domain or multiplier.

The Heralds implementation has a non-negotiable one-active-manifestation limit.
The latest user clarification separates this limit from Actualization itself:
other setting implementations could have different limitations or lack this one.
Breadth of Access is not universal mastery or unlimited Scale.

Switching to a different manifestation releases the current one and ends effects
dependent on that support. Independent consequences already produced do not
automatically disappear. One manifestation is not automatically one object or
target; that boundary remains open.

The tested sequence distinguishes establishment through borrowed thematic Access
from subsequently backing the existing magic through native Sorcery. The initial
test used the lower thematic contribution; later support used native Sorcery.
It did not repeatedly multiply the Sorcery bonus or add new restraint layers.
The newer different-HOLT averaging candidate has not automatically revised this
establishment procedure.

## Active Maintenance

The user clarified that an applicable Expression can maintain or restore an
existing effect. Maintaining a grip, reasserting mental restraint, redirecting
water pressure, repairing a damaged barrier, and adding fresh material need
not be identical operations.

Maintenance requires the actual applicable action; it is not free restoration
or another automatically stacked multiplier. Lack of a maintenance action is
not a universal decay rule. Dependencies and opposition determine what happens.
Exact numerical updates after weaker or failed renewal remain unresolved.

## Compound Domain Candidate

Intended benefit: genuinely broader usable function, Access, or possible effects,
not a bonus for describing one contribution with two Domain names. Unequal
development should reduce execution without forcing it to the weakest level.
Equal development retaining its rating is an accepted generalist benefit; no
extra focus penalty was added to cancel it.

Latest tested candidate: arithmetic mean of two applicable higher-order
contributions. Everything else in the Expression remains fixed.

| Contributions | Stronger alone | Minimum | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| x1.8 / x1.2 | x1.8 | x1.2 | x1.5 |
| x1.8 / x1.0 with Open Access | x1.8 | x1.0 | x1.4 |
| x1.8 / x1.8 | x1.8 | x1.8 | x1.8 |

The test reports 121 rank-pair arithmetic checks and six selected dice pairs per
scenario. These are behavior checks, not complete balance or empirical play data.
For the recorded x1.8/x1.2 matched example, stronger-alone/minimum/mean generated
4/1/3 Effect Levels. Additional available effects share the earned Effect Levels;
they do not each receive a duplicate allocation.

Rank distributions 8/2 and 5/5 both produce a mean of x1.5. The first favors one
standalone application; the second has more balanced standalone capability.
Equal Axis-rank totals are not proof of equal purchase cost while pricing is open.

Closed Access cannot be averaged open. Countering one contribution may leave an
independent residual Expression, while removing a necessary delivery component
can prevent dependent effects. Removing control does not undo completed injury.

At the original retrieval, the mean was a candidate and the same-HOLT minimum
was still locked. Jamie subsequently approved the mean for two genuinely
required higher-order Axis contributions, within one HOLT or across different
HOLTs. That explicit handoff replaces the old minimum for the two-Axis case.
It does not settle three or more contributors, mixed non-HOLT contributions,
team rolls, or change Sorcery's initial-access procedure.

### Approved Heralds Technology Requirement

Each technological Expression requires appropriate technological means. Those
means may already exist, be constructed beforehand, or be improvised from
available resources. This is normal Heralds Technology Access, not an optional
restriction on only selected characters. It does not demand preparation anew
for each use of a functioning device.

Prepared devices provide readiness. Loose components provide an opportunity
for construction, not a completed capability. Cannibalizing a device can
exchange an existing function for a needed one. That sacrifice must actually
occur before being counted. Improvisation does not automatically impose
single-use operation, instability, reduced power, and consumption together.

Advanced reconfigurable means can support many functions. Several independent
devices can coexist; Sorcery's one-manifestation limit does not transfer.
Existing action rules still govern use. Actualization and Technology do not
automatically become two purchases or two multipliers.

### Cost-Comparison Implications

These are main-task inferences for the pending cost discussion, not prices
adopted in Heralds:

- Broad potential scope and immediately usable capability are different.
- Sorcery constrains simultaneous supported manifestations. Technology
  constrains ready capability through suitable means and their dependencies.
- Both can have broad scope while creating substantially different practical
  burdens. Neither limitation has an established percentage value.
- A limitation built into a HOLT's normal definition still belongs in its
  whole-package estimate. Calling it normal Access does not erase its burden;
  counting it in that estimate does not justify a second automatic discount.
- Preparation time, parts, and sacrificed equipment must be assessed as the
  actual combined burden, not several automatic discounts for one dependency.
- Rating advancement remains distinct from acquiring permission. These records
  do not supply HOLT acquisition prices, HOLT Rank prices, Axis advancement
  prices, or a new multiplier progression.

The existing area-count x5 aid can compare permitted scope; the current
combined-burden guidance can describe its restrictions. Neither alone prices
the readiness, breadth within an area, or simultaneous function shown here.
