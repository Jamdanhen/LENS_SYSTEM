# Sustaining Action Accounting Comparison v0.1

Date: 2026-09-12

Status: user-authorized scenario comparison complete. The user subsequently
explicitly approved active-sustaining action accounting on 2026-09-12. The
baseline and terminology reference now record Active Sustaining. The comparison
below retains its decision context; no universal duration, maintenance roll,
action-per-effect tax, or new interruption system is adopted.

## Existing Rules Used

- One ordinary action per round; additional-action attempts at reduced
  capability remain available. Ordinary accompanying movement remains subject
  to its own rules and actual feasibility.
- Separate activities follow existing action accounting. Automatic execution
  can still take an action; absence of a roll does not mean absence of effort
  or time. Do not charge an integrated part of an activity twice.
- Repeating an already-resolved activity's description does not generate
  further rolls or Effects. Resolve actual new uncertainty and opposition.
- A hit or interruption alone does not automatically cancel an activity.
  Actual Effects and changed circumstances determine continuation.
- An independently continuing effect does not vanish merely because its
  original Source stops acting. A genuinely dependent effect requires its
  necessary means. Already committed actions are not refunded, and resuming
  the same unfinished action is not charged again.
- Exposure and further harm follow the approved meaningful-exposure rule;
  maintaining an effect is not itself another damage award.

These rules already answer most of the cases. The open practical clarification
is when sustaining counts as an activity, rather than merely a dependency.

## Alternatives

### Charge For Every Continuing Effect

Charging an action solely because an effect remains would consume an operator's
action for an autonomous projector or for smoke already released. It conflates
continued existence with activity and can charge repeatedly for completed work.
Reject as the general rule.

### Treat All Sustaining As Free

Allowing deliberate continuous steering or exertion alongside unrelated attacks
without accounting would provide extra substantial activities for free. Calling
an activity maintenance does not erase its effort. Reject as the general rule.

### Account For The Actual Sustaining Activity

Recommend: a substantial ongoing activity uses ordinary action accounting while
the character performs it. Mere persistence or a continuing requirement does
not itself consume an action. Establish what must remain true and what the
actor must actually keep doing from the capability and the declared fiction.

These are descriptions of the means, not new compulsory power categories.

## Acting While An Effect Continues

| Example and stipulated means | Character attacks or moves | Accounting result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A smoke canister has released smoke that persists independently | The thrower acts elsewhere | The completed release is not charged again; ordinary new actions remain available |
| A switched-on projector sustains a barrier autonomously while supplied with power | The operator attacks, or carries it while moving if its design permits | Power and functioning machinery are dependencies, not an automatic operator action |
| A character holds a gate through continuous bodily or extraordinary exertion | They also attempt an independent attack | Holding is their ongoing activity; the attack uses the existing additional-action procedure if feasible, rather than becoming free |
| A barrier requires deliberate continuous steering to stay between moving allies and incoming threats | The sustaining character also attacks | Steering is an ongoing activity; the independent attack is accounted for separately under existing rules |
| An effect explicitly requires only the user's continuing presence or an available connection, not substantial active effort | The user performs another compatible action | The requirement alone does not consume an action; loss of that presence or connection still matters |

Ordinary accompanying movement is not automatically prohibited by sustaining.
Moving while steering a mobile barrier may be feasible. Moving away while
physically bracing a fixed gate may remove the necessary support. Do not use
sustaining as a blanket movement penalty or as permission to ignore physical
or extraordinary requirements. The movement system is not reopened here.

An autonomous device does not receive unlimited life, fuel, control, or reach
from this example. Its established capabilities and limits still govern.

## One Activity Versus Additional Activity

A modest course adjustment inherent in steering a barrier can be part of that
same activity. Do not charge one action to sustain and a second to perform the
steering that constitutes sustaining. A separate strike, new manifestation,
or materially different manipulation remains an actual activity.

Where one coherent permitted Expression genuinely combines functions, use
existing compound construction. Do not call two independent full activities
one compound Expression merely to remove their action cost. Conversely, do
not require a new maintenance Expression for ordinary execution already
included in the action being resolved.

In combat, devoting the ordinary action to substantial active support accounts
for that ongoing work. Attempting a separate attack in the same round uses
existing additional-action handling, not a special sustaining divisor or an
automatic prohibition. Apply reduced capability where relevant under that
procedure; an otherwise automatic hold is not forced to roll merely because
it occupies an action. If divided effort makes the hold genuinely uncertain,
resolve the actual uncertainty normally.

Previously imposed Conditions or independent completed output are not
retroactively recalculated merely because the Source subsequently takes
another action. New performance and changed supporting facts can affect what
is currently sustained, but an action choice does not rewrite past results.

## Interruption Comparison

| Event | Released smoke | Autonomous powered barrier | Barrier requiring active support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source takes a minor hit that does not remove necessary means | No automatic change | No automatic change | No automatic collapse or obligatory concentration roll |
| Source becomes unconscious | Continues if the smoke's own conditions still support it | Continues if operator consciousness is not a requirement | Cannot retain support that actually requires the actor's conscious activity |
| Projector loses necessary power | Not applicable to already independent smoke | Barrier loses its sustaining means | Relevant only if this effect also requires that power |
| A specific counter disperses or suppresses the effect | Apply its actual reach and Effects | Apply its actual reach and Effects | Apply its actual reach and Effects |
| Character completes a feasible open defensive reaction | Reaction alone does not remove smoke | Reaction alone does not turn off the device | Check actual compatibility; reacting is not automatically loss of support |

For an actively braced gate, a defensive movement that actually releases the
brace can remove support even though the reaction is normally available.
A defense that leaves the necessary support intact need not end it. Do not
add a universal check merely to ask whether the character was distracted.

If an interruption leaves the same activity feasible, resume under existing
committed-action rules without paying for that same action again. If the effect
has actually ended and must be recreated, that is new work under normal rules,
not a refunded or resumed successful manifestation. Preserve viable residuals
and previously inflicted Conditions.

## Output And Exposure

Sustaining an effect preserves what its means can maintain; it does not add
another baseline Effect or increase strength each round. A genuinely new
hazardous exposure can have consequences under the approved exposure rule.
Those consequences are not created merely by the Source choosing maintenance
or a round advancing.

There is no numerical encounter simulation in this comparison. Its checks are
the above counterexamples against action accounting, actual dependencies,
interruption, and conservation. It identifies a coherent procedure without
claiming to price persistence or balance unlimited maintained effects.

## Recommended Clarification

Count substantial active sustaining as the character's ongoing activity under
ordinary action accounting. Do not charge an action merely because an effect
persists or depends on something remaining available. Establish the required
activity from the actual means; do not grant effortless sustaining by narration
alone. Resolve genuinely uncertain sustaining normally, without a universal
maintenance roll. Interruption matters through its actual consequences.

This is the decision now needed. Duration valuation and capability-specific
maintenance requirements remain unresolved; neither requires adopting new
universal power categories or reopening the approved exposure procedure.
