# Special Results and Comparative Placement

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: Ten calculated fixtures completed; outcome-first comparative placement
user-approved within the independent-Effects candidate, 2026-09-10.
Independent-Effects full-model adoption remains unresolved.

## Subsequent Decision

Determine comparative placement by success or failure first, then signed Margin
within the same outcome. Include special results without granting 01 automatic
first place, manufacturing Effects from failure, or changing Effect cancellation
and separate complications. This approves the bounded ordering below, not the
entire candidate or a new decision about retaining 99.

## Question

Ordinary placement now uses signed Margin without changing Effect cancellation.
A special-result failure can retain a positive Margin, while the guaranteed
minimal 01 success can have Margin -1. Raw Margin alone can therefore place a
failed attempt above a successful attempt to accomplish the same objective.

## Approved Ordering Within the Candidate

Complete each roll and determine its success or failure normally. For declared
comparative placement, place successful attempts ahead of failed attempts.
Within the same outcome, compare signed Margins. Equal outcome and Margin tie.

No new roll, Margin clamp, complication subtraction, or special-result prestige
bonus is added. A natural 01 guarantees individual success, not comparative
first place. A 00 failure can still be a comparatively better failure; its own
complication remains separately applicable. The declared criterion must actually
make the performances comparable. This does not invent a rank for an ineligible
entry or an unrelated outcome.

## Consequences

Ordinary placement is unchanged because ordinary successes have nonnegative
Margins and ordinary failures have negative Margins. Special outcomes now retain
their success/failure meaning rather than having it overturned by raw Margin.

Effect cancellation remains independent of relative placement. Three Effects
against three cancel in an actually opposed exchange even with Margins +29/+21.
No new baseline Effect follows comparison. Counter limits and separate own-00
complications remain unchanged. Paid rerolls replace the complete outcome before
settling placement and consequences under the existing timing rule.

## Recommendation

Use outcome first, then signed Margin within that outcome, for comparative
placement including special results. Do not give 01 automatic first place or
00 automatic last place among other failures. Retain complication severity as
its own consequence rather than another placement penalty. The user subsequently
approved this extension to special results; the ordinary-placement approval
alone had not decided it.
