## Current Locked Doctrine

```text
LENS is an independent generic engine.

The Character is the local source of action.

Everything changing chance, access, effect, cost, scale, recovery,
or opposition is a Lens.

Classification defines permission.

Scale defines the natural frame and scope of expression.

Difficulty defines resistance within that frame.

Scene conditions remain objective.

Characters answer the scene through their own Lenses.

Settings implement the engine through their own classifications,
permissions, vocabulary, and resistance profiles.
```

## New High-Value Extraction

The source sweep preserved these major candidates:

```text
A Lens contributes only when it genuinely applies.

Each counted Lens must make a fictionally distinct contribution.

Synonymous descriptions do not create several contributions.

A stable Lens persists until the fiction disables, removes, changes,
or makes it inapplicable.

A named capability may use the shared mechanical grammar while its
fictional identity determines applicability, permissions, boundaries,
persistence, and vulnerabilities.

Compound Expressions may support partial counters and residual effects.

An Expression Constraint may bound a broad permission without becoming
Dissonance or a bespoke power package.
```

These are preserved as working candidates with their source status and
reasoning. They are not all locked rules.
