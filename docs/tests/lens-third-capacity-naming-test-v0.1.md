Version: 0.1  
Status: Working Naming Test; No Final Capacity Name Adopted  
Date: 2026-09-01

---

## Purpose

This test evaluates four names for the Capacity governing Expressions carried
through Leverage:

```text
Institutional
Establishment
Position
Worldly
```

The test also records `Background` as the current fifth Leverage Derived
Attribute name, replacing `Heritage`.

The test does not adopt a final third-Capacity name.

The unnamed third Capacity measures the current functional integrity of the
Character's established position in the world.

It does not erase the truths represented by Leverage. Its Function Band limits
how effectively the Character can currently maintain and mobilize those truths.

## Background Replacement Control

Background covers the mechanically relevant parts of:

```text
culture
upbringing
origin
formative history
ancestry
lineage
migration
diaspora
exile
belonging
inherited identity
defining events
historical associations
inherited claims or burdens
```

Routing boundaries:

```text
Material inheritance and possessions                 Assets
Current relationships and affiliations               Network
Recognized office, credentials, and jurisdiction     Authority
Public expectations, fame, trust, and disgrace       Reputation
Mechanically relevant formative or historical past  Background
Learned competence                                   Skill / Specialization
```

Finding:

```text
PASS

Background contains both inherited and acquired history without implying that
the system measures how much culture or ancestry a Character possesses.

The routing boundaries preserve the other four Leverage Derived Attributes
and the Skill layer.
```

## Required Rules-Language Test

### Institutional

```text
Institutional Capacity
Institutional Load
Institutional Function Band
Institutional Condition

Expressions carried through Leverage are reduced by the current Institutional
Function Band.
```

Grammar finding:

```text
PASS

The adjective produces complete and readable rules terms.
```

Concept finding:

```text
FAIL AS THE UNIVERSAL UMBRELLA

Institutional naturally describes formal office, credentials, organizations,
and public systems. It does not naturally cover private possessions, informal
friendships, family belonging, or an isolated Character's resources.

Using it universally would make Authority feel like the model for all five
Leverage derivatives.
```

### Establishment

```text
Establishment Capacity
Establishment Load
Establishment Function Band
Establishment Condition

Expressions carried through Leverage are reduced by the current Establishment
Function Band.
```

Grammar finding:

```text
PARTIAL

The phrases are technically complete but sound like they refer to a business,
premises, organization, or dominant social order rather than one Character's
total established position.
```

Concept finding:

```text
PARTIAL

Establishment captures something built, inherited, or maintained, but it
strains when applied to an informal friendship, formative exile, or a lone
Character carrying valuable possessions.
```

### Position

```text
Position Capacity
Position Load
Position Function Band
Position Condition

Expressions carried through Leverage are reduced by the current Position
Function Band.
```

Grammar finding:

```text
PARTIAL

The terms are concise, but Position Capacity, Position Load, and Position
Condition can be read as spatial, tactical, or bodily-position language.
```

Concept finding:

```text
PASS

Position accurately contains material, relational, institutional,
reputational, and historical standing. It also describes collapse well.

Its weakness is rules-language ambiguity, not conceptual scope.
```

### Worldly

```text
Worldly Capacity
Worldly Load
Worldly Function Band
Worldly Condition

Expressions carried through Leverage are reduced by the current Worldly
Function Band.
```

Grammar finding:

```text
STRONG PASS

Worldly is an adjective and forms a clean parallel with Physical and Mental.
All five required rules terms remain readable.
```

Concept finding:

```text
STRONG PASS WITH TERMINOLOGY CAUTION

Worldly is broad enough to include possessions, relationships, office,
reputation, and formative history without making one of those subjects the
default model for the others.

The word can also imply sophistication, travel, or secular concerns. A formal
definition must establish that Worldly means the Character's established
position in the world, not cosmopolitan experience.
```

## Seven-Case Scope Test

### Case 1: Personal Wealth

```text
INSTITUTIONAL
Partial. Works for institutional accounts or corporate wealth, but not cash,
private property, or personal stores without stretching the word.

ESTABLISHMENT
Partial. A fortune may be part of an establishment, but the term implies an
organized structure beyond the assets themselves.

POSITION
Pass. Wealth contributes to established position.

WORLDLY
Pass. Personal means are an ordinary part of worldly position.
```

### Case 2: Informal Friendship Network

```text
INSTITUTIONAL
Fail. Friendship does not require an institution.

ESTABLISHMENT
Weak. The relationship is established, but calling it part of a Character's
Establishment is unnatural.

POSITION
Pass. Trusted relationships can support or constitute position.

WORLDLY
Pass. Informal relationships are part of how the Character stands in and can
act through the world.
```

### Case 3: Formal Office and Credentials

```text
INSTITUTIONAL
Strong pass.

ESTABLISHMENT
Pass.

POSITION
Pass.

WORLDLY
Pass.
```

This is the strongest case for Institutional, but a universal name must survive
all five Leverage derivatives rather than only Authority.

### Case 4: Public Reputation

```text
INSTITUTIONAL
Partial. Works for institutional standing but strains for fame, notoriety, or
public belief outside formal systems.

ESTABLISHMENT
Partial. Established reputation is understandable, but Establishment is not a
natural name for it.

POSITION
Pass. Reputation directly affects position.

WORLDLY
Pass. Public standing is part of worldly position.
```

### Case 5: Cultural or Formative Background

```text
INSTITUTIONAL
Fail to partial. Culture may contain institutions, but personal formation,
migration, exile, captivity, war, and defining events do not require them.

ESTABLISHMENT
Partial. Formation contributes to what is established, but the noun does not
naturally describe the whole field.

POSITION
Pass. Established history can create claims, burdens, permissions, and place.

WORLDLY
Pass. Background describes how the Character came to occupy a particular
place in the world.
```

### Case 6: Isolated Character with Significant Possessions

```text
INSTITUTIONAL
Fail. No institution is required.

ESTABLISHMENT
Weak. Possessions alone do not naturally form an Establishment.

POSITION
Pass. The Character still possesses a material position.

WORLDLY
Strong pass. Worldly means remain present even when social and institutional
relationships are absent.
```

### Case 7: Established Position Nearly Collapsed

```text
INSTITUTIONAL
Partial. Works only if the collapse is primarily institutional.

ESTABLISHMENT
Pass conceptually, although the phrase can sound like an organization or
premises collapsing.

POSITION
Strong pass. A Character's position can plainly collapse.

WORLDLY
Strong pass. Worldly Load naturally describes cascading pressure across the
Character's ability to mobilize an established life.
```

## Leading Result

```text
WORLDLY IS THE LEADING CANDIDATE.

NO FINAL THIRD-CAPACITY NAME IS ADOPTED.
```

Recommended working sentence:

```text
Worldly Capacity measures the current functional integrity of the Character's
established position in the world.
```

This construction uses `Worldly` as the compact mechanical label and
`established position in the world` as the precise definition.

`Position` remains useful inside the definition rather than as the formal
Capacity label.

`Institutional` remains useful for specific Authority, office, credential, or
organization Conditions. It is not broad enough for the Capacity umbrella.

---

## Remaining Test Pressure

Before final adoption, Worldly should be tested in spoken play and against:

```text
a spiritual or cosmological Background;
a Character outside ordinary society;
a nonhuman Character with no culture recognizable to humans;
a Character whose Background remains strong while Assets and Reputation fail;
and a Condition that crosses from Worldly pressure into Mental or Physical
Load through an explicit consequence.
```

The next test should determine whether the possible meanings `sophisticated`,
`well-traveled`, and `secular` create actual table confusion once the formal
definition is present.
