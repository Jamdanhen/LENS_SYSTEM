# LENS Fantasy Equipment Package Comparison Test

Version: 0.1  
Status: Cross-Profile Equipment Test; No Universal Item Placements Adopted  
Date: 2026-09-01

---

> **Subsequent working decision:** The tested `x1.00` through `x1.30` range in
> `x0.05` increments was adopted as the working ordinary personal-equipment
> modifier range. The fantasy item map, armor values, and shield functions in
> this record remain provisional.

---

## Purpose

Compare the grounded-modern equipment package against a grounded-fantasy
package using the same candidate equipment range and universal LENS
procedures.

The test asks:

```text
Does fantasy equipment require a different modifier system?

Does the same x1.00 through x1.30 range support fantasy weapons and tools?

Must every Campaign Profile populate every band?

Can armor and shields remain inside the universal Effect and Mitigation rules?

Can weapon form create meaningful choices without a bespoke damage chart?

Do siege weapons move cleanly into Scale rather than extending the personal
equipment ladder?
```

---

## Control Package: Grounded Modern

The preceding concrete-placement test used:

```text
x1.00  unarmed or function-only equipment
x1.05  small weapon or modest personal tool
x1.10  standard melee weapon or purpose-built professional tool
x1.15  heavy personal melee weapon or advanced personal tool
x1.20  service handgun or comparable strong personal equipment
x1.25  long gun or very strong personal equipment
x1.30  high-powered personal firearm or upper personal equipment
```

That package remains a test control rather than adopted doctrine.

---

## Fantasy Campaign Profile

Diagnostic profile:

```text
Grounded Heroic Fantasy

Ordinary personal weapons are Scale 1.

Magic does not automatically raise an equipment modifier.

Extraordinary magical permission belongs to an applicable HOLT, Artifact,
Access relationship, Effect function, Scale, or other distinct Lens.

Armor supplies matching Mitigation rather than a second damage contest.

Range remains Difficulty.

Siege weapons may possess higher-Scale attack functions while their ordinary
operation, aiming, loading, repair, or crew coordination remain at the Scale
appropriate to those functions.
```

| Equipment position | Test examples | Modifier | Principal additional functions |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Baseline | Unarmed attack; required function-only tool | x1.00 | Impact or required Access |
| 1 | Dagger, knife, light club, modest hand tool | x1.05 | Cutting, Piercing, or Impact form; concealability when relevant |
| 2 | Sword, spear, mace, shortbow, purpose-built trade tool | x1.10 | Appropriate form, Reach, ranged delivery, or professional function |
| 3 | Heavy axe, warhammer, polearm, war bow, advanced hand tool | x1.15 | Heavy form, Reach, stronger ranged support, or advanced function |
| 4 | Great weapon, heavy crossbow, unusually powerful personal mechanical weapon | x1.20 | Strong personal function; handling, loading, or bracing exposure when relevant |
| 5 | Very heavy arbalest or similarly cumbersome upper personal weapon | x1.25 | Strong justification; pronounced loading, bracing, crew, or mobility requirements |
| 6 | No default grounded-fantasy assignment | x1.30 | Reserved rather than filled merely because the band exists |

The profile does not need to populate `x1.30`.

An enchanted sword is not automatically placed above a mundane sword. Its
enchantment must state what it actually changes:

```text
permission;
Access;
Expression modifier;
Effect Enhancement;
Mitigation interaction;
Conversion;
Redirection;
Storage;
Scale;
or another distinct Lens function.
```

The word `enchanted` is not itself a numerical category.

## Form Test

The modifier answers how strongly the equipment enhances the Expression.

Weapon form answers what kind of Effect the successful Expression produces
and which protections or vulnerabilities apply.

Diagnostic forms:

```text
Dagger
Cutting or Piercing.

Sword
Cutting or Piercing.

Spear
Piercing, with Reach when the fiction supports it.

Mace or warhammer
Impact.

Bow or crossbow
Piercing at range.
```

The listed forms do not create automatic extra Effect Levels.

A sword and mace can possess similar modifiers while remaining meaningfully
different because a particular defense may mitigate Cutting differently from
Impact.

No universal elemental, weapon, or armor counter chart is adopted by this
test. The Campaign Profile assigns relevant forms and matching defenses.

## Armor Package

Test three grounded-fantasy armor functions:

```text
Light armor
Mitigation 1 against a narrow set of matching covered physical Effects.

Mail
Mitigation 1 against matching covered Cutting or Piercing Effects.

Plate
Mitigation 2 against matching covered Cutting Effects.
Mitigation 1 against matching covered Impact or Piercing Effects.
```

These assignments are diagnostic. They test form-specific protection rather
than historical simulation.

Possible bulk, noise, heat, fatigue, mobility interference, cost, maintenance,
and social meaning remain separate and apply only when fictionally relevant.

### Common Opposed Results

| Attack | Armor relationship | Chance of any applied Effect | Average applied Effect |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Sword x1.10 | No armor | 16.6% | 0.236 |
| Sword x1.10 | Mail: Mitigation 1 | 7.0% | 0.070 |
| Sword x1.10 | Plate: Mitigation 2 against Cutting | 0.0% | 0.000 |
| Warhammer x1.15 | Plate: Mitigation 1 against Impact | 7.9% | 0.079 |
| Heavy crossbow x1.20 | Plate: Mitigation 1 against Piercing | 8.8% | 0.088 |

### Finding

Plate can make an ordinary sword ineffective without making the wearer immune
to every attack.

The attacker answers the armor through the fiction:

```text
use an Impact weapon;
use a Piercing weapon whose form receives less mitigation;
create a stronger margin;
target an uncovered area at increased Difficulty;
remove, bypass, damage, or compromise the armor;
or use another applicable Expression.
```

Called shots, armor damage, and coverage procedures remain separate future
tests. They are not required to explain why form-specific Mitigation works.

## Shield Test

Two candidate shield functions were compared against a Common Specialized
sword attack at `x1.10`.

```text
Sword Final Rating: 18.5625
Unmodified defense: 16.875
```

| Shield treatment | Defense Rating | Attack wins | Any applied Effect | Average applied Effect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No shield | 16.875 | 16.6% | 16.6% | 0.236 |
| Shield as x1.10 active-defense modifier only | 18.5625 | 16.3% | 16.3% | 0.232 |
| Shield as matching Mitigation 1 only | 16.875 | 16.6% | 7.0% | 0.070 |
| Shield as x1.10 defense modifier plus Mitigation 1 | 18.5625 | 16.3% | 6.9% | 0.069 |

### Shield Finding

The small active-defense multiplier is nearly invisible at common capability.
Matching Mitigation produces the intuitive protective difference.

The leading test interpretation is therefore:

```text
A shield's primary ordinary numerical function is matching Mitigation when it
can be brought to bear.
```

The shield may still grant cover, interposition, a blocking surface, bash
permission, or another distinct function. It should not automatically receive
both a defensive modifier and Mitigation for the same protective contribution.

This is a strong test result, not adopted shield doctrine.

| Weapon | No Range Difficulty | D1 x0.98 | D2 x0.588 | D3 x0.3528 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Shortbow x1.10 | 18.5625 | 18.19125 | 10.91475 | 6.54885 |
| War bow x1.15 | 19.40625 | 19.018125 | 11.410875 | 6.846525 |
| Heavy crossbow x1.20 | 20.250 | 19.845 | 11.907 | 7.1442 |
| Heavy arbalest x1.25 | 21.09375 | 20.671875 | 12.403125 | 7.441875 |

As in the modern package, range Difficulty dominates small equipment-band
differences at demanding distances.

The weapon grants ranged delivery and establishes its relevant physical reach.
Bracing, elevation, a prepared firing position, exceptional construction, a
spotter, or magical guidance may supply separate support when applicable.

Reloading and rate of attack belong to the action economy and multi-action
procedure. They do not need to change the equipment modifier.

## Trade Tool Test

The fantasy package uses the same tool progression:

```text
x1.00
Required ordinary tool that primarily establishes Access.

x1.05
Modest portable trade tool.

x1.10
Purpose-built professional tool or complete ordinary kit.

x1.15
Advanced personal workshop tool or exceptional mundane field kit.
```

Larger workshops, forges, mills, laboratories, libraries, ships, or estates
may become Infrastructure rather than increasingly large personal-equipment
modifiers.

## Siege Weapon Test

A catapult, trebuchet, siege ballista, or comparable engine should not be
placed at `x1.30` merely because it is stronger than a personal weapon.

Diagnostic Scale 2 attack function:

```text
Common Specialized Base 16.875
x Scale 2 modifier 1.60
= 27.000 at Scale 2
```

Diagnostic strong equipment contribution inside that frame:

```text
Common Specialized Base 16.875
x strong equipment contribution 1.20
x Scale 2 modifier 1.60
= 32.400 at Scale 2
```

The siege engine may separately require:

```text
crew;
loading time;
ammunition;
positioning;
line of fire;
Infrastructure Access;
and an appropriate operating Skill.
```

Those requirements do not reduce its Scale. They determine whether and how the
higher-Scale function can be brought to bear.

Cross-Scale Effect against a Scale 1 target remains unresolved.

| Question | Grounded modern package | Grounded fantasy package |
|---|---|---|
| Modifier procedure | x1.00 through x1.30 candidate range | Same |
| Commonly occupied offensive bands | x1.05 through x1.30 | x1.05 through x1.20 |
| Conditional upper personal band | x1.30 | x1.25 |
| Unused ordinary band | None in the diagnostic map | x1.30 |
| Primary ranged distinction | Firearm form, range, Access, ammunition | Bow/crossbow form, range, loading, ammunition |
| Ordinary protection | Matching Mitigation | Matching form-specific Mitigation |

The packages differ in content, not in engine architecture.

## Findings

```text
STRONG PASS:
Fantasy equipment does not require a separate modifier system.

STRONG PASS:
Campaign Profiles may occupy different portions of the same equipment range.

STRONG PASS:
No Campaign Profile must populate every x0.05 band.

PASS:
Weapon form and matching Mitigation create meaningful equipment choice without
an independent damage subsystem.

PASS WITH CAUTION:
Mitigation 2 can make common matching attacks almost completely ineffective.
This works when narrowly assigned by form and coverage, but should not become
the default value for ordinary general protection.

PASS WITH REVISION:
A shield represented only by a small defensive Expression modifier is too weak
at common capability. Matching Mitigation is the stronger ordinary shield
candidate.

PASS:
Ranged fantasy weapons use the same relationship among modifier, delivery,
range Difficulty, and supporting Lenses as modern firearms.

PASS:
Trade tools use the same band. Larger facilities transition into
Infrastructure rather than extending the personal-equipment ladder.

PASS:
Siege weapons transition into Scale rather than requiring higher ordinary
equipment modifiers.

UNRESOLVED:
Final item placements within any Campaign Profile.

UNRESOLVED:
Final armor and shield Mitigation assignments.

UNRESOLVED:
Range bands, loading, ammunition, armor coverage, called shots, armor damage,
and cross-Scale Effect.
```

---

## Recommendation

The two-profile comparison supports promoting the following from an informal
idea to a documented working candidate:

```text
Ordinary personal-equipment modifier range:
x1.00 through x1.30

Available increment:
x0.05

Campaign Profiles determine which bands are used and what equipment occupies
them.

Equipment form, permission, Access, range, Effect Enhancement, Mitigation,
exposure, Infrastructure, and Scale remain separate when they perform distinct
functions.
```

Do not adopt either the modern or fantasy item map as a universal catalogue.

The next decision is whether the range and Campaign Profile placement rule now
have enough support to become a working lock while individual equipment
placements remain provisional.
