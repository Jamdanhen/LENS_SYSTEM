# Independent Persistence Difficulty Comparison v0.1

Date: 2026-09-13

Status: authorized comparison complete. The Difficulty placements are sensitivity
fixtures and a proposed working reference, not adopted universal duration bands.

## Same Manifestation, Different Persistence

Use one stationary, non-sentient phantasm. Hold its size, fidelity, location,
form, and behavior constant. It is not changed into a physical duplicate,
an autonomous actor, an indestructible object, or a broader area effect.

Access permits each tested persistence outcome. The character can leave after
establishing the phantasm. Compare two rounds, one hour, and no inherent
expiration. These spans make the cases concrete; they do not establish duration
cutoffs for other capabilities. Indefinite persistence remains disruptable.

Raw Expression is the complete contribution before Difficulty. Compare 80,
160, and 320, with 640 as an additional high-output control. These are not
new character benchmarks or Rank caps. No opposition or paid spending is added.

## Two Candidate Spreads

| Independent span | Shallow spread | Wider spread |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief: two rounds | D1 | D1 |
| Extended: one hour | D2 | D3 |
| Indefinite: no inherent expiration | D3 | D5 |

These are the final Difficulty values for the isolated comparison, not additive
fees to attach to another Difficulty. How different challenge dimensions combine
is not decided here. Neither assigning the highest Difficulty to permanence nor
making it automatically possible follows from the test.

Use the existing curve, 0.98 * 0.6^(D-1), flooring only the Final Rating.
Natural 01 succeeds for the permitted attempt and natural 00 fails; ordinary
rolls 2-99 compare normally. Continuations affect output and complications but
do not change the success probabilities reported here.

## Results: Success Probability

| Raw Expression | Shallow: brief | Shallow: extended | Shallow: indefinite | Wider: brief | Wider: extended | Wider: indefinite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 80 | 78% | 47% | 28% | 78% | 28% | 10% |
| 160 | 99% | 94% | 56% | 99% | 56% | 20% |
| 320 | 99% | 99% | 99% | 99% | 99% | 40% |
| 640 | 99% | 99% | 99% | 99% | 99% | 81% |

The shallow spread ceases to distinguish success probability at raw 320.
It does not cease to have a mechanical cost: with ordinary roll 40, the three
cases still generate 28, 15, and eight Effects. Lower surplus remains relevant
where the manifestation has other valid output uses.

The wider spread keeps indefinite establishment uncertain at raw 320 and allows
higher capability to improve it to 81% at raw 640. It does not create a universal
probability ceiling for permanence. At raw 160, brief establishment is highly
reliable, an hour is plausible but uncertain, and indefinite establishment is
a difficult attempt rather than impossible.

## Active-Maintenance Control

The same phantasm may instead remain only while its user supplies the required
support. That is not the independent-persistence request tested above. Apply
actual maintaining effort and existing action/compound rules, rather than
charging for an independently persisting result the character did not request.

This control receives no invented numerical maintenance discount. Long active
support can still encounter strain, interference, or other real Difficulty.
Leaving or losing required support follows actual dependencies. No indefinite
maintenance capacity is granted by this comparison.

Natural continuation is separate again: an ignited fire can last through its
fuel and conditions without using this independent-power-duration fixture.
Elapsed time alone is not a purchase requirement.

## Recommendation

Retain D1 / D3 / D5 as the stronger provisional comparison reference for this
manifestation if the design intent is to keep indefinite independent persistence
uncertain within the tested capability range. It differentiates the requested
outcomes without automatically treating permanence as the hardest possible feat.

This is a design judgment from the observed probability spread, not mathematical
proof that two rounds belongs at D1, an hour at D3, or every permanent effect
at D5. Do not populate missing time bands or impose these values across Profiles.

Actual Access, other challenge dimensions, available modifiers, and meaningful
attempt scope remain relevant. This test does not balance repeated production,
stockpiling lasting effects, or a surplus-to-duration extension procedure.
Those are not silently solved by a single establishment probability.

The next decision is whether to retain this wider spread as a working reference.
There is no reason to retest natural continuation, sustaining, or exposure to
make that bounded decision. The final area-growth choice and queued pricing
review remain separate outstanding work.

## Verification

The adjacent CJS script checks the displayed cases and 3,846 raw-Expression/
Difficulty combinations across 0-640. It verifies nonincreasing success chance
with harder durations and agreement between direct percentile enumeration and
the probability formula. All assertions pass. No extension conversion is tested
or adopted.
